## Merge proposals

**CRX1. Verify an early correction by what the learner can learn next.** Parts: CV2-01 later-state inverse, CV2-04 optimizer-response correction, CV2-06 repair commutator, CV2-23 future learning response, CV2-25 order audit, NP4-13 evidence-response distillation, NP4-19 equivalence under future operations, NP5-01 interaction between lessons. *Mechanism:* Start with paired fixed logs and an early erroneous teaching write. Transport the correction in shared action contrasts and responses to clean probes, including optimizer-state effects. Estimate a joint correction for two edits only if it survives held-out orderings; otherwise leave them inseparable. Retain only coordinates that preserve the declared probe family, including composed lessons, and abstain outside it. *New:* Two repairs can agree on today's action scores but disagree on tomorrow's learning. Try retaining the response difference rather than another checkpoint or a static negative gradient. *First test:* In an enumerable toy sorter, compose two finite clean updates in opposite orders after an early correction. Compare fixed-log v2 replay, static negation, an exact local Jacobian pushforward, optimizer reset and response distillation at equal cost. Score the first held-out lesson and old skills separately.

**CRX2. Buy the missing experience, not another label for the old route.** Parts: CV2-02 first changed exposure, CV2-07 executable tutoring queries, CV2-08 indistinguishable logs, CV2-09 replacement credit budget, CV2-20 exposure-versus-judgment factorial, CV23 premise attribution, AL4-02 ambiguity witness, AL4-17 shared-tail cancellation. *Mechanism:* Use the disputed premise to nominate the first policy fork that could change exposure under a declared coupling. If two toy worlds fit the log but imply different repairs, keep both live and reissue a tutoring query on the smallest missing branch that distinguishes them. Reuse the suffix only if task state, learner state, remaining budget and reward semantics recouple. *New:* A repair log can keep instructions for obtaining fresh evidence from the repaired learner instead of memorizing labels from the unrepaired route; it also says when the old data cannot identify the correction. *First test:* Build paired toy packing worlds with identical logged rewards but different payoffs on an unvisited route. Cross grading and collection versions in the simulator. Compare checkpoint replay, fixed-log relabeling plus off-policy estimation and targeted resampling against clean online v2 training, giving all arms the same query access and auditing randomly sampled skipped forks.

**CRX3. Carry a progress sign only while the joint solver-and-reward bound supports it.** Parts: CV2-10 paired occupancy difference, CV2-12 reconvergence condition, CV2-14 switchable capability, CV2-16 explicit progress remainder, CV2-17 non-telescoping audit, CV2-19 fourth-corner witness, CV16 split validity and progress, AL4-16 change footprint, AL4-17 shared-tail cancellation, AL4-26 accumulated change bounds. *Mechanism:* Keep the checked prefix separate from its policy-relative credit. On fixed toy dynamics, subtract the two single-axis changes before estimating the joint term from changed reward and changed paired occupancy. Cancel a suffix only after checking sufficiency of the shared state and matching budget and reward semantics. Keep a bound for omitted occupancy and all change since the last independent check; when it can cross the margin, query the largest uncertain block or branch fresh. *New:* The stored object can rule out a joint sign flip without a full continuation panel, even when checking the axes separately would miss it. A checked proof can survive a credit failure under its original assumptions. *First test:* Freeze sketches before drawing new solver capabilities and reward edits in arithmetic toys. Include cases where each edit alone keeps the sign. Match bytes, coverage and fresh calls against successor features plus #408 revalidation and full four-corner branching; score false carries and path incoherence without treating consistency as usefulness.

**CRX4. Turn an unresolved comparison into either a bounded write or a better question.** Parts: CV2-11 feasible reversing edit, CV2-13 partially identified credit, CV2-15 persistent query pair, CV2-18 transport obstruction, CV2-24 distinguishing repair experiment, AL4-23 random skipped-fork audit, AL4-28 preserve competing outcomes, NP5-26 protect reads through later operations. *Mechanism:* Keep the competing continuation outcomes when withdrawing a ranking. Permit a bounded write only in directions that help the identified part, do not worsen any unresolved ranking to first order on frozen probes, and protect declared future reads in the linear fixture. Independently check the finite write and one subsequent lesson. If no eligible write remains, ask a feasible query that discriminates the proposed action changes, not merely their scalar rewards. *New:* The question can outlive its last answer, and its value comes from separating candidate corrective writes. Partial credit need not force either a full recheck or an unjustified binary label. *First test:* Hide a toy tactic that flips only some arithmetic comparisons. Give interval abstention and robust policy optimization the same evidence and extra queries. Measure independent successor accuracy, old-task retention, failures after a clean follow-on write and miss rates from random audits.

**CRX5. Retract a bad reason without erasing a behavior that has an independent good one.** Parts: CV2-03 mixed premise effect, CV2-05 effect substitution, CV2-21 matched correction stencil, CV2-22 quarantined credit, CV2-26 follow-on closure, CV22 independent teaching receipt, CV24 provisional credit, NP3-22 retractable source residual. *Mechanism:* On a matched toy batch, toggle both premises and measure the mixed difference in policy effects before assigning any correction to their conjunction. Quarantine only the disputed effect; use independently verified alternatives to support behavior both rule versions approve. Require the matched unaffected half to stay unchanged, and hold the corrective write provisional until a fresh branch and held-out lesson confirm it. *New:* An independently justified replacement can preserve useful behavior without preserving the bad teaching signal; failure on a later lesson can reopen just the implicated interaction. *First test:* Plant correlated cues, an independent good reason and a real conjunction in a short synthetic sorting task. Hold out their crossed combinations. Compare factorized rewards, ordinary relabel/replay, unlearn-then-relearn and clean online v2 retraining at matched budgets; score old tasks that use the same features and distinguish reward repair from write closure.

On a premise edit, compare against both fixed-log v2 replay and clean online v2 learning without equating the two. Resample at the first identifiable exposure fork, carry credit only within its joint change bounds, and close a replacement write only after an independent successor lesson. Keep uncertain branches as evidence, not targets. *New:* One record tells the next verifier exactly what it can still check mechanically, what it must observe again, and what should remain provisional even after the current reward is fixed. *First test:* In a short synthetic packing-and-arithmetic stream, change a premise and solver together, then add a clean lesson that exposes an earlier repair error. Compare the combined record and its ablations against fresh branching, rollback plus v2 and clean online retraining with matched calls, bytes and compute. Score false carries, old-task retention, cost and correctness after the next lesson separately.
