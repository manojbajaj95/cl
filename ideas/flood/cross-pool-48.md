# Cross-pool merges, round 48

Focal sources: `tree/safe-order-families.md` (PR #195, SO#) and `tree/successor-recovery.md` (PR #196, SC#). Crosses with witness carriers (PR #177, WC#), split-timed deadlines (PR #180, SD#), slot splices (PR #185, SL#), two-clock examples (PR #187, TC#), carrier recovery (PR #188, CR#), deletion licenses (PR #190, DL#), demand-shaped neutrality (PR #191, DN#), teaching cycles (PR #192, CY#), fold-earned headroom (PR #193, FH#), obstruction bundles (PR #194, OB#), and earlier rounds 1–47. These are raw proposals. Freeze the bridge, the witness, truthful data and blind demand. Match utility and protect acquisition-anchored floors, including the spend and all later skills. Use only lawful state and the public transcript at launch; charge delays, proof work, memory and failed checks.

**OSR1. Let the next real skill buy a family of ways back to the repair.** Parts: SO01 partial orders, SO03 frontier checks, SO12 common teaching, SC01 shared bottleneck, SC02 live-interface inverse, CR03 successor counterweight, TC03 joint clocks. *Mechanism:* Acquire a successor on truthful data before seeing the delayed job. Its shared computation must support a return bridge whose fixed launch rule and lease are valid for every history and every admitted linear extension. Require the same frozen witness to serve after the bridge without undoing the spend or the successor. *New:* Acquiring one useful feature could buy scheduling freedom for the recovery, not just one scripted return. *First test:* Match acquisition utility, width and margins across shared and separate successors; compare a checked order family with the same bridge under a cached winning order, ordinary stacking and exact replay as an oracle.

**OSR2. Erase a schedule edge only if the same interface can finish the bridge without it.** Parts: SO02 necessary edges, SO04 later reads, SO06 same cut, SC08 stateful transitions, SC13 later clock, SL07 optimizer clocks, SD05 bridge completion. *Mechanism:* Prove the required receiver-side relation on each exposed scheduling frontier before relaxing precedence. A swap that preserves today's outputs but changes a later slot read is admissible only if the declared launch still finishes and the lease covers it on the same histories. *New:* Minimal scheduling constraints could be indexed by the witness's future read rather than the optimizer's full state. *First test:* Compare update-vector-matched Adam histories with swapped clock-dependent batches, including an exact-safe case a cheaper checker cannot certify.

**OSR3. Hand off a half-finished acquisition without smuggling in a checkpoint.** Parts: SO07 partial handoff, SO08 adversarial arrivals, SO09 local reopening, SC09 atomic recovery, SC21 nonaccumulating handoff, CR16 interruption, SL19 aborts. *Mechanism:* Publish completed events, remaining precedence, the live successor obligations and a residual bridge/lease proof. An independent host checks that its reached frontier fits the proof or uses a specified abort. The receiver must finish without private replay or an assumption about future batch arrivals. *New:* Transfer of runtime scheduling rights could preserve recoverability across a handoff that cannot transfer the original optimizer. *First test:* Stop at each legal event boundary, including states where endpoint recovery is safe but a non-atomic handoff exposes broken service.

**OSR4. Forget slot directions only when the new skill cannot make them matter again.** Parts: SO14 irrelevant directions, SO15 correlated histories, SC14 proof cuts, SC19 shared optimizer, WC04 read transport, DL14 lawful relation queries. *Mechanism:* Compile a proof cut covering both the witness and stateful successor. Preserve the needed correlations in a bounded relation rather than keeping point values. If a future reader voids the cut, buy a lawful timely relation query or refuse, without resurrecting discarded data. *New:* Forgetting optimizer coordinates can remain compatible with forward recovery even as two skills share the same optimizer. *First test:* Add a dormant moment that becomes visible after a permitted callback; compare full checkpoint, output-only cut and scoped relation transport.

**OSR5. Report the smallest obstruction to one lesson working for every hidden spend.** Parts: SO12 one common example, SO13 impossibility, SO17 cheapest refinement, SC17 joint obstruction, SC22 unidentified spends, OB01 obstruction import, DL08 recovery frontier. *Mechanism:* Look for one truthful example and one public recovery mode that suffice on the entire observed history class. If none exists, certify counter-histories against the legal action menu; ask only for a timely refinement whose every cell has a checked disposition. A small obstruction of one grammar does not rule out a richer one. *New:* A refusal can distinguish inadequate evidence from a successor that is physically unable to cover all spends. *First test:* Build a three-history case with no common safe example despite pairwise agreement and compare an exact referee with pair sampling and per-history privileged teaching.

**OSR6. Merge two live return paths without spending the same receiver twice.** Parts: SO10 compose without double-spending, SO20 public modes, SC04 distributed replacement, SC10 two bindings, FH05 joint absorption, FH08 priced receivers, DN14 either pin. *Mechanism:* Treat the receiver and lease as resources shared only across actual joint paths. Prove a simultaneous or ordered handoff that preserves both installed successors, or issue contingent reservations when the paths are alternatives. Reissue any partner credit separately from the freed slot. *New:* Two recoveries can share a distributed successor computation without claiming that two individually checked receipts compose. *First test:* Use compatible and incompatible receiver overlaps with the same solo outputs and charge peak scratch and spent merge options.

**OSR7. Keep the bridge order-flexible when the successor changes gauge.** Parts: SO11 local revocation, SO18 proved slack, SC05 C-equivalent gauges, SC06 nonlinear regional checks, SC11 lift under new C, DN21 support transplant, WC09 quantized binding. *Mechanism:* Give each proof clause a named dependency on the receiver's gauge, arithmetic or region. When a leaf changes, recheck only touched frontier relations if untouched dependencies still match; require exact installed outputs and safe continuation through the transformed addresses. *New:* A physical reroute can keep legal order flexibility alive without forcing a full proof restart, but a coordinate match alone buys no rights. *First test:* Compare exact permutation, coarse quantization and an extra pinned read, measuring proof work at matched admitted jobs.

**OSR8. Turn recovery into a cycle only if each new skill still needs its contribution.** Parts: SO16 successive teachers, SO19 unlock several orders, SC07 canceling lever, SC12 repay displaced reads, SC21 repeat handoff, CY03 return maps, CY04 blind jobs, CR22 successive wakes. *Mechanism:* At each half-cycle, acquire a real successor that contributes a feature used in its own service and by the renewed witness. Verify the return relation under all certified public orders, plus repayment of displaced reads before the promised maturity. Treat repeat acquisitions with unbounded obligations as growth, not a closed cycle. *New:* A recurring recovery could replenish its own interface without a growing bank of deferred teacher state. *First test:* Cross independent versus shared successors with an idle cycle, a real cycle and erased-feature controls at fixed width and equal task floors.

**OSR9. Cache the recovery proof at the smallest complete cutoff, not whichever epoch looked safe.** Parts: SO03 frontier proofs, SO05 unsuitable DAGs, SO21 amortization, SC14 common cuts, SC20 minimal useful successor, TC15 slot-sensitive span, DL06 retained clauses, SD14 serialized checkpoints. *Mechanism:* Identify the frontier representation that actually separates safe launches from blocked ones. If a single partial order cannot express disconnected order families, publish distinct checked alternatives. Attach the real work needed to establish the successor computation and price proof reuse across new importers. *New:* The natural unit of reusable proof may be a cutoff together with one jointly held recovery relation, not a whole checkpoint or a favorite schedule. *First test:* Use two safe order families whose union admits an unsafe interleaving; compare one DAG, a schedule whitelist and frontier summaries with the same checks.

**OSR10. Test whether the learner paid for recovery with useful learning or with delay.** Parts: SO19 multiple orders, SO22 compile away, SC03 informative successor examples, SC18 causal sharing, SC20 minimal acquisition, TC19 gradient accumulation, CR19 crossed demand, RSL9 safe abort. *Mechanism:* Match independently fixed successor utility, legal data, buffer residence and deadline across arms. Re-run with isolated shared-feature substitution, a sham binding, accumulation and a same-state planner; count reusable service only when the same witness restores useful delayed demand. *New:* This isolates a genuinely acquired shared computation from a schedule that happens to buy more time. *First test:* Cross smooth, reopening and no-carry scenarios with label- and utility-matched separate successors. Compare net work, lost admissions and restored service under underspecified checkers versus exact oracle.

**OSR11. A learner that acquires replacements and can choose when to use them without redoing the proof.** Parts: SO01 partial orders, SO02 necessary edges, SO03 frontier checks, SO04 later reads, SO05 disjunctive order families, SO06 joint cut, SO07 partial handoff, SO08 arrivals, SO09 local reopening, SO10 budget composition, SO11 optimizer revocation, SO12 common examples, SO13 impossibility, SO14 irrelevant directions, SO15 correlated histories, SO16 successive teaching, SO17 refinements, SO18 certified slack, SO19 multiple orders, SO20 public modes, SO21 amortization, SO22 stacked controls, SC01 shared bottleneck, SC02 live-interface inverse, SC03 successor examples, SC04 distributed replacement, SC05 changing gauges, SC06 nonlinear regions, SC07 canceling lever, SC08 stateful transition, SC09 atomic swap, SC10 two bindings, SC11 later lifts, SC12 displaced reads, SC13 later clock, SC14 proof cuts, SC17 joint obstructions, SC18 causal sharing, SC19 shared slots, SC20 minimal acquisition, SC21 repeated recovery, SC22 hidden spends, WC04 read transport, WC09 quantized entry, SD05 finish before split, SD14 checkpoint validity, SL07 optimizer clock, SL19 abort safety, TC03 common future, TC15 lease span, TC19 accumulation, CR03 successor leverage, CR16 interruption, CR19 crossed demand, CR22 second wake, DL06 retained clauses, DL08 evidence frontier, DL14 relation queries, DN14 pin overlap, DN21 transplant, CY03 return map, CY04 blind jobs, FH05 joint absorption, FH08 receiver costs, OB01 matching bounds, RSL9 safe abort. *Mechanism:* Freeze the witness and later demand. Learn a successor whose own useful computation can physically replace the spent read. Export a family of public, lawful update orders with a common launch rule, a forward recovery map and a proof that entry and omission leases hold through each shared history. As work arrives, recheck affected premises or take a safe abort; never use hindsight or a revived checkpoint. *New:* The next learning job could buy both a new carrier and the freedom to decide later how to reach it. *First test:* Use a finite stateful host and blind arrivals with shared and separate acquisitions, hidden slots, changing pins and lease expiries. Compare exact planning, same-information accumulated/trained controls and cached full replays against the receipt workflow. Report useful admissions, missed launches, prefix retention, proof cost, donor debt and net carrier reuse.
