**WCS1. Let today's repair seed tomorrow's slot state before it becomes expensive to reach.** Parts: WC01 spend seeds renewal, WC18 lawful job evidence, OS03 phased slot witnesses, OS04 repair windows, LL14 amplification, WE3 delayed response, CP13 re-pairing. *Mechanism:* Before freezing the witness, choose a lawful recipe whose repair update prepares state that a paid donor move can use for the next binding. Check whether that preparation keeps a later frozen edit inside its feasible repair window. Detach the preparation in a same-utility shadow control; a surgical splice can diagnose the contribution but cannot count as a legal repair. *New:* One successful repair could fund the next carrier in optimizer state rather than by leaving another unit idle. *First test:* Match initial weights and immediate update vectors, then vary whether a later slot intervention receives the seed. Compare repeat service and donor repayment under blind delayed edits.

**WCS2. Buy a probe only if it tells the carrier where to move while it still can.** Parts: WC02 probe renewal, WC11 expiring observations, OS07 shadow warning batches, OS18 expiring proof purchases, DQ06 decision deadlines, EP10 source-free prefixes, QRL1 timed queries, XP04 refinance skips. *Mechanism:* When equally useful output-neutral transfers open different shadow observations, price the one whose answers can choose an independently verified repair before the relevant slot window closes. If an earlier skip-probe record is expiring, compare renewing it by truthful teaching with buying this observation; charge preparation even when no edit arrives. Check cover during the wait and retain a common fallback if neither answer can arrive usefully. *New:* Renewal can move a diagnostic opportunity earlier without making the optimizer easier to observe in general. *First test:* Give optimizer twins matched immediate responses and different actionable continuations. Sweep probe latency, repair windows and fallback price at fixed demand.

**WCS3. Hand a witness over with the slot relation it reads, not the slot log.** Parts: WC04 read-scoped transport, WC08 host information, OS08 irrelevant slot bits, OS09 optimizer boundaries, LL5 rejoin relations, EP8 checked adapters, SW4 paired proofs, SD06 pre-visibility revocation. *Mechanism:* Prove that a projection of the optimizer state suffices for the frozen recipe over a bounded suffix. Let a receiver establish entry under its own deployed law before inheriting the shared proof; any omitted coordinate that returns must satisfy a checked boundary relation before use. Revoke omission as soon as a later rebind can no longer prevent the cross-read from invalidating the proof, even if the omitted slot has not yet changed an output. Keep proof that a slot bit is irrelevant separate from the claim that its value can be discarded for all future jobs. *New:* A carrier can preserve enough of the training dynamics to re-run the repair without migrating its entire optimizer history. *First test:* Compare output-only and full-state transfer with projected transfer on exact permutations, changed clocks and a dormant moment that returns through normalization.

**WCS4. Move into a known proof only if it leaves the witness just as callable.** Parts: WC07 local proof repair, WC09 quantized transfers, OS12 arithmetic and clocks, OS15 movement into a proof, OS16 proof-preserving splices, LL8 unit conversions, CP11 numerical bounds, SD03 proof queue, SD17 abstraction checks. *Mechanism:* Compare refining a sound but inconclusive checker with a neutral move into an already certified carrier region. Verify deployed arithmetic, clock and the same witness binding before taking the cheapest option that can finish in time. Use exact full-state and transcript-level controls to discriminate a closed action window, insufficient information and a cheap checker's unknown. Recheck any port whose premises the move changes rather than scoring a smaller proof as a better repair. *New:* Optimizer translation can lower the cost of permission without improving the edit itself or erasing the previously available repair. *First test:* Cross true late slot failures with exact-safe but interval-unknown cases, at several precisions. Compare checker refinement, neutral relocation and a same-cost sham.

**WCS5. Make two probe ports jointly useful without assuming their renewals are independent.** Parts: WC06 jointly honored promises, WC13 paired probes, OS05 adjacent gates, OS17 shared proof premises, CP21 branch-proof interference, DQ04 bundled answers, LL9 shared caps. *Mechanism:* Offer two output-neutral read ports whose joint transcript would select a named witness. Verify both deployment orders, their shared slot premises and their use of one reserve, including the cost of delayed reads. Issue a joint offer only when a concrete lawful sequence or atomic protocol supports both. *New:* The correct unit of diagnostic value can be a pair of probes that consumes one shared future option rather than two independently rentable ports. *First test:* Plant a case where neither read changes a decision alone, then add a shared-moment conflict. Compare greedy probe choice, independent quotes and a verified joint offer.

**WCS6. Keep a fallback that can still diagnose the next refusal.** Parts: WC12 robustness to probe errors, WC14 diagnosis after repair, WC17 cheap incumbents, OS01 probe-equivalent risk, OS07 early warnings, DQ02 fallback pricing, RW19 bounded burial, QRL6 observation-aware pruning, XP09 observability through other writes. *Mechanism:* Among repairs that safely admit the current frozen write, favor a carrier that preserves a lawful separating observation or a verified common reply on the next job. Recheck the entire residual belief after an intervening writer rather than trusting a separation proved on the original twins. Keep the incumbent when the separation is unproved; bound a recovery option independently and do not count it as live service. *New:* Two safe repairs can have equal current cost yet differ because one overwrites the only state that makes the next repair decision cheap. *First test:* Construct equal first-response twins and force delayed demand after either branch. Compare immediate utility selection, cheapest verified fallback and diagnostic-preserving renewal.

**WCS7. Recover the carrier that can learn again, not the pre-edit checkpoint.** Parts: WC03 taught return routes, WC20 regenerated substrates, OS02 minimal slot splices, OS11 slot warranty, WE9 refresh compatibility, RW15 carrier relay, CP20 recurrence. *Mechanism:* After a legitimate spend, search for an output-equivalent recipient that can run the same witness while keeping all skills installed since the original carrier. Use controlled slot exchanges to identify which latent reads matter, then test whether a lawful continuation produces those reads. Repay the donor before claiming a cycle. *New:* A wake can restore a particular executable training capability without restoring the old parameter or momentum state. *First test:* Compare snapshot rollback, exact transplant and source-free return on hosts with a newly installed skill whose head depends on the spent gate.

**WCS8. Sell the same recipe across two admission modes only if the slow one really fits.** Parts: WC10 reversible probes, WC16 orthogonal modes, OS13 multiple proof buyers, OS14 cheap premise checks, LL7 residual rights, EP11 multiple entrances, SW8 prefix budgets, SD01 importer clocks, SD05 bridge completion. *Mechanism:* Keep a single frozen witness but publish separate entrances for a shadow atomic path and a live-served path. Validate the actual observation and slot budget before either path executes; charge a serial importer the inherited error envelope and remaining horizon. Reuse a shared suffix proof only where both modes meet its premises. For each transcript, certify a common launch decision that lets the bridge finish safely on all compatible states; a slow importer cannot borrow the fast one's deadline. *New:* Two buyers can share expensive proof work without treating the cheaper atomic endpoint as evidence that intermediate requests remain safe. *First test:* Vary open requests, scratch and sampler clocks; compare one generic certificate, independent verification and mode-specific entry proofs.

**WCS9. Keep an evidence receipt that expires before it turns into a false slot diagnosis.** Parts: WC15 refusal-preventing probes, WC18 evidence from current work, OS06 naturally occupied boundaries, OS10 retirement warnings, OS20 verifier carryover, DQ18 scoped revocation, WE21 hidden decoder costs, EP21 disclosure costs. *Mechanism:* Retain a compact set of lawful separating observations before retiring the evidence source. Track whether an observation establishes a naturally reached split, supports only an injected control, or merely tightens a verifier. On a changed editor or checker, reuse only the observation and premises that still apply, without reopening the teacher. *New:* A learner can preserve a warning about a slot boundary without keeping the trajectory or advertising that the warning proves irreversibility. *First test:* Blind new requests spanning occupied, injected-only and unresolved cases; compare equal-byte logs, unscoped verdict caches and checked receipts.

**WCS10. Discard a carrier only if the replacement can match its future repairs and questions.** Parts: WC05 donor maturity, WC19 unavailable separators, WC21 sham renewal, WC22 continuation pruning, OS19 invalidated proof costs, OS21 refinement choices, OS22 cost versus answer, DQ05 stopping proofs, CP17 simulation. *Mechanism:* Require the retained carrier to match each useful transcript-dependent reply within the same obligations, timing and donor costs. Compare a real transfer with a same-cost sham that leaves the relevant slot dynamics unchanged. If no sound separator or affordable simulation exists, keep the branch or narrow the promise instead of pruning by immediate menu size. *New:* A branch can be costly to keep but impossible to replace cheaply because its best future action is a question another branch cannot answer. *First test:* Enumerate carrier games where two descendants match the first two rounds then diverge on a slot-dependent diagnosis; report withheld promises and repeated net utility.

**WCS11. Lease the right not to look across a carrier transfer.** Parts: WC04 read transport, WC07 local proof repair, OS08 irrelevant slot bits, OS14 cheap premise checks, XP01 transferable skips, XP17 composition, XP21 migration or withdrawal, LL7 residual rights, DQ18 scoped revocation, SD11 jointly extended windows. *Mechanism:* Before the hidden edit is realized, install truthful examples that certify one affordable reply for every hidden edit in a scoped transcript class. Bind the no-probe receipt to the projected optimizer relation and residual error budget. On renewal, check entry and all compatible hidden states; if a premise fails, revoke only the affected skip and restore a still-useful probe or refuse. Compare an example that extends both omission and entry windows against buying the two extensions separately. *New:* A carrier could import permission to act without measuring rather than inherit a guessed slot value or a stale probe answer. *First test:* Transfer the same recipe into hosts with a cosmetic slot relabeling, a harmful moment change and a cheap-checker unknown on an exactly safe state. Compare replan, unconditional cache and local revalidation at the same service floors.

**WCS12. Teach a safe first reply that makes a fixed probe see the slot split next round.** Parts: WC02 diagnostic renewal, WC13 paired ports, OS01 response-equivalent risk, OS07 shadow warnings, XP10 continuation-wide separation, XP12 indirect slot reads, XP13 rollback safety, XP15 taught gateways, XP16 measurement debt, SW4 paired proofs, DQ06 expiring distinctions, SD02 partial visibility, SD15 contingent reservations. *Mechanism:* Buy truthful examples that make an equal-cost first reply safe for the whole belief and prepare a renewed carrier for a fixed later probe. Verify its separation over the full residual belief, including other writers and rollback. Reserve the bridge conditionally and measure the debt deadline at the last transcript-safe launch that leaves time to finish, not at the visible gate flip; if the probe is merely postponed, it is still billed. *New:* The learner can buy time to distinguish hidden optimizer dynamics without exposing raw moments or pretending that a safe first reply eliminated the future question. *First test:* Match immediate update vectors in Adam twins and include a third feasible continuation that aliases an isolated two-branch probe. Compare myopic first replies, immediate permitted sensing, truthful teaching without observability shaping and an exact joint planner.
