Raw proposals.

**CVX1. Do not split a habit until both contexts disagree on evidence they share.** Parts: T5-01 matched items, T5-02 overlap-adjusted estimates, T5-03 temporal disambiguation, T5-06 approved bridge examples, T5-08 noise and recombination, T5-13 exposure-confound assay, T4-04 context split, D3-09 witness identity, D3-13 compatible histories. *Mechanism:* Require authorized shared fixtures before acting on opposing corrections. Compare contemporaneous contexts separately from a before/after change, and retain the unsplit rule with explicit uncertainty when overlap is absent or the sign is unstable. Reassess sibling branches on later matched evidence. *New:* A disagreement can justify a split, a version replacement, or no write; missing overlap need not force any of those decisions. *First test:* Mix true simultaneous disagreements, exposure-only differences, one global revision and random noisy labels. Compare an opposition-only splitter, matched evidence and a no-overlap abstention arm.

**CVX2. Share the stable trunk without merging away the next local correction.** Parts: T5-04 shared trunk, T5-05 observable context predicate, T5-07 multi-habit regimes, T5-09 hierarchical split and merge, T5-10 multiway split, T5-11 abstain on ambiguity, T5-12 symbolic versus routed transfer, T5-29 forecast-gated merging, TCX2 scope-preserving factoring, D4-24 future lesson, L9W14 observable inputs. *Mechanism:* Let held-out authorized examples decide whether local corrections should be shared, routed or kept separate. Factor only the behavior invariant across supported scopes, and require a future branch-specific edit to still change just its own scope. If the active context is not identifiable, keep an approved shared response or ask for clarification. *New:* A compact shared model earns the merge only if it leaves room for valid differences and fresh branch-local updates, including after a multiway split. *First test:* Use toy style rules with three active scopes, an unseen combination and an underspecified context. Compare full duplication, routed adapters and a checked common trunk on transfer and the next approved correction.

**CVX3. Return a failed text promotion to the original behavior, not to an approximation of it.** Parts: T5-14 promotion stability, T5-15 exact rollback, T5-16 nonverbal adaptation, T5-17 functional text gap, T5-19 paraphrase fragility, T5-20 expiration by approved change, T5-26 discourse side effects, T4-05 protect continuous behavior, T2-21 reversible journal, CR29 versioned relearning. *Mechanism:* Trial a text representation in scratch state and perturb its wording without changing the intended rule. If errors persist across edits, restore the saved contribution and check unrelated behavior rather than distilling the failed description. Keep the verified continuous update route available; reopen text promotion if an approved change makes the behavior discrete. *New:* Rollback removes the failed experiment without making the original behavior permanent or blocking a later representation change. *First test:* Cross discrete formatting and continuous style tasks with paraphrases and approved revisions; compare text re-distillation, exact rollback and rollback plus protected continuous adaptation.

**CVX4. Diagnose text instability by crossing context overlap with representation choice.** Parts: T5-18 partial return, T5-21 tacitness prior, T5-22 joint diagnosis, T5-23 scoped continuous residual, T5-24 medium tournament, T5-25 shared vocabulary, T5-27 transition cost, T5-28 joint assay, T2-10 preserve unexplained behavior, D5-13 reading versus learning. *Mechanism:* When text edits oscillate, first check matched examples for genuine context disagreement, then compare one text rule, scoped text, a discrete core plus continuous residual and a weights-only update at equal work. Treat any learned prior as a way to order scratch tests, not to decide the outcome. *New:* The same apparent edit failure can call for different fixes: clarify the vocabulary, separate conflicting scopes, or leave only the continuous part in weights. *First test:* Factorially vary matched contradictions, measurable continuous effects and the availability of shared vocabulary in toy rules. Compare a fixed residual split and always-text with the diagnostic choice, measuring stability and cost.

**CVX5. Retire solo context but keep the short interaction note until the next edit survives.** Parts: T5-30 equal-token interaction note, TCX3 interaction-only context, T4-16 compact interaction contract, T4-19 precedence, T3-07 joint absorption, D5-26 teaching after compression, SD2-09 distinguishing continuation. *Mechanism:* Compare a short note, an interaction delta and routed adapters under matched serving budgets, after verifying the solo rules without context. Check ordered continuations and an authorized later amendment before removing the note. Keep the unsupported interaction explicit without undoing solo compression. *New:* An interaction representation is tested for editability as well as immediate correctness, so a cheap patch does not hide the cost of changing the rule later. *First test:* Use harmless ordering and formatting streams where the same solo rules have different interactions in supported contexts. Match context and account separately for update compute and stored weights.

**CVX6. Keep shared behavior, scoped disagreements and continuous adjustments separately editable.** Parts: T5-01 matched items, T5-02 overlap-adjusted estimates, T5-03 temporal disambiguation, T5-06 approved bridge examples, T5-08 noise and recombination, T5-13 exposure-confound assay, T4-04 context split, D3-09 witness identity, D3-13 compatible histories, T5-04 shared trunk, T5-05 observable context predicate, T5-07 multi-habit regimes, T5-09 hierarchical split and merge, T5-10 multiway split, T5-11 abstain on ambiguity, T5-12 symbolic versus routed transfer, T5-29 forecast-gated merging, TCX2 scope-preserving factoring, D4-24 future lesson, L9W14 observable inputs, T5-14 promotion stability, T5-15 exact rollback, T5-16 nonverbal adaptation, T5-17 functional text gap, T5-19 paraphrase fragility, T5-20 expiration by approved change, T5-26 discourse side effects, T4-05 protect continuous behavior, T2-21 reversible journal, CR29 versioned relearning, T5-18 partial return, T5-21 tacitness prior, T5-22 joint diagnosis, T5-23 scoped continuous residual, T5-24 medium tournament, T5-25 shared vocabulary, T5-27 transition cost, T5-28 joint assay, T2-10 preserve unexplained behavior, D5-13 reading versus learning, T5-30 equal-token interaction note, TCX3 interaction-only context, T4-16 compact interaction contract, T4-19 precedence, T3-07 joint absorption, D5-26 teaching after compression, SD2-09 distinguishing continuation. *Mechanism:* Use contemporaneous matched evidence to identify which conflicts really need a scope split. Trial text and weight representations in scratch state, preserve continuous effects that text cannot carry, and retire context only when both joint behavior and a later valid edit survive. Keep an exact, versioned rollback path for failed transitions. *New:* A single continual learner can distinguish apparent contradictions caused by exposure or representation loss from real simultaneous differences, without paying for duplicate context everywhere. *First test:* Build harmless synthetic streams with exposure shifts, simultaneous contradictions, global revisions, continuous style effects and conditional precedence. Compare global text, full context routing, isolated adapters and evidence-gated representation changes at matched cost.
