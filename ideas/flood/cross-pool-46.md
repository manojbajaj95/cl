# Cross-pool merges, round 46

Focal sources: `tree/merge-aware-budgets.md` (PR #181, MB#), `tree/stopping-proofs.md` (PR #182, SP#), and `tree/neutral-groups.md` (PR #183, NG#). Crosses with witness carriers (PR #177, WC#), optimizer-slot splits (PR #178, OS#), examples versus probes (PR #179, XP#), split-timed deadlines (PR #180, SD#), mergeable writes (PR #156, MW#), merger certificates (PR #110, MC), leakage leases (PR #176, LL#), X1/Y1 override budgets, rented constraints (PR #145, RC#), and earlier rounds 1–45. These are raw proposals. Freeze demand, the merge operator and protected domains. Separate booked interaction allowance, realized liability and release capacity; prove any refund against the original scope. Deleting question state does not delete service obligations or authorize a write.

**MNS1. Reserve a circuit for a coalition, not for every possible neighbor.** Parts: MB1 local spend, MB4 actual coalitions, SP01 deleting question tables, SP21 independent publishers, NG07 two useful gates, NG16 overlapping releases, LL9 shared caps, MW04 group merges. *Mechanism:* Offer a joint reservation backed by a concrete neutral release and a verified common fallback; charge it only against deployments where the named branches meet. Keep the scoped fallback proof and delete the unanswered partner-specific release table only if no compatible history can improve the joint decision beyond the declared regret allowance. Independent alternatives cannot consume each other's local balances. *New:* A learner could reserve a physical release opportunity without keeping a private table of every partner it declined to distinguish. *First test:* Use forks with a neutral triple shared by two alternative coalitions, then add a deployment requiring both; compare full tables, global quotas and coalition-scoped reservations at equal useful merged service.

**MNS2. Earn merge headroom by folding new work into a live receiver.** Parts: MB13 uniform contraction, MB14 interaction-only repair, SP11 refunding a question, NG13 spent-gate folding, NG14 jointly absorbable gates, WC20 regenerated substrates, RC6 B ratchet, LL15 proved headroom. *Mechanism:* Fold installed gates into compatible live carriers while keeping both new-task gains. Reissue only the measured reduction in a certified joint bound, then check whether the fold also creates a common reply under which partner diagnostics can be deleted. Neither refund follows from preservation of present outputs alone. *New:* A single paid refactor could release a slot, shrink the joint liability and retire a question, with a different proof required for each gain. *First test:* Cross cases where folding lowers interaction, preserves it or worsens the suffix; compare endpoint compaction, ordinary recycling and joint certification with slot and regret costs itemized.

**MNS3. Publish an interval of merge coefficients over which nobody needs to ask.** Parts: MB6 signed cross terms, MB9 merge coefficients, SP03 valid prices, SP06 irreducible detail, NG04 disputed boundaries, NG12 deployed cancellation, OS12 arithmetic and rounding, MW06 safe fallback ranges. *Mechanism:* Publish a joint region in coefficient and cost space where a neutral release path and a no-query fallback are both certified. Keep signed interactions only under their checked sign premises; treat coefficient-dependent gate crossings and arithmetic changes as regime boundaries, not as a license to interpolate. *New:* A continuous range of usable merge settings could come with a bounded sensing bill, including explicit holes where asking again is necessary. *First test:* Sweep merge coefficients across gate flips and rounding modes, compare exact per-setting planning with independently checked intervals and worst-case one-size bounds.

**MNS4. Make a late partner pay for the proof, not for a guess about the missing trace.** Parts: MB2 partner classes, MB3 late entry, SP08 counterexample-only reacquisition, SP13 second editors, NG09 effect-addressed gates, NG22 source-free release, MC retention accounting, XP21 migrate or withdraw, MW10 rebase from premises. *Mechanism:* After old partner comparisons are deleted, a blind new partner imports the fallback only by checking its actual state and addressing gates by verified effects. If entry fails, buy the smallest lawful counterexample read that changes a certified release, or withhold. Never backdate a local lease into a joint entitlement. *New:* An independently authored branch can inherit an option to merge without inheriting the comparisons used to create it. *First test:* Admit held-out writers with permuted units and a moved consumer; log actual source reads, newly admitted useful merges and falsely compatible offers.

**MNS5. Price the last bridge launch, not the first visible cancellation failure.** Parts: MB17 repaired bridges, SP16 truthful bridges, SP17 nonmonotone windows, SP18 expiring queues, NG10 live readers, NG15 spare receivers, SD05 bridge completion, SD15 contingent reservations, WC11 probe timing. *Mechanism:* A shared receiver can be booked conditionally only where a common launch action lets all still-possible hosts complete the fold without interrupting live readers. Compare proving the bridge now, buying a timely query and reserving a common fallback. Delete the query queue only when its legal paths cannot beat that fallback by enough before launch. *New:* A booked physical fold can make a late answer unnecessary even while the underlying partner distinction is unresolved. *First test:* Include reopening windows and different bridge lengths on matched-output hosts; distinguish missed launch, information shortage and verifier refusal.

**MNS6. Make a coalition answer for the mixed term no pair can see.** Parts: MB7 shared gate, MB8 pair-invisible coalitions, SP02 bundled questions, SP10 joint witnesses, NG01 minimal triples, NG02 legal circuit release, MW19 coalition counterexamples, LL16 signed cancellation. *Mechanism:* Enumerate legal deployment coalitions and require joint release and regret proofs only for requested ones. Search for a coalition that defeats both pairwise budget certificates and singleton stopping proofs before deleting the corresponding table. Keep exact removability distinct from a reweighting direction that still needs a lawful compensation. *New:* The same higher-order interaction can decide whether a live group is expendable and whether an unresolved bundle of questions is expendable. *First test:* Construct a case where every pair appears safe but the triple opens a gate and makes joint sensing useful; compare pair summaries, full enumeration and a scoped coalition proof.

**MNS7. Keep the receiver, not the answer log, through an optimizer change.** Parts: MB5 optimizer handoffs, MB16 amplifying suffixes, SP07 auditable deletion, SP19 transient cover, NG05 all readers, NG11 slot resurrection, OS16 proof-preserving splices, WC04 read transport, LL5 returning relations. *Mechanism:* Bind a dormant receiver to the continuation-relevant slots and declare which omitted coordinates can return through a checked relation. Audit real deletion of obsolete diagnostics, including cache and rollback copies; retain any state the frozen witness or protected training still reads. An optimizer change must either re-establish both proofs or route to a bounded fallback. *New:* The bytes needed to keep a neutral group renewable can differ from the bytes needed to explain which failed plan would once have been best. *First test:* Inject an equal-next-update slot swap and a hidden diagnostic cache; compare full-state retention, output-only migration and read-scoped transport with isolated oracle scoring.

**MNS8. Teach tomorrow's cheap reply instead of storing why today's plans disagree.** Parts: MB18 selective partner repair, MB19 low coupling, SP09 teach a common reply, SP14 option cost, NG17 train toward a fold, NG19 demand-shaped arrangement, XP15 teach a gateway, RC12 commuting wake, WC18 job-generated evidence. *Mechanism:* Before blind offers, select truthful examples that prepare a reusable fold destination and reduce worst-case joint coupling. Issue a stopping license only after checking a common reply over all compatible partners and pricing the cheaper paths an informed planner could still take. Protect the installation itself from unrelated writers. *New:* Past demand could be compressed into a foldable arrangement that makes future partner questions genuinely unnecessary, rather than merely predicting their answers. *First test:* Cross trained and untrained arrangements with the same receiver search and a same-byte external hint cache; compare blind service after deleting the detailed partner table.

**MNS9. Sell override capacity as an ordered handoff, not a scalar token.** Parts: MB10 merge order, MB15 no double refund, SP05 staged deletion, SP19 revocable cover, NG03 new obligations, NG16 competing groups, MW02 explicit prerequisites, X1 typed override, Y1 potential budgets, RC7 lease chains. *Mechanism:* Attach a named fold or compensation step to each override that depends on another writer. Verify all served prefixes and transfer only residual allowance through the approved order. After a step settles which successor menus remain possible, delete only the diagnostics it provably makes irrelevant; a potential drop is not itself a refund. *New:* The same remaining override count can buy different futures depending on which carriers survived and which irreversible prerequisites have already executed. *First test:* Use two competing orders with identical final widths and opposite intermediate liabilities; compare scalar budgets, atomic publication and ordered conditional overrides.

**MNS10. Split the resource ledger when a merge is canceled but a skill survives.** Parts: MB1 declared merges, MB12 alignment, SP12 erase a failure not a disagreement, SP22 forced re-teach, NG06 owed signal, NG18 exhaustion, MC obstruction accounting, RC2 access not payload, LL7 residual rights. *Mechanism:* Cancel the joint obligation while keeping the branch-local error and all installed utility on the books. A receiver reserved only for that deployment can be retasked after checking all remaining obligations; any deleted compatibility table stays gone. If the merge is revived, price new entry, lawful re-teaching and any delay against withholding. *New:* A canceled coordination plan can free future choices without restoring either the old learner state or the old evidence store. *First test:* Cancel and revive merge requests with unchanged local demand; compare refunding everything, refunding nothing and obligation-scoped rebooking.

**MNS11. Put a lower bound on the cost of throwing away the wrong release witness.** Parts: MB20 indistinguishable partners, MB21 recomputation costs, SP15 every probe answer needs a reply, SP20 smooth-decay controls, NG20 targeted erasure, NG21 external hints, NG24 spendable differences, SD17 abstraction checks, OS22 cost versus answer. *Mechanism:* Erase a candidate diagnostic while preserving legal release moves, live outputs and remaining transcripts. Compare exact full-state and information-constrained oracles across every answer cell, including timely probes that also change the state. A costly refusal by a weak checker is not evidence that the question was indispensable. *New:* A lower bound could distinguish demand memory carried by live grouping from proof artifacts or useful but replaceable logs. *First test:* Match width, bytes and immediate responses, then vary the joint edit and price of lawful reacquisition; include smooth costs without a planted cliff.

**MNS12. Merge learners that can forget questions because they can rebuild choices.** Parts: MB1 local scope, MB2 partner classes, MB3 late proof, MB4 actual coalitions, MB5 optimizer handoff, MB6 signed bounds, MB7 joint gates, MB8 higher-order interaction, MB9 coefficient ranges, MB10 ordered merges, MB13 earned reissue, MB14 interaction repair, MB15 singular refunds, MB16 suffix liability, MB17 checked bridges, MB18 partner targeting, MB19 low coupling, MB20 indistinguishable partners, MB21 recomputation, SP01 deletion proof, SP02 bundles, SP03 price regimes, SP05 staged deletion, SP06 sensing lower bound, SP07 deletion audit, SP08 reacquisition, SP09 common replies, SP10 joint witnesses, SP11 question refunds, SP12 distinct explanations, SP13 second editors, SP14 option cost, SP15 answer-cell coverage, SP16 truthful bridges, SP17 nonmonotone windows, SP18 expiring queues, SP19 transient cover, SP20 smooth controls, SP21 independent publishers, SP22 re-teach, NG01 minimal triples, NG02 legal circuit release, NG03 acquired duties, NG04 disputed boundaries, NG05 hidden readers, NG06 owed signal, NG07 two gates, NG09 effect addressing, NG10 live release, NG11 slot closure, NG12 arithmetic, NG13 spent-gate folds, NG14 joint absorption, NG15 reserved receivers, NG16 overlap, NG17 learned fold destinations, NG18 exhaustion, NG19 learned arrangements, NG20 erasure, NG21 hint control, NG22 source-free proof, NG23 arity, NG24 spendable differences, WC04 read transport, WC20 renewal, OS16 known proofs, XP21 scope migration, SD05 bridge completion, SD15 contingent reservations, LL5 rejoin, LL7 residual right, LL9 joint caps, LL15 earned headroom, MW04 group mergers, MW19 coalition controls, MC retention certificates, X1 typed overrides, Y1 potentials, RC6 B ratchet, RC12 commuting wakes, WCS13 renewable repair substrate. *Mechanism:* Give each learner local spend rights and give declared coalitions independently checkable merge reservations. As new work installs, fold spent gates only where the full protected domains and continuation law allow it. Lower joint liabilities only after checked contraction; renew the same witness when a different physical carrier can support it. Delete unresolved comparisons only when a separate safe-incumbent regret proof covers all legal alternatives, including timely training and sensing paths. When a partner or optimizer changes, recheck entry, bridge timing and the residual belief before using any old proof; fall back without resurrecting a deleted table. *New:* Rebuilding useful common choices could finance both a future merge and the right to stop remembering which unused plan would have won. *First test:* Run an enumerable gated learner with independently authored writes, actual coalitions, a finite receiver pool and future partial observations. Compare exact planning as an oracle, the best same-information stack of merging plus compaction and stopping, and ablations that erase the arrangement or remove reissue. Report repeated useful merged admissions at fixed old-task floors, false refunds, lost questions, joint debt and checked live prefixes.
