**PBX1. Teach only the residual and measure who can actually inherit it.** Parts: L2-01 heterogeneous proxy disagreement, L2-05 shortcut-speed control, L2-06 selective portability tax, L2-07 pretrained residual anchor, L2-10 cross-capacity headroom, LM28 portable bequest, CE3-18 negative control. *Mechanism:* Test packets on proxies with different capacity and shortcut access, beginning at what each proxy already knows. Track absolute success as well as spread, and test how much predecessor performance was traded for the improvement. Keep expensive or unreliable skills marked nonportable rather than forcing every skill through the same packet. *New:* A portability score could distinguish a packet that helps several successor types from one that makes all proxies agree on the same wrong shortcut. *First test:* Use synthetic rule learners varying capacity and pretrained coverage, including one shortcut that trains faster than the rule. Compare uniform regularization, disagreement alone and a selective residual policy.

**PBX2. Require a bequest to survive both selective deletion and the next lesson.** Parts: L2-02 subset-reconstructible packets, L2-04 order variation, L2-08 later-task stability, L2-22 independently editable slots, D5-13 learning beyond reading, SD2-05 third-edit blocker. *Mechanism:* Rebuild small packet subsets in varied orders, then apply the same permitted lesson to each reconstruction. Mark dependencies when a packet omitted as redundant changes a later update even though present answers match. Store the shortest tested lesson sequence that exposes the dependency. *New:* A packet would be independently removable only when its absence leaves future learning intact, not simply when current readouts agree. *First test:* Plant one dependency visible only after a third lesson. Compare modular packet distillation, exhaustive rebuild and a sparse dependency record at equal bytes.

**PBX3. Keep the triage probe apart from the exercises it selects.** Parts: L2-03 discrete boundary witnesses, L2-09 held-out certificate, L2-19 span rebasing, L2-23 compiled triage probe, LM17 tokenizer boundaries, D3-09 prediction identity, CE4-24 corrupted-observation margin. *Mechanism:* Compile a cheap probe to propose what to keep, but never feed the separate acceptance set into the successor's rebuild. Compare lawful predictions at common spans across tokenizers, including a distinct rare and perturbed set. Escalate ambiguous probe outcomes instead of treating probe agreement as proof. *New:* A small bequest can route migration efficiently without using its own training items to certify that nothing was lost. *First test:* Use two tokenizers for a simple symbolic grammar and a probe that predicts common-case absorption but misses one rare boundary. Compare exact verification, probe-only triage and scoped triage plus held-out checks.

**PBX4. Distinguish an absorbed skill from an obsolete workaround without guessing from agreement.** Parts: L2-11 drop migrate retire contrast, L2-12 workaround evidence, L2-18 shadow canaries, L2-21 write provenance, L2-27 origin and confidence, D2-11 opposed worlds, DF08 transport scope. *Mechanism:* Compare old base, adapted predecessor and replacement on verified minimal pairs. Keep origin tags as hypotheses, not labels. When the same answers fit both a private convention and a repaired base bug, retain both explanations until a lawful shadow probe separates them; carry scope and confidence into the disposition. *New:* Upgrade triage can explicitly defer a poorly identified decision rather than teaching a redundant or obsolete rule to the stronger base. *First test:* Create private conventions and workarounds with identical observed frequencies but different intervention signatures. Compare provenance-only decisions, plain agreement and targeted three-model contrasts.

**PBX5. Excise only the absorbed part of an update and test what it supported.** Parts: L2-13 sub-adapter excision, L2-14 gated logit delta, L2-15 residual tail, L2-16 retirement anchors, L2-17 composition probes, L2-26 compatibility routing, LM01 relative donor delta, L4-17 protect peer effects. *Mechanism:* Peel away verified absorbed behavior and keep retired workaround cases as anti-regression checks. For the remaining residual, compare transported weights with packet rebuild; admit the cheaper valid write only after mixed composition probes check dependencies on what was removed. *New:* A migration can shrink a packet without reviving a bug or losing a rare private rule that depended on the absorbed interface. *First test:* Use equal-capability upgrades with aligned and misaligned hidden features, plus a tail convention depending on an apparently absorbed one. Compare whole migration, independent pruning and joint excision checks.

**PBX6. Shrink the living bequest without throwing away the right to revisit a dropped skill.** Parts: L2-20 multihop tombstones, L2-24 residual recondensation, L2-25 counterfactual upgrade rehearsal, L2-28 granular readmission, LM32 lineage contract, CR29 versioned relearning, D5-18 lawful substitute. *Mechanism:* Keep a compact versioned record of why a slice was dropped or retired, what observation would reopen it, and a permitted lesson or reference where one still exists. Rehearse regression in a later replacement. Re-admit only the newly verified slice and do not revive retired behavior from an outdated observation. *New:* A shrinking residual bequest can remain reversible on declared scopes without promising recovery for every discarded item. *First test:* Run a three-generation synthetic upgrade where a common absorbed rule regresses, a retired workaround stays obsolete and a consented teaching route disappears. Compare retained full packets, tombstones alone and versioned local recovery.

**PBX7. Replace the base without making the predecessor's useful distinctions unteachable.** Parts: L2-01 heterogeneous proxy disagreement, L2-05 shortcut-speed control, L2-06 selective portability tax, L2-07 pretrained residual anchor, L2-10 cross-capacity headroom, LM28 portable bequest, CE3-18 negative control, L2-02 subset-reconstructible packets, L2-04 order variation, L2-08 later-task stability, L2-22 independently editable slots, D5-13 learning beyond reading, SD2-05 third-edit blocker, L2-03 discrete boundary witnesses, L2-09 held-out certificate, L2-19 span rebasing, L2-23 compiled triage probe, LM17 tokenizer boundaries, D3-09 prediction identity, CE4-24 corrupted-observation margin, L2-11 drop migrate retire contrast, L2-12 workaround evidence, L2-18 shadow canaries, L2-21 write provenance, L2-27 origin and confidence, D2-11 opposed worlds, DF08 transport scope, L2-13 sub-adapter excision, L2-14 gated logit delta, L2-15 residual tail, L2-16 retirement anchors, L2-17 composition probes, L2-26 compatibility routing, LM01 relative donor delta, L4-17 protect peer effects, L2-20 multihop tombstones, L2-24 residual recondensation, L2-25 counterfactual upgrade rehearsal, L2-28 granular readmission, LM32 lineage contract, CR29 versioned relearning, D5-18 lawful substitute. *Mechanism:* During the predecessor's life, maintain selective teaching packets and disjoint acceptance probes. At each upgrade triage the behavioral residual, check dependencies before dropping slices, then migrate only validated gaps and retire only verified workarounds. Track which results and later lessons remain available, preserving a versioned reopening condition for discarded slices without asserting they are all recoverable. *New:* The object passed between generations is a small revisable teaching and verification contract, not a fixed copy of every old answer or weight. *First test:* Use several synthetic base upgrades with shared priors, obsolete bugs, private conventions, cross-skill interactions and nonmonotone regressions. Compare full migration, standalone triage plus distillation and dependency-aware packet shrinkage at equal budgets. Measure successor gain, old-service failures, later teachability and valid recoveries.
