# Cross-pool merges, round 107

Focal source: `tree/test-time-learners-2.md` (PR #374, T2-#). Older sources: `tree/root-test-time-learners.md` (PR #373, TT#; focal in round 106), [round 106](cross-pool-106.md) (TFX3, TFX5), `tree/learner-world-coevolution-9.md` (PR #372, L9W#), `tree/learner-world-coevolution-8.md` (PR #371, L8W#), deployment feedback 5 (PR #357, D5-#), deployment feedback 4 (PR #355, D4-#), deployment feedback 3 (PR #354, D3-#), relearnability as memory (PR #336, CR#), and sleep and dreaming II (PR #334, SD2-#). Raw proposals.

**HEX1. Conserve the rule’s contribution without freezing the rest of the prompt.** Parts: T2-01 normalization-preserving subtraction, T2-02 input-dependent absorption, T2-03 layer-matched transfer, T2-04 filler control, T2-05 pathwise calibration, T2-27 context-versus-memory steering, TT10 double-dosing cancellation, D5-13 reading-only control. *Mechanism:* On a shadow copy, estimate the contribution of an authorized synthetic rule separately from the prompt-length effect. Transfer it only along paths that keep old-rule responses and unrelated context calibration within measured bounds. Retain the original context where the estimate fails, rather than globally suppressing it. *New:* The acceptance criterion is stable behavior at intermediate handoff states, not just agreement at the endpoints. *First test:* On benign rule streams compare abrupt deletion, retained context, linear blending and locally measured handoff using matched-length filler. Report peak calibration error on rule cases and unrelated context cases.

**HEX2. Require a teaching witness before evicting a partially absorbed rule.** Parts: T2-07 active completion check, T2-25 volatility-aware placement, T2-29 rule-versus-fact transfer, TFX3 three-state handoff, TT11 episodic separation, L9W14 observable input boundary, D5-26 retained teaching response. *Mechanism:* Keep ephemeral facts and expiring exceptions in the session. For a stable rule, test context-only, intermediate and weights-only states against independent fixtures. Also check that a versioned lesson expressed in observable inputs can still teach a legitimate correction after removing the original span. *New:* A successful transfer preserves amendability and the current rule, without spreading session facts into durable weights. *First test:* Vary how often a harmless convention recurs and when it changes. Compare fixed-threshold eviction and a held-out behavior gate with an amendment-aware gate; measure context cost, retention and later correction.

**HEX3. Pause a handoff at the version boundary rather than finish the wrong write.** Parts: T2-08 abort and reconcile, T2-21 reversible journal, T2-22 reverse handoff, T2-23 round-trip transport, T2-24 conflict-triggered pause, TT25 staged commitment, L8W22 scratch round trip, CR29 versioned recovery. *Mechanism:* Bind both the partial delta and its contextual source to a rule version. On an approved correction, pause that transfer and use a scratch state to check reversing its own write or accepting a scoped replacement. Keep unrelated writes and do not replay a stale journal entry across an incompatible edit. *New:* Reversibility becomes selective: a changed rule can move back into editable context without rolling the rest of the stream back with it. *First test:* Interrupt transfers at multiple fractions with authorized edits, retractions and unrelated writes. Compare finishing then editing, full rewind and versioned per-rule reconciliation.

**HEX4. Expose the smallest faithful editable slice and keep the rest serving.** Parts: T2-09 conflict-scoped readout, T2-10 retained residual, T2-11 counterfactual text swap, T2-12 shared-delta disentanglement, T2-13 boundary-limited summary, T2-14 contrastive witnesses, T2-15 provenance index, TT14 editable decompilation, D3-09 witness identity. *Mechanism:* Use write lineage to pick candidate rule slices and verify a compact editable description by substituting it only for its proposed slice in scratch state. If coverage is uncertain, preserve the original residual and abstain from recombining affected behavior that has not been checked. *New:* Editable text becomes a checked interface to a limited part of the learned rule, not a claim to explain the whole update. *First test:* Create synthetic format deltas with shared features and rare exceptions. Compare full readout, direct overwrite and slice substitution on intended edits, untouched behavior and faithfulness on held-out fixtures.

**HEX5. Compress successive exceptions only if the next exception can still be taught.** Parts: T2-16 guarded overrides, T2-17 edit-only re-absorption, T2-18 repeated round trips, T2-19 exception hierarchy compression, T2-20 negative scope, D4-24 future gradient usable, D5-18 replace a lost teaching witness. *Mechanism:* Apply only the approved behavioral difference, check nearby negatives, and keep an editable precedence record. Refactor accumulated patches only if a held-out lawful edit remains teachable; if its old fixture became uninformative, validate an authorized replacement in the learner’s native update path. *New:* Equivalent current outputs are not enough to justify deleting the structure needed for the next correction. *First test:* Run several rounds of overlapping, expiring and retracted toy exceptions; compare full re-distillation, stacked overrides and checked refactoring on near-misses, rank and the cost of the next correction.

**HEX6. Treat a pairwise conflict as a clue, not the limit of the check.** Parts: T2-06 concurrent transfer matrix, T2-26 coupled calibration, T2-28 handoff-edit assay, T2-30 pairwise conflict ledger, TFX5 combined fast deltas, TT34 compositional sandbox, D3-22 shared repair witness, SD2-05 third-edit witness. *Mechanism:* Use a pair ledger to prioritize scratch tests, then check jointly reachable combinations and their next edits before committing shared refactors. Save a small failing continuation when a combination breaks, regardless of how many rules it involves, and separate regressions in current calibration from loss of later editability. *New:* Cheap local diagnostics can guide testing without falsely certifying a set whose problematic interaction appears only in a larger combination. *First test:* Compare independent, pairwise-only and trajectory-based validation in harmless streams with matched pair interactions and a separate higher-order-only conflict. Track missed regressions and verification cost.

**HEX7. Keep rules correct and editable while moving them between context and weights.** Parts: T2-01 normalization-preserving subtraction, T2-02 input-dependent absorption, T2-03 layer-matched transfer, T2-04 filler control, T2-05 pathwise calibration, T2-27 context-versus-memory steering, TT10 double-dosing cancellation, D5-13 reading-only control, T2-07 active completion check, T2-25 volatility-aware placement, T2-29 rule-versus-fact transfer, TFX3 three-state handoff, TT11 episodic separation, L9W14 observable input boundary, D5-26 retained teaching response, T2-08 abort and reconcile, T2-21 reversible journal, T2-22 reverse handoff, T2-23 round-trip transport, T2-24 conflict-triggered pause, TT25 staged commitment, L8W22 scratch round trip, CR29 versioned recovery, T2-09 conflict-scoped readout, T2-10 retained residual, T2-11 counterfactual text swap, T2-12 shared-delta disentanglement, T2-13 boundary-limited summary, T2-14 contrastive witnesses, T2-15 provenance index, TT14 editable decompilation, D3-09 witness identity, T2-16 guarded overrides, T2-17 edit-only re-absorption, T2-18 repeated round trips, T2-19 exception hierarchy compression, T2-20 negative scope, D4-24 future gradient usable, D5-18 replace a lost teaching witness, T2-06 concurrent transfer matrix, T2-26 coupled calibration, T2-28 handoff-edit assay, T2-30 pairwise conflict ledger, TFX5 combined fast deltas, TT34 compositional sandbox, D3-22 shared repair witness, SD2-05 third-edit witness. *Mechanism:* Separate session facts from durable rules, measure each rule’s effect throughout both directions of transfer, and treat an approved exception as a checked edit to the smallest faithful slice. Commit only when unrelated rules and independently verified future corrections survive, including for jointly active rule sets. *New:* One versioned handoff and edit protocol can surface failures hidden by endpoint accuracy, by faithful-looking summaries, and by pairwise compatibility checks. *First test:* Use benign streams with partially absorbed rules, expiring exceptions, interacting updates and rule revisions. Compare context-only, direct weight overwrite, and endpoint-only handoff with checked bidirectional transfers at matched context and compute, measuring calibration, retention and future editability.
