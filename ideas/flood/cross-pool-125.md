# Cross-pool merges, round 125

Focal source: `tree/never-ending-pretraining-5.md` (PR #394, NP5-#). Older sources: `tree/never-ending-pretraining-4.md` (PR #393, NP4-#; focal in round 124), `tree/never-ending-pretraining-3.md` (PR #392, NP3-#), `tree/never-ending-pretraining-2.md` (PR #390, NP2-#), `tree/root-never-ending-pretraining.md` (PR #389, NP#), open-ended learners 5 (PR #388, OE5-#), population learners V (PR #383, PL5-#) and root (PR #379, PL#), test-time learners root (PR #373, TT#), learner–world co-evolution VIII (PR #371, L8W#), and deployment feedback 5 (PR #357, D5-#). Raw proposals for one continuously pretrained lineage in harmless synthetic streams; branches are experimental controls only.

**OPX1. Turn interactions between lessons into a checked instruction.** Parts: NP5-01 commutator, NP5-02 transported cancellation, NP5-03 contextual substitution, NP5-04 positive-dose compilation, NP5-24 joint correction, NP5-25 quotient inverse, NP4-21 sibling capsules, NP2-05 rollback without unlearning, NP14 two-shock witness, OE5-09 order checks. *Mechanism:* Try reversible actions in both orders on scratch states and verify that inverses really are available. Where raw lessons cannot be undone, compile only effects reachable using nonnegative lesson doses, or invert only the protected readout when justified. Reuse an instruction only if later contexts cannot distinguish it from the original sequence. *New:* The mixed effect can be an action in its own right, but successful cancellation on today’s reads does not establish full rollback. *First test:* Use finite toy learners with reversible and lossy lessons. Compare direct retraining, simple inverse replay and checked composition on mixed effects and later continuations.

**OPX2. Retain uncertainty about missing state until a lesson can safely remove it.** Parts: NP5-08 set-valued checkpoint, NP5-09 paired counterfactuals, NP5-10 neutral synchronizer, NP5-11 cost-aware checkpoint substitution, NP5-12 new instruction vocabulary, NP5-13 directional error, NP4-19 future-operation equivalence, NP3-10 observable equivalence, PL03 successor stress test, TT28 frozen shadow. *Mechanism:* Keep a set of possible optimizer states plus the shared draws needed for paired comparisons. Propagate uncertainty through each declared action; either execute a neutral synchronizer, materialize additional state or refuse if the bounds no longer keep protected reads and costs acceptable. *New:* An approximate checkpoint can keep learning without an invented point estimate, while stating which future actions its compression does not support. *First test:* Drop selected moments in toy checkpoints, then compare point completion, full state and set-valued continuation under budgeted lessons and an added operation.

**OPX3. Prime one future response without changing current predictions.** Parts: NP5-05 selective priming, NP5-06 covariance instruction, NP5-07 compensating transfer, NP5-23 training-triggered expiry, NP5-26 future-visible directions, NP4-13 future response map, NP26 drift versus diffusion, D5-26 teaching after compression. *Mechanism:* Use protected readouts and a declared future action family to identify state changes they cannot reveal. Within that set, search for a change that increases response to a named target lesson outside the protected family, passes a reference-lesson check and expires after a declared ordinary training action. Compare mean shifts, zero-mean variation and compensating transfers. *New:* A temporary learning aid should be judged on what it changes at the next update, and on whether that change disappears when promised. *First test:* In finite synthetic learners with known response geometry, compare ordinary warm starts and scoped priming under matched work. Test unseen lesson sequences for leaked effects.

**OPX4. Commit a training step and its bookkeeping as one operation.** Parts: NP5-14 atomic learning commit, NP5-15 writable overlay, NP5-16 linear budget, NP5-17 private repair phase, NP5-18 shared reversible prefix, NP5-19 in-flight versioning, NP2-15 publication as one write, L8W22 scratch round trip. *Mechanism:* Stage changed weights, moments, consumed examples and budget in one version. Allow private experiments with a shared reversible prefix, but publish only after the chosen suffix restores protected reads. Migrate an interrupted step only if the new version can still satisfy its continuation contract. *New:* Replaying a half-finished step can teach an example twice or spend its budget twice, even when the visible weights look correct. *First test:* For a toy next-token stream, interrupt training after each staged operation and compare ordinary restart with transactional recovery. Check next-step effects and exactly-once consumption against uninterrupted training.

**OPX5. Validate sequences of writes rather than approving each write in isolation.** Parts: NP5-20 companion views, NP5-21 sequence monitor, NP5-22 inductive obligation, NP2-11 protected functional changes, PL5-10 held-out effects. *Mechanism:* Give each write observable obligations shared by two views. A sequence monitor tracks outstanding repairs, and a proof may replace tests only when it covers the declared action family. Check held-out joint effects and refuse when no allowed completion satisfies both views. *New:* Two individually acceptable writes can compose into a violation, while a privately staged repair can make a checked sequence safe. *First test:* Build small synthetic streams with mismatched byte and dated-evidence views. Compare per-step checks, explicit sequence contracts and inductive checks on both ordinary and withheld action combinations.

**OPX6. Give a pretraining lineage a checked vocabulary of executable learning operations.** Parts: NP5-01 commutator, NP5-02 transported cancellation, NP5-03 contextual substitution, NP5-04 positive-dose compilation, NP5-24 joint correction, NP5-25 quotient inverse, NP4-21 sibling capsules, NP2-05 rollback without unlearning, NP14 two-shock witness, OE5-09 order checks, NP5-08 set-valued checkpoint, NP5-09 paired counterfactuals, NP5-10 neutral synchronizer, NP5-11 cost-aware checkpoint substitution, NP5-12 new instruction vocabulary, NP5-13 directional error, NP4-19 future-operation equivalence, NP3-10 observable equivalence, PL03 successor stress test, TT28 frozen shadow, NP5-05 selective priming, NP5-06 covariance instruction, NP5-07 compensating transfer, NP5-23 training-triggered expiry, NP5-26 future-visible directions, NP4-13 future response map, NP26 drift versus diffusion, D5-26 teaching after compression, NP5-14 atomic learning commit, NP5-15 writable overlay, NP5-16 linear budget, NP5-17 private repair phase, NP5-18 shared reversible prefix, NP5-19 in-flight versioning, NP2-15 publication as one write, L8W22 scratch round trip, NP5-20 companion views, NP5-21 sequence monitor, NP5-22 inductive obligation, NP2-11 protected functional changes, PL5-10 held-out effects. *Mechanism:* Compile permitted lessons into operations with explicit preconditions, future-read guarantees and recovery costs. Retain uncertainty for incomplete checkpoints, stage temporary excursions privately and commit the chosen change together with its consumed training state. *New:* The lineage can say what a training operation does, what later actions may reveal and which effects it cannot undo, rather than relying on output parity alone. *First test:* On a harmless synthetic next-token stream, combine mixed lessons, partial checkpoints, private repairs and interrupted updates. Compare ordinary replay, full checkpoints and checked operations under equal compute and storage budgets.
