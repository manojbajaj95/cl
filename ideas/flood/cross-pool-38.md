# Cross-pool merges, round 38

Focal sources: `tree/training-readers.md` (PR #161, TR#) and `tree/unannounced-consumers.md` (PR #162, UC#). Crosses with declared footprints (PR #159, DF#), room-making writes (PR #160, RM#), make-before-break (PR #157, MB#), precondition writes (PR #158, PW#), unregistered dependence (PR #150, UD#), learned invalidation (PR #148, LI#), adapter contracts (PR #117, AC#), and earlier rounds 1–37. These are raw proposals. A harmless read still pins a version until it is drained or rebound. An unannounced consumer brings a frozen payload and checks the live state; it has no grandfathered right to reclaimed bytes. Track actual guard crossings, admitted utility and displaced opportunities separately. Charge migration, overlap, evidence and rejected work.

**TUC1. Replace a teacher with a response contract that a different editor can use.** Parts: TR7 update-effect distillation, TR22 portable read interface, UC03 coordinate invariants, UC05 reaction to a write, AC# downstream contracts, UD1 producer–consumer pairs, RM13 behavior-only requests. *Mechanism:* Fit a substitute to preserve scoped corrective responses to interventions across reachable training states, not merely today's teacher predictions. Move admitted readers only after checking their continuations and rebinding their accesses. Publish the functional effect before revealing fresh payloads from a different editor; each payload must show that its own checked intervention uses the effect. *New:* A teacher migration could leave behind an interface that helps a writer whose optimizer and request were unknown at the cutoff. *First test:* Match substitute bytes, teacher logits and pre-cut accuracy; compare response distillation, logit matching and same-byte replay on migrated jobs and guard crossings by frozen unseen editors.

**TUC2. Keep a conditional reservation until a pair of providers removes the branch that needs it.** Parts: TR3 conditional readers, TR4 joint dependencies, UC09 checked partner offers, UC11 state-dependent effects, DF8 reader receipts, PW03 combined suppliers, LI# invalidation. *Mechanism:* Reserve teacher storage for the reachable low-margin refresh branch of an admitted job. Let two providers, each learning its own task, jointly establish an invariant that makes the branch unreachable over the remaining window. Rebind the job to a path that cannot access the bank, close and drain the old version, then check whether a waiting frozen write can use the release. *New:* Providers can create room by eliminating a future access together, even if neither changes the current dispatch or makes the branch impossible alone. *First test:* Enumerate single-provider and joint trajectories with both helpful and destructive cross-effects; compare unconditional pinning, one-step margin checks and exact branch certificates.

**TUC3. Ask for the evidence that distinguishes two supposedly equivalent continuations.** Parts: TR2 shortest separating continuation, TR18 evidence that unlocks a build, UC13 one cross-effect query, UC19 information floor, DF6 confined probes, UD12 independent audits, RFW3 behavioral negotiation. *Mechanism:* When the public offers agree but a joint retirement depends on one unresolved training read, ask for the shortest allowed suffix or checked bound that separates the alternatives. Run probes off-route with all effects declared. If the answer still cannot settle coverage, keep the group pinned rather than treating probe agreement as proof. *New:* The most useful message can be about future access under one intervening write, rather than the private examples or a full parameter dump. *First test:* Overlay latent branch switches on behavior-matched models; compare broad audit, static probes and targeted query on verified release, retained bits and real admissions.

**TUC4. Buy examples now that a later editor will no longer need to borrow.** Parts: TR9 anchor witnesses, TR10 sampler capabilities, TR12 dependency-changing data, UC02 counterfactual crossing, UC07 blind test split, UC08 new editor objective, RM15 cheaper establishment, LI# retirement tests. *Mechanism:* Choose equally sized, truthful current curricula that teach a replacement to supply the constraint its next refresh would otherwise read. Keep the admitted continuation schedule fixed, and check a bounded capability contract instead of preserving particular sample IDs. Freeze an independent editor's payload before revealing the descendant; count its guard crossing only against the pre-provider state and not if the payload needs a refit. *New:* Data can buy a transferable learning opportunity by changing who needs a protected training bank, without changing the advertised editor or inflating inference margins. *First test:* Compare random, boundary-balanced and reader-aware curricula at equal bytes; measure actual bank release and net utility on withheld editor objectives and incompatible controls.

**TUC5. Don't sell the same retired bank twice to consumers whose effects collide.** Parts: TR1 pre-read reservation, TR14 buildable release, TR20 stale receipts, UC06 disposable versus surviving effects, UC20 borrowed room, DF15 receipt versions, PW05 split guards, MPC2 drain authorization. *Mechanism:* Issue a one-use capacity receipt unless a checked trace language shows how multiple consumers can share the bank and preserve their own training continuations. When a previously unseen consumer uses it, record new acquired obligations before testing another; only prove reuse over declared noninterfering effects. *New:* A guard can stay numerically true while its underlying usable capacity has already been spent on an admitted reader. *First test:* Blind the provider to the succession of disjoint, rival and impossible consumers. Compare reusable passes, serialized single-use receipts and joint admission at equal memory, tracking net accepted utility and false release.

**TUC6. Switch training admissions before retiring a teacher that has no inference traffic.** Parts: TR8 optimizer state transport, TR11 separate handovers, TR19 lifetime cycles, UC12 alternative concessions, UC14 safe joint publication, MB4 in-flight work, DF2 delayed optimizer reads, PW12 atomic admission. *Mechanism:* For two continuations holding each other's retiring banks, train a compact bridge that one can use before the other moves. Validate separate on-load and training handovers, including old-version callbacks. If only a joint replacement works, require a real atomic publish and enough capacity for old work to finish; otherwise withhold both releases. *New:* The minimum bridge may exist only to carry an optimizer across a training-only lifetime cycle, and can disappear once a blind later consumer takes the freed space. *First test:* Enumerate live admission and drain schedules for coupled momentum and teacher jobs, vary overlap bytes, and compare full cloning, checked joint staging and an independently useful bridge.

**TUC7. Invalidate a retirement receipt when a newly binding continuation changes what can read.** Parts: TR20 stale receipt versus failure, TR21 bounded unforeseen jobs, UC04 nonconvex alternatives, UC22 refusal, LI# dependency-triggered invalidation, DF12 scoped receipt repair, PW08 newer obligations, MPC7 selective rebase. *Mechanism:* Keep a registry distinguishing previously admitted continuation families from potential new ones. Expire only unspent receipts whose premises are threatened by an authorized family or changed adapter. If storage has already been reused, a late job must prove an alternate route or pay for a new build before admission; revoking a receipt cannot resurrect the old bytes. *New:* Invalidation can make the cost of a newly discovered reader explicit without turning every possible future training job into a permanent reservation. *First test:* Delay rare refresh requests across both pre- and post-reuse cutoffs and vary family changes. Compare time-to-live, complete rechecks and evidence-triggered rebase on blocked jobs and recovered utility.

**TUC8. Learn complementary replacements around the last reader, not the best solo migration.** Parts: TR6 shared-teacher effects, TR15 marginal last reader, TR16 complementary menus, UC10 mixed effects, UC15 third supplier necessity, UC17 reusable composition, RM07 independent providers, AC# cross-adapter checks. *Mechanism:* Have private providers train menus to cover different training readers of one bank. Check conditional effects on each other's continuation, not just reader counts. Export the least coordination that actually released critical storage, then ask a frozen consumer with a distinct objective to use that same composition. Claim a supplier was indispensable only against the admitted menu and trace domain. *New:* Individually inferior replacements can be the best way to create a durable opportunity for several unseen editors, if they jointly eliminate the same last dependency. *First test:* Place decoy private banks beside one high-value shared bank; compare solo selection, fixed-menu exact planning and jointly trained menus using identical check budgets and held-out consumers.

**TUC9. Make retirement useful even to writers that bring a weaker proof engine.** Parts: TR5 negative readers, TR13 shared substitutes, UC16 targeted slack, UC18 conservative evidence, DF19 future writer families, UD21 shared cuts, RFW6 continuation choice. *Mechanism:* Compare a checked reset that removes an actual read with a substitute that also leaves a broad behavioral entry region. Use simple independently verifiable summaries where sound; hold service and acquisition thresholds fixed. Count whether less expressive later verifiers admit the same frozen transition, and separate proof accessibility from a state that truly crossed a false guard. *New:* A replacement can create new functional room and separately make existing room cheaper for an independent writer to demonstrate. *First test:* Cross narrow versus broad exact entry regions with cheap interval and exhaustive verifiers; log exact crossings, certifiable use, net B work and collateral loss of opportunities.

**TUC10. Rent a training bank only until a genuinely new write can pay for its migration.** Parts: TR17 bank rent, TR18 selective evidence, UC01 unseen write languages, UC12 negotiation, UC21 planner control, RM17 replenishment, MB11 peak overlap, RFW9 live-checked templates. *Mechanism:* Price a bounded reservation by how long it delays useful writes, not merely by bytes. Learn a provider to lower the migration cost using past clients, then commit its menu prior to the blind consumer stream. Replenish only when the next checked admission yields utility without undoing the last one; let a strong planner choose from the same offers as a control. *New:* A learner can test whether learning to create general-purpose room is worth more than better scheduling of already known migrations. *First test:* Sweep job arrival, overlap and rival cost in a finite simulator and a small learned host. Compare long leases, planner-chosen migration and learned room creation on net frozen-write utility and byte-time.

**TUC11. A continual learner that frees training state for writes nobody has seen yet.** Parts: TR1 pre-read reservations, TR2 separating continuations, TR3 conditional readers, TR4 joint dependencies, TR5 negative readers, TR6 teacher effects, TR7 update distillation, TR8 optimizer transport, TR9 anchor witnesses, TR10 replay capabilities, TR11 split handovers, TR12 dependency-changing data, TR13 shared substitutes, TR14 usable capacity, TR15 last reader, TR16 complementary menus, TR17 byte rent, TR18 targeted evidence, TR19 lifetime cycles, TR20 stale receipts, TR21 bounded unknown jobs, TR22 portable interfaces, UC01 unseen languages, UC02 true crossings, UC03 invariants, UC04 branch alternatives, UC05 intervention responses, UC06 consumable effects, UC07 blind splits, UC08 objective transfer, UC09 private offers, UC10 mixed effects, UC11 state dependence, UC12 concessions, UC13 targeted messages, UC14 atomic pairs, UC15 indispensable suppliers, UC16 targeted slack, UC17 reusable composition, UC18 weaker evidence, UC19 minimum information, UC20 opportunity costs, UC21 planner controls, UC22 refusals, DF#, RM#, MB#, PW#, UD#, LI#, AC#, MPC1, RFW2, RFW6. *Mechanism:* Track versioned access reservations separately from evidence about learning effects. Train useful providers that replace a shared training dependency or make it unnecessary for a bounded family. Rebind and drain all covered readers before reclaiming the bank, and publish verified effect descriptions before isolated editors reveal their frozen writes. Admit each consumer only against the live version, full current obligations and the checked write footprint; account for new reservations and spent or invalidated effects. *New:* The system would pass on useful ways to change the learner, not just ways to keep old answers, while treating unannounced jobs as new admissions rather than indefinite storage claims. *First test:* Use an enumerable stateful training world with private providers, a shared teacher and optimizer, rare callbacks, and a blinded stream of related, rival and impossible consumers. Compare inference-only retirement, exact scheduling of the same fixed menus, and independent replacement learning with live checks. Report post-refresh retention, served prefixes, actually reused capacity, guard crossings versus publication, net new-task utility, and the full overlap and proof bill.
