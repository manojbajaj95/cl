Decrement a certified teaching-supply bound on joint discharge, not once per hypothetical finish. Use one lawfully observed signal to commit; retain the first job and incurred charge on timeout, but reclaim the unused partner reservation once no lawful future read needs it. Prove the fallback does not depend on a purchased observation or rescue from the absent partner. *New:* A shared opening can preserve late choice and early protection without duplicating the discharge that pays for them. *First test:* Fork from the same useful first-job state, compare both blind arrivals and a timeout with defer-all, fixed-order and charged branch caches.
