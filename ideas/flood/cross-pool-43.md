# Cross-pool merges, round 43

Focal sources: `tree/certificate-entry-ports.md` (PR #171, EP#), `tree/irreversible-splits.md` (PR #172, IS#), and `tree/indistinguishable-edits.md` (PR #173, IE#). Crosses with correction classes (PR #169, CC#), self-replenishing capacity (PR #170, SR#), return corridors (PR #168, RC#), accommodation games (PR #167, AG#), merger certificates (PR #110, MC#), adapter contracts (PR #117, AC#), and earlier rounds 1–42. These are raw proposals. Prove source-free entry separately from the shared suffix proof. A split is relative to the future law, available information, witness grammar and budget; distinguish lost repairability from an incomplete verifier. Keep the original edit and acquired-task floors fixed. Under partial observation, one reply per transcript must work across every compatible full state, not just a representative edit.

**PSP1. Put the entrance before the last recoverable turn, not at the first convenient endpoint.** Parts: EP7 blind entry, EP10 source-free prefixes, IS01 repairability search, IS04 cliffs versus ramps, RC7 transient cover, MC2 deadline parity, AGR2 handoff timing. *Mechanism:* Give a frozen port an entry predicate and a latest safe entry policy per admitted branch. Measure feasible witness placements across the full prefix and use bisection only when revealed repairability is proved monotone. An independent importer proves every legal path enters under source-free cover before its relevant opportunity closes. *New:* Two jobs with the same eventual entry projection can differ in whether the witness can safely take over. *First test:* Enumerate cliff, gradual and reopening cases at equal horizon and buffer size; compare endpoint entry, fixed early handoff and certified branch deadlines.

**PSP2. Buy either a better verifier or an earlier lesson, but don't pay twice for the same refusal.** Parts: EP12 entry obstructions, EP19 truthful example cost, IS02 split typing, IS09 verifier arbitrage, RC9 verifier work, SRW3 proof corridors. *Mechanism:* For a refused import, compare an exact shadow repairability oracle on a finite scope with the deployed entry checker. If an allowed repair exists but the checker straddles a boundary, offer proof refinement or a truthful example that moves the tube away from it. If the exact oracle rules out late repair, target an earlier intervention instead. *New:* The same refusal can demand proof capacity or teaching capacity, and entry costs can reveal which resource is actually exhausted. *First test:* Plant real slot lock-in and spurious interval straddles with identical refusal logs; compare blanket retraining, stronger checking and typed repair on unchanged payloads.

**PSP3. Lease a hidden coordinate until the optimizer can make it visible.** Parts: EP3 hidden paths, EP4 leakage budgets, IS03 slot-only splits, IS11 tube pinching, SR08 optimizer renewal, AC# downstream contracts, CRR4 slot revalidation. *Mechanism:* Let importers vary work in a proof-irrelevant projection fiber while a checked full-state bound limits its future influence. Tighten the remaining allowance before steps that amplify momentum or reactivate a gate. Renew only through a checked state transform that preserves installed outputs and the later correction. *New:* A branch can lose its right to keep a private coordinate before any parameter or prediction the witness reads has changed. *First test:* Match visible weights and vary slot histories across a legal warmup and delayed edit. Compare weight-only ports, fixed leakage budgets and prefix-indexed renewals.

**PSP4. Cache a split atlas as a route to the right lesson, not a routing permission.** Parts: EP1 invisible branch channels, EP16 reusable entry lemmas, IS12 split atlases, IS13 split groups, CC04 checked covers, CC12 parent reuse, AG16 losing trees. *Mechanism:* Index source-free bridge lemmas by candidate boundary and the frozen witness that repaired it. Treat a shared split signature only as a lookup key; bind an actual cover to payloads and suffixes checked with the same artifact. On a separating continuation, suspend the failed cell while retaining the parent witness where it still passes. *New:* A library can organize entry proofs by when repair becomes impossible without confusing a shared failure location with a shared cure. *First test:* Plant two editors that hit the same boundary but require opposite corrections and two that share a real fix. Compare endpoint retrieval, split-only retrieval and checked covers.

**PSP5. Relay two ports only if the first handoff preserves the second repair window.** Parts: EP13 cross-reads, EP14 relays, IS05 noncontiguous causes, IS15 noncommuting repairs, AG12 factored menus, MC19 residual handoff, AGR3 public ports. *Mechanism:* Verify the first exit implies the second entry with enough time and budget remaining. Check that neither bridge reads the other's retired source and search for distant prefix interactions through shared slots. If a different order is required, publish that trace or keep the ports separate rather than combining their endpoint certificates. *New:* A relay can satisfy both local entry tests and still arrive too late for its second buffer to be useful. *First test:* Enumerate two buffers with coupled warmup and late gate effects; compare independent linking, full re-verification and residual-window receipts.

**PSP6. Keep the smallest collar that lets the next importer distinguish a real split.** Parts: EP7 retirement, EP21 scoped disclosure, IS16 boundary collars, IS18 pre-emptive probes, MC1 cut-or-carry, CC16 retired evidence, CRR6 separation costs. *Mechanism:* Before retirement, retain checked local observations around candidate repair boundaries and account for every side decoder or query they need. A new importer can use these to prove a local diagnosis or entry lemma without reading the old teacher. Refuse unsupported branches rather than relabeling surrogate predictions as independent evidence. *New:* A merger certificate can name not only which history is erasable, but which scraps near a deadline still let future learners diagnose their own refusals. *First test:* Hold out edit families whose splits lie inside, near and outside the collars. Compare equal-byte bulk traces, uniform checkpoints and priced boundary evidence.

**PSP7. Transport the order of boundary crossings, not their step numbers.** Parts: EP8 checked adapters, EP9 batch abstractions, EP17 optimizer translation, IS17 topology transport, IS10 multiple verifiers, AC# caller-side compatibility, CRR4 versioned repair. *Mechanism:* Map a split diagnostic through a checked abstraction of the new host, including order of relevant gate and optimizer events. Check that the actual caller, batch semantics and deployed arithmetic preserve the required inequalities over the declared prefix. Rebuild only affected lemmas if the ordering changes; a matching signature is not a proof of entry. *New:* Two hosts can share the same useful intervention rule even if it must fire at different steps. *First test:* Cross exact coordinate permutations, changed batch orders and rounded deployment with swapped crossing order. Compare numerical deadline reuse, fresh checking and scoped event transfer.

**PSP8. Replenish an entry window without reopening the old source.** Parts: EP11 multiple entrances, EP15 patch entry, IS14 cascading splits, IS19 delay cost, SR04 donor recovery, SR15 new obligations, AG17 growing games, AGR7 scoped renewal. *Mechanism:* When a new acquisition blocks a previously safe entry, try a costed donor renewal that preserves installed outputs and lets the importer reach the same frozen port by another route. Check any new split induced by the renewal, account for restoration of the donor and keep the full updated obligation set. Do not infer endless recurrence from a few successful returns. *New:* A port can outlive an entrance even when the lost path genuinely cannot be repaired inside its old budget. *First test:* Alternate editor arrivals and donor moves under fixed width; compare restoring old checkpoints, rebuilding buffers and checked entrance repair on repeated utility and time to exhaustion.

**PSP9. Share a projected proof only if both sources can be retired at their own deadlines.** Parts: EP18 shared quotients, EP22 existing-operation control, IS06 closure recheck, IS08 correction versus proof clearance, MC4 joint incompatibilities, RC2 obligation quotients, CC23 factored signatures. *Mechanism:* Search for a common buffer and projection with separate suffix claims for two retired sources. Test mixed reachability and both intervention windows, then choose examples that either repair a dynamic split or clear a verifier obstruction. If one source still needs a live bridge, account for it rather than calling the quotient a joint release. *New:* Sharing proof bytes and sharing retirement timing can be independent design choices. *First test:* Plant two sources with overlapping read projections but different gate deadlines. Compare separate ports, joint condensation with incremental verification, and joint deadline-aware entry.

**PSP10. Price a port against smooth decay, not just against a planted cliff.** Parts: EP6 read-free baseline, EP20 break-even, IS20 smooth-decay null, IS21 verifier tightening, IS22 endpoint repair control, SR18 sham renewal, AG20 equal menus. *Mechanism:* Freeze import jobs, service floors and permitted repairs. Publish a curve of verified witness, bridge and proof cost versus first intervention time, including unresolved points. Compare split-targeted teaching with a stronger verifier, a checked endpoint repair and an ordinary read-free bridge. Claim a deadline only where the admissible repair set actually closes for the scoped job. *New:* A portable port might buy useful transfer even in hosts where repair gets steadily more expensive and there is no sharp split to exploit. *First test:* Compare finite cliff toys with smooth activations and varying momentum in learned hosts; report compatible imports, observed repair costs and exact conclusions only on enumerable scopes.

**PSP11. A probe has to arrive before the repair it would identify expires.** Parts: IE04 transient replies, IE08 teaching versus sensing, IE11 sound belief splits, IE18 hidden optimizer state, EP10 source-free prefixes, EP12 entry obstructions, IS03 slot-only splits, IS19 delay pricing, AG07 partial observation, RC7 transient cover. *Mechanism:* Derive the last safe decision time over edits and training histories consistent with the current transcript. An importer may use a probe to split that set only with verified transient cover until the answer arrives and a reply still available for every resulting group. Otherwise teach a truthful early bridge that gives all possible states a common reply, or withhold the edit. Keep an unproved reply separate from a proved absence of one. *New:* Even when each hidden state has a safe reply and the probe would tell them apart perfectly, sensing can be too slow to make the port usable. *First test:* Give two opaque editors identical forward outputs and different momentum-driven deadlines. Sweep probe latency under fixed edit demand and compare immediate sensing, a full-information ceiling, and pre-entry teaching for a shared reply.

**PSP12. Hand off the unresolved possibilities, not a guessed hidden branch.** Parts: IE12 intersected residual menus, IE14 informative replies, IE16 joint branch and edit uncertainty, IE17 belief-safe renewal, EP13 cross-reads, EP14 relays, EP16 transferable entry lemmas, IS15 repair order, IS16 boundary collars, CC04 checked covers, SR14 compatible reservations, AGR3 public ports. *Mechanism:* Relay a verified over-approximation of the reachable full states after a common reply. The second importer proves source-free entry and keeps only residual requests with an observation-based reply policy that works for that entire set before any branch's repair boundary. Where two replies are equally safe now, prefer one whose next scheduled observation separates actionable cases while there is still time, without reopening the retired teacher. *New:* A port can pass useful uncertainty forward; it need not reveal the hidden edit, discard every option, or turn per-branch compatibility into permission. *First test:* Compose two ports over a finite host with matched transcripts and noncommuting donor moves. Compare point estimates, per-state menu intersections that ignore reply identity, and a checked relay at equal evidence and accommodation budgets.

**PSP13. A continual learner that can hand off a proof before it runs out of ways to enter.** Parts: EP1 hidden channels, EP2 scoped projection, EP3 optimizer leakage, EP4 leakage leases, EP5 moving bases, EP6 read-free controls, EP7 blind imports, EP8 adapters, EP9 abstractions, EP10 source-free prefixes, EP11 multiple entrances, EP12 obstructions, EP13 cross-reads, EP14 relays, EP15 entry patches, EP16 shared lemmas, EP17 optimizer translation, EP18 shared quotients, EP19 teaching costs, EP20 break-even, EP21 disclosure, EP22 compilation, IS01 repairability search, IS02 split typing, IS03 slot-only splits, IS04 sharpness, IS05 distant causes, IS06 closure, IS07 verifier clearance, IS08 separating effects, IS09 verification costs, IS10 verifier comparison, IS11 tube pinching, IS12 split atlases, IS13 split groups, IS14 cascades, IS15 repair order, IS16 boundary collars, IS17 transport, IS18 anticipatory probes, IS19 delay prices, IS20 smooth decay, IS21 tighter verification, IS22 endpoint controls, IE01 winning entry, IE02 reply quotients, IE03 losing suffixes, IE04 transient replies, IE05 bundled ports, IE06 natural corridors, IE07 common replies, IE08 data versus probes, IE09 joint design, IE10 full probe fibers, IE11 sound splits, IE12 residual intersections, IE13 multi-round beliefs, IE14 informative replies, IE15 shared repair directions, IE16 joint uncertainty, IE17 belief-safe renewal, IE18 hidden slots, IE19 contingent planning, IE20 blindness costs, IE21 scoped impossibility, IE22 fixed demand, CC#, SR#, RC#, AG#, MC#, AC#, CRR6, AGR3, SRW1. *Mechanism:* Publish a frozen buffer and suffix proof with source-free entry conditions and checked repair windows for the declared job family. Independent importers prove entry under their actual transition semantics and budgets. For opaque histories, verify one reply per transcript over every compatible full state and prove entry before each branch loses that reply. Reserve time for any required observation and carry the unresolved state set into the next port rather than guessing which branch occurred. Relay safe pieces, narrow repair covers on separating evidence, and distinguish a lost repair from a verifier that needs help. Replenish only with an output-preserving move that keeps all current duties, and abstain where the retired evidence no longer suffices. *New:* A learner could share the proof of how to continue and the evidence of when it is already too late to take over, without shipping its source teacher or training history. *First test:* Use an enumerable stateful ReLU stream with blind editors, tempting adapter aliases, two retiring sources, opaque edit and slot histories, and both true and spurious repair cliffs. Compare ordinary contract composition and incremental verification, endpoint repair plus condensation, and deadline-aware ports with same-probe belief-state planning. Report verified independent admissions, old-service prefixes, retiring-state reuse, fallback and probe costs, and the separate cost of finding and checking entry.
