# Cross-pool merges, round 42

Focal sources: `tree/correction-classes.md` (PR #169, CC#) and `tree/self-replenishing-capacity.md` (PR #170, SR#). Crosses with accommodation games (PR #167, AG#), return corridors (PR #168, RC#), self-closing witnesses (PR #165, SW#), rejected-edit witnesses (PR #166, RE#), room-making writes (PR #160, RM#), developmental growth (PR #127, DG#), the Y6 plasticity economy, and earlier rounds 1–41. These are raw proposals. Overlapping repair coverage is not equivalence. Renewal must preserve installed outputs on the declared domains and restore useful options, not merely create inactive units. Hold external payloads and their utility targets fixed. Charge proof, routing, peak state, donor debt and the opportunities a chosen branch closes.

**CRR1. Replenish the ability to use one witness, not a threshold in isolation.** Parts: CC13 repair after replanning, CC14 shared artifacts, SR01 dormant donors, SR05 invariant entry, AG06 replenishment, RC14 entry ports, RM17 replenishing a spent guard. *Mechanism:* After an admitted edit spends a shared repair, move dormant reserve into a carrier that can run the same frozen witness for the remaining checked demands. Choose today's examples so multiple legal paths return to that carrier's entry region. Verify output equality across the re-buffer itself, recover the donor's promised reserve, and independently check the next edits. *New:* What gets renewed could be the usefulness of an existing training artifact rather than an empty unit or a newly fitted substitute. *First test:* Compare threshold resets, per-request re-fitting and repair-aware renewal on a finite stateful edit game with fixed donor costs and a blind demand bank.

**CRR2. Grow two repair routes before retiring the class label that used to choose one.** Parts: CC02 separating continuations, CC08 operational splits, CC12 parent witnesses, SR02 carrier distillation, SR09 certified descendants, DG11 consumer-verified pruning, DG14 temporary scaffolds, RE07 correction classes. *Mechanism:* When a suffix splits a repair signature, keep the incumbent where its use still checks. Build a temporary second route only for newly uncovered demands; compare its full continuation costs before choosing to keep, merge or remove a carrier. Check every old and newly acquired readout before reclamation, including clients admitted just before the split. *New:* A class refinement can trigger targeted growth without making every bookkeeping split into a permanent allocation. *First test:* Alternate splits that do and do not invalidate the incumbent, at equal peak width. Compare grow-on-split, one frozen route and verified temporary branching.

**CRR3. Never sell two renewal options that only work in different descendants.** Parts: CC04 witness covers, CC05 canonical tie rules, SR10 shared base, SR14 compatible client offers, AG12 residual factorization, RM16 reciprocal capacity, Y6 plasticity economy, AGR6 opportunity pricing. *Mechanism:* Keep named witness covers and their required branches as separate objects. Quote simultaneous reservations only when one descendant or a verified sequence can honor both at the promised cost. Share immutable state when possible, but debit actual donor overlap and disclose which alternative reservations are mutually exclusive. *New:* Pairwise sharing at the repair layer can make a market overpromise even when every individual quote is sound. *First test:* Use repairs of the form {a}, {a,b}, {b} plus branch-specific donors. Compare pairwise bundling, serialized single-client offers and joint checked quotes under simultaneous demand.

**CRR4. Reset a donor's optimizer only if the old correction is still reachable afterward.** Parts: CC07 diagnostic suffixes, CC21 versioned class maps, SR08 optimizer re-buffer, SR19 exact transfer checks, RC19 full optimizer state, SW10 optimizer clocks, DG17 regeneration-gated plasticity. *Mechanism:* Before recycling a carrier, compare its reset, transformed and untouched slot histories under the same frozen suffixes. Transport a repair signature only where the reset preserves the verified continuation effect; otherwise suspend the affected cells and check a fresh witness. Require deployed output equality during the move, separate from later retention. *New:* A weight-preserving renewal can split a class because the old witness needs a momentum history the new donor does not have. *First test:* Match weights and readouts at cutover while varying moments and delayed batches; compare output-only renewal, exact slot carry and scoped class revalidation.

**CRR5. Grow a spare direction that also makes the next refusal easier to diagnose.** Parts: CC06 actionable separation, CC24 diagnostic repairs, SR03 canceling carriers, SR12 blind frontier quotes, DG8 diagnostic gating, RE12 targeted questions, AG07 partial observation. *Mechanism:* When two verified renewals meet the same current demand, prefer the one that enables a lawful later probe to distinguish competing repairs. Offer the new directions by a predeclared coarse type before inspecting the next payload. Keep diagnostic probes in shadow unless independently safe to serve, and pay for slots, observations and elapsed time. *New:* A spare unit can be worth growing because it makes later choices distinguishable, even if both candidates preserve the same immediate utility. *First test:* Plant version twins with identical first replies and different next repairs. Compare largest-frontier selection, probes alone and renewal chosen for actionable separation.

**CRR6. Retire the evidence source but keep a paid way to resolve the next operational split.** Parts: CC16 post-reclamation repair, CC17 split bits, SR04 donor debt, SR21 option storage, SW12 independent source summaries, RE15 late refusals, Y6 plasticity rent, SRW4 post-retirement questions. *Mechanism:* Compare keeping a versioned teacher lease with storing a scoped witness and the cheapest lawful separators for successor classes. Discharge readers before reclaiming the donor. Permit a later split only from evidence that remains available or can be acquired within budget; do not relabel buffer predictions as old teacher answers. *New:* A retirement receipt can free capacity while preserving the ability to distinguish some future repairs, with the uncovered cases stated explicitly. *First test:* Hide distinct source responses behind the same initial cover and vary when rare requests arrive. Compare indefinite leases, blanket retirement and scoped evidence retention on verified repair utility and byte-time.

**CRR7. Prune a renewal branch only if its competitor can answer the same suffixes for no more.** Parts: CC09 repair-state transitions, CC18 suffix depth, CC23 factored signatures, SR11 continuation dominance, SR15 added commitments, AG10 game quotients, RC2 obligation quotients, AGR8 compact states. *Mechanism:* Propose a factored representation of reply signatures, then check a simulation relation between two descendants over the declared continuation alphabet. Keep both when no same-or-cheaper replies preserve their successor obligations. When a new acquisition or an interaction counterexample breaks factorization, reopen the affected branch proof rather than flushing every class. *New:* A small frontier can owe its size to genuine future substitutability rather than forgetting profitable but currently untested replies. *First test:* Plant two equal-utility descendants separated only by a mixed suffix after a new commitment; compare score-based pruning, flat tables and verified factorization.

**CRR8. Keep repair promises valid when demand arrives faster than reserve can return.** Parts: CC13 follow-on coverage, CC19 true amortization, SR13 one-off versus repeated service, SR17 burst costs, AG22 exhaustion, RC7 transient cover, RM06 avoid displacement, Y6 plasticity taxes. *Mechanism:* Treat each repair as both a consumer of directional reserve and a job that may delay other renewals. Compute a bounded service policy over reserve, workload and the demands one witness actually covers. Quote one-call offers separately from repeat service; do not declare recurrence unless the queue and donor recover while all admitted duties still fit. *New:* A cover that is cheap for isolated callers can be the bottleneck in a burst if everyone needs the same renewal step. *First test:* Use equal-total-demand schedules with different burst patterns and shared versus independent donors. Compare cooldown heuristics, nominal invariant sets and backlog-aware contracts.

**CRR9. Test whether a class gains from regeneration or was sitting on spare room all along.** Parts: CC15 checker artifacts, CC20 metadata controls, SR06 fixed versus preallocated growth, SR18 transfer controls, SR22 frontier removal, DG12 competitive capacity, AG20 fixed menus, RE21 fixed demand. *Mechanism:* Cross real and sham re-buffer moves from the same post-edit state with correct, shuffled and absent repair-class metadata. Give each arm identical witness examples, quotas, checker and blind edits. Separately remove the stored frontier while retaining the live weights to see whether useful reserve was learned or simply paid for in kept descendants. *New:* Regeneration, class routing and speculative storage could each explain accepted edits; the interventions separate them. *First test:* Use a host with both preexisting and genuinely depleted directions, and report repeated verified utility under the same fixed-width allocation.

**CRR10. Let obstructions steer temporary growth without blacklisting the editor.** Parts: CC01 editor-independent targets, CC11 synthesized witnesses, CC22 transferable impossibility, SR16 growing obligations, SR20 planner controls, DG7 reversible commitment, RE16 negative evidence, RM11 scoped refusal. *Mechanism:* Retain checked failures of a witness grammar or reserve budget separately from training examples. When an unrelated provider can loosen the binding resource without changing installed outputs, try a temporary branch that teaches the missing correction, then recheck the exact frozen demand. If the obstruction still holds, keep the refusal scoped; unresolved search is not evidence that growth will help. *New:* A proof of what cannot fit can tell a learner what to grow without granting either the failed edit or the new branch automatic permission. *First test:* Mix identical-looking refusals from insufficient grammar, exhausted reserve and incompatible tasks. Compare growth-on-failure, planning with fixed witnesses and obstruction-guided development.

**CRR11. A continual learner that grows and renews capacity around reusable corrections.** Parts: CC01 persistent targets, CC02 separating suffixes, CC03 failed-check buckets, CC04 explicit covers, CC05 tie audits, CC06 actionable queries, CC07 causal suffixes, CC08 operational splits, CC09 class transitions, CC10 slope twins, CC11 shared synthesis, CC12 parent reuse, CC13 replanning, CC14 artifact sharing, CC15 checker controls, CC16 retired sources, CC17 split bits, CC18 future scope, CC19 amortization, CC20 metadata controls, CC21 version maps, CC22 obstruction proofs, CC23 factored signatures, CC24 diagnostic repairs, SR01 dormant donors, SR02 carrier distillation, SR03 cancellation pairs, SR04 donor cycles, SR05 invariant entry, SR06 fixed width, SR07 coordinate invariance, SR08 optimizer state, SR09 certified descendants, SR10 shared base, SR11 continuation dominance, SR12 blind quotes, SR13 repeat rights, SR14 compatible options, SR15 new commitments, SR16 task growth, SR17 bursts, SR18 sham controls, SR19 transfer checks, SR20 planner equivalence, SR21 storage bounds, SR22 frontier removal, AG#, RC#, SW#, RE#, RM#, DG#, Y6, AGR6, AGR7, SRW2. *Mechanism:* Diagnose refused frozen edits in shadow and maintain versioned repair signatures plus checked covers, keeping unknown cells explicit. Train useful providers to restore carriers for the repairs that can serve declared future demand. Stage temporary growth and collapse it only after verifying installed outputs and all reader obligations. Quote jointly compatible residual options, recheck them at publication, then update class scopes and donor liabilities as new tasks become protected. *New:* A learner could decide what to grow or recycle using the corrections it can still teach, rather than counts of dead units or summaries of past errors. *First test:* Use a finite stateful ReLU host with private providers, late separators, training readers, bursts and independent editor families. Compare same-menu contingent planning, fixed-width recycling and case retrieval with the full loop. Report repeated admitted utility, old-service prefixes, split overhead, donor recovery, peak bytes and verification work.
