Raw proposals.

**REX1. Calibrate cheap absorption estimates against independent counterfactuals.** Parts: T3-01 key-projection meter, T3-02 early recall probe, T3-03 draft-prefix calibration, T3-04 paraphrase distance, T3-05 conservative lower bound, T3-08 entropy-matched measurement, T3-12 shadow dropout audit, T2-02 per-query transfer, TT28 shadow comparison, D5-13 reading control. *Mechanism:* Treat geometry, prefix agreement and paraphrase distance as estimates, not commitments. On sampled benign queries compare matched same-length contexts and a scratch no-context state. Calibrate separately by query type, and retain context when the lower bound does not support withdrawal. *New:* A cheap meter can be judged by its actual rule-drop errors instead of how well it predicts average confidence. *First test:* Compare global, single-pass and periodically checked meters over canonical, paraphrased and shifted toy queries, measuring false suppression, double-counting and inference cost.

**REX2. Use fragile continuations to decide what context can be retired.** Parts: T3-06 trajectory-dependent absorption, T3-07 joint absorption, T3-09 paraphrase-triggered teaching, T3-10 alignment distillation, T3-11 lower-tail compression, T3-13 head-role split, T3-14 four-slice assay, T2-26 coupled calibration, SD2-09 distinguishing suffix. *Mechanism:* Preserve small authorized examples of continuations where partial absorption fails, especially when multiple harmless rules act together. Compare a targeted micro-lesson with keeping those examples in context or using the same compute without updating. Retire only the context whose difficult continuations still pass. *New:* The unit of completion becomes the continuation a rule must support, not a canonical prompt or the average of independent rules. *First test:* Test streams where first-token agreement is high and later joint decisions differ. Compare mean thresholds, lower-tail thresholds and held-out continuation gates for accuracy and context cost.

**REX3. Make editability a behavioral split rather than a geometric claim.** Parts: T3-15 functional projection, T3-16 residual taxonomy, T3-17 short faithful text, T3-18 projected readout, T3-21 residual necessity, T3-29 write-record slice, T2-10 preserving the residual, HEX4 smallest faithful slice, L9W14 observable predicates. *Mechanism:* Propose a short editable slice from a known synthetic rule’s write record, then separately substitute its text and hold out each proposed residual on a scratch copy. Validate formatting, calibration and tacit cases as separate responsibilities. Keep the original if the text and residual cannot be separated on observable behavior. *New:* A text explanation can be sufficient for the edit without being assumed to account for every effect of the old weight update. *First test:* Plant a short formal rule, a calibration shift and a tacit corner case in one delta. Compare whole-delta text, parameter-space partition and checked text substitution across held-out combinations.

**REX4. Preserve a residual only where it still serves the corrected rule.** Parts: T3-19 scoped residual, T3-20 calibration anchors, T3-22 localized residual adjustment, T3-23 joint retention constraints, T3-24 promote a tacit condition, T3-25 cumulative drift, T2-20 negative scope, D4-04 native update check, CR29 versioned correction. *Mechanism:* After an authorized edit, retain the old residual where counterfactual tests show it is still necessary. Where it contradicts the new rule, try checked localized changes and preserve unrelated calibration and later teaching responses. When a former tacit condition becomes explicit, version the new rule instead of silently restoring the previous behavior. *New:* The residual can be neither sacred nor disposable: its preservation is a conditional, testable obligation. *First test:* Run repeated benign format exceptions that alternately conflict with and rely on residual behavior. Compare total residual retention, full reset and selective revision on old cases and nearby negatives.

**REX5. Close the handoff loop without spending the budget for the next edit.** Parts: T3-26 residual-aware forward transfer, T3-27 reverse transfer, T3-28 unified assay, T3-30 editability-gated compression, HEX5 future correction after refactoring, L8W22 checked scratch round trip, D5-18 authorized replacement lesson, D5-26 retained teaching response. *Mechanism:* Track the editable and retained contributions separately in both directions. Before compressing an exception stack, test each previously accepted behavior and teach a separate held-out exception to the compressed state. If an old fixture stopped teaching, check a lawful replacement before deleting the editable record. *New:* Forward and reverse parity alone do not certify a compression that erased the learner’s ability to absorb the next exception. *First test:* Compare direct rewrites, stacked exceptions and checked compaction on benign streams with retractions, paraphrases and another unseen authorized edit after every commit.

**REX6. Treat context withdrawal and text editing as separate claims about the same learned rule.** Parts: T3-01 key-projection meter, T3-02 early recall probe, T3-03 draft-prefix calibration, T3-04 paraphrase distance, T3-05 conservative lower bound, T3-08 entropy-matched measurement, T3-12 shadow dropout audit, T2-02 per-query transfer, TT28 shadow comparison, D5-13 reading control, T3-06 trajectory-dependent absorption, T3-07 joint absorption, T3-09 paraphrase-triggered teaching, T3-10 alignment distillation, T3-11 lower-tail compression, T3-13 head-role split, T3-14 four-slice assay, T2-26 coupled calibration, SD2-09 distinguishing suffix, T3-15 functional projection, T3-16 residual taxonomy, T3-17 short faithful text, T3-18 projected readout, T3-21 residual necessity, T3-29 write-record slice, T2-10 preserving the residual, HEX4 smallest faithful slice, L9W14 observable predicates, T3-19 scoped residual, T3-20 calibration anchors, T3-22 localized residual adjustment, T3-23 joint retention constraints, T3-24 promote a tacit condition, T3-25 cumulative drift, T2-20 negative scope, D4-04 native update check, CR29 versioned correction, T3-26 residual-aware forward transfer, T3-27 reverse transfer, T3-28 unified assay, T3-30 editability-gated compression, HEX5 future correction after refactoring, L8W22 checked scratch round trip, D5-18 authorized replacement lesson, D5-26 retained teaching response. *Mechanism:* Verify per-query absorption on full continuations before reducing context. When an edit arrives, expose only the verified text slice and retain residuals where current behavior calls for them. Check the return to weights and a future amendment before discarding either context or the edit history. *New:* The same workflow tests three independent failure modes: withdrawal on an unlearned query, damage to behavior the text misses, and loss of capacity for the next correction. *First test:* Use harmless streams that cross paraphrase distance, compositional continuations, tacit formatting and multiple revisions. Compare fixed handoff, whole-delta editing and the checked split under matched work and context.
