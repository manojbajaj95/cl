**SCD1. Sell a crossing with a next write and a way to pay for it.** Parts: PX01 named successor, PX17 return then C, OS10 guarded Hall cut, OS19 feasible permutation, TE17 choose a continuation, PC13 successor safety. *Mechanism:* Admit the co-batch only when the native slots support the named C job and each live commitment has an executable payment schedule through the return. If a report changes payer eligibility, authorize only the complete continuation supported by that public cell. Preserve a common prefix until disclosure. *New:* A crossing could carry a genuine next-write promise rather than a locally safe landing followed by an unfunded queue. *First test:* Compare commit-only certification, guarded matching without order and exact finite planning on equal-landing repairs with different post-C payers. Check old-reader floors at each cut.

**SCD2. Let a useful contrast expose a scheduling edge without granting a right early.** Parts: DR01 second difference, DR05 clipping bound, OS17 relevant edge check, OS07 tick expiry, PX11 disclosure, RV07 relay. *Mechanism:* Before choosing among due records, fix report times and legal nuisance ranges. Compute a sound feasible set of public contrasts and use it to narrow only the state premise for the relevant lost payer edge. For ambiguous or stale reports keep a backed common action or defer rather than infer a refusal. *New:* Three ordinary reports might repair a specific gap in a scheduling proof without reconstructing the optimizer or consuming an extra diagnostic batch. *First test:* Compare offset-only and exact observation policies under affine drift, one saturation, and a missed clock tick. Check that useful old work and rare retention survive the sequence.

**SCD3. Return a tiny continuation key instead of keeping a cleared diagnostic carrier.** Parts: PX10 policy equivalence, DR09 prevent recreation, DR19 compact entitlement, OS05 slot-aware eligibility, RK12 demanded contraction, RS10 selective deletion, SK03 heterogeneous keys, SK05 immutable key after write. *Mechanism:* Combine two reached histories only if one executable return and payment policy works for both. Clear the diagnostic carrier through due work, but retain a bounded entitlement that answers lawful dispatch without the retired reports. Allow two sites different fixed decoders of the same key using only their respective lawful weight reads. Check the immutable key after each promised inter-site write, without sending a tag or letting either decoder read the other site's weights. Check the next native update for a slot that could recreate influence and retain any still-needed exception. *New:* A useful observation could leave a transferable scheduling right without forcing the model to keep its transient diagnostic state. *First test:* Compare retaining the raw transcript, weight-only folding and exact continuation equivalence. Overwrite released bytes before exercising the right after a held-out mandatory step.

**SCD4. Use one due record to read, discharge and open the next repair without double payment.** Parts: DR17 read then clear, DR20 single-use cycle, OS11 actual spend, PX07 consume slot sensitivity, TE11 next contention, IS13 no refund, SK09 coupled residual and edge, SK10 preservation invariant. *Mechanism:* Take the native pre-update report, then let the same due write settle the old obligation, clear its diagnostic influence, and move the next queue into a useful predecessor. Count its single executed payment once and grant only after clearance. Require the clearance to remove a storage conflict only by actually making the residual demands agree; guard the other site's already valid decoder from collateral damage. Back any fresh carrier with distinct future clearance work. *New:* Recurring diagnosis could reuse necessary work without accruing new hidden backing or counting one record twice. *First test:* Compare matched independent report, clear and repair stages under a write that clears the diagnostic but fails the proposed grant; test every old read and the next queue's executable payment.

**SCD5. Move a refusal boundary only when actual work changes whom the queue can pay.** Parts: OS01 scoped refusal, OS13 smaller guarded cut, OS16 record substitution, PX13 no weight move, DR15 blind clear substitution, SI11 timed matching. *Mechanism:* Keep a capacity deficit only while its missing-edge guards hold. After a truthful substitution or update that leaves published weights unchanged, recompute touched guards on executed optimizer state; if an edge returns, retain only a proven smaller cut. Never confuse a failed branch with denial of the entire right. *New:* Refusals might be reusable across old work without pinning learned schedules to a single historical optimizer state. *First test:* Compare full proof replay and stale cut caching on a near-zero weight step that changes successor eligibility, including a substitution that changes no relevant guard.

**SCD6. Verify old reads and proposed fallback schedules.

**SCD7. Test each due component separately as a control, not as a substitute obligation. *New:* A range of useful successors could remain safely available across both hidden optimizer differences and diagnostic variability. *First test:* Compare a preview-based crossing and separate exact clearance with coarse quantization, one unseen repair class, and a mixed batch whose individual components fail.

**SCD8. Reverse the cross-curvature edge, check actual old-task progress and probe both directions after clock ticks.

**SCD9. Measure false admissions and old retention.
