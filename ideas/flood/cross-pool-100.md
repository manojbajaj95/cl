# Cross-pool merges, round 100

Focal source: `tree/learner-world-coevolution-4.md` (PR #366, L4W#). Older sources: `tree/learner-world-coevolution-3.md` (PR #365, L3W#; focal in round 99), `tree/learner-world-coevolution-2.md` (PR #364, L2W#), `tree/root-learner-world-coevolution.md` (PR #363, LW#), `tree/root-learning-from-other-models.md` (PR #358, LM#), deployment feedback 5 (PR #357, D5-#), deployment feedback 4 (PR #355, D4-#), deployment feedback 3 (PR #354, D3-#), cultural evolution IV (PR #351, CE4-#), and relearnability as memory (PR #336, CR#). Raw proposals for bounded synthetic workflows and independently verified safeguards.

**WPX1. Protect the failure directions the outside guard cannot reliably cover.** Parts: L4W01 conservative readiness, L4W02 soft projection, L4W05 severity-stratified stiffness, L4W06 asymmetric hysteresis, L4W07 shared-subspace conflict, L3W12 guard-conditioned pinning, CE4-24 evidence under corruption. *Mechanism:* Estimate lower bounds on guard coverage for each severity slice and test the proposed native update against all affected prevention constraints. Allow gradual stiffness release as evidence accumulates, but restore protection promptly on credible loss of coverage. Treat shared directions jointly rather than summing separate stiffness requests. *New:* The same parameter direction could be protected for an unguarded tail without freezing its unrelated uses or treating a noisy guard heartbeat as proof. *First test:* In a finite classification toy, vary guard error by severity and rotate correlated prevention directions. Compare fixed consolidation, plug-in readiness and joint conservative constraints.

**WPX2. Refresh an anchor only after the new learner demonstrates the protected behavior.** Parts: L4W03 verified re-anchoring, L4W08 shadow recovery witness, L4W09 adapter rank reclamation, L4W24 fast reacquisition, LW02 rehearse suppressed recovery, D4-04 native update metric. *Mechanism:* Keep a verified behavioral anchor and a small drill that tests reacquisition after rank or stiffness is reduced. If coverage disappears, test a bounded repair from the current parameters using the actual optimizer rather than snapping to a stale anchor. Preserve unrelated gains and report recovery that misses the deadline. *New:* An external guard could temporarily substitute for prevention without imposing an indefinite freeze or implying the learner can automatically get the behavior back. *First test:* Reduce and later restore protection in a low-rank toy with an unrelated intervening task. Compare old-anchor restoration, weight replay and witness-guided native relearning.

**WPX3. Measure plasticity lost to guard dependence, not the number of pinned tasks.** Parts: L4W10 effective pinned dimensions, L4W12 guarded partial reset, L4W13 joint pin compression, L4W16 intrinsic versus external slowdown, L4W18 learning-velocity floor, D5-12 selective control update, D4-25 shared plastic space. *Mechanism:* Compare held-out update directions whose reachable effects do and do not overlap the protected slice. Use their actual learning rates to separate world-induced pinning from general optimization failure. Test partial reset and shared pin compression without allowing either to lower the tested old floors. *New:* A learner can diagnose whether to repair its optimizer or negotiate more space with the world instead of reflexively adding parameters. *First test:* Make equal-loss toy checkpoints with either intrinsic saturation or overlapping pins. Compare pinned-task counts, spectral rank alone and protected versus disjoint updates.

**WPX4. Buy back the learning directions the next task genuinely needs.** Parts: L4W11 marginal re-arming value, L4W19 pre-emptive re-arming, L4W20 task-specific demand, L4W21 escrowed release, L4W22 shared attention pool, L3W13 verified re-arming, D3-13 compatible histories. *Mechanism:* Allocate finite re-arming work by the gain it unlocks for the actual upcoming task. Account for shared reviewers and restoration delays rather than pricing each guard independently. Keep protection until joint tests show that the guard works under concurrent load; otherwise return a constrained update or defer it. *New:* The cheapest guard to restore on paper may free no usable learning directions, or may only work when no other guard needs the same reviewers. *First test:* Enumerate a toy reviewer queue with different latencies and overlapping projections. Compare cheapest-first restoration, independent allocation and escrowed joint scheduling.

**WPX5. Do not let temporary world dependence justify permanent model growth.** Parts: L4W04 endogenous versus exogenous loss, L4W14 conditional elastic capacity, L4W15 prospective pinning debt, L4W17 expiring leases, L2W05 causal extinction, L2W22 prevention-induced guard withdrawal, CR24 scoped limits. *Mechanism:* Distinguish guard losses a learner can influence from independent losses before purchasing extra capacity. Price future pinning when accepting new prevention work, and revisit leases on schedule; expiration triggers a fresh audit, not automatic release. Grow only when a verified coverage obligation persists and cheaper protected alternatives fail. *New:* Plasticity accounting can distinguish obsolete obligations, negotiable dependencies and real capacity limits. *First test:* Simulate one removable dependency, one genuinely extinct failure and one permanent external loss. Compare growth on stalled loss, hard pin caps and costed renewal.

**WPX6. Share prevention responsibility without counting the same safeguard twice.** Parts: L4W23 hybrid coverage, L4W25 quotas against free-riding, L4W26 compact controller state, L3W16 transitive guard dependence, LM26 verified capability handoff, D5-26 bounded state sketch. *Mechanism:* Record coverage, evidence expiry, accountable owners and training cost for each prevention duty. Measure joint failures before dividing responsibility or compressing the record; do not treat contract quotas as substitutes for minimum safety. When the sketch cannot establish a new handoff, retain uncertainty and run the scoped test. *New:* A compact record can support shared responsibility and future model replacement without double-counting correlated safeguards as independent redundancy. *First test:* Use a synthetic multi-team service with correlated guard outages and uneven re-arming costs. Compare all-internal, all-external and checked hybrid allocation with a compact handoff.

**WPX7. Recover plasticity without assuming the world will stay redundant.** Parts: L4W01 conservative readiness, L4W02 soft projection, L4W05 severity-stratified stiffness, L4W06 asymmetric hysteresis, L4W07 shared-subspace conflict, L3W12 guard-conditioned pinning, CE4-24 evidence under corruption, L4W03 verified re-anchoring, L4W08 shadow recovery witness, L4W09 adapter rank reclamation, L4W24 fast reacquisition, LW02 rehearse suppressed recovery, D4-04 native update metric, L4W10 effective pinned dimensions, L4W12 guarded partial reset, L4W13 joint pin compression, L4W16 intrinsic versus external slowdown, L4W18 learning-velocity floor, D5-12 selective control update, D4-25 shared plastic space, L4W11 marginal re-arming value, L4W19 pre-emptive re-arming, L4W20 task-specific demand, L4W21 escrowed release, L4W22 shared attention pool, L3W13 verified re-arming, D3-13 compatible histories, L4W04 endogenous versus exogenous loss, L4W14 conditional elastic capacity, L4W15 prospective pinning debt, L4W17 expiring leases, L2W05 causal extinction, L2W22 prevention-induced guard withdrawal, CR24 scoped limits, L4W23 hybrid coverage, L4W25 quotas against free-riding, L4W26 compact controller state, L3W16 transitive guard dependence, LM26 verified capability handoff, D5-26 bounded state sketch. *Mechanism:* Maintain checked guard coverage and the learner directions each obligation consumes. Adjust protection in the native update metric, test whether apparent loss of learning comes from the world or the optimizer, and release pins only against demonstrated alternatives or scoped extinction. Carry a current recovery plan through anchor changes and model replacement. *New:* A learner can trade internal plasticity for verified redundancy explicitly, without a permanent freeze or hidden transfer of responsibility. *First test:* Run synthetic coupled workflows with noisy guard evidence, common-cause outages, finite reviewers, changing priorities and an optimizer confound. Compare fixed consolidation, independent guard scheduling and the coupled controller at matched failure floors, costs and new-task exposure.
