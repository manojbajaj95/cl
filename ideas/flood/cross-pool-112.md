# Cross-pool merges, round 112

Focal source: `tree/population-learners-2.md` (PR #380, PL2-#). Older sources: `tree/root-population-learners.md` (PR #379, PL#; focal in round 111), `tree/test-time-learners-5.md` (PR #378, T5-#), `tree/root-test-time-learners.md` (PR #373, TT#), deployment feedback 5 (PR #357, D5-#), deployment feedback 4 (PR #355, D4-#), deployment feedback 3 (PR #354, D3-#), and relearnability as memory (PR #336, CR#). Raw proposals for harmless synthetic populations.

**PGX1. Protect actionable disagreement rather than every difference in scores.** Parts: PL2-01 one-sided margin, PL2-02 calibrated action rank, PL2-03 common-mode write, PL2-04 missing common support, PL2-07 falsifiable basis, PL2-08 diagnostic reversal, PL02 recipient contrast, T5-01 matched items. *Mechanism:* Define which signed decision contrasts actually need to survive. Check an import on authorized paired fixtures and, where evidence is missing, limit the import to a supported scope. Use counterexamples to update the protected basis and verify margins after the actual write rather than preserving all score differences. *New:* A recipient can adopt real improvements without making legitimate forks agree or treating exposure-only differences as policy differences. *First test:* Compare raw transfer, recipient-only retention and matched contrast limits on toy rules with real disagreements, disjoint exposure and irrelevant score shifts.

**PGX2. Test incompatible imports as a coalition before spending the disagreement margin.** Parts: PL2-05 finite-step crossing, PL2-06 order ledger, PL2-09 donor motion, PL2-10 feasibility witness, PL2-11 recipient rebuild, PL2-12 coalition witness, TT34 joint sandbox, D3-22 joint repair witness. *Mechanism:* Measure both forks after the candidate writes, not just the recipient at the old donor state. Replay jointly reachable write sequences in scratch state to spot a cumulative crossing. If the original deltas will not fit, try a recipient-native reconstruction on approved examples or keep the coalition out, retaining a small failing witness. *New:* The comparison can tell apart an incompatible capability and a useful capability carried by incompatible parameter updates. *First test:* Use toy streams whose imports pass separately and in pairs but fail together in a particular order. Compare pairwise checks, donor-frozen checks and recipient-native repair after a coalition failure.

**PGX3. Make a transfer reversible until the next local correction remains possible.** Parts: PL2-13 reversible contrast escrow, PL2-14 future plasticity, PL2-15 scoped teaching receipt, TT25 staged commitment, D4-24 future native update, D5-26 teaching response after compression. *Mechanism:* Keep a proposed write in scratch state until it preserves both current decision contrasts and the native response to a fresh branch-specific lesson. Record which branch and lesson were checked, and keep unresolved scopes uncommitted. Revalidate if either branch or its authorized evidence changes. *New:* Two forks can keep giving different answers yet lose the ability to revise those answers independently; a receipt can distinguish those outcomes. *First test:* Compare retention-only acceptance, protected outputs and staged acceptance on toy fork pairs with equal present predictions and unequal responses to the next approved update.

**PGX4. Preserve unknown provenance when retraining alternatives disagree.** Parts: PL2-16 independence challenge, PL2-17 paired uncertainty witness, PL2-21 independent relearning, PL2-25 attribution disagreement, PL2-26 ambiguous retirement order, PL11 evidence lineage, PL25 ancestor ablation, D3-13 compatible histories. *Mechanism:* Use declared synthetic histories to compare scratch successors trained with and without a retired teacher’s material. When several histories fit the observations but imply different retirement edits, retain those alternatives and request an authorized discriminating lesson. Do not equate identical performance with independent origin. *New:* A retirement report can preserve an explicit unknown rather than presenting a convenient attribution as established. *First test:* Generate toy genealogies with independently learned and inherited rules plus histories indistinguishable on ordinary outputs; measure false attribution and verified retained coverage.

**PGX5. Replace obsolete teaching routes without reviving them through descendants.** Parts: PL2-18 descendant frontier, PL2-19 curriculum edge excision, PL2-20 functional splice, PL2-22 delayed reconnect, PL2-23 replacement cut, PL2-24 selective reteaching, PL2-27 nonlinear interaction quarantine, PL2-28 retirement-stable audit, PL30 executable path, PL32 independent convergent skill, CR29 versioned relearning. *Mechanism:* Audit which declared teaching routes still depend on a retired source, including shared artifacts and composed behaviors. Validate a current independent replacement before withdrawing a needed route, then retest both fork-specific behavior and later authorized transfers after reconnecting to the synthetic population. Isolate unresolved interactions rather than claiming they are clean. *New:* Retirement is tested as a change to the surviving teaching network, not just a one-time deletion with matching outputs. *First test:* Compare deleting an old checkpoint, retraining all descendants and scoped replacement on toy networks with dormant reconnections and convergent independent skills.

**PGX6. Share useful updates while preserving the right to disagree and the ability to retire a teacher.** Parts: PL2-01 one-sided margin, PL2-02 calibrated action rank, PL2-03 common-mode write, PL2-04 missing common support, PL2-07 falsifiable basis, PL2-08 diagnostic reversal, PL02 recipient contrast, T5-01 matched items, PL2-05 finite-step crossing, PL2-06 order ledger, PL2-09 donor motion, PL2-10 feasibility witness, PL2-11 recipient rebuild, PL2-12 coalition witness, TT34 joint sandbox, D3-22 joint repair witness, PL2-13 reversible contrast escrow, PL2-14 future plasticity, PL2-15 scoped teaching receipt, TT25 staged commitment, D4-24 future native update, D5-26 teaching response after compression, PL2-16 independence challenge, PL2-17 paired uncertainty witness, PL2-21 independent relearning, PL2-25 attribution disagreement, PL2-26 ambiguous retirement order, PL11 evidence lineage, PL25 ancestor ablation, D3-13 compatible histories, PL2-18 descendant frontier, PL2-19 curriculum edge excision, PL2-20 functional splice, PL2-22 delayed reconnect, PL2-23 replacement cut, PL2-24 selective reteaching, PL2-27 nonlinear interaction quarantine, PL2-28 retirement-stable audit, PL30 executable path, PL32 independent convergent skill, CR29 versioned relearning. *Mechanism:* Accept transfers only on supported decision contrasts and next-lesson responses, accounting for joint writes and moving descendants. When a teacher retires, verify remaining independent coverage and test whether later authorized exchanges reintroduce its obsolete material. Keep explicit unknowns where evidence does not distinguish teaching histories. *New:* The protocol can distinguish present agreement from shared provenance and current disagreement from independent future learnability. *First test:* Use benign synthetic populations with correlated ancestry, valid fork differences, order-dependent transfers and obsolete teaching routes. Compare unconditional merging, isolated forks and checked transfer with route-aware retirement at matched trial cost.
