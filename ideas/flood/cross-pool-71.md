Raw proposals for benign synthetic learning economies. Permissions are simulated; freeze authorized actions, observable evidence, old service and full costs.

**ELM1. Buy a lesson whose first real customer can switch suppliers.** Parts: LE01 end dependence, LE02 rival continuation, LE19 independent process, HW20 independent public reconstructions, TP25 replace source, CA30 teach-back, ER08 explicit binding, ER25 lexical argument. *Mechanism:* Make the buyout conditional on a fresh learner continuing with a permitted alternative supplier after the original one exits. Lift the original supplier's hidden interface dependency into an explicit argument a replacement can lawfully supply; test the new supplier under a fresh binding rather than merely renaming it. Audit shared upstream dependencies and require a second public path to the same verified decision. Keep old obligations payable while testing the switch. *New:* A curriculum could be worth paying for because it creates practical future alternatives, not merely because it improves today's learner. *First test:* Run a synthetic supplier outage after matched initial learning. Compare a cheap exclusive contract, equal-budget replay, and a transferable lesson with a hidden shared dependency.

**ELM2. Separate the right to draw an example from the evidence that can renew a claim.** Parts: LE08 independent use verbs, LE12 draw versus learn, EL21 scoped renewal, LW19 independent referee, BM3-22 observe versus modify. *Mechanism:* Attach distinct simulated permissions for bounded observation, learning and successor teaching. Use a permitted first draw for its stated purpose; require a separately originated referee for renewal. Neither transfer nor repeated receipt of the example adds independent support. *New:* A purchasing mechanism could allocate diagnostic access without letting permission or reuse inflate the evidence for a live write. *First test:* Cross a single broad token with per-purpose rights on toy streams containing relayed labels, an independently produced verdict and a lawful diagnostic-only draw.

**ELM3. Price a whole learning path against the fallback the buyer can actually use.** Parts: LE13 lawful path price, LE18 clearing subsidy, RB11 actual fallback, RB14 query changes menu, WS09 ongoing alternative cost, ER11 split by action effect, ER32 typed rewrite. *Mechanism:* Treat a representation rewrite as a priced edge only if its type and action-effect checks preserve the buyer's declared old obligations. Search the permitted acquisition graph from the learner's actual history, charging query slots, retained bytes and future servicing costs. Quote against the cheapest executable fallback with equivalent outcomes and permissions; do not use a cheap edge whose downstream continuation depends on rights the buyer cannot acquire. *New:* A buyer could uncover which useful lesson is overpriced only after accounting for the alternatives it really can execute. *First test:* Use a finite puzzle market with identical dataset contents and different re-use permissions. Compare static bundle pricing with exact path search and the strongest non-weight alternative.

**ELM4. Clear a complementary teaching lot only when its joint observation schedule is real.** Parts: LE11 joint license, LE17 all-or-none lot, LE25 consent cycles, LW24 future referee, OS20 ordered deadlock, RB12 joint certificate. *Mechanism:* Clear conditional subscriptions and individually scoped grants only when every supplier can deliver and the resulting learner has one schedule for all dependent evidence. A pair of separately cleared tests does not imply a joint one. Return subscriptions on failure without treating a failed opening as evidence of safe learning. *New:* An all-or-none market could enforce learning complementarity and the observation order that makes it useful. *First test:* Plant a complete static matching with a three-way referee deadlock and a second fixture where one alternative order works. Compare bundle clearing without order and exact causal scheduling.

**ELM5. Let a coalition leave only with a portable old-service contract.** Parts: LE09 successor grant, LE26 inherit liabilities, LE31 partial release after default, CX21 denial through failover, BM4-03 outstanding loss, VD24 transfer adverse route, ER26 correlated output bundle, ER29 continuation. *Mechanism:* Transfer the permitted teaching inputs along with unresolved old obligations, original deadlines, and the adverse observation path for each carried promise. Carry the result and its unresolved receipt path as a correlated return, or hand off a continuation that still obtains that receipt under the original deadline. Use a scoped denial reason when an effect cannot migrate; allow independently supported service to continue. No coalition may leave paid legacy work on the original pool's books. *New:* Portability might support useful breakaways without stranding the old users who made the shared learner valuable. *First test:* Compare full transfer and exit with only positive assets on a toy market where an observer changes and one old cohort has an unresolved failure.

**ELM6. Buy independent supply and independent evidence separately.** Parts: LE03 second supplier, LE19 process reserve, LE29 economic independence, CA01 unique events, DA07 joint uncertainty, LW11 original evidence. *Mechanism:* Check whether suppliers share a controlling party or upstream production process, then track provenance of the separate observations they deliver. Fund backups with real allowed work and estimate joint loss under shared outages; economically distinct suppliers may still report one copied event, and vice versa. *New:* Resilient supply and reliable confirmation need not collapse into a misleading count of vendor names. *First test:* Cross beneficial control and data-origin dependence independently in a finite simulated market. Test fresh service after correlated default and inspect old-skill retention.

**ELM7. Preserve access to rare lessons even when popular ones subsidize early training.** Parts: LE16 outside supplier exit, LE22 coverage frontier, LE23 unbundle old data, EL07 separate fallback, ODC4 unsolved canary, IS13 no refund of spent work. *Mechanism:* Keep a separate old coverage floor and a verified supply path for the rare lesson. Compare a subsidized popular bundle with an unbundled offer that keeps a real future fallback. Never count previously consumed deliveries as reserve; charge any buyout for the genuinely uncovered old work it leaves. *New:* Public coverage accounting could prevent a market from discounting today's learning by quietly losing tomorrow's rare old examples. *First test:* Shift demand from common to rare at matched initial success. Compare bundle price, static minimum quotas and an executable independent fallback.

**ELM8. Pay for new teaching data with an independently verified delivery.** Parts: LE05 mobilization versus delivery, LE06 data generated by teaching, LE32 matched service, HW15 shared prerequisite, DR20 count one job once, BM4-22 compatible receipts. *Mechanism:* Clear a triangular simulated trade only when the third party accepts a genuinely useful delivery and the supplier makes the authorized lesson available. Link the receipts to the same declared transaction, while crediting each delivered job once. Treat a promised mobilization as a separate reservation, not as already delivered data. *New:* A learner may finance an acquisition through real useful work without making either payment or evidence circular. *First test:* Plant a missing third-party delivery and a copied confirmation. Compare bilateral credits, naive receipt matching and causal clearing at the same service and data budget.

**ELM9. Let a future cohort buy a public decoder without buying the current cohort's preferred answer.** Parts: LE20 buy compatibility once, LE24 authorized commons, LE27 apprenticeship, LE28 credible future demand, TP22 new goal, RB19 partial dictionary, SK03 site-local key, ER10 learned atlas, ER30 effect and callers, ER31 shared interpreters. *Mechanism:* Fund reusable teaching structure through an explicit simulated purchase commitment. Demonstrate that a new caller can use only its lawful local reads to reconstruct the relevant distinction under a new goal. Share only the interpreter operations that remain valid in both callers' local vocabularies; test a new effect implementation against old callers instead of exporting the original coordinates. Keep source-specific payloads and permissions separate; open the decoder only when supported rights and actual usage cover its setup. *New:* Funding future portability may favor general teaching mechanisms over cheap imitation of the current cohort. *First test:* Match decoder setup and lesson budgets against per-caller imitation, then hold out goals, suppliers and lawful local vocabularies.
