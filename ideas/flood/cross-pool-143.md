## Merge proposals

**CVO1. Send a repair with a test that separates missing capacity from missing evidence.** Parts: CV4-01 separating lesson, CV4-02 expressibility versus identification, CV4-04 target-blind calibration, CV4-05 minimal extension, CV3-05 cross-updater transplant, NP4-13 future evidence response, AL4-02 ambiguity witness. *Mechanism:* Export two finite lesson responses and use unrelated lessons to calibrate the recipient. If the clean target is uncertain, ask for a lesson that distinguishes attainable from unattainable targets. If a certified reachable region excludes a known target, return a separating lesson combination and the smallest candidate recipient extension. A failed optimization proves neither case. *New:* Reuse the separator as a hypothesis about a missing response direction across recipients, not as a blanket declaration that another updater cannot learn the lesson. *First test:* In toy packing, hide which of three failures occurred: insufficient calibration, a missing correction direction, or a bad fit in a capable class. Compare per-recipient UMU and exact constrained stitching with the same separation certificates. Test extensions on unseen targets before accepting them.

**CVO2. Make two anchor lessons earn their keep on a new updater and a new order.** Parts: CV4-03 frozen donor export, CV4-06 identical-lesson control, CV4-07 unseen order interaction, CV4-16 one-endpoint refresh, CV4-18 amortization, NP4-19 future-operation equivalence, NP5-01 interaction between lessons. *Mechanism:* Freeze the donor before drawing the recipient and premise. When one finite anchor response drifts, constrain the still-verified anchor to stay fixed and request one discriminating forced branch for the other. Test both orders of the anchors and a held-out step size; do not consume those results in fitting. *New:* A compact export should save checks when one component changes, but two correct endpoints need not characterize the response to composing their lessons. *First test:* Swap toy SGD and Adam learners and compare donor-free imports against shuffled anchors, naive donors seeing the same lessons, equal-byte trajectories and per-recipient UMU. Charge reference acquisition, fitting, storage and wall time; report cost to match finite clean responses and retain old skills across multiple recipients.

**CVO3. Transfer the experiment before deciding whether to carry the preference.** Parts: CV4-08 separate baseline and toggle bounds, CV4-09 intermediate signed footprint, CV4-10 intervention-language obstruction, CV4-11 baseline-drift control, CV4-12 pairwise applicability, CV4-13 experiment versus progress claim, CV4-14 fresh search, CV3-10 witness rebinding, AL4-16 change footprint, AL4-23 random skipped-fork audit. *Mechanism:* Use paired forced branches to localize which intermediate of a replacement tactic fails to reproduce the old signed contrast. Check the no-toggle difference and toggle effect independently, and transport a sign only if their combined uncertainty leaves the margin intact. An applicable predicate licenses running a shared experiment, not inheriting its result. If no legal replacement in an enumerated grammar matches, return the unresolved contrast for a fresh check. *New:* Match only the contrasts that could change the sign, and distinguish a reusable experiment from evidence that its sign survives on a new pair. *First test:* In a toy proof game substitute a two-step tactic and vary voluntary use independently. Give fresh search and signed causal alignment plus #408 the same grammar, oracle budget and observables. Match coverage and audit skipped forks; score false carries separately from unchanged proof validity.

**CVO4. Split a shared repair coordinate only when it blocks both goals.** Parts: CV4-15 joint infeasibility, CV4-17 retire a workaround, CV4-19 strongest shared-data stack, CV4-20 disagreeing future lesson, CV30 caller obligations, AL4-10 smallest rescue view, AL4-28 retain competing evidence, NP5-26 protection through future operations. *Mechanism:* Test whether one local gate can realize both the clean lesson response and signed intervention; two marginal fits need not fit jointly. In a finite repair class, a conflicting lesson and forced branch can justify testing a minimal split while preserving protected old responses. Where two feasible joint fits disagree on the next lesson, retain both and buy the smallest permitted discriminating observation. Retire a temporary workaround only after both obligations are independently confirmed. *New:* An obstruction can tell a later recipient which part of its interface needs to change, without mistaking two separately successful fits for a successful joint handoff. *First test:* Plant a shared gate conflict and an independent-gates control in synthetic arithmetic. Compare the joint artifact against UMU or response distillation stacked with signed causal alignment and #408, sharing calibration and optimization budgets. Require cheaper valid resolution or gains on both clean learning and sign transport, with overlapping A/B skills unchanged.

**CVO5. Pass on what transfers and the next test for what does not.** Parts: CV4-01 separating lesson, CV4-02 expressibility versus identification, CV4-04 target-blind calibration, CV4-05 minimal extension, CV3-05 cross-updater transplant, NP4-13 future evidence response, AL4-02 ambiguity witness, CV4-03 frozen donor export, CV4-06 identical-lesson control, CV4-07 unseen order interaction, CV4-16 one-endpoint refresh, CV4-18 amortization, NP4-19 future-operation equivalence, NP5-01 interaction between lessons, CV4-08 separate baseline and toggle bounds, CV4-09 intermediate signed footprint, CV4-10 intervention-language obstruction, CV4-11 baseline-drift control, CV4-12 pairwise applicability, CV4-13 experiment versus progress claim, CV4-14 fresh search, CV3-10 witness rebinding, AL4-16 change footprint, AL4-23 random skipped-fork audit, CV4-15 joint infeasibility, CV4-17 retire a workaround, CV4-19 strongest shared-data stack, CV4-20 disagreeing future lesson, CV30 caller obligations, AL4-10 smallest rescue view, AL4-28 retain competing evidence, NP5-26 protection through future operations. *Mechanism:* Export bounded lesson responses and a signed paired-branch experiment with separate assumptions. A recipient tests what it can realize, preserves independently verified components, and distinguishes missing evidence from a certified expressive limit. Split a shared coordinate only when joint fitting requires it; keep progress unknown while checking unsupported signs and retain unchanged proof validity separately. *New:* One handoff could reduce the cost of a later transfer by turning a failure into a reusable lesson or branch test, rather than requiring the old donor or an unrestricted refit. *First test:* On synthetic packing and arithmetic, freeze a donor before multiple recipient and premise draws. Interleave a lesson-order change, tactic substitution and shared-gate conflict. Compare the combined package, its separate parts, and the strongest shared-data refit stack at matched evidence, bytes and compute. Report held-out lesson response, unsafe carries, coverage, old-skill retention and full break-even cost separately.
