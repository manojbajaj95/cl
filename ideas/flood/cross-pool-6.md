# Cross-pool merges, round 6

New sources: `ideas/flood/hardware.md` (PR #66, H##), `tree/fwd-negotiated-constraints.md` (PR #67, NC#), `ideas/flood/pl-compilers.md` (PR #68, PL##), `tree/weights-self-header.md` (PR #69, WH#), `tree/rl-forward-operations.md` (PR #70, OP#), `ideas/flood/multiagent.md` (PR #71, M#), `ideas/flood/inversions.md` (PR #72, IV##), `tree/obligation-identifiability.md` (PR #73, OI#), `ideas/flood/control-dynamics.md` (PR #74, CD#), `ideas/flood/immuno-dev.md` (PR #75, ID##), `tree/edit-transfer.md` (PR #76, ET#). Tree prefixes use the numbered raw ideas. These are raw and unfiltered.

**S1. Forward-only write insurance that buys labels.** Parts: M11 buy information, OI9 dual prices, NC3 edit-conditioned probes, WH8 expiry, R1. *Mechanism:* Use the price of a retention constraint times uncertainty about whether it applies to buy one external keep bit. The old model supplies the target; a falling header coverage rate freezes spending until the cheapest constraint can be inspected again. *New:* Verification budgets buy decisions over disputed scope instead of more task labels. *First test:* On a small ambiguous two-domain MLP, compare approved-write harm per external bit with uncertainty sampling and a tuned static penalty.

**S2. Version transfer by tolerance holonomy.** Parts: ET12 cycle-consistent transport, ID18 tolerance boundaries, IV07 executable vetoes, PL07 parametricity. *Mechanism:* Transport same/different/veto contrasts along two routes through a family of checkpoints. If the round trip fails only on renamed types, expand a local compiler rather than storing a fresh edit per checkpoint; if real contrasts fail, fork the contract. *New:* The transported object can change coordinate frames while keeping relative behavior and distinguishing semantic drift from aliases. *First test:* Train three independently seeded versions of the PR #53 MLP with permuted feature names and one changed relation; compare route discrepancy with activation alignment as a predictor of failed migration.

**S3. Credit leases spanning a reader upgrade.** Parts: H15 acknowledge-before-forgetting, ID02 split witness join, NC9 edit escrow, PL03 linear evidence. *Mechanism:* An episode leases only its affected write routes. A late outcome is joined to its old observation by event key and charged once even if both reader versions recognize it; before releasing the lease, the new reader must cite a semantically equivalent route or redirect the update. *New:* Delayed credit protects a transferable claim, not a physical index or an entire checkpoint. *First test:* Simulate asynchronous updates in the PR #57 probe and insert a mid-episode reader swap; measure misattributed writes and address occupancy against fixed TTL and full checkpoint pinning.

**S4. Spectroscopic write shaping.** Parts: H05 pulse spectroscopy, WH3 finite secants, OP19 antithetic curvature, CD4 input shaping. *Mechanism:* Measure a route's old-task response to small bipolar writes at several lags. Use it to pick the spacing and signs of a short write sequence with the same net dose but limited predicted overshoot; abort if live secants leave their calibrated band. *New:* The old task is protected from transient update dynamics, rather than just the final displacement. *First test:* In a momentum-trained tanh MLP, hold new-task progress fixed and compare peak and final old loss with a single step, uniform microsteps, and gradient projection.

**S5. Trade permission to collide.** Parts: IV17 collision gates, M2 cross-term sales, OP2 double differences, CD13 small gain. *Mechanism:* Train pairs of individually valid writes so a measured interaction acts as an AND on a designated slice. Auction the interaction only if its behavior on that slice is reproducible and its feedback gain outside the slice stays below a stated bound. *New:* A cross term becomes a contracted reusable operation rather than an error to cancel. *First test:* Build a four-cell Boolean router; compare learned interaction gates to explicit gates and untuned adapter sums at equal active rank, then add an unrelated write.

**S6. Forget by expiring opposing guard fragments.** Parts: IV12 expiry as sensor, PL14 negative dependency, OI7 instrumentation requests, ID05 missing-self sentinels. *Mechanism:* Attach staggered expiry to redundant guard fragments, not answer paths. A missing sentinel after only one expiry identifies an unlogged variable worth instrumenting; learning can continue on unrelated regions while the guard is renewed. *New:* The expiry schedule doubles as a designed diagnostic intervention for latent context. *First test:* In the PR #60 trace world, introduce a hidden mode bit and compare logged bits and post-shift errors against simultaneous expiry and random diagnostic probes.

**S7. No raw replay, but old behaviors can still counteroffer.** Parts: PL13 backward obligation slices, NC5 old ghost copies, H17 independent write veto, ET1 edit support sets. *Mechanism:* At consolidation, compile each old commitment into a branch-local surrogate at its smallest retained cut. During a proposed write the surrogate can veto or suggest a parallel route, while the original inference path keeps running unchanged. *New:* The old behavior's negotiating port can be much smaller than a replay buffer and remains separable from its production reader. *First test:* In a two-branch student, drop trajectories after task one and compare localized veto quality with a byte-matched global ghost and exemplar replay.

**S8. Frequency-routed truces for ambiguous edits.** Parts: CD6 frequency-division plasticity, M20 contingent truce, OI1 disagreement witnesses, WH6 scope-carrying headers. *Mechanism:* Persistent changes on certified support go to slow weights; a disputed alternating component goes to a fast bypass keyed by disagreement regions. Apply a witness-confirmed release condition before the bypass can alter slow weights. *New:* The crossover moves with the epistemic scope of the edit, not a fixed frequency threshold. *First test:* Use a two-timescale stream in the PR #57 CPU world; compare recovery after confound reversal to fixed fast/slow routing and simple confidence gates.

**S9. Signatures of operations, not weight vectors.** Parts: OP5 behavioral quotient, ET11 alignment by edit responses, ID18 tolerance boundaries, R7. *Mechanism:* Represent an operation as a quotient over finite edit responses, but preserve a short negative receipt describing where two apparently equal operations split. A receiver searches only for a local write matching both. *New:* Two operations can transport as the same effect class without concealing unsupported equivalences. *First test:* Cross-compile operations between two small tanh networks trained on overlapping features; compare exact task transfer and response-matched negatives against quotient-only matching.

**S10. A continual learner that can trade, transfer, and retire write rights.** Parts: H05 pulse probes, H15 leases, H17 write vetoes, NC3 scoped probes, NC5 ghosts, NC9 escrow, PL03 evidence rights, PL07 parametricity, PL13 slices, PL14 negative dependencies, WH3 secants, WH8 coverage, OP2 double differences, OP19 curvature, M2 interaction prices, M11 information buys, M20 truces, IV07 negative rules, IV12 expiry diagnostics, OI7 instrumentation, OI9 dual prices, CD4 shaping, CD6 crossover, ID02 witness join, ID18 tolerance transfer, ET12 cycle checks, R10. *Mechanism:* Build a write exchange where a transaction buys the missing keep bit, gets a shaped lease on a live cut, and clears only when both the deferred outcome and retention scope match. Open disputes ride a fast bypass; reader upgrades port their tolerance contracts before re-solving write prices. Expiry makes dormant ambiguities queryable again. *New:* Transfer and forgetting operate on write rights and their evidence, not just on stored answers or adapter blobs. *First test:* Add one delayed outcome, one hidden mode and one checkpoint swap to a four-regime CPU stream; compare post-swap retention and settled correct writes per probe to the same components without shared leases or evidence accounting.
