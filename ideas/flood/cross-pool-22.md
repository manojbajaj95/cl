# Cross-pool merges, round 22

Focal sources: `tree/self-invented-vocabulary.md` (PR #128, SV#) and `tree/immune-memory.md` (PR #129, IM#). Crosses with tokenizer and vocabulary growth (PR #115, BI#), oral-tradition codes, the Z4 anti-memory graph, MZ1 lesson vaccination, and defect transmission (PR #37). These are raw software-learning proposals. Test invented words on fresh meanings and listeners after removing the teaching transcript; score old raw inputs through the ordinary decoder. Count stored bits, transmitted bits, trials and repair work. A shared defect signature motivates selective quarantine, not a claim about epidemic spread.

**VI1. Coin a question that discovers when its old answer stopped working.** Parts: SV1 decision boundaries, SV4 names for missing evidence, IM1 experiment auditions, IM13 remember the experiment, CT4 caller context, FCU1 reusable experiments. *Mechanism:* Let candidate responders propose contrasting outcomes for legal scouts; name the observation that decides between their repairs, including its answer type but not its current value. On recurrence, a new listener can expand the word into a scout suitable for its own world, instead of retrieving the old result. *New:* A coinage can preserve an executable test of applicability while forgetting the temporary fact it once retrieved. *First test:* In tiny gate worlds, swap which scout reveals whether a returning route is traversable. Compare to active diagnosis in the fixed language, retrieved experiment/answer pairs and frequency-matched macros; hold out worlds and score old commands on first return.

**VI2. Quarantine a bad interpretation, not the word that carried it.** Parts: SV7 meaning collisions, SV10 runnable non-examples, IM6 confusion between repairs, IM9 sets of legitimate answers, NB33 anti-memory, NB37 dependency alarms, Z4, MZ1, V5 relay firewall, PR #37 defect inheritance. *Mechanism:* A failed action creates a signed contrast between the interpretation that fired and a still-useful alternative. Follow the interpretation's consumption edges to downstream routines; temporarily bypass only the implicated sense and only where a playable counterexample distinguishes it. Keep ordinary literal use and independent paraphrases live while the repair propagates. *New:* One lexical item can remain productive even while a particular reading and its inherited defect are isolated. *First test:* Seed a wrong sense into one lineage of agents in a refer-and-act game. Compare targeted gating against banning the term, dropping its originating lesson and fixed redirection; measure inherited signature frequency and service on unaffected old tasks separately.

**VI3. Make a new grammatical operator pass a tolerance test on chains nobody rehearsed.** Parts: SV12 noun to operator, SV14 repair to syntax, SV16 productive construction, IM9 legitimate answers, IM11 chain tolerance, NB34 tolerance nursery, GP3 compatible generation, CT6 observer checks. *Mechanism:* Expand the candidate operator inside withheld sequences of old commands. Test whether literal readings remain reachable and whether scope errors emerge only in composition. A counterexample requests a narrower scope marker or an exception to the rewrite rather than suppressing the construction everywhere. *New:* The immune check guards the language's productive use, not just individually rehearsed definitions. *First test:* Let a symbolic agent discover a shorthand scope correction, then test fresh verb/order combinations. Compare against stored macros, example-wise tolerance and conservative expansion with equal query budget; count valid alternative action traces as successes.

**VI4. Pass on a word in stories that disagree in form and agree on when to act.** Parts: SV9 reachable consequences, SV17 teaching micro-worlds, IM2 teaching packets after contraction, IM15 branch protocol transfer, E30 lineage reconstruction, E32 metered transmission, E34 parables, Y9 oral-tradition codes. *Mechanism:* Select a fixed-size packet of independently generated episodes whose outcomes agree on the governing distinction but differ in tempting shortcuts. Track descent so copied stories do not count as independent evidence. When one teaching line mutates, ask its recipient to run the shortest contrast before revising the convention. *New:* Redundant verbal form can catch corruption, while independently playable stories determine whether the repaired meaning transfers to new actions. *First test:* Relay names for gate and container relations through several finite-state peers with systematic copying errors. Compare to metered paraphrase alone, ordinary distillation and a retrieved glossary at equal bytes and game calls.

**VI5. Vaccinate the listener against a tempting short form, not against novelty.** Parts: SV5 exception words, SV8 speaker/listener role swaps, SV10 counterexamples, IM18 distinction vaccination, IM19 interference rehearsal, IM21 withholding boosters, MZ1, Y4, BR3. *Mechanism:* Before rollout of a learned term, train recipients on a few role-reversed scenarios in which the same short form is useful on one branch and misleading on another. Select boosters by whether another playthrough could separate the branches; do not repeat a booster if uncertainty has moved outside its scope. Preserve normal old decoding, not a blanket requirement to override. *New:* A vaccination packet can teach when to trust a future coinage and when to ask for context without teaching the future answer or merely hardening against all change. *First test:* Hide future relation families and introduce matched harmless and misleading alias collisions. Compare with corrupted-lesson vaccination, same-budget mixed rehearsal and a frozen plain-language fallback.

**VI6. Let a new dialect inherit a repair protocol before it inherits the vocabulary.** Parts: SV11 transferable lessons, SV19 audience-sensitive jargon, SV22 dialect families, IM12 inheritable tolerance, IM15 branching recovery, LG3 independent references, BR4 portable contracts. *Mechanism:* Onboard a listener by transferring a branching procedure that asks for a grounding contrast, tries a paraphrase and falls back to literal instructions. Grant compact daughter forms only after the procedure succeeds across another receiver's interface; keep failure branches that discover which kind of translation is missing. *New:* The durable shared object can be a way to negotiate meaning under unfamiliar sensors, not a universal table of translations. *First test:* Train two symbolic agents with different observations and onboard a third with shuffled features. Compare protocol-first transfer with bilingual dictionaries, demonstrations, retrieval and centralized active learning at matched calls and bits.

**VI7. Invent the provenance marker that chooses a cheaper next check.** Parts: SV6 split observations, SV13 evidence affixes, IM5 benefit of adaptation, IM7 response delays, KT4 audit funding, V10 detection versus repair. *Mechanism:* Let the speaker invent a marker when two identically worded claims systematically need different follow-up actions. Reinforce it on the recipient's time to resolve the disagreement, not on early flagging; transfer the marker to new predicates while retaining unmarked old commands. *New:* A productive grammatical marker would carry an actionable repair branch, not merely lower the perceived confidence of the claim. *First test:* In a toy workshop, hide whether an assertion came from a direct read or a peer inference. Compare invented marking to confidence scores, fixed explicit evidence annotations and uniform rechecking with the same communication and fallback budget.

**VI8. Keep legacy meanings serving while new ones mature in the margins.** Parts: SV12 literal and productive use, SV20 public disambiguation, IM8 conserved coverage, IM10 measured lost service, BI1 output partition, BI2 segmentation, BR1, LG8. *Mechanism:* Let an emerging sense compete in probation only where the learner can expose a taught, public disambiguator. Audit mixed-language traffic and ordinary old byte strings for routing and softmax spillover; if a protected region loses service, roll back that sense's shared readout exposure without deleting its local grounding. *New:* A convention can grow through a measured semantic expansion and still be stopped for tokenizer or decoder side effects before authority over old calls changes. *First test:* In a small byte-language/game hybrid, introduce a task-relevant form and a frequency-matched placebo. Compare public disambiguation plus rollback to partition regularization, isolated routing and a frozen old vocabulary at matched costs.

**VI9. If peers keep running different corrective drills, turn the disagreement into a new word.** Parts: SV2 rent in teaching, SV15 productive families from near misses, SV18 newcomer-driven abstraction, IM20 future teaching uncertainty, IM22 cross-agent practice gaps, Q6 channel attribution, GP2 coverage versus ability. *Mechanism:* Peers exchange descriptions of unlearnable contrasts rather than settled lessons. Run cross-auditions on the same near-miss tasks to distinguish common representational gaps from missing practice. Allow a coinage only when it reduces the amount of teaching needed by a fresh recipient who sees neither peer's transcript. *New:* Distributed rehearsal can mint a shared concept from gaps nobody observed together, with recipient sample efficiency as the selection signal. *First test:* Split relevant world features among three tiny agents and hold out a fourth world family. Compare shared gap codes with pooled demonstrations, ordinary predicate induction and communication-matched paraphrase.

**VI10. Retire the spelling, preserve the drill that can bring the concept back.** Parts: SV19 audience fallback, SV21 relearning, IM14 resident signals, IM16 verified retirement, IM17 feedback clocks, BI5 tombstones, KT8 revival options, CT5 delayed reads. *Mechanism:* When a spelling falls dormant, reclaim its active slot but keep a small grounding drill and the horizon over which its interpretation can safely be checked. Route first returning calls through a literal bridge; only reintroduce the short form after the old and newly reconstructed meanings separate correctly on later contexts. *New:* What survives retirement is an executable way to restore the convention, not a permanent redirect or a promise that it will eventually return. *First test:* In a finite-memory interpreter with delayed consequences, cycle names out and bring them back under an unchanged or revised contract. Compare rehearsal archives, tombstones, exact backups and drill-based recovery at equal storage and trial budgets.

**VI11. A living lexicon that learns the tests for when its own words should be used.** Parts: SV1 decision boundaries, SV2 teaching rent, SV4 questions, SV7 collisions, SV8 role swaps, SV9 consequences, SV10 counterexamples, SV12 operators, SV13 provenance, SV14 scope repairs, SV17 micro-worlds, SV19 audience, SV20 disambiguation, SV21 relearning, SV22 dialects, IM1 auditions, IM2 teaching packets, IM6 repair confusions, IM7 response delay, IM8 coverage, IM11 chain tolerance, IM13 experiment memory, IM15 protocol transfer, IM16 retirement, IM17 feedback clocks, IM18 distinctions, IM21 booster restraint, IM22 practice gaps, BI1 partition, BI2 segmentation, NB33 anti-memory, NB34 tolerance, NB37 dependency alarms, MZ1, Z4, E30 lineage, E32 meter, Y9, V5, CT4, LG8. *Mechanism:* A candidate word packages a grounding contrast, a playable near miss and a compact rule for observation or fallback when its meaning no longer settles an action. Require speaker/listener swaps and use in held-out compositions before admitting the form broadly; preserve the literal route and check mixed-language output. Peers may circulate independent drills and local daughter forms. When a defect appears, quarantine only witnessed uses and their dependents, leave valid service live, and preserve a relearning route if the form retires. *New:* The learner would build conventions that carry the means to teach, diagnose, and repair their own use, not just new names or cached responses. *First test:* Join PR #128's reference-and-action game to PR #129's changed-return stream. Cross a legitimate world change, a corrupted teaching lineage and a harmful shared decoder expansion. Compare fixed-language active diagnosis, macro learning, ordinary vaccination plus retrieval, and a same-budget controller with the same tools but no portable drills. Measure held-out bidirectional use, first-return old service, inherited false actions and time to stable repair.
