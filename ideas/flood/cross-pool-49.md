**JRO1. Prepare for a pairing without knowing either future payload.** Parts: JR01 bundle-only release, JR13 support swap, JR17 repeated structure, JB1 all-single-moves obstruction, JB2 escrowed handshake, GO01 higher-order core, DN06 conjunction preparation, NG07 two useful gates. *Mechanism:* Shape a fixed-width neutral host on historical pairings, then certify an atomic or prefix-safe release for fresh co-demand. A proof of a unilateral barrier establishes a reason to coordinate, not safety. Keep the compact counter-history core instead of the failed solo-plan table only if the declared fallback covers all unobserved histories. *New:* Equal solo release menus could hide a reusable joint capacity advantage learned from which edits arrive together. *First test:* Swap co-occurrence graphs at identical singleton counts, holding out whole edges and payloads. Compare a paid edge cache and the strongest exact joint release stack on every host.

**JRO2. Sell paired capacity with a lease that survives a half-used release.** Parts: JR07 both orders, JR08 half-use abandonment, JB3 co-owned credit, JB4 abort-safe commit, GO20 cell-level recertification, FH08 receiver costs, SO07 partial handoff, SC09 atomic recovery. *Mechanism:* Escrow the shared support until both blind writes clear utility and resource checks. If only one arrives or checks in time, keep its installed behavior on the fallback path without letting either writer spend the other's reservation. Any hidden-state query needs a checked commit or fallback in every answer cell. *New:* A joint release can survive partial use as a safe service, without booking both successes. *First test:* Replay delays and dropout at every publication boundary; compare best serial, atomic-only and partial-release contracts at matched scratch and retention.

**JRO3. Rebase the choices people can make, not just the weights at the favorite merge.** Parts: JR05 defer partner choice, JR06 unseen-partner equation, JB11 non-aliasing, JB14 curved pullbacks, JB16 suffix checks, GO11 gauge invariants, MR8 coefficient maps, SC05 C-equivalent gauges. *Mechanism:* Export a rebase map carrying distinct promised deployment behaviors and the joint release proof on every authorized cell. If a gauge change keeps solo outputs but collapses two B-relevant choices, emit an aliasing witness and refuse or explicitly narrow the offer. Use a checked invariant slot predicate only if it preserves the necessary continuation relation. *New:* A deployment menu can have a provable transportable identity independent of the chosen coordinates. *First test:* Compare full-rank, ill-conditioned and rank-deficient changes with hidden late preferences and matched solo checkpoints.

**JRO4. Buy one relation only if it breaks the coalition that matters.** Parts: JR11 partner bit, JR21 immovable bottleneck, JB6 sketch negotiation, JB10 staleness, GO03 overlapping cores, GO09 slot predicate, GO16 k-ary read, DL14 relation reacquisition, OB09 next-reply partitions. *Mechanism:* When every pair of histories has a safe reply but their joint intersection is empty, price a lawful relation probe whose answer partitions eliminate the relevant blocking cores in time. Recheck every resulting cell independently, including untouched obstructions; distinguish a stale certificate from a true veto. *New:* A single optimizer relation can replace raw states where it changes the feasible coalition action rather than identifies the realized history. *First test:* Use two overlapping three-history cores, one helpful bit, and a decoy that splits a nonblocking pair. Compare raw-slot reads and adaptive predicate recovery at equal deadlines.

**JRO5. Move three branches only after two cannot buy the needed range.** Parts: JR03 quota-aware joins, JR22 triples, JB8 minimal coalition, JB9 discrete-grid artifacts, GO06 pairwise blindness, GO07 query-budget duals, MR9 higher-order liability, NG16 overlapping releases, SB01 common-charge bound, SB21 no shared charge. *Mechanism:* Find a certified three-way refactor in solo-equivalent fibers and prove the lower-arity obstruction under the same allowed moves, including multi-step paired candidates. If a joint lesson can satisfy two necessary obligations at once, cap its combined contribution before using the bound; never add two minima by default. Charge the common receiver and unilateral vetoes; never infer a continuous barrier from a coarse grid. *New:* The minimum coordination group can be an explicit deployment resource rather than an artifact of pairwise merge scores. *First test:* Compare a true triple-only instance with one solvable by a continuous paired path missed on the sampled grid; score range coverage and total coordination cost.

**JRO6. Keep a profitable pairing alive if its next successor pins the shared support.** Parts: JR09 next co-demand, JR10 absorb old work, JR12 live pin, JB7 paired slot realignment, JB19 rescue a rebase, GO10 proof-derived predicates, DN14 either pin, SC04 distributed replacement, AC06 common itinerary, AC16 excursion checks. *Mechanism:* Let the arriving acquisition provide a necessary computation for its own job and a complementary support for a forward-only release. If the release relies on a shifted attracting cycle, certify the entire excursion and actual downstream service, not just contraction at return. Prove a safe route around the pinned reader; recover only the optimizer relation used by the unchanged witness and check both independent branches before merging. *New:* A new obligation could both block the old joint release and supply the replacement that keeps the next conjunction feasible. *First test:* Cross shared and separately routed successors on the same pin stream, including a case where no admissible release survives.

**JRO7. Charge a slot refund, a range refund and a retired question to three different proofs.** Parts: JR15 verifier setup, JR20 what cannot be returned, JB21 credit attribution, GO08 synthesize a shared reply, GO20 recertify cells, FH03 separate gains, MR17 changed coefficients, DL05 vanishing facet. *Mechanism:* After a joint fold, verify freed capacity by useful subsequent use, coefficient coverage by a same-reference joint proof, and deletion of partner diagnostics by a safe-incumbent regret bound. A collapse of one obstruction cannot close unrelated scopes or manufacture credit after an unsafe prefix. *New:* The same transaction can improve three ledgers independently, making it possible to choose the fold for the resource that is actually scarce. *First test:* Cross folds with no joint credit, real new cells and unchanged diagnostic value at matched outputs; compare one blended score with separate checked accounting.

**JRO8. Preserve a paired release through legal training orders, not just a well-behaved endpoint.** Parts: JR18 deployed arithmetic, JB2 escrowed serial moves, JB17 chained conditioning, GO12 relation decay, GO14 fresh predicates, SO10 no double spend, BO12 precedence, BO21 compose windows. *Mechanism:* Admit a family of precedence-legal moves and training orders only if a common launch rule preserves pending readers and every necessary slot relation through publication. Treat arithmetic changes as possible rechecks, not permission to reuse a pointwise proof. *New:* A release can be robust to scheduling without holding the whole optimizer still or accepting a decayed predicate. *First test:* Use equal-multiset training orders and matched precision controls with one stale relation and one exact-safe yet unproved path.

**JRO9. Publish which future uncommitted cells are no longer offered and revalidate only affected obstruction clauses. Charge nondelivery of previously sold choices. *New:* A learner can forget preparation for an obsolete conjunction while serving both constituent skills and respecting the choices it already sold. *First test:* Reverse pair frequencies with fixed marginals and pending reservations; compare rebuild-from-scratch, stale-cache reuse and scoped retreat.

**JRO10. Audit whether an obstruction is real by changing the editor's addressing and nothing else.** Parts: JR16 changed addresses, JR21 immovable bottleneck, JB12 alias witness, JB18 hidden readers, GO17 predicate arity limits, GO21 disclosure costs, OB03 portable dual, BO08 coupled order gap, SB09 target witness map, SB14 scope expansion. *Mechanism:* Cross host and editor conventions while keeping legal moves, protected semantics and useful targets fixed. If a purported obstruction moves with a cosmetic relabeling, recheck its proof; if a hidden reader genuinely prevents rebasing, instantiate a reachable counter-history under the new editor before exporting the narrow counterexample and charge the smallest useful extra evidence. *New:* A portable lower bound can distinguish physical contention from notation, incomplete checks or unnecessary disclosure. *First test:* Compare exact relabelings, true changed consumers and lawful but insufficient low-arity probes on held-out combinations.

**JRO11. Bound the lesson's combined contribution across both obligations and show transportable counter-histories for any claimed minimum; separately certify protected behavior at every phase and distinct late choices on the new orbit. *New:* A joint edit might both live in the renewed dynamics and open a new deployment menu, without counting the same teaching work twice. *First test:* Compare an idle swap, a leaking retained offset, separately routed edits and absorption into the same frozen loss. Match data, width and initial margins, and test held-out pairings plus a legal shorter reply that invalidates only the lower bound.
