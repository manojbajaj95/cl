**ELR1. Consolidate a failed skill only when independent worlds agree on what failed.** Parts: BM05 reverse branching credit, ODA2 proof residual, ODB1 pivot credit, CA06 sealed attempt, CA01 unique events, DR17 read then clear, LT01 authorship-masked consequences, LT04 independent replication. *Mechanism:* Collect independent failed attempts on benign symbolic tasks before agents read the shared library. A verifier names a missing obligation, and a locally replayed intervention checks whether repairing it changes the relevant subgoal. For live traffic, keep only verifier outcomes that the learner could not author, and require an independently originated stream before promotion. Combine only distinct world events; retire transient tags after consolidation and a fresh held-out read. *New:* A population could learn reusable intermediate signals even when nobody passes the whole task, without treating repeated copies of one diagnosis as confirmation. *First test:* Use sequential proof games with zero initial pass rate and repeated copied failures. Match verifier calls and replay bytes against trace distillation and individual verified pivot search.

**ELR2. Let slow shared memory retain a rare strategy that fast agents do not agree on.** Parts: BM16 multi-timescale cascade, BM35 niche partitioning, ODC1 mechanism portfolios, ODC4 unsolved canaries, CA13 extinction fingerprints, CA27 population timescales, OS21 distinguish failures. *Mechanism:* Maintain distinct strategies in a bounded fast library, then use independent rare-task canaries rather than majority votes to choose what the slow library must keep. Distinguish missing verification evidence from an actual loss of executable coverage before reallocating storage. *New:* Specialized methods may stay reachable after population turnover without permanently reserving a full copy of every skill. *First test:* Compare diversity bonuses, reservoir replay and isolated archives through rotating rare tasks, matched initially on margin, storage and environment samples.

**ELR3. Route new learning near the old skill it needs without opening the whole library.** Parts: BM11 disinhibitory gate, BM17 coactivation neighborhood, ODD3 seam supervision, ODF2 readout sufficiency, CA20 functional conflict fork, RN01 narrowed executable write. *Mechanism:* Start with a readout-only bridge at a verified interface. Open a local feature writer only where readout-only fails and a frozen joint old/new verifier bounds the interface damage. Keep incompatible variants forked until a fresh independent example can authorize one useful merge. *New:* Local edit permission can be conditioned on what a transfer actually needs rather than a task label or broad similarity. *First test:* On sequential program-composition rules, compare static adapter routing and parameter-matched global feature training; hold out both interface directions and the new skill's old-task partner.

**ELR4. Teach a transferable mechanism through unchanged behavior as well as changed behavior.** Parts: BM29 dual-stream credit, ODB2 null effect, ODD2 structural morphism, CA30 fresh teach-back, CA24 living exam, PX05 outgoing boundary. *Mechanism:* Write a small contract with verifier-backed invariants and allowed structural changes. A fresh learner must produce the right positive transfer and preserve the declared unchanged properties without access to the author's trace. Only admit where a later ordinary update also keeps the protected interface usable. *New:* A teach-back item might preserve causal boundaries that a demonstration-only lesson leaves implicit. *First test:* Match lesson bytes and verifier calls against trace and input-output teaching, with distractor rewrites and a useful follow-up update.

**ELR5. Sleep in shifts and consolidate only independent evidence.** Parts: BM03 alternating sleep phases, BM13 expiring tags, ODC5 verification-gated commitment, ODF4 dynamic retention, CA01 independent evidence, CA10 lease renewal, CA26 population sleep, PN19 mandatory old work, LT07 falsifiable writes, LT13 scope ladder, LT30 next-traffic consolidation. *Mechanism:* Keep serving agents online while a rotating group consolidates short-lived candidates. On each phase change, require fresh evidence from independent world interactions before unlocking the slow write; copy lineage neither renews leases nor buys additional admission. Pin a prediction and its falsifier before a local write; widen its scope only after independent outcomes on later traffic. Carry old service commitments through both phases. *New:* A rotating population might separate speculative recombination from verified memory formation without interrupting old service. *First test:* Compare equal-format and teacher-generated replay, fixed sleep scheduling and no-lineage controls at matched environment calls and serving throughput.

**ELR6. Replace experience-count novelty with a verifier-backed transfer residual.** Parts: BM08 uncertainty broadcast, BM26 predictive error, ODD1 mechanism residual, ODE2 sub-check surprise, CA11 fresh reality allocation, CA22 transitive retraction, TE19 actionable observation. *Mechanism:* Choose which shared skill to inspect based on residual error on independent verifier sub-checks, not disagreement alone. Buy an informative fresh observation before the last release cut. If a common premise fails, scope revalidation to descendants that actually used it while protecting unrelated old commitments. *New:* A population could spend scarce direct observations where its shared mechanisms cease to transfer, even when all agents confidently agree. *First test:* Compare uniform and disagreement-driven sampling under a common shortcut, a legitimate minority solution, and a retraction planted late in an unrelated branch.

**ELR7. Make a fixed newcomer lesson depend on a lawful local context rather than an author trace.** Parts: BM25 context-gated branches, BM28 separated keys, ODB3 suffix transfer, ODC3 reusable bottleneck, CA31 newcomer compaction, SK03 shared key local meaning, SK05 immutable key. *Mechanism:* Compile one small behavioral lesson that two sites can interpret with their own already-available verifier state. Train the shared lesson so an ordinary update at one site does not change what another can execute. Charge decoders, support examples and evidence; do not smuggle site labels into hidden precision. *New:* One frozen lesson might teach different local steps of the same transferable mechanism without making every receiver retain the author's experience. *First test:* Compare per-site teaching and a larger shared replay corpus at equal total bytes; switch one local interface and measure new learner sample efficiency and old retention.

**ELR8. Let an independent failed attempt open a short write window, then erase the diagnostic.** Parts: BM04 phase-gated window, BM14 cross-episode rescue, ODA6 minimal hints, ODE3 probe versus act, CA06 sealed attempt, DR09 prevent recurrence, DR19 small entitlement, LT08 delayed write verdict, LT20 retention canaries. *Mechanism:* Seal each attempt and use bounded verifier hints solely to authorize a temporary structural edit. Open only the common safe prefix before evidence matures; then clear the transient diagnostic with naturally useful work. Keep the tentative write isolated until its declared delayed consequence arrives, and compare matched nonces before and after clearance to check old behavior. Preserve an independently executable bounded commitment, not a raw transcript or a hidden task ID. *New:* A temporary acquisition signal could support one-shot cross-task transfer without becoming permanent memory or an uncontrolled action cue. *First test:* Compare fixed hints, persistent tags and a matched verifier with no special write gate; delay the diagnostic and add a subsequent update that can reactivate it.

**ELR9. Preserve a minority hypothesis under a shared update without calling it consensus.** Parts: BM21 staged plasticity, BM30 write competition, ODC2 support floors, ODF6 calibration, CA07 validated migration, CA14 private residuals, CA23 staggered rollout, IS09 disjunctive grants. *Mechanism:* Maintain two backed candidate updates: a narrow shared change and a private alternative. Use disjoint held-out benign task slices to decide promotion, and buy only sufficient shared permission for the selected branch. Keep known minority competence when its evidence is independent but too sparse to support the shared claim. *New:* A population might learn faster from compatible evidence without erasing low-frequency skills or granting contradictory simultaneous writes. *First test:* Match private capacity and world observations against averaging, island validation and diversity-only controls. Track rare performance, calibration, and reaction to a distribution shift.

**ELR10. Retire a skill representation only when the next learner can reconstruct its useful boundary.** Parts: BM22 structural turnover, BM33 reacquisition bookmark, ODD6 boundary kernels, ODF2 readout gate, CA13 extinction check, CA30 teach-back, WM11 check before release, RK22 actual reuse, LT22 coverage birth certificate, LT32 provenance split. *Mechanism:* Keep self-authored proposals in scoped memory while independent evidence is insufficient for a weight write. Register the tested scope of a born skill so a future reader knows what it must recover. Use verifier-certified boundary cases as a small reacquisition contract. A fresh learner must recover both valid and invalid transfer cases after the old representation is overwritten. Require rare-task performance and old service at every cut; missing evidence delays reclamation rather than silently lowering the support floor. *New:* A tiny memory may preserve the ability to relearn a disappearing skill, rather than merely recognizing that it vanished. *First test:* Compare equal-byte replay, retained full skills and a newcomer test scored only on familiar cases; actually use the returned space for an unrelated useful skill.
