**VEC1. Follow an old read across a handoff and debit the version it actually saw.** Parts: VD01 upstream arrival, VD24 adverse path transfer, BM3-01 exposure debit, BM3-07 served order, BM3-19 route-linked liability, EL24 separate clocks. *Mechanism:* Key each synthetic request and its old outcome contract before routing. Transfer both the failure-observation path and its outstanding liability; settle each cohort against its actual served versions. Unmatched exits remain unresolved, not successful. A clean final version does not refund an earlier degradation. *New:* An update can be judged by the behavior it actually exposed rather than the easiest terminal receipts or the last model snapshot. *First test:* Compare route-local receipts, final-state checks and exact event accounting when a candidate diverts precisely the difficult requests.

**VEC2. Renew only the decision that all admissible completions support.** Parts: DA01 action equivalence, DA11 unfinished audit, DA19 decision bracket, VD19 missing-label bounds, VD20 reversal count, LW21 common effect, TP11 stop across decoders, TP12 stopping expiry. *Mechanism:* Publish uncertainty over unfinished outcomes on a fixed upstream cohort. Renew only an action whose safety and utility floor pass in every admissible completion. When the action depends on a taught decoder, apply the same stopping rule across all surviving interpretations and expire it when support changes. Keep the unresolved rival explanation and send its usable decision bracket rather than raw observations; do not interpret the sensitivity count as an estimate. *New:* Useful authority can survive unresolved explanation and missingness without promoting an unsupported general claim. *First test:* Construct twin symbolic worlds with identical receipts and opposite outcomes for the missing cohort. Compare forced verdicts, total abstention and scope-limited action.

**VEC3. Spend scarce observation on the edit that would erase it.** Parts: DA20 reserve test, DA26 paired proxy, VD17 missing disagreement, VD18 observation-only switch, BM3-20 fixed meter, BM3-22 observation separate from modification, HW10 public sensing, TP09 affordable distinguishing query, TP25 replace source. *Mechanism:* Before a tentative version can reroute the last useful observation, reserve a lawful old-task test or overlap it prospectively with a replacement. Check that the observation switch does not change the action forecast. Teach the cheapest lawful query that separates the live action choices; if its source disappears, re-test the choice rule with a public replacement rather than carrying its answer. Give an observational probe its own budget, never implicit permission to modify. *New:* Protecting the ability to disprove a change may matter more than collecting many verdicts after deployment. *First test:* At matched observation spend, compare a fixed audit, a retrospective proxy and prospective overlap when a candidate eliminates a rare discriminator.

**VEC4. Share an audit without pretending its drift is independent.** Parts: DA04 compound contrast, DA07 joint error, DA15 independent outcome, VD21 shared outage, BM3-05 plausible old reads, EL17 separate noises. *Mechanism:* Reuse jointly valid views only under a checked joint uncertainty set. Where two views share a calibration shift, carry the common nuisance into the worst-case decision and reserve old-read margin across all surviving branches. A predecessor lane with a verified comparable intake may separate a shared outage from candidate-specific missingness. *New:* Two individually weak views can support a useful contrast without letting correlated evidence inflate authority. *First test:* Compare independent-product scoring and an exact joint bound on harmless trials with one shared outage and one selectively missing adverse outcome.

**VEC5. Prevent two independently approved revisions from disabling each other's verdicts.** Parts: DA21 joint deployment, DA23 evidence order, VD22 trajectory contrast, VD25 selective reversal, BM3-03 one trajectory account, BM3-23 no bootstrapping, OS20 ordered deadlock. *Mechanism:* Verify complete served trajectories in every advertised exercise order. Count downstream observation and old-reader headroom in a joint account. A revision may not create its partner's only evidence; if the two orders erase different adverse routes, offer one backed deployment or defer both instead of calling their local wins composable. *New:* The limiting resource for parallel safe changes could be the future ability to falsify them, not available parameter capacity. *First test:* Compare independent edits, static shared budget and exact ordered scheduling; require the second edit to remain separately reversible after a delayed negative receipt.

**VEC6. Give delayed evidence power to revoke a narrow edit without rebooting old service.** Parts: VD03 delayed negative evidence, VD11 scope by contrast, BM3-06 no refund of served loss, BM3-21 revoked scope, LW09 exposed descendants, LW20 recantation. *Mechanism:* Pin scope to an independent contrast and treat any verdict that arrives outside the settlement window as a provenance event, not a new vote. Refit or retire only changes that relied on the invalid premise, including those trained on its downstream exposures. Release unused reservations without reviving the revoked scope or forgiving logged old-read loss. *New:* A learner could correct a late mistake without deleting unaffected old progress or disguising the cost of the exposed version. *First test:* Compare adapter rollback and exact observed-log refit in a small staged task where the revoked edit changes the examples seen by one later learner but not another.

**VEC7. Use a shadow contradiction to buy observation, not to buy publication.** Parts: BM3-11 pre-window read, BM3-17 separating probe, BM3-24 stricter public settlement, BM3-26 missing bit, DA12 sealed assignment, VD13 hard rival, RN01 narrowed write, TP01 evidence versus temptation, TP05 causal public intermediate. *Mechanism:* Re-read a fresh contradiction against the predecessor and the tentative version on the same public input. If only a write-dependent route sees it, run a separately budgeted benign probe that separates meaningful rival causes. Cross the true public evidence with a tempting instruction from another world and check whether intervening on the proposed intermediate changes the decision. Keep irreversible publication blocked until a verified common scope remains and a utility veto passes. *New:* A local correction can earn better evidence without manufacturing the read that would authorize its own expansion. *First test:* Compare fixed confirmation and unrestricted shadow promotion on indistinguishable stories that separate only on a participating old read.

**VEC8. Treat an unsupported completion claim as distinct from an earned refusal.** Parts: DA22 refusal worlds, DA24 wrong rival menu, VD10 nonfalsifiable forecast, VD26 twin logs, BM3-25 approval-score audit, HW06 reproducible disproof. *Mechanism:* Register both a real action claim and a refusal claim on lawful synthetic cases, with a falsifier shared by all named explanations. If observed outcomes invalidate that family, pause its edit authority; preserve an independently supported fallback rather than substituting a more flattering rival after the fact. *New:* Correct abstention can be measured as an independently supported behavior, not merely the absence of a successful completion. *First test:* Use two tasks with the same completion rate but different valid refusal regions, and inject evidence that contradicts the entire forecast family.

**VEC9. Teach a new caller only the authority whose adverse test travels too.** Parts: DA03 interface pair, DA18 transfer diagnostic, DA26 paired proxy, VD08 interface witness, VD24 transfer adverse route, BM3-13 hand off read role, CA30 teach-back, SK03 site-local decoding, TP17 crossed bindings, TP20 decoder composition, TP22 new goal. *Mechanism:* Treat the bridge between two receivers as a separately scoped contract. Cross procedures and payloads during training, then withhold both payloads when asking the receiving caller to compose the procedures under a new goal. Use only receiver-local reads to reconstruct the candidate behavior, then prove that both favorable and unfavorable contrasts remain observable after a useful local update. Transfer the action and budget only at that tested interface; a favorable prediction alone can migrate only as a hypothesis. *New:* A compressed transferable lesson could keep live falsification without carrying the entire old audit trace or copying edit permission. *First test:* Match teach-back and replay bytes on a receiver with a familiar positive route but a changed refusal channel, then verify the previously supported old alternative.
