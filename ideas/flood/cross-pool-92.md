**TCX1. Prove that the accumulator killed the lesson before repairing it.** Parts: D5-01 reciprocal moment transplant, D5-04 cancellation hides load, D5-05 selective reset, D5-12 control lesson, D4-12 hidden optimizer collision, D4-17 isolate a state writer, CE3-18 negative control. *Mechanism:* Use reciprocal moment transplants in scratch copies at fixed weights, first moments and clock. Look for the predicted loss and rescue on a protected lesson while a matched disjoint lesson still works. Only offer a selective reset if subsequent legitimate updates stay within collateral bounds. *New:* A pair with near-zero net weight change can be blocked for an identified cause, without treating all slow learning as collateral damage from that pair. *First test:* In a two-coordinate Adam toy construct canceling gradients along one axis and an unaffected lesson on the other. Compare weight checks, global resets and reciprocal slice tests at matched service.

**TCX2. Offer a legal order and recovery window instead of a permanent veto.** Parts: D5-03 certified reopening, D5-06 directed blocker, D5-07 route-specific margin, D5-11 microbatch provenance, D3-14 observable scheduling, SD2-05 third-edit obstruction, IP19 multi-writer record. *Mechanism:* Carry a post-write teaching-response floor for each lawful order, plus a sparse trace of which batches consume its margin. If neither order works now, offer only a declared delay schedule with a checked reopening before the evidence expires. Recheck the approved order when a third writer changes its premise. *New:* A blocker can answer what to reorder, defer or narrow without encoding a permanent incompatibility between the individual changes. *First test:* Enumerate two orders, a third write, and two actual intervening gradient schedules with the same wall-clock delay. Compare a static veto and exact prospective unrolling with margin-guided scheduling; count late and unsafe admissions.

**TCX3. Transport bounds on the actual step across optimizer representations.** Parts: D5-02 projected denominator bounds, D5-08 conflicting step sizes, D5-09 compressed state collision, D5-10 remapped optimizer coordinates, D5-26 teaching-response sketch, D4-04 native update metric, IP20 optimizer transfer, D2-19 compression bound. *Mechanism:* Track lower and upper bounds on the teaching-to-readout response across state compression or feature remapping, not the largest stored moment. Charge residual uncertainty against the diagnostic margin. Escalate an inconclusive certificate to real unrolling and reject global rate rescue if it violates another old response floor. *New:* A compact certificate can survive some optimizer changes while naming the precision and update directions on which it depends. *First test:* Compare equal-size sketches on a remapped sparse toy in full and quantized precision. Include a globally rescalable case and a case only a targeted state repair can fix; use exact unrolling as the oracle.

**TCX4. Measure what taking the learner step adds beyond reading the lesson.** Parts: D5-13 update versus read baseline, D5-15 teaching-to-report cross-response, D5-17 held-out binding, D5-22 wrong-lesson control, D5-23 equal adaptation gain, D4-20 loss of next lesson, DF03 separate intervention from report, CE3-17 detect invalid exercise. *Mechanism:* At the same information budget, compare reading the example plus a follow-up to reading it and taking the native update plus the same follow-up. Score a held-out binding under a compatible and a wrong lesson. Reject an apparent hit that merely mirrors the last trained answer or reflects ordinary adaptation gain. *New:* A teaching route earns protection for diagnosis the learner actually gains by updating, not because it can memorize or read the example. *First test:* Make worlds with the same immediate adaptation gain and full local output rank but different held-out response. Compare read-only, ordinary adaptation, and update-dependent discrimination before single and paired writes.

**TCX5. Preserve diagnostic power in joint reports rather than average deltas.** Parts: D5-14 synthetic gradient triage, D5-16 readout versus update, D5-19 tail probability, D5-20 joint report law, D3-18 observability margin, CE4-24 corruption tolerance, L4-21 hit versus noise. *Mechanism:* Use a small gradient predictor only to triage which paired writes need exact shadow execution. Audit authorized joint report power at a fixed error budget and test whether loss comes from a vanished activation change or a saturated readout. A good mean response or two intact marginals does not establish power. *New:* A cheap admission screen could retain rare or distributed evidence without mistaking either a predictor error or a decoder failure for a lost learning direction. *First test:* Give a quantized two-field toy equal marginal laws, then vary cross-field dependence and rare hits independently. Compare mean tests, predicted-gradient triage, and exact audit at matched query counts.

**TCX6. Repair the cheapest cause without accepting an uncalibrated answer.** Parts: D5-18 lawful teaching substitute, D5-21 old-service constraint, D5-24 polarity recalibration, D5-25 crossed state and lesson repairs, D3-22 shared counterexample, D2-23 same closure, L4-17 protect peer effect, CE4-21 surviving third write. *Mechanism:* Evaluate state repair, lawful substitute lesson, and their combination on the same existing obligation. Require old service and both writes to survive; if separation returns with reversed polarity, charge an independent calibration to the original query budget. Return an explicit unresolved case when no tested option meets the floors. *New:* A local repair can save a paired update without implicitly changing either what is being diagnosed or the service it was meant to protect. *First test:* Cross a localized moment collision with a changed legal lesson menu in a finite learner. Compare state-only, substitution-only, full replay and joint repair, including a case where the apparently cheapest route reads backward.
