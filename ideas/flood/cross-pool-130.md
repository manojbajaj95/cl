Any path-sensitive extension requires EC26. *New:* An archive earns its role in learning only if it improves retention and transfer on virtual bodies that were not used to fit the extension. *First test:* Introduce a skill on one virtual body and score it on held-out past bodies. Compare one-shot transfer, ghost-body regularization and harmonic extension against ED10 matched RMA.

**ABX3. Across upgrades, test both replayed evidence and a new lesson, not simply compatibility of current actions. *New:* A persistent driver is valuable only if policy upgrades can reuse it without hiding a change in what old observations teach. *First test:* Swap virtual unit and policy versions independently and compare per-policy calibration, a shared conjugator and a persistent driver with ED10 matched RMA.
