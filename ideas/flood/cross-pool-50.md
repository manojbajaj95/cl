# Cross-pool merges, round 50

Focal sources: `tree/fixed-budget-handoffs.md` (PR #203, FB#), `tree/partner-folds.md` (PR #204, PF#), `tree/order-handoffs.md` (PR #205, OH#), and `tree/successor-funded-returns.md` (PR #206, SF#). Crosses with renewable witness carriers (PR #177, WC#), fold-earned headroom (PR #193, FH#), safe order families (PR #195, SO#), successor recovery (PR #196, SC#), batch-order interventions (PR #197, BO#), joint releases (PR #198, JR#), joint branch moves (PR #199, JB#), group obstructions (PR #200, GO#), shared-coverage bounds (PR #201, SB#), absorbing cycles (PR #202, AC#), and earlier rounds 1–49. These are raw proposals. Freeze the witness, useful jobs, merge commitments and protected domains. Cap persistent storage and transient peaks separately. Spent proof allowance, physical storage and partner-specific merge credit are distinct ledgers.

**HFP1. Relay the witness, the remaining orders and the exact bill in one bounded packet.** Parts: FB01 ring handoff, FB04 liveness, FB13 bounded receipts, OH01 residual poset, OH04 multi-hop relay, PF02 partner-vector folds, PF15 nonfungible credit, SO07 partial execution, SC21 reusable carriers. *Mechanism:* Retire a carrier only after every continuing reader holds an exact replacement or has finished. Transfer an executed down-set, a public entry test, charged carry channels and a residual proof along with the frozen witness binding. A newly installed fold earns only its verified same-scope credit for each committed partner; do not turn that into free storage or a reset lease. *New:* A chain of useful acquisitions might keep both a repair and schedule choice available without storing a chain of old checkpoints. *First test:* Run at least three real spends under fixed persistent and peak caps. Compare accumulating maps, cached replay, and blind relays with swapped partners and a still-live old reader.

**HFP2. Teach at the seam without guessing the old spend or the next schedule.** Parts: FB02 observation classes, FB06 synchronizing data, FB12 witness-invisible aliasing, OH10 full product, OH13 non-factorability, PF06 dormant drift, PF07 lookahead, GO09 slot relations, SB05 shared curriculum, SF02 preserve alternatives, SF05 coupled choices. *Mechanism:* Choose one predeclared truthful example and public recovery mode for the full product of aliased histories and residual schedules. Require at least two genuinely distinct useful orders to survive and bill partner exposure on all futures the packet admits. Verify that excluded history/order pairs are unreachable from the public seam, not merely inconvenient to certify. The example need not erase differences between histories; it just has to make the same recovery legal. *New:* One lesson could preserve option value simultaneously in the witness binding, the schedule and the partner menu. *First test:* Include a product where every row and column has its own winning example but none works globally. Compare point estimates, charged probes, projected updates and exact robust teaching.

**HFP3. Fold jointly only when both the refund and the scratch bill beat the alternatives.** Parts: FB07 exhaustion, FB08 compaction, PF08 feasible-partial super-additivity, PF09 minimal arity, PF12 staging, OH05 escrow branches, OH06 residual spend, FH03 separate gains, JB4 abort-safe paired commits. *Mechanism:* Compare a joint absorption against the best lawful single folds, including cases where each single fold is profitable. Check transition safety for staged modes or the actual atomic boundary. Reject an otherwise favorable joint credit if its receivers or temporary copies exceed the peak cap; no credit before installation. *New:* The best merge refund can require a strictly worse storage move, so the admissible fold frontier is not given by width or by interaction alone. *First test:* Cross strict super-additivity, a joint-only feasibility jump and an excessive-scratch case while matching solo behavior and reference scope.

**HFP4. Reclaim old storage mid-bridge only after transferring its last reader.** Parts: FB05 no-gap binding, FB21 overlapping handoff, OH03 launch latch, OH08 rollback-free abort, PF11 domain guards, PF16 paired compensation, WC04 read transport, SC08 stateful transition. *Mechanism:* If a launch already committed, transfer its outstanding reads and lease phases before clearing the old storage. Use a paired invariant for stateful readers and include delayed optimizer reads. Price the partner debit of any temporary fold; if the replacement cannot fit, finish in place if safe or take the certified residual fallback. *New:* Precise liveness can support mid-flight carrier reuse without pretending an endpoint-neutral fold is safe while readers still run. *First test:* Interrupt every bridge step with an outstanding read or no read and compare exact liveness, whole-prefix snapshots and naive early reclamation.

**HFP5. Spend the reclaimed slot on another witness without pledging it to two branches.** Parts: FB11 multiplexing, FB16 optimizer storage, OH05 parallel handoffs, OH20 lease surplus, PF04 partner routing, PF15 separate partner credits, JR08 half-use fallback, SO10 shared lease composition, SF03 joint calls, SF10 displaced service. *Mechanism:* Allocate one slot to alternative residual paths under a public reservation rule. If two jobs can both materialize, prove simultaneous or serialized use meets both deadlines and account for the donor jobs displaced by the booking or refuse the second booking. Treat merge-interaction refunds as specific to their partners; a slot used for one repair cannot buy an extra merge liability entitlement. *New:* A counted piece of optimizer storage could act as a time-shared carrier without giving rise to an imaginary common budget across independent obligations. *First test:* Use simultaneous and mutually exclusive arrivals with different latencies; measure actual service and blocked handoffs, not reservation count.

**HFP6. Reprice a handoff when a partner class refines, without decoding the spend.** Parts: FB09 targeted refinement, FB15 future validity, OH07 hidden seam conflict, OH17 teaching versus probes, PF16 debit escrow, PF18 partner refinement, GO03 overlapping obstructions, JB10 asymmetric staleness. *Mechanism:* Reopen only the claims whose dependencies the new partner or prefix changes. When one public seam aliases incompatible carrier relations, choose a lawful timely observation only if every answer permits a verified handoff or fallback. Inherit no credit across a changed partner class without a sound bound, and compensate any newly exposed debit before commit. *New:* One narrow read can renew a broken handoff without rebuilding either the ancestor or the old partner table. *First test:* Include a query that refines the wrong history pair and a favorable old partner whose new class would violate its cap.

**HFP7. Export the least evidence needed to distinguish a true budget wall from a weak checker.** Parts: FB07 exhaustion certificates, FB19 no ancestor recovery, OH09 cut placement, OH13 product obstruction, PF09 arity witnesses, PF20 certified interaction versus surrogate, GO17 predicate arity, SB09 target witnesses. *Mechanism:* Separate a global storage obstruction, a prefix-restricted schedule obstruction and a deadline-sensitive proof unknown. Export a reachable counter-history recipe and scoped lower bound, not the snapshot it was found on. Show whether any allowed fold or observation can shrink the obstruction under the original caps. *New:* An importer can know whether to coordinate, reacquire one relation or withhold without learning which old spend occurred. *First test:* Create matched cases of true capacity failure, pairwise-only feasibility and exact-safe but unproved transfer; compare a repaired certificate with more compute and a guessed reset.

**HFP8. Replace the old batch order before paying for more data or private state.** Parts: FB03 executable nondifferentiable witness, FB06 synchronizing batch, OH11 no required coalescence, OH18 seam-time recovery, PF03 nonpermutation folds, PF11 guarded domains, BO01 held multiset, BO17 compile splices. *Mechanism:* Offer a checked permutation over batches the receiver already holds as an alternative to a new lesson. Certify endpoint tolerance, safe live prefixes, the required slot relation and a sound merge bound for every promised partner. A successful order may leave slots unequal across histories; the same frozen witness must still run. *New:* Some alias classes can be repaired through legal order and a fold rather than a private state write or accumulating data bank. *First test:* Compare same-latency accumulation and clock-matched optimization, cheap projected recovery, ordinary replay and direct authorized slot transfer on the same held-out jobs.

**HFP9. Keep a repeatable handoff alive through a useful off-cycle job.** Parts: FB12 alias robustness, FB17 adversarial spends, FB22 robust binding, OH12 distinct arrival regimes, OH16 interior orders, PF13 staged cascades, PF14 negative synergy, AC02 changed attractor, AC16 full excursions, SF12 launch windows, SF17 causal funding. *Mechanism:* Allow ordinary useful work to shift the carrier's cycle, then re-establish a forward return relation through every admitted schedule. Check old readers, all installed jobs, and partner residuals throughout the excursion. Cross useful shared and equally useful separate successor training at identical width, and report whether C buys earlier or later feasible launches rather than just cheaper inference. Stability at return cannot justify dropping an interior order or crediting a merge refund. *New:* A changing scheduler and growing obligations could be allowed inside a renewable service contract rather than treated as off-cycle exceptions. *First test:* Compare an idle coordinate swap, separate storage, an absorbing cycle that loses a protected phase and a cycle with verified service under adversarial arrivals.

**HFP10. Withdraw a witness deliberately when no useful schedule can keep it on budget.** Parts: FB14 declared-value retirement, FB18 measured horizon, OH08 safe abort, OH21 equal-byte amortization, PF14 joint veto, PF22 proof-cost break-even, JR20 nonrefundable obligations, JRO7 separate refund proofs. *Mechanism:* Predeclare which future offered services may be withdrawn and price their loss. Compare paying coordination and proof work to preserve the witness with a safe refusal, retaining every already installed skill and committed partner duty. Overwrite only obsolete receipts whose live readers and outstanding rights have been discharged. *New:* Planned retirement can bound archival growth without concealing failed repeated handoffs as useful service. *First test:* Increase jointly committed jobs without increasing caps; compare full replay, uncapped archival ceiling, constant-byte caches and honest retirement at matched prefix floors.

**HFP11. Keep a return safe when the quote changes, but don't call it funded until the second job clears.** Parts: FB13 bounded logs, FB18 service horizon, PF15 partner credit, PF17 coalition credit, OH04 multi-hop relay, OH20 residual allowance, FH03 separate gains, SF04 repricing, SF09 delayed credit, SF14 coefficient holes, SF17 causal comparison, SF20 principal repayment, JRO7 separate proofs. *Mechanism:* Publish a safe relation over legal order, carrier and coefficient choices and a separate affordability region for an actual common policy. Test whether acquiring useful C widens the joint safe launch set or lowers its price relative to an equally useful separate C. If tariffs move, keep the unchanged safety proof, reprice only the surviving policies, and book recurring credit only after both calls serve and the donor is repaid. *New:* A relay can preserve safe options across repricing without pretending that an unserved or singly served promise already funded its next hop. *First test:* Blind-reveal two co-calls and a new tariff with a hole between two safe coefficient bands. Compare frozen quotes, full rechecking and safe-scope reuse while charging failed imports, reacquisition and displaced donor jobs.
