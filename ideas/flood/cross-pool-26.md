# Cross-pool merges, round 26

Focal sources: `tree/momentum-fossils.md` (PR #136, MF#) and `tree/fact-free-skill-inheritance.md` (PR #137, FF#). Crosses with optimizer-state memory (PR #109, OM#), cross-generation inheritance (PR #130, GI#), reversible training (PR #131, RT#), fossil beliefs (PR #132, FB#), information dumping (PR #133, ID#), conservative extension (PR #122, CE#), Y1 hysteresis, Y10 Baldwinian assimilation, Z5 endosymbiotic consolidation, and earlier rounds. These are raw proposals. Audit direct facts, compiled derivatives, procedure use on held-out supplied premises, explicit owner policies and untouched old tasks separately. A slot swap demonstrates a carrier only under the tested continuation; claims beyond the first update need first-update and linearized controls. Charge scratch training and all retained state.

**MFH1. Inherit how hard a skill is to disturb, not what the ancestor believed.** Parts: MF2 second moments, MF6 pinned importance, FF15 alien worlds, FF16 inheritance exam, OM12 cross-optimizer protection, GI8 plasticity marks, IR5. *Mechanism:* Train scratch teachers on opposing premise assignments and extract a proposed update-scaling rule from what they agree protects across those assignments. Install that rule on the successor's own feature basis without transplanting raw moments into unrelated coordinates. Compare to fresh slots, raw slots where compatible, and the same rule estimated from successor data alone. *New:* A successor might inherit a transferable rule for protecting the procedure while discarding premise-specific inertia. *First test:* Transfer a small route ranker to a widened or permuted host. Test retained procedure and stale derivatives through a common new-task stream against EWC and a matched-plasticity fresh preconditioner.

**MFH2. Install an unknown value before retiring an old binding, and test whether the slots respect it.** Parts: MF11 cleanup after correction, MF19 delayed release, FF6 unbinding, FF7 typed unknown, CE2 caller context, FB4 clarification, FI2. *Mechanism:* First establish a public request or abstention for unresolved premises and preserve the known-value paths. Probe the same next lessons with intact, selectively altered and reset slots to see whether an unsupported old answer reappears instead of the request. Admit the change only when unrelated learning does not silently fill the hole from history under the declared test horizon. *New:* An uninstall can preserve a procedure that knows what to ask, while treating the optimizer as another possible source of an obsolete default. *First test:* Use a tiny rules learner with closures, temporary uncertainty and explicit policy overrides. Compare against direct correction replay, an unknown lookup-only route and all-slot reset at matched live coverage and compute.

**MFH3. Absorb a helper one operation at a time, with separate clocks for weights and learning state.** Parts: MF4 half-life maps, MF8 per-source slots, MF16 denominator attribution, FF4 decompile before forgetting, FF10 deconsolidation, Y10, Z5, IR10. *Mechanism:* Move a helper's candidate step into the learner only after swapping the premise source in scratch shows that the step reads the new value. Then give the paired branches identical examples and defer release of the helper until the inherited optimizer schedule no longer preferentially trains on its old bindings. A failing step can revert without undoing the rest of the handoff. *New:* Handoff has separate tests for a callable procedure and for the training state that will reshape it after the helper retires. *First test:* Transfer a three-stage symbolic/neural workflow under a fixed helper-call budget. Compare joint assimilation, op-wise assimilation alone and a permanent helper, with intact and reset slots and a held-out new premise family.

**MFH4. Find the cheapest training cycle that can resurrect a fact nobody asked about.** Parts: MF3 readability ladder, MF5 counters, MF18 slot order, FF18 skill-only resurrection, FF23 fact twins, Y1 hysteresis, FT13 history-by-future crossover (PR #123), LA4. *Mechanism:* Give equal-output pairs the same materialized new minibatches in opposite orders, never replaying the obsolete fact. Measure the first point where direct or derived answers relapse and retest with swapped first moment, second moment, or schedule state under explicit effective-step controls. Plot relapse and recovery on repeated approaches to and away from the disputed premise, rather than assuming the same threshold in both directions. *New:* A short, fact-free cycle can challenge an expiry decision and reveal which history-dependent protection or residue the release should change. *First test:* Use a nonlinear shared-readout ranker and a fixed-feature control. Compare to the best one-update and linearized forecasts, margin-matched generic challenge batches and a norm-matched reset.

**MFH5. Compile a pending repair into a portable instruction, then erase the donor slot.** Parts: MF19 delayed releases, MF20 upcoming shocks, FF17 fact holes, FF19 entanglement asymmetry, OM6 discharge into a predictor, RT2 compute–copy–uncompute, IR1. *Mechanism:* In a reversible scratch audition, compare a pending slot-written repair with applying the same initial displacement directly. If a benefit survives several changed-premise futures, compile it as a small conditional instruction keyed to observable changes in procedure behavior. Rewind or discard the scratch run as permitted, and transfer only the instruction with its limits, not the original velocity. *New:* A latent adaptation can be inherited as a checked response to a future event rather than as an unexplained impulse tied to donor coordinates. *First test:* In a small reversible heavy-ball host, cross a known shift with several compatible successor gradients. Compare the instruction to a stored delta, repeated replay and transferred momentum at equal tape, bytes and updates.

**MFH6. Make a conservative extension certificate cover supported retraining, not just today's calls.** Parts: MF13 slot covariance, MF15 inherited checkpoint audit, FF16 alien-world certificates, FF22 schema migration, CE5 recurrent induction, CE13 retraction, CT1, CT5. *Mechanism:* When a premise moves to a new slot or namespace, check the declared old behavior on unchanged scopes and check a separate bounded family of subsequent updates on migrated state. Cross optimizer migration with no migration, reset, and a documented symmetry transformation. Retain a checked retraction for archived uses; do not use sampled continuation tests as proof for unbounded futures. *New:* A migration could preserve an old interface and still fail the narrower, distinct promise that resume-training will not revive stale derivatives. *First test:* Widen a routed interpreter and migrate a historical fact field while changing an ordinary default. Compare semantic checks alone, continuously frozen versioning, and continuation-gated migration at matched cost.

**MFH7. Swap the site of erasure to spare the evidence the procedure needs.** Parts: MF14 quantized slots, MF16 second-moment attribution, FF1 routed damping, FF9 synthesized retain sets, ID1 valid collisions, ID7 friction routing, FL1 carrier liveness, FI1. *Mechanism:* Under the same bit or update budget, alternate compressing a candidate slot subspace and reducing precision at a weight commit. Require a forward intervention to demonstrate that the choice reduces stale derivatives without wiping out transfer to held-out premise combinations. Test real many-to-one state collisions separately from injective shrinkage; only count a cast as erasure when the relevant source copy is no longer live. *New:* Expiry can choose where to discard information using the fate of a premise-conditioned procedure, not a global slot norm or tape size. *First test:* Combine an enumerated fixed-point case with a compatible tiny MLP, comparing directed erasure to random quantization, damping without quantization, and direct correction replay.

**MFH8. Let old Adam statistics buy protection only if they pay for it without a stale premise.** Parts: MF2 second moments, MF7 age-resolved audit, MF22 slot cost, FF13 measured bottleneck price, FF14 volatility-aware rates, Y1, KT1, SP13 (PR #112). *Mechanism:* Lease a second-moment contribution for one training window after expiration. Measure whether keeping it reduces the real replay or repair needed to protect the procedure on new premises; charge any lost new-task speed and cancel renewal when derivative relapse or repair costs exceed that benefit. Test separate numerator, denominator and step-counter controls rather than calling a scalar LR change protection. *New:* Long-lived optimizer state would be retained only as verifiable service capacity, not because a large statistic is presumed useful. *First test:* Alternate stable and volatile world facts in a two-task Adam toy. Compare leases to fixed decay, an always-pinned second moment, tuned EWC and a same-signal allocator at equal service floors.

**MFH9. Hand over a procedure whose old world is never needed to teach it.** Parts: MF12 full-state cleanup, MF17 slot forecasts, FF3 premise diversity, FF15 alien worlds, FF23 paired twins, GI9 teaching artifacts, FB5 correction-robust evidence, VI4 independent stories. *Mechanism:* Ask an ancestor to generate small teaching worlds without its actual expired binding. Hold the rule fixed and vary roles and premises independently so counterfactual combinations cannot be solved by one shortcut. Rank packets by what a fresh recipient can do after the tape and original teacher are gone, including a common training suffix that could reveal hidden dependence. *New:* The transmission bottleneck could select exercises that teach rule composition, instead of filtering inherited answers one at a time. *First test:* Use navigation with correlated bridge and vehicle constraints and hold out whole joint assignments. Compare alternate-world drills to same-byte replay, a compiled exercise library and distillation from a correctly conditioned ancestor; separately audit the historical query route.

**MFH10. Let two corrections commute only when neither leaves the other a bad training state.** Parts: MF11 post-correction cleanup, MF18 slot commutator, FF11 derivative truth maintenance, FF20 full-state cleaning, FB21 composing inherits, RT9 commuting segments, ID22 interacting sinks, FI9. *Mechanism:* Apply two scoped corrections in both orders with matched seeds. At the same served endpoint, run an independent common lesson and swap slot state between the orders. If a derivative reappears in only one order, stage a joint cleanup or keep an explicit precedence in the dependency graph; don't assume independent success implies safe composition. *New:* Correction compatibility becomes a property of resumed learning, not just whether both edits return the right facts at commit. *First test:* Plant two premise corrections sharing a learned routing operator. Compare joint tests with ordinary endpoint gates, unconditional reset between edits and correction mixing at matched total budget.

**MFH11. A successor that can keep a skill and retire both an old premise and its momentum.** Parts: MF1 paired velocity assay, MF2 second moment, MF3 readability, MF4 half-lives, MF5 counters, MF7 age audit, MF11 cleanup, MF12 continued-training audit, MF13 covariance, MF14 quantization, MF15 checkpoint inheritance, MF16 denominator, MF18 order, MF19 pending repair, MF22 budget, FF1 damping, FF2 census, FF3 diversity, FF4 decompilation, FF6 unbinding, FF7 unknown, FF9 retain sets, FF10 deconsolidation, FF11 derivatives, FF15 alien worlds, FF16 certificate, FF17 fact holes, FF18 resurrection, FF20 slots, FF21 history, FF22 migration, FF23 twins, OM6, GI8, RT2, FB21, ID1, CE13, Y1, Y10, Z5, FI3, LA4. *Mechanism:* Before assimilation, use paired premise worlds to determine which paths read the supplied premise and which replay a compiled answer. Unbind or migrate only authorized premises, preserve deliberate overrides and dated reads, and use reversible auditions to test narrowly scoped weight and slot repairs. Resume normal learning on a shared stream under crossed state controls, admit the successor only if delayed stale derivatives stay bounded and held-out procedure use survives, and retain a fallback where the carrier is unresolved. *New:* The inheritance gate checks whether the successor can use a procedure without learning its ancestor's world again from its own optimizer. *First test:* Extend PR #130's small corrected-world model with an Adam resume stage and both read-through and compiled skill regions. Plant direct, derived and delayed relapse. Compare tuned disagreement gating, new-fact replay, explicit lookup, a weight-only intervention and full norm-matched reset. Report ordinary live service, independent supplied-premise transfer, delayed derivatives and new acquisition at matched budget.
