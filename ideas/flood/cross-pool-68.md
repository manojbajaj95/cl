**HVR1. Make a hint earn withdrawal on the same event that could falsify the live write.** Parts: HW07 erase before acting, HW20 independent reconstructions, LW19 independent referee, LW22 test observability, EL21 scoped renewal, TE19 last usable read, BM2-01 version-specific opening, BM2-07 no self-granted permission. *Mechanism:* Train from one lawful public reconstruction of a previously assisted decision, then use a separately originated outcome to test the resulting unassisted route. Bind the opening to the recalled version and require new evidence from outside the candidate write before reopening. Publish the minimum opportunity to observe a counterexample before training; if withdrawal removes it, defer promotion. Renew only the clause that the observed outcome separates. *New:* The hint, temporary write and lease can disappear on different evidence schedules rather than sharing a single success flag. *First test:* Hold out one reconstruction path and suppress the distinguishing outcome in a matched synthetic task; compare fixed hint annealing and independent promotion checks at equal observation cost.

**HVR2. Preserve two backed routes when the smallest hint is not the best teacher.** Parts: HW03 minimal successful sets, HW19 old-skill margins, HW24 alternative route, EL07 separate fallback, LW03 utility veto, ODC1 strategy portfolio, BM2-03 shared-channel meanings, BM2-15 finite debit. *Mechanism:* Keep a frontier of nonmonotone hint sets and tag each by its impact on a specific unaided old route. Verify the fallback with the preferred route disabled. Charge the finite edit against the old read that actually sees it, including the shared channel's other meaning. A live edit that forecasts correctly but harms that fallback cannot graduate; keep a separately valid predictor if its interface and state are actually separable. *New:* Assistance can become smaller without concentrating all future success on the tutor's favorite mechanism. *First test:* Match current unaided success and teacher bandwidth against a monotone ladder and portfolio replay, then hide the preferred route and measure old margins and recovery cost.

**HVR3. Carry a corrected update across versions without carrying its authority blindly.** Parts: EL12 version-sensitive correction, EL24 separate clocks, EL25 correction coverage, LW25 rival contrast, HW16 translated lesson, SK05 immutable key. *Mechanism:* Predeclare the receiver class and bound how the correction transports under its actual features. Move the lesson or provisional delta only if the registered rival and intended effect remain distinguishable at the destination. Reuse of the correction adds no new evidence; a changed observer can force a new verdict without invalidating an independently verified fact. *New:* Transport could reuse computation and lessons while making confidence explicitly nonportable when a policy change erases the relevant contrast. *First test:* Compare stale replay, fresh local correction and frozen-key transport after an ordinary receiver update and an observation-equivalent version change.

**HVR4. Earn the right to stop showing a tutor by restoring skipped observations.** Parts: EL14 skipped actions, EL22 positive support, LW10 verdict exposure, LW22 disarmed test, HW10 public sensing, DR11 blind clear. *Mechanism:* Where training causes the preferred action to disappear from ordinary data, use a bounded harmless randomized choice in a synthetic stream to restore observable support. Freeze assignment probabilities, estimator, scope and a public alternative sensor before learning. Clear the privileged hint on every branch only after the unaided route and fallback meet their declared floors. *New:* A teacher could restore the evidence needed to remove itself, rather than being judged on the biased states left by its own advice. *First test:* Match verification slots against a fixed holdout and an uncorrected live audit, with a world shift that changes whether the skipped action is necessary.

**HVR5. Retract a false premise without undoing unrelated earned learning.** Parts: LW09 exposed descendants, LW20 revoked authority, EL02 dependent premises, EL05 split lease, HW18 invalid hints, CA22 transitive retraction, BM2-09 local reopening, BM2-13 no refund for intermediate mistakes. *Mechanism:* Record which provisional edits, hint clauses and future examples depend on each independent observation. When that premise is corrected, check downstream data exposure and refit or retire only affected branches on still-valid data. Keep the cost of any intermediate old-read failure even if the final correction erases its visible effect. Preserve unrelated old reads and keep independent fallback leases active. *New:* Selective retraction may remove the tutor's downstream influence that simple weight subtraction leaves behind. *First test:* Compare full observed-log refit, adapter subtraction and ancestry-only removal under one planted recantation with a coupled descendant and one independent old skill.

**HVR6. Let evidence settle common ground even when rival stories demand different edits.** Parts: LW21 shared partial effect, LW26 indistinguishable rivals, EL26 discriminating audit, EL17 separate noises, HW21 distill queries, RN01 narrow capability. *Mechanism:* When echo and world drift predict the same observed labels but disagree on off-path utility, authorize only the verified common behavior. Keep the unresolved part provisional and request a bounded public observation only if it separates the rivals. If no admissible distinguishing event exists, defer rather than letting partial evidence authorize the entire write. *New:* A learner might keep useful partial progress without confusing better prediction on shared support with causal resolution. *First test:* Construct equal observed logs in two benign symbolic worlds with opposite off-path effects. Compare full early commitment, total refusal and the common-part update at matched old-task retention.

**HVR7. Schedule one rare referee for two contenders without selling it twice.** Parts: LW23 joint falsification, LW24 future referee reserve, EL08 evidence order, EL10 lease coverage, HW09 decisional timing, OS20 joint deadlock, BM2-21 joint debit, BM2-23 single old-behavior budget. *Mechanism:* Schedule updates and observation opportunities together. Evidence may serve different declared analyses when it remains valid for both, but a write that changes how the referee outcome is produced cannot also spend its uncontaminated version. Check interacting edits as one finite change when they have indivisible consequences; charge the joint effect to one shared old-read balance, not two independent allowances. If both edits need incompatible observation orders, offer only one backed route and preserve the prior skill. *New:* The binding limit on parallel learning may be independent future verdicts rather than update or memory capacity. *First test:* Plant two separately valid writes and a third that closes the only joint order. Compare static observation quotas, exact causal scheduling and a duplicate-counting control.

**HVR8. Use successful hint pairs to identify a mechanism and leave neither hint live.** Parts: HW11 swapped hints, HW26 paired intervention, LW14 naturally matched pair, EL11 paired echo correction, EL21 exact claim, BM13 expiring tags. *Mechanism:* On a small task with independently manipulable hint clauses, freeze the four intervention cells and estimate which interaction survives public reconstruction and answer swaps. Remove both hints and test a fresh unaided continuation. A residual estimated using shared random draws may help training but buys no extra renewal votes. *New:* Short-lived assistance can teach a joint decision rule whose parts neither author nor agent could validate independently. *First test:* Compare individual hint distillation, equally cheap matched correction and joint training with inhibitory hint combinations and an independent retained old query.

**HVR9. Compress proof of what a lesson still teaches without caching the hint itself.** Parts: HW13 reusable decoder, HW25 public retest, LW06 separate active writes, EL03 translation lease, EL20 compress residual, WM11 check before release, CA30 teach-back. *Mechanism:* Give a fresh receiver the lawfully available lesson decoder and a separate scoped test, not the original hint payload. Withhold teacher traces, require old and new behaviors after overwriting the payload, and expire the translation if its next required observation cannot disambiguate overlapping writes. *New:* Memory reclamation can preserve a teachable public check without retaining privileged assistance or a global confidence tag. *First test:* Match proof and decoder bytes against ordinary replay and per-receiver teaching; change one interface, deliver verdicts out of order and perform a real overwrite.
