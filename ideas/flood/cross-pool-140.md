## Merge proposals

**CVX1. Carry verdicts through authorized rule changes without carrying retired authority.** Parts: CV01 scoped verdict migration, CV02 scoped clarification, CV03 proved release diff, CV04 surviving authority sets, CV05 boundary transplant, CV06 fixed acceptance kernel, CV07 exception rejection certificate, CV27 premise-bound veto, NP3-22 retractable source contribution. *Mechanism:* Keep alternative sufficient supports for each verdict. For an independently signed amendment, check agreement and flip regions, then lift clarification constraints and vetoes only where their bindings and authority still hold. Reopen unsupported regions while retaining the underlying evidence. Search may adapt; a timeout remains unknown. *New:* An evidence source can lose authority without forcing a recheck of verdicts with independent support, and a reused clarification can still expose an unintended flip at a new exception boundary. *First test:* On toy baggage tariffs, interleave a harmless rewrite, source withdrawal and a signed exception. Compare incremental dependency invalidation, full recheck and scoped migration on stale accepts, missed authorized flips, and unchanged and historical verdicts.

**CVX2. Turn a missing premise into the smallest justified evidence purchase.** Parts: CV08 evidence receipt, CV09 checked repair witness, CV10 separating observation, CV11 shared observation costs, CV12 guarded verdict, CV13 random coverage audit, CV20 two uncertainty types, CV21 independent evidence gate, AL4-02 ambiguity witness, AL4-10 smallest rescue view. *Mechanism:* Start from one premise-to-trace receipt per verdict. If a wrapper drops a field, return two compatible toy executions giving opposite verdicts and request the least-cost permitted observation that separates them. Share one acquisition across all premises it settles. If candidate checkers disagree after the evidence is fixed, purchase independent adjudication instead; keep prior conclusions conditional until their own guards are discharged. *New:* The verifier can reuse a plan for resolving uncertainty without treating a missing field, a genuine violation and a checker error as the same kind of negative example. *First test:* Hide two fields independently in toy packing traces, including one shared by several rules and one that cannot be restored. Compare provenance slicing plus active querying, full restoration and matched-byte replay at equal coverage; pay for observations and adjudications separately and estimate error from a reserved random audit.

**CVX3. Separate what a solver update invalidates from what a checker update can teach.** Parts: CV15 crossed shift probes, CV16 expiring process progress, CV17 selection-weighted repair, CV18 patch scope, CV22 paired teaching receipt, CV28 shadow policy audit, CV33 hidden-factor assay, AL4-26 accumulated change bounds, PL5-10 held-out effect family. *Mechanism:* Replay the same synthetic execution under crossed solver, trace, checker and rule changes. Carry proved step validity separately from progress, tied to the last independently verified continuation; accumulated change that could cross the progress margin reopens only that judgment. Ship checker patches with boundary witnesses, and release them as rewards only where same-start updates improve independently scored successor choices. *New:* The same patch can be an accurate evaluator but a poor teacher, and a stronger solver can invalidate a progress label without making a step invalid. The release receipt distinguishes these effects instead of allowing aggregate reward to pick which component learns. *First test:* In a toy arithmetic curriculum hide a new family of solver continuations and two releases with equal aggregate score shifts but different causes. Compare a co-evolving grader, generic disagreement repair and impact-only audits at matched calls and updates; score erroneous writes, successor behavior, and untouched old-task retention with a held-out oracle.

**CVX4. Undo the training consequence of a bad reward without undoing a lawful rule change.** Parts: CV23 decisive-premise attribution, CV24 provisional credits, CV25 supported rankings, CV26 version-change examples, CV29 independent evidence lineage, AL4-28 retain competing outcomes. *Mechanism:* Attach each provisional update to the versioned premise and independently observed evidence that tipped a choice. Group verdicts sharing evidence rather than counting them as independent corroboration. When delayed evidence arrives, freeze only eligible suspect updates and replay matched alternatives to test the causal effect before replacing credit. Preserve independently supported rankings unless authority explicitly reverses them. *New:* A dependency is enough to route a recheck but not enough to prove training harm; paired replay can find affected updates while avoiding resets based only on a revised score. *First test:* In a toy sorting stream, correct a shared observation and separately sign a rule change that produces the same reward shift. Compare full replay, dependency-only rollback and paired replay at matched checks, bytes and compute; count old skills unnecessarily changed and erroneous credits left active.

**CVX5. Replace a checker using obligations that survive its next update.** Parts: CV14 mechanically checked witness compression, CV19 proof-checked invariance, CV30 caller obligations, CV31 future-sensitive witness reservoir, CV32 isolated release audit, CV34 portable signed obligations, NP4-13 future evidence responses, NP4-19 equivalence under future operations, NP5-26 protection under future operations. *Mechanism:* Freeze the kernel and retain signed obligations, witness alternatives and checked invariances. Pick compressed witnesses by whether they distinguish declared future amendments and interface changes, including compositions that reveal a currently hidden premise. A replacement inherits only verified caller obligations and returns the first unsupported query otherwise. Keep release samples isolated with limited looks. *New:* Success is not imitation of an old checker: a new checker must respond correctly to novel evidence and permitted edits, while keeping protected cases fixed through later edits. *First test:* Swap two small judge families in a toy tariff world, then combine a trace compression with a held-out exception. Compare distillation, equally sized replay and rules-plus-fixtures retrieval by new independent labels to target, unchanged-scope decisions, and blind release error; report unsupported amendments.

**CVX6. Keep a verdict only as long as its rule, evidence and checker warrant it.** Parts: CV01 scoped verdict migration, CV02 scoped clarification, CV03 proved release diff, CV04 surviving authority sets, CV05 boundary transplant, CV06 fixed acceptance kernel, CV07 exception rejection certificate, CV27 premise-bound veto, NP3-22 retractable source contribution, CV08 evidence receipt, CV09 checked repair witness, CV10 separating observation, CV11 shared observation costs, CV12 guarded verdict, CV13 random coverage audit, CV20 two uncertainty types, CV21 independent evidence gate, AL4-02 ambiguity witness, AL4-10 smallest rescue view, CV15 crossed shift probes, CV16 expiring process progress, CV17 selection-weighted repair, CV18 patch scope, CV22 paired teaching receipt, CV28 shadow policy audit, CV33 hidden-factor assay, AL4-26 accumulated change bounds, PL5-10 held-out effect family, CV23 decisive-premise attribution, CV24 provisional credits, CV25 supported rankings, CV26 version-change examples, CV29 independent evidence lineage, AL4-28 retain competing outcomes, CV14 mechanically checked witness compression, CV19 proof-checked invariance, CV30 caller obligations, CV31 future-sensitive witness reservoir, CV32 isolated release audit, CV34 portable signed obligations, NP4-13 future evidence responses, NP4-19 equivalence under future operations, NP5-26 protection under future operations. *Mechanism:* Keep claim, evidence, authorized rule and checker versions separate. Carry independent witnesses through approved edits; route missing observations, disputed judgments and unsupported rule bindings to different actions. Allow provisional training credit only within a receipt's tested scope, audit successor choices independently and rebuild a new checker from signed obligations rather than old predictions. Distinguish proved under stated assumptions, empirically supported, refuted and unknown throughout. *New:* A verifier can decide more cases after multiple kinds of change without changing acceptance by accident, buying the same evidence twice or mistaking better grading for better teaching. *First test:* On a synthetic sequence, change the solver, obscure a trace field, amend a rule and swap the checker. Compare frozen, replay/refit, co-evolving and full-recheck controls, plus successive removal of each receipt type, at matched evidence, storage, compute and coverage. Score authorized flips, historical and unchanged decisions, abstentions, recheck cost and independent solver success separately.
