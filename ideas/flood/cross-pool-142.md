## Merge proposals

**CVT1. Translate a clean lesson without replacing the recipient's updater.** Parts: CV3-01 lesson input translator, CV3-03 discriminating lesson suffix, CV3-05 updater transplant, CV3-08 conflicting lesson programs, CV2-23 future response equivalence, NP4-13 evidence-response distillation, NP4-19 closure under future lessons. *Mechanism:* Fit two signed residuals for a permitted lesson: one for the retracted premise and one constraining a matched unaffected context. Calibrate in observable responses to two successive updates, not parameter coordinates, and bind the held-out premise with one independently witnessed disagreement. Keep only coordinates that maintain the specified lesson sequences; when the unchanged updater cannot realize both responses, return the smallest conflicting programs and test a proposed added coordinate on a fresh pair. *New:* A repair can travel with the next lesson rather than with the checkpoint, and a failed transplant can show which response the recipient cannot express. *First test:* In a toy packing process swap SGD and Adam and hold out a premise, update schedule and lesson order. Match clean-reference access, checked labels and compute against direct response distillation, repair of the starting state and synthetic lesson generation; measure finite-sequence error and overlapping old-skill retention.

**CVT2. Restore the ability to relearn an authorized rule without keeping a head start from the revoked one.** Parts: CV3-02 authorized relearning, CV3-06 neighboring lesson search, CV3-07 slowed-learner control, CV3-19 right-to-learn witness, NP5-26 protected future reads. *Mechanism:* Designate a valid lesson that shares a feature with the revoked rule. In a linear toy fixture, protect its measured response under the declared next lessons while fitting a repair that removes excess relearning savings on the revoked rule. After explicit reauthorization, compare uptake to a clean v2 learner and a fresh matched-difficulty rule. A failed positive lesson limits the repair's scope; it is not permission to revive old behavior without new evidence. *New:* A repair should remove the bad history, not make its subject intrinsically harder to learn. Preserving one clean response now is not enough if a later correction suppresses it. *First test:* In an arithmetic toy with two revisions, split the budgets for choosing neighboring lessons and fitting the repair. Compare clean retraining, a matched slow learner and a repair regularized only on immediate scores. Measure unauthorized residue, authorized first-step and finite-step uptake, and old skills after both revisions.

**CVT3. Renew reversal witnesses when a tactic's implementation or use changes.** Parts: CV3-09 availability versus use, CV3-10 replacement intervention, CV3-11 indirect intervention, CV3-12 two-sided bounds, CV3-13 scoped sub-comparison, CV3-14 tactic substitution, CV3-15 action redistribution, CV3-16 shared intervention program, CV3-23 passive-probe obstruction, CV2-11 feasible reversing edit, CV2-14 capability toggle, CV16 split validity and progress, AL4-02 ambiguity witness, AL4-16 change footprint, AL4-23 skipped-fork audit. *Mechanism:* On each forced branch, measure making a tactic available separately from forcing or preventing its execution. For a successor, accept a direct or indirect substitute only if it preserves the signed branch contrast and passes a negative control; bound no-toggle drift separately. Reversal and restoration may both occur as capabilities combine. When the bound no longer supports the full comparison, retain checked validity and any independently justified prefix comparison, but leave full credit unknown. Share intervention execution across labels only when each has its own identified result. *New:* A probe can transfer even when a tactic name does not; neither an unchanged menu nor failure to reproduce the old reversal establishes a safe carry. *First test:* In toy proofs substitute a two-step tactic and independently redistribute voluntary use. Match calls and retained bytes against footprint-based revalidation, fixed panels, robust search and fresh branching. Include successors indistinguishable on passive probes and randomly audit skipped forks at matched coverage.

**CVT4. Use an authorized lesson to locate both the bad update and the credit that needs renewal.** Parts: CV3-04 changed next query, CV3-17 crossover lesson, CV3-18 teaching witness, CV3-20 first use divergence, CV3-21 suppressed-uptake assay, CV3-22 lesson-order seam, CV2-02 first exposure divergence, CV2-26 provisional repair closure, NP5-01 interaction between lessons. *Mechanism:* On scratch copies, cross an ordinary authorized lesson with a verified tactic intervention on paired forced branches. Locate the first difference in tactic use or requested feedback relative to clean training. Test separate corrections to lesson uptake and the associated progress judgment, using the two lesson orders to isolate a genuine interaction. Keep unaffected lesson sequences free to commute where measured; close neither correction just because the old sign persists. *New:* The same later lesson can distinguish residual teaching from a changed solver and show whether the learner, the credit label or both need a repair. *First test:* In a benign arithmetic game, hide a tactic that appears only after a permissible lesson and a question asked only by the corrected learner. Compare separate state repair plus credit invalidation, a four-cell response fit and the joint intervention at matched feedback. Require both clean adaptation and verified credit, with a held-out lesson guarding against suppressed uptake.

**CVT5. Hand a replacement verifier executable obligations instead of its predecessor's scores.** Parts: CV3-24 verifier handoff, CV30 caller obligations, CV34 rebuild from signed obligations, AL4-10 rescue view, AL4-28 retain competing evidence. *Mechanism:* Export paired lesson responses and executable tactic contrasts tagged with the caller and the checked statement they support. The receiving verifier independently checks those obligations and chooses which progress labels to renew. If a lesson cannot be expressed or a contrast is unobservable, keep competing traces and ask for the smallest permitted extra read separating the two failures; unsupported credit stays unknown. *New:* A failed handoff can teach which evidence a different verifier needs without using the old verifier's judgments as targets. *First test:* Swap verifier families in a toy packing curriculum while hiding whether the break is a new lesson request or a missing tactic observation. Match examples and calls against adaptable interpreters, replay and fresh search; test whether the returned counterexample lowers repair cost on another held-out premise without harming untouched tasks.

**CVT6. Carry forward a repair only with a lesson it preserves and a reversal it can still test.** Parts: CV3-01 lesson input translator, CV3-03 discriminating lesson suffix, CV3-05 updater transplant, CV3-08 conflicting lesson programs, CV2-23 future response equivalence, NP4-13 evidence-response distillation, NP4-19 closure under future lessons, CV3-02 authorized relearning, CV3-06 neighboring lesson search, CV3-07 slowed-learner control, CV3-19 right-to-learn witness, NP5-26 protected future reads, CV3-09 availability versus use, CV3-10 replacement intervention, CV3-11 indirect intervention, CV3-12 two-sided bounds, CV3-13 scoped sub-comparison, CV3-14 tactic substitution, CV3-15 action redistribution, CV3-16 shared intervention program, CV3-23 passive-probe obstruction, CV2-11 feasible reversing edit, CV2-14 capability toggle, CV16 split validity and progress, AL4-02 ambiguity witness, AL4-16 change footprint, AL4-23 skipped-fork audit, CV3-04 changed next query, CV3-17 crossover lesson, CV3-18 teaching witness, CV3-20 first use divergence, CV3-21 suppressed-uptake assay, CV3-22 lesson-order seam, CV2-02 first exposure divergence, CV2-26 provisional repair closure, NP5-01 interaction between lessons, CV3-24 verifier handoff, CV30 caller obligations, CV34 rebuild from signed obligations, AL4-10 rescue view, AL4-28 retain competing evidence. *Mechanism:* Keep checked validity, authorized lesson uptake and policy-relative credit separate. Translate a lesson within its observed scope, require it to remain learnable with fresh authority, and renew a credit label only if its reversal contrast is still executable and its margin survives. Use failure witnesses to request the next missing lesson or observation, not to bless an unsupported carry. *New:* A handoff can jointly preserve the right to learn an authorized rule and the ability to detect when learning it changes a step preference, without equating either to restored weights or old scores. *First test:* Stage a premise revocation, an optimizer swap, a tactic substitution and an authorized reintroduction in short synthetic tasks. Compare combined and separate repair with fresh branching, clean retraining and replay at matched evidence, storage and compute; score qualified relearning, false credit carries, uncovered cases and old-task retention separately.
