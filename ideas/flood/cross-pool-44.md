# Cross-pool merges, round 44

Focal sources: `tree/decision-relevant-queries.md` (PR #174, DQ#), `tree/cancelling-pairs.md` (PR #175, CP#), and `tree/leakage-leases.md` (PR #176, LL#). Crosses with indistinguishable edits (PR #173, IE#), irreversible splits (PR #172, IS#), certificate entry ports (PR #171, EP#), correction classes (PR #169, CC#), label-free forgetting detection (PR #126, LF#), minimal wake anchors (PR #149, WA#), accommodation games (PR #167, AG#), and earlier rounds 1–43. These are raw proposals. Hold frozen demand and old-task floors fixed. Prove pointwise output equality for a renewal on its declared domain; separately check substitution error, closure and old-task retention. A query can change the available decision, not grant permission by itself. A transfer in one continuation carries the spent balance and outstanding obligations; independent alternatives do not share a cap by default.

**QRL1. Buy the query that changes which neutral pair to renew before the last opening closes.** Parts: DQ06 expiring distinctions, CP03 complementary gates, CP14 exhaustion, LL4 prepaid reads, LL14 future amplification, IE08 teaching versus sensing, IS19 delay costs, PSP11 probe deadlines. *Mechanism:* Compare a common verified reply with a permitted observation that could select a cheaper pair renewal. Check cover while waiting and reserve the leakage cost of any later read before choosing to ask. If the answer would arrive after a compatible branch loses its repair, choose the common reply or refuse rather than count the query as useful. *New:* A query can have positive repair value in every possible world and still be worth nothing at its actual latency. *First test:* Hold probe accuracy fixed while varying read deadlines, delayed slot amplification and renewal duration; compare immediate sensing, deadline-aware sensing and teaching for a common reply.

**QRL2. Retire a query by installing a common reply, not by erasing the distinction.** Parts: DQ05 stopping proofs, DQ22 future query removal, CP18 synthesized descendants, LL15 proved headroom recovery, LL19 lower-leakage teaching, IE16 joint uncertainty, CC04 checked covers. *Mechanism:* When two affordable repairs both serve today, try a third output-neutral descendant that supports the same frozen witness across the unresolved worlds. Prove the shared reply over the full compatible state set, including its next rejoin condition, then discard detailed query answers only within that scope. Preserve neighboring repair classes where the proof does not apply. *New:* Working capacity can substitute for retained knowledge about which failure occurred, without claiming that the failures are equivalent. *First test:* Use hidden branches needing different specialists and a costlier common pair; compare probe-and-specialize, unverified class merging and neutral synthesis on repeated useful admissions and stored bits.

**QRL3. Cancel a charge only if the two effects share the same counterfactual.** Parts: DQ09 single-witness products, DQ10 missing corners, CP01 paired transactions, CP21 independent proofs, LL13 directional balances, LL16 signed cancellation, IS05 distant interactions, EP13 cross-reads. *Mechanism:* Establish a signed joint bound on the two gate effects for the same paired states, with deployed arithmetic and all relevant prefixes. Never subtract two independently bounded unsigned charges. If a joint corner fails, use a checked local patch or a flat relation, and reuse only unaffected proof claims. *New:* Two gates can cancel exactly in today's readout and still be unable to cancel each other's later proof liabilities. *First test:* Compare independent sample audits, signed joint verification and exact enumeration on hosts with hidden shared optimizer slots, coupled versus independent errors and varied rounding.

**QRL4. Pass the useful question and the remaining balance, not the original teacher.** Parts: DQ17 transferable tests, DQ19 vanishing sources, CP22 renewal interfaces, LL7 residual transfer, LL11 hidden-branch handoff, EP16 entry lemmas, WA# minimal wake anchors, PSP12 uncertain relays. *Mechanism:* Export an entry lemma, a checked test that selects between named renewal witnesses, and the local evidence needed to answer it. For a serial borrower validate the receipt, including its discrepancy envelope, rejoin obligations and remaining horizon, before authorizing a move. If an answer needs the retired teacher or reveals too little to license either witness, keep the fallback. *New:* A later learner can inherit the right question and the means to act on its answer without reconstructing the earlier learner's trajectory. *First test:* Hold out importer families and cross valid, stale and wrong-unit receipts; compare full trace transfer, fresh local planning and scoped test reuse.

**QRL5. Make label-free sentinels nominate a renewal, not certify one.** Parts: DQ07 diagnostic disagreement, DQ20 independent challengers, CP07 probe-only cancellation, CP10 slot dynamics, LL3 read-path costs, LL20 compactness obstructions, LF# label-free detection, IE18 hidden slots. *Mechanism:* Use ungraded shadow responses to decide whether to inspect opposing gates, latent slot effects or a reintroduced reader. Buy a continuation check only if it can change the safe action; keep a separate audit of verification-only gains. Do not spend the lease until an independent check establishes pointwise renewal equality and covers the actual stateful successor. *New:* A cheap alarm can direct proof effort to the read path that matters without mistaking output agreement for neutral dynamics. *First test:* Plant forward twins with equal traces and distinct delayed updates; compare fixed audits, label-free query routing and an equally budgeted labeled canary.

**QRL6. Prune a descendant only after accounting for what it can learn cheaply.** Parts: DQ03 block-level experiments, DQ08 verified ties, CP17 budgeted simulation, CP19 competing demand, LL9 genuinely shared balances, LL21 accounting costs, AG07 partial observation, CC09 successor classes. *Mechanism:* Compare descendants on the same requests and allowed observations. For each useful response from the branch being removed, require a no-dearer observation-and-response policy in the retained branch, with compatible disclosures and a related successor for every allowed outcome. Carry shared-cap conflicts into that relation. Do not pay to split a checked tie. *New:* Two branches with the same frozen-edit menu can have different real value because only one knows how to find the correct affordable reply. *First test:* Enumerate multi-round graphs with cheap first moves, hidden answers and competing uses of one pair; compare menu inclusion, full-information simulation and observation-aware pruning.

**QRL7. Restore only the information an omitted coordinate needs to return.** Parts: DQ04 joint-answer tests, DQ12 hidden optimizer coordinates, CP08 protected residue, CP15 drifting representations, LL1 checked rejoin, LL5 boundary relations, EP# certificate ports, WA# minimal anchors. *Mechanism:* Move a cancelling pair's protected residue into a checked readout and omit its gates from the witness projection only for a verified phase. Before reactivation, buy the smallest lawful bundle of observations that can establish the paired boundary relation or select a supported alternative. Pre-stage the rejoin outside service and reject it if even the joint evidence falls short. *New:* The cheapest witness for waking renewed capacity may identify a relation to the old readout rather than reconstruct either original gate. *First test:* Cross redundant, individually useless and jointly sufficient observations in a finite host with a retired source. Compare full gate checkpoints, single-query acquisition and scoped boundary checks.

**QRL8. Reverse the cost of a read only after returning to a genuinely renewable state.** Parts: DQ11 interaction-versus-witness cost, DQ16 factor patch rent, CP12 closed return cycles, CP13 re-pair spent gates, CP20 recurrence costs, LL10 no invented slack, LL15 proved recovery, IS14 cascades, CRR8 renewal under load. *Mechanism:* After a useful accepted edit, compress installed behavior into existing carriers and reconstitute a neutral pair only after verifying every retained output on its domain. Credit headroom only when a new paired error envelope and closure proof justify it, including all live obligations. If neither a factor patch nor a new witness can close the cycle affordably, sell a one-call option rather than recurring access. *New:* Physical capacity can return while the proof budget stays spent, so renewal needs two separate receipts. *First test:* Run repeated blind edits with equal initial openings but different accumulated leakage and active obligations; compare slot-only recycling, budget resets and checked dual renewal.

**QRL9. Stage an output-neutral move without assuming its intermediate checks commute.** Parts: DQ13 legal support, DQ14 order-aware products, CP05 exact neutral paths, CP11 deployed arithmetic, LL8 unit conversions, LL17 horizon extension, IS15 noncommuting splits, EP14 proof relays. *Mechanism:* Where atomic publication is unavailable, verify each served step of a neutral path. Treat reversed steps and changed charge units as new proofs unless a checked relation justifies transporting the old one. Reserve the transient budget before starting; an illegal order is not a failed data point supporting general independence. *New:* A renewal can be neutral in either final arrangement and still have only one legal, certifiable route between them. *First test:* Compare atomic staging, sequential updates and exact neutral paths with the same target gates under limited scratch and rounded arithmetic.

**QRL10. Preserve the evidence when a demand shock invalidates the authorization.** Parts: DQ01 reusable receipts, DQ02 cheap fallback, DQ18 revocation, DQ21 demand shifts, CP16 training renewable pairs, CP04 spare-width control, LL6 on-demand tightening, LL12 scoped refusal routing, CC21 versioned maps, LF# forgetting alerts. *Mechanism:* On new demand, invalidate only the menus, factor claims and wake authorizations that depend on an expired premise; keep still-valid observations. Ask about the newly binding read or train useful, output-neutral structure only where it can beat the safe fallback. Compare training against equally sized spare capacity rather than crediting all available width to renewal. *New:* A refuted authorization can still leave useful evidence about which renewal or query to try next. *First test:* Introduce unannounced requests that vary whether they invalidate only a proof, consume reserve or need a genuinely new repair; compare full cache flush, unconditional reuse and dependency-scoped repair.

**QRL11. A continual learner that trades questions, neutral capacity and verified headroom.** Parts: DQ01 reusable receipts, DQ02 priced fallback, DQ03 block queries, DQ04 answer bundles, DQ05 stopping proofs, DQ06 deadlines, DQ07 diagnostic disagreement, DQ08 verified ties, DQ09 single-witness products, DQ10 local patches, DQ11 evidence costs, DQ12 hidden slots, DQ13 legal support, DQ14 order, DQ15 dense limits, DQ16 patch rent, DQ17 transferable tests, DQ18 revocation, DQ19 source retirement, DQ20 challengers, DQ21 demand shocks, DQ22 query-free repairs, CP01 atomic pairing, CP02 protected scope, CP03 blind openings, CP04 spare width, CP05 neutral paths, CP06 neutral groups, CP07 separating cells, CP08 protected residue, CP09 coordinate invariance, CP10 optimizer transport, CP11 numeric bounds, CP12 return cycles, CP13 re-pairing, CP14 exhaustion, CP15 drift transport, CP16 learned renewal, CP17 simulation, CP18 successor synthesis, CP19 competing demand, CP20 recurrence, CP21 interference boundaries, CP22 reusable interfaces, LL1 rejoin proofs, LL2 horizon leases, LL3 read paths, LL4 prepayment, LL5 boundary relations, LL6 local tightening, LL7 residual transfer, LL8 conversions, LL9 shared caps, LL10 no manufactured slack, LL11 opaque handoffs, LL12 refusal routing, LL13 directional balances, LL14 amplification, LL15 proved recovery, LL16 signed relations, LL17 extensions, LL18 risk contracts, LL19 lower leakage, LL20 obstructions, LL21 check costs, LL22 ordinary controls, IE#, IS#, EP#, CC#, LF#, WA#, PSP11, PSP12, CRR8. *Mechanism:* Train carriers whose joint contribution can be replaced without changing protected outputs, then quote distinct useful continuations. Buy an observation only where its answer can change a checked decision in time, or build a common reply if that is cheaper. Transfer partial-state proofs with residual exposure and obligations; check rejoin and all served prefixes before publication. Keep evidence separate from authorization so a failed premise can revoke an offer without erasing what was learned. *New:* The learner can choose whether to buy a distinction, build capacity that makes it immaterial, or defer it under a bounded contract. *First test:* Use a finite stateful ReLU host with opaque editor arrivals, alternate renewals and a retiring teacher. Compare same-budget observation-based planning, fixed-width recycling with incremental verification, and certified substitution with ordinary accounting. Report net useful frozen edits, old-service prefixes, probe and delay costs, valid transfers, restored reserve, proof work and peak bytes.
