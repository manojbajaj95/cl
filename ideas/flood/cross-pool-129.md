Compare action-space transport with policy-specific transport and verify actor and critic effects on independent outcomes. If EC26 is null, retain the simple conjugator and the EC12 next-lesson check. *First test:* Run a simulation-only skill-introduction schedule with body shifts. Report held-out backward transfer, retention and added training cost against matched RMA and EB12.
