# Cross-pool merges, round 2

New sources: `self-modifying.md` (PR #43, SM#), `physics-math.md` (PR #44, PH#), `econ-society.md` (PR #45, E#). These are also combined with round 1 (X#) and the files in PRs #36/#39/#41/#42. They are raw and unfiltered; each has a mechanism, why it's new, and the cheapest first test.

**Y1. Hysteresis memory with an override budget you can read off a potential.** Parts: PH1 coercive double-well weights, PH8 shape-memory weights, PH13 cusp-catastrophe map, MZ3 trust dial, X1 override-typed weights, MX1 (passed). *Mechanism:* protected behaviors live in double-well parameters. The coercive field, the push needed to flip a well, *is* the override budget, so an editable behavior is a shallow well and a frozen one a deep well. The cusp map over (LR, replay) predicts where retention flips abruptly. *New:* correctability becomes a physical, tunable barrier instead of a route accident. *First test:* toy MLP with double-well regularized units: measure the coercive field vs the follow index of contradicting data; then sweep LR × replay for a hysteresis loop.

**Y2. Commutator-corrected learning operator.** Parts: PH6 Lie-bracket/BCH order correction, O2 commutative operators, X3 learning algebra, F1-26 order-invariance, the two-order comparator (ρ = 1.0, PR #11/#17), SM29 path certificates. *Mechanism:* estimate the BCH commutator [g_A, g_B] from two short orders (the comparator already measures this), and subtract the half-commutator so that sequential learning matches joint learning to second order. *New:* the validated comparator becomes an executable order correction instead of only a diagnostic. *First test:* two synthetic tasks: sequential vs sequential+BCH vs joint. Measure the gap closed.

**Y3. Seed-log autobiography with bisect, blame and an exact undo.** Parts: SM1 seed-log ZO updates, SM8 undo in weights, PH47 reversible optimizer, L2/P12 bisect, P15 blame, X2 weight git, E17 double-entry bookkeeping. *Mechanism:* every self-update is a (seed, scalar) pair, so the whole life is a few KB and exactly replayable and reversible. A double-entry ledger records which tasks each entry debited and credited, so bisect, blame and selective rewind are exact. *New:* exact version control for a continually learning model at the cost of a log. *First test:* 0.5B MeZO on 3 sequential tasks: rewind one entry exactly and measure blame accuracy against ablation.

**Y4. Kinetic-proofreading write gate for self-written lessons.** Parts: PH21 kinetic proofreading, contamination law R (PR #4), MZ1 vaccination, X4 Popperian memory, E4 emphasis hyperinflation, E5 Gresham's law, SM20 generational test immunity. *Mechanism:* a lesson must pass n irreversible, energy-costly checks (each a falsification test on a fresh state sample) before it is promoted to weights. Defect transmission then falls as ε^n while the cost grows linearly, which pushes R below 1. Emphasis words are normalized out first so loud lessons can't buy priority. *New:* a quantitative accuracy–cost law for memory writes, taken from biochemistry, plugged into the epidemic threshold. *First test:* challenge world with n = 1..4 proofreading stages; fit transmission vs n and check for exponential decay.

**Y5. Parallel tempering + replica overlap for memory policies.** Parts: PH22 parallel tempering of memories, PH17 replica-overlap fracture detector, A5 fork/merge, SM24 budding/fusion, F2-5 counterfactual twin, E36 threshold diversity. *Mechanism:* run K agent replicas whose memory-rewrite step has different temperatures, swapping their memory stores by a Metropolis rule on outcome. A sudden drop in replica overlap flags a phase change (drift) and triggers budding. *New:* MCMC over memory stores, with a built-in drift alarm. *First test:* G1 router with 4 replicas: tempering vs a single best temperature, and the detector's lead time on a drift injection.

**Y6. The economy of plasticity: rent, credit, taxes and a central bank.** Parts: E1 Georgist rent, E8 Taylor-rule LR, E9 plasticity credit lines, E15 VCG gradient taxes, E10 insurance pools, SM31 plasticity market, MZ4/X8 lesson market, F2-27 memory metabolism. *Mechanism:* parameter blocks pay rent proportional to gradient demand; idle blocks are reclaimed for new skills. Each update pays a VCG tax equal to its predicted externality on other tasks (X7's simulator). The LR follows a Taylor rule on output gap vs forgetting inflation, and urgent learning can borrow plasticity that must be repaid. *New:* a full mechanism-design layer whose prices are the interference measurements. *First test:* 5 sequential tasks on a small MLP: VCG-taxed updates vs EWC vs plain training at equal compute, comparing the stability–plasticity frontier.

**Y7. Hopfield dreaming at the boundary with a Red Queen parasite.** Parts: PH19 anti-Hebbian unlearning, W1 dream-erase, MZ2 boundary dreaming, E25 Red Queen parasites, F1-13 forgetting adversary, SM36 weight dreaming, X9 sleep as compilation. *Mechanism:* during sleep a parasite generator searches for boundary states (margin < shift) where the current model has forgotten. The model rehearses those states, and applies anti-Hebbian unlearning on spurious high-temperature attractors. *New:* boundary rehearsal and spurious-attractor removal in one sleep pass, with the margin law telling which states matter. *First test:* M3 world: parasite + unlearning sleep vs boundary-dream-only vs replay at equal tokens.

**Y8. Lindy, Chesterton and Muller: a retirement law for memories.** Parts: E37 Lindy protection, E38 Chesterton audits, E21 Muller's ratchet + recombination, PH23 glassy aging clocks, N33 mutation testing, F1-14 lesson half-life, M3 retirement (PR #30: deleting a lesson cost 13.25 pp calibration). *Mechanism:* a memory's protection grows with its age × verified use (Lindy). Deletion requires a mutation-test audit of what it does (Chesterton). Recombination of lesson variants stops harmful edits from accumulating (Muller). *New:* one retirement policy that combines age, causal audit and recombination, tested against the measured cost of deleting a lesson. *First test:* M3 world over 10 cycles: Lindy+audit vs recency vs keep-all, measuring calibration and accuracy.

**Y9. Oral-tradition memory: metered, redundant, self-correcting lessons.** Parts: E32 metered oral tradition, E34 parables, E30 stemmatic reconstruction, F2-23 population-coded memory, MY4 format is a route, SM17 decompile–recompile. *Mechanism:* lessons are stored in a fixed redundant template (meter and formula) across several paraphrases. Drift in any copy is corrected by majority/stemma reconstruction at each sleep, and lessons are compressed into parables that keep only the actionable moral. *New:* error-correcting codes for natural-language memory, inspired by how oral cultures kept texts stable. *First test:* 20 self-rewritten lessons over 10 rewrite generations: metered+stemma vs free rewriting, measuring semantic drift and task lift.

**Y10. Baldwinian assimilation with a budget: when context becomes weights.** Parts: E26 Baldwin effect, E11 Coasean boundary, SM16 JIT lessons, SM38 prompt–weight swap, X5 margin paging, F2-6 bidirectional consolidation, MX2 margin gating. *Mechanism:* a lesson is learned in context in every "lifetime" and assimilated into weights only when its per-use context cost × use frequency exceeds the one-time consolidation cost plus the expected correction cost (lower for editable routes). This is the Coasean make-or-buy boundary, with JIT tiering. *New:* a cost model for the context↔weights boundary whose terms are measurable (tokens, follow index, margin). *First test:* M3 world with lessons of varying frequency: Coase-rule assimilation vs always vs never, measuring total tokens and accuracy.

**Y11. Many-body-localized learning: quenched LR disorder + percolation.** Parts: PH10 MBL via LR disorder, PH25 percolation threshold, E42 crop rotation/fallow layers, functional-syndrome diffuse damage (PR #24). *Mechanism:* the ledger found damage is diffuse. MBL theory says quenched disorder stops information spreading, so random per-unit LR disorder should localize damage, stopping its spread to old-task subnetworks below the percolation threshold. *New:* turns "damage is diffuse" into a lever: localize it by disorder. *First test:* #24 harness with log-uniform per-row LR disorder at matched mean LR. Measure top-decile damage concentration and functional drop.

**Y12. Spin-echo refocusing of forgetting.** Parts: PH11 spin echo, PH12 Zeno consolidation, MZ5 global antidote, F1-5. *Mechanism:* if old-task damage is diffusive drift in directions the new task ignores, a π-pulse (reflecting the drift component halfway through training) makes the second half undo the first half's drift. *New:* forgetting is refocused by symmetry instead of penalized. *First test:* #24 harness: reflect the drift component orthogonal to the new-task gradient at the midpoint, measuring functional drop vs L2-to-init.

**Y13. Generational inheritance stack.** Parts: SM15 ontogeny distillation, SM23 curated inheritance, SM25 Lamarckian optimizer state, SM26 leash adapter, A2 testaments, E18 masterpiece exams, E19 inheritance tax, E44 epigenetic silencing. *Mechanism:* a successor is trained by replaying the parent's checkpoint order, given the parent's optimizer state and a testament with its misconceptions withheld (marked silenced), and promoted only after passing a masterpiece exam. A leash adapter lets the parent correct it. *New:* a complete protocol for model succession in continual learning. *First test:* 0.5B → 0.5B successor: the full stack vs plain distillation, measuring the parent's rare skills retained and misconceptions transmitted.

**Y14. Sheaf-consistent lesson stores.** Parts: PH31 sheaf cohomology, B3 interference (PR #28: real second lessons drop 1.114 nats vs 0.670 filler), F1-16 interference-aware packing, F2-36 conflict synthesis, N16 diamond conflicts. *Mechanism:* contexts are open sets and lessons local sections. A non-zero first cohomology flags lesson sets that are pairwise consistent but globally contradictory, and those sets are never packed together and get a reconciling lesson. *New:* a global consistency check for memory that pairwise similarity checks miss. *First test:* B3 harness: predict the interference of lesson triples by an H¹ estimate vs pairwise NLI.
