Raw proposals for harmless synthetic populations.

**PSX1. Exchange a common effect without tying the two implementations together.** Parts: PL3-01 common update coordinates, PL3-02 split evidence rails, PL3-06 two-speed patches, PL3-08 directional exchange, PL3-10 conjugated heads, PL3-13 activation compensation, PL2-03 paired write, T5-01 matched items. *Mechanism:* Specify the improvement using matched fixtures both forks can evaluate. Fit recipient-specific changes and check each branch’s signed decisions after both writes, including after temporary compensation is withdrawn. Keep private disagreements outside the shared effect. *New:* The portable object is a checked effect, not a shared adapter or a claim that the two learners use the same features. *First test:* Use two small synthetic learners with different feature bases and a shared formatting improvement. Compare copied deltas, separately projected updates and checked coupled changes.

**PSX2. Spend extra capacity only when a coupled step would consume future plasticity.** Parts: PL3-03 escape slot, PL3-04 cross-fork compensation, PL3-05 curvature-aware flow, PL3-07 paired perturbations, PL3-11 capacity bargaining, PL3-12 one lesson two updates, PL2-14 next-step contrast, D4-24 future gradient. *Mechanism:* Test candidate coupled moves in scratch state on current contrasts and fresh branch-local teaching fixtures. If all existing directions spend the room needed by a later correction, try an isolated slot with its own cost, or leave the update uncommitted. *New:* A coupled step must retain both branches’ ability to learn independently, not just preserve their decisions immediately after the write. *First test:* Compare separate projection, coupled fitting and an extra-slot arm at matched training work on toy branches with a planted late exception.

**PSX3. Make a shared update reversible even after a third write.** Parts: PL3-09 paired inverse, PL3-14 recoverable common residual, TT25 reversible commitment, TT34 joint sandbox, D3-22 joint repair witness. *Mechanism:* Record the checked effect and a small rollback witness on each fork. Before finalizing, replay compatible interleavings that include later local writes and verify that removing the shared contribution preserves those writes. If an inverse cannot separate the effects, keep the update provisional or retain an independently validated recovery path. *New:* Rollback can fail even when the paired update and its inverse both work before a subsequent local correction. *First test:* Use benign parallel streams with two compatible local writes and a third that makes simple subtraction incorrect; compare vector negation, full rewind and scoped checked reversal.

**PSX4. Keep independently supported behavior while containing a source-dependent interaction.** Parts: PL3-15 source-subset layers, PL3-16 missing-source gate, PL3-19 main-effect bypass, PL3-22 source-held-out checks, PL3-24 joint-target cancellation, PL3-27 optimizer-state separation, PL2-27 quarantine synergy, PL32 keep independent learning. *Mechanism:* For declared synthetic sources, partition candidate main effects and interaction effects. After disabling a source, check both immediate output and a later update from retained optimizer state before claiming the removed contribution is absent. Keep the interaction out if any approved check depends on the retired source. *New:* A retirement check can catch an interaction returning after training even when it disappears at the initial checkpoint. *First test:* Use synthetic sources with known main effects and a cross-term. Compare weight-only removal, full retraining and checked source isolation with and without segregated optimizer state.

**PSX5. Rebuild an interaction from current sources only when new evidence supports it.** Parts: PL3-17 allowed-only mixed contrast, PL3-18 replacement factor, PL3-20 approved synthetic pairing, PL3-21 clean-child comparison, PL3-23 read-only transfer, PL3-25 expanded curriculum, PL3-26 masked imitation, PL3-28 abstaining residual, PL2-17 explicit unknown, D5-18 replacement lesson, CR29 versioned recovery. *Mechanism:* Learn a candidate cross-term from approved current fixtures; keeping main effects fixed in scratch state is one control, not a proof. Validate novel combinations with independent held-out outcomes and abstain outside the checked overlap. Use no outputs from the retired teacher as targets for the replacement. *New:* A current-source replacement can restore supported composition without assuming that matching old marginals implies the old interaction is still valid. *First test:* Compare no interaction, full allowed-only retraining and isolated replacement when identical marginal fixtures are compatible with two different joint rules.

**PSX6. Share what both forks can learn and restore only what current sources justify.** Parts: PL3-01 common update coordinates, PL3-02 split evidence rails, PL3-06 two-speed patches, PL3-08 directional exchange, PL3-10 conjugated heads, PL3-13 activation compensation, PL2-03 paired write, T5-01 matched items, PL3-03 escape slot, PL3-04 cross-fork compensation, PL3-05 curvature-aware flow, PL3-07 paired perturbations, PL3-11 capacity bargaining, PL3-12 one lesson two updates, PL2-14 next-step contrast, D4-24 future gradient, PL3-09 paired inverse, PL3-14 recoverable common residual, TT25 reversible commitment, TT34 joint sandbox, D3-22 joint repair witness, PL3-15 source-subset layers, PL3-16 missing-source gate, PL3-19 main-effect bypass, PL3-22 source-held-out checks, PL3-24 joint-target cancellation, PL3-27 optimizer-state separation, PL2-27 quarantine synergy, PL32 keep independent learning, PL3-17 allowed-only mixed contrast, PL3-18 replacement factor, PL3-20 approved synthetic pairing, PL3-21 clean-child comparison, PL3-23 read-only transfer, PL3-25 expanded curriculum, PL3-26 masked imitation, PL3-28 abstaining residual, PL2-17 explicit unknown, D5-18 replacement lesson, CR29 versioned recovery. *Mechanism:* Use matched evidence to fit coupled recipient-specific writes and right-size rollback checks. Verify each fork’s future update response and distinguish shared behavior from interaction terms tied to an expired source. Rebuild only supported interactions from freshly authorized material, preserving explicit unknowns. *New:* The same population can retain live disagreement through transfer and preserve legitimate independent behavior through retirement, without treating either present agreement or immediate removal as conclusive. *First test:* Cross harmless toy forks with contrasting decisions, later write interactions, synthetic sources and missing joint fixtures. Compare independent updates, unconditional coupling and verified scoped coupling with source-aware retirement.
