Focal source: `tree/never-ending-pretraining-4.md` (PR #393, NP4-#). Raw proposals for one continuously pretrained lineage in harmless synthetic streams.

**IFX1. Buy the observation that can change which dependence lesson is allowed.** Parts: NP4-01 swap packet, NP4-02 asynchronous joining, NP4-03 conflict witness, NP4-04 price paired observations, NP4-06 bounded moment sketch, NP4-11 multiresolution moments, NP3-02 fixed marginals, NP2-22 support sets, PL5-18 independent observation histories. *Mechanism:* Accept separate views for marginal training and consume joined evidence once for dependence. Maintain a feasible set of couplings given the credited observations and their uncertainty. Spend the next observation budget on a paired record when rival couplings disagree on the intended update, or on resolving a minimal incompatible subset. *New:* The best next label depends on which proposed learning action is underdetermined, not on the least confident marginal prediction. *First test:* In a synthetic stream with late partners, duplicated arrivals and sketch collisions, compare cheap labels, random pairing and targeted acquisition at equal observation cost.

**IFX2. Carry an association into a new regime without carrying the old sampler.** Parts: NP4-05 rank coupling, NP4-07 invariant-exposure export, NP4-08 admissible-step contract, NP4-09 randomized intake correction, NP4-10 permitted interventions, NP4-12 held-out residual products, NP07 byte-update conjugacy, PL5-10 held-out effects, TT28 frozen shadow. *Mechanism:* Estimate dependence on common support using held-out residuals and logged intake probabilities. Transport the association in rank coordinates only where it passes checks in the new regime. Export a fresh pairing of fixed marginal bags and compare the resulting lesson with a protected direct write. *New:* A reusable association must survive changes both to the sampler and to the recipient's marginals; conserving counts alone need not conserve the learning effect. *First test:* Use harmless synthetic paired streams with a real association and an intake-induced shortcut. Compare invariant-exposure lessons, direct writes and ordinary replay under a marginal and policy shift.

**IFX3. Keep the checkpoint state that makes future lessons work.** Parts: NP4-13 future response map, NP4-16 label credit routing, NP4-19 closure under future operations, NP4-20 substitute prerequisites, NP06 learning-equivalent deduplication, NP14 two-shock witness, PL03 successor stress test, D5-26 teaching after compression. *Mechanism:* Save a small observable response map and credit-routing rule, then compress only directions not needed by declared future operations. Teach with certified prerequisites rather than replaying a schedule. Compare two-lesson orders and longer continuations on copies that initially agree on all answers. *New:* A state is redundant only for an explicit future operation family, and individually successful lessons can still fail in combination. *First test:* In a single toy lineage, compare score-only compression with response-map compression on held-out delayed labels and paired lessons with substituted prerequisites.

**IFX4. Compose teaching artifacts without crediting the common ancestor twice.** Parts: NP4-14 coalition effect, NP4-15 evidence interpreter, NP4-21 sibling capsules, NP4-22 hypothesis class versus examples, NP3-22 revocable distillation residual, NP2-21 root deduplication, PL32 preserve independent support. *Mechanism:* Package a lesson with its precondition, evidence interpreter and ancestor. Compose sibling patches only where each is still valid, keeping the effect that needs both sources separate from the effects either source teaches. Allow an independent replacement of the hypothesis class and refit on retained evidence. *New:* An overlap in starting state is not new evidence, and a jointly taught rule is not attributable to either sibling on its own. *First test:* Create synthetic branches with one shared ancestor, one genuinely joint rule and separately supported facts. Compare naive merging, ancestry deduplication and checked composition before and after a source withdrawal.

**IFX5. Teach the successor what to verify and when to stop trusting the hint.** Parts: NP4-17 independent student evidence, NP4-18 evidence acquisition policy, NP4-23 prompt versus answer teacher, NP4-24 support certificate, NP4-25 transferable likelihood ratio, NP4-26 observation channel, NP29 randomized reveals, OE25 independent verifier, D5-13 same-example control. *Mechanism:* Keep a teacher's proposal, answer mechanism, support limits and noise model separate. Let the successor reuse identifiable likelihood information with a local base rate, then buy a source-blind observation to test a discriminating implication before treating the hypothesis as independently supported. *New:* A successor can inherit how to evaluate evidence without inheriting a bias about which prompts matter or a claim it has never verified. *First test:* On synthetic streams with changed priors, informative prompt selection and noisy reports, compare answer distillation, transferring an acquisition policy and independently checked evidence transfer at equal label cost.

**IFX6. Give one pretraining lineage a checked interface for acquiring, composing and passing on lessons.** Parts: NP4-01 swap packet, NP4-02 asynchronous joining, NP4-03 conflict witness, NP4-04 price paired observations, NP4-06 bounded moment sketch, NP4-11 multiresolution moments, NP3-02 fixed marginals, NP2-22 support sets, PL5-18 independent observation histories, NP4-05 rank coupling, NP4-07 invariant-exposure export, NP4-08 admissible-step contract, NP4-09 randomized intake correction, NP4-10 permitted interventions, NP4-12 held-out residual products, NP07 byte-update conjugacy, PL5-10 held-out effects, TT28 frozen shadow, NP4-13 future response map, NP4-16 label credit routing, NP4-19 closure under future operations, NP4-20 substitute prerequisites, NP06 learning-equivalent deduplication, NP14 two-shock witness, PL03 successor stress test, D5-26 teaching after compression, NP4-14 coalition effect, NP4-15 evidence interpreter, NP4-21 sibling capsules, NP4-22 hypothesis class versus examples, NP3-22 revocable distillation residual, NP2-21 root deduplication, PL32 preserve independent support, NP4-17 independent student evidence, NP4-18 evidence acquisition policy, NP4-23 prompt versus answer teacher, NP4-24 support certificate, NP4-25 transferable likelihood ratio, NP4-26 observation channel, NP29 randomized reveals, OE25 independent verifier, D5-13 same-example control. *Mechanism:* Buy observations that can change a writable lesson, preserve protected marginals when transporting dependence, and retain the state needed for later teaching. Keep evidence interpretation and coalition effects explicit, then demand independent confirmation before giving a successor its own grounds. *New:* Training can pass forward what to ask, how to learn from the answer and which effects survive composition, instead of passing forward only a prediction. *First test:* Run one harmless synthetic lineage with delayed joins, intake shifts and sequenced lessons. Use shadow checkpoints only as controls and compare against ordinary training and answer-only distillation at equal evidence and compute.
