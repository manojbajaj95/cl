# P1: utility eviction and order sensitivity

[P1](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/7) proposes that successful memories reinforce their own retention, leading to persistent order sensitivity that a one-run estimate of reinforcement (κ) predicts and score decay (γ) limits. This is the first CPU stop gate from the [reviewer ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/6).

## Gate 1: does the budget-matched direct comparator degrade?

- Reuse all eight policies, transition code, probes, four orders and episode-indexed random draws from [PR #11](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/11) at `a6bbbf370483e9124f3bc7d3894cdbe3bae6b889`. Only extend the world from 200 to 1,000 episodes. Each 20-step cycle permutes the same episodes across orders, so horizons 20, 100, 200 and 1,000 are balanced and nested. New generation at the larger size does not recreate the original 200-episode worlds; comparisons between horizons here use prefixes of the same world.
- Freeze 48 seed blocks: the three PR #11 seeds (101, 202, 303) and 1000–1044. Within each seed share the world and exogenous draws across policies, orders and horizons. Use the same 128 fixed behavioral probes as PR #11.
- The primary comparator is RMS action-probability divergence at step 100 between the first two prescribed orders (200 simulator transitions; count the two probes separately). The target at each horizon is mean RMS divergence over all six pairs among the four orders. Compute signed Spearman ρ between seed means across the eight policies. Primary degradation is ρ(100→200) minus ρ(100→1,000), with the predictor held fixed. Also report the historical 20-step comparator against both targets, and the first-pair distances by policy at steps 20/200/1,000 to check the motivating crossover without conflating it with rank degradation.
- Use 10,000 paired percentile bootstrap draws over seed blocks, holding the policy panel fixed and reusing the sampled blocks across all policy/horizon cells. Also report a policy-and-seed resampling sensitivity check for rank statistics, not a second gate. Use average ranks for ties and omit undefined bootstrap draws, reporting the valid count.
- **Stop/KILL** unless the paired 95% CI for primary degradation lies above zero. A failure means the reviewer’s prerequisite was not established on this panel, not that success reinforcement never exists. Independently, if ρ(100→1,000) exceeds 0.8, the +0.20 margin is arithmetically unavailable on this panel; that bound does not transfer to a different policy panel.

## Conditional follow-on

Only after the first gate passes, specify the success-coupled retention/decay intervention and audit costs before running it. Use one 200-step logged trajectory to estimate κ (not the nominal setting) versus two 100-step trajectories for the comparator; match transitions and account for any extra probes. Sweep nominal reinforcement and γ per P1, including the success-blind and common-last-50-episodes controls. Report matched-bootstrap CIs for the κ-minus-comparator ρ margin, decay contrasts and growth beyond step 200. KILL if κ misses +0.20 ρ, if the proposed no-decay growth is absent, or if the decay/common-suffix tests do not support the mechanism. Endpoint divergence alone does not establish non-ergodicity.
