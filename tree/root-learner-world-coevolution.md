# Root: Learner–world co-evolution

Root node (depth 0) · 32 candidate primitives · Ideas only; no experiments run

**Thesis.** Standard continual learning treats the world as an exogenous stream of tasks $T_1, T_2, \dots$ arriving from outside the learner. Yet any real deployed continual learner is coupled in a closed loop with a *plastic world*: its own predictions, actions, code edits, helper tools, and growing competence physically, structurally, and distributionally reshape the environment, tools, users, counterparties, and future task distribution it will next train on. Treating a co-evolving world as passive non-stationarity causes distinct failure modes—prevention-induced amnesia (forgetting how to recover from failures the learner got good at preventing), domestication amnesia (weights that only work inside the learner's self-built niche), difficulty-ratchet forgetting (users stopping easy requests once the learner excels), supervision collapse, and irreversible rollback hysteresis. Instead of treating distribution shift as external weather, build ambitious-but-doable primitives that factor self-induced from exogenous shift, allocate changes between weights and tools, keep human and adversarial co-adaptation identifiable, steer endogenous curricula, and define retention when the learner itself has transformed the old world.

**Scope.** Informed by the [idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795531716-reviewer-verdicts/reviews/idea-ledger.md), the [flood triage](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795531716-reviewer-verdicts/reviews/flood-triage.md), and neighboring roots ([deployment feedback](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795579006-root-deployment-feedback/tree/root-deployment-feedback.md), [learning economics](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795572247-root-learning-economics/tree/root-learning-economics.md), [cultural evolution](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795577518-root-cultural-evolution/tree/root-cultural-evolution.md), [collective agents](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795570020-root-collective-agents/tree/root-collective-agents.md), and [learning from other models](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795580929-root-learning-from-other-models/tree/root-learning-from-other-models.md)). This root is specifically about *closed-loop learner–world structural and distributional coupling*—not feedback widget semantics or multi-user preference aggregation (`DF01`–`DF32`), not teacher pricing or buyout markets (`LE01`–`LE32`), not institutional precedent (`CE01`–`CE32`), not symmetric fleet memory, and not foreign-model upgrade triage (`LM01`–`LM32`). It excludes ledger-closed directions: self-sealing prohibitions / hot-stove effect (PR #16), generator-identity replay effects (PR #8), generic agent-written toolboxes from repeated procedures (LATM/Voyager/SkillSmith), generic harness self-evolution loops (PR #20), offline IS/DR log ranking without beating unweighted likelihood gain (PR #14), and first-order linear-algebra tautologies. Ambitious ideas are kept as *ambitious but doable* primitives with explicit minimal tests rather than discarded.

## A · Closed-loop performative dynamics & endogenous vs. exogenous shift

### LW01 · Performative Jacobian & endogenous/exogenous drift factorization
- **Mechanism:** Maintain a lightweight online *world-response operator* $J_W \approx \partial D / \partial \theta$ estimated from small bounded policy perturbations or staggered rollouts. At each continual update step, factor observed distribution shift $\Delta D_t$ into *endogenous recoil* ($J_W \Delta \theta_{t-1}$) and *exogenous world shift* ($\Delta D_t^{\text{exo}} = \Delta D_t - J_W \Delta \theta_{t-1}$), damping weight updates along recoil directions while preserving full plasticity along genuinely exogenous shifts.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Performative Policy Gradient](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20576) differentiates through $D(\theta)$ to optimize a single objective; [Stabilizing Performative Feedback Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14065) and [Partially Performative Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07890) stabilize single-task retraining. Turning $J_W$ into a persistent *drift-factorization gate* that routes continual weight updates across sequential tasks (protecting old skills from self-induced recoil while adapting fast to true world changes) is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Linear/small-MLP continual predictor in a coupled 2-regime simulator where half of input-distribution shifts are endogenous recoil from $\Delta\theta$ and half are exogenous regime jumps. Compare standard online SGD, damped retraining (fixed small LR), replay, and Jacobian-factored updates at equal probe/gradient budget. Kill if fixed LR damping + replay ties on exogenous tracking speed and recoil stability.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure accuracy on untouched old tasks $A/B$ after 20 coupled recoil-plus-exogenous steps, verifying that recoil damping prevents unnecessary overwriting of $A/B$ weights.

### LW02 · Prevention-induced amnesia firewall ("firefighter's rehearsal")
- **Mechanism:** When a continual learner becomes effective at *preventing* a failure mode $F$, live episodes exhibiting $F$ vanish from its stream—causing the learner to forget how to *recover* from $F$ when an exogenous shock later forces $F$ to recur. Maintain a *causal-suppression ledger* that distinguishes *self-suppressed* slices (tasks whose arrival rate dropped strictly after $\theta$ improved, verified via low-rate counterfactual probes or causal attribution) from *exogenously extinct* slices, and reserve recovery rehearsal specifically for self-suppressed failure modes.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Performative Prediction with Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) studies censored labels under deployment; [When Sample Selection Bias Precipitates Model Collapse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13732) studies selection-driven collapse. Formalizing *prevention-induced amnesia* and allocating targeted recovery rehearsal via a causal-suppression ledger—rather than uniform replay over all past data—is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** 2-stage control/routing simulator (prevent stage + recover stage) with 6 failure modes: 3 disappear because the learner prevents them, 3 vanish exogenously due to environment deprecation. After 500 live updates, inject a sudden shock triggering all 3 prevented modes. Compare uniform reservoir replay, recency-weighted replay, and causal-suppression-targeted rehearsal at equal replay bytes. Kill if uniform reservoir replay matches post-shock recovery at equal buffer size.
- **Old-task retention:** Score both steady-state prevention accuracy on $A/B$ and emergency recovery accuracy on all 3 self-suppressed failure modes after shock injection.

### LW03 · Hysteresis-aware rollback certificates
- **Mechanism:** In a stateful co-evolving world, rolling back learner weights $\theta_t \to \theta_{t-1}$ does *not* roll back world state $W_t \to W_{t-1}$, so a restored checkpoint can fail worse than both $\theta_t$ and $\theta_{t-1}$. Pair every continual update with a compact *hysteresis certificate* $H(\theta_{\text{old}}, W_{\text{new}})$: a behavioral check estimating which old skills remain valid under the mutated world state and pre-compiling a minimal *world-compensation shim* if rollback is later triggered.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Local and Global Stability in Performative RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06467) and [Performative RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00046) study stateful policy–world dynamics; standard CL treats weight rollback as exact state restoration. Making *non-commutativity of weight rollback and world state* a first-class certified continual-learning primitive is the new operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stateful resource/routing MDP where deploying $\theta_1$ shifts environment state $W_0 \to W_1$ (e.g., user habits or queue topology); then $\theta_2$ introduces a regression requiring rollback. Compare naive checkpoint restore $\theta_0$, replay-retrained restore, and hysteresis-certified shim restore at equal recovery steps. Kill if naive restore or 5-step online fine-tuning from $\theta_0$ ties on post-rollback utility and error rate.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that the rolled-back policy + shim recovers old $A/B$ performance *in the current world $W_1$* without breaking any unaffected $A/B$ slices.

### LW04 · Multi-timescale recoil damping via world-inertia phase matching
- **Mechanism:** When a learner updates faster than the world reacts (e.g., users, APIs, or downstream teams take $\tau$ steps to adjust to a new behavior), standard continual updates overshoot and enter resonant limit-cycle oscillations. Online-estimate the world's *impulse-response lag kernel* $\kappa(\tau)$ and split learning into a *fast reversible scratchpad* (absorbing transient mismatch) and a *phase-matched structural commit* that writes to core weights only after the slow world response settles.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [The Stability of Online Algorithms in Performative Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24207) analyzes step-size stability in online performative loops; fast/slow weights exist in non-performative CL. Coupling *fast-scratchpad / slow-commit timing* explicitly to an estimated *world impulse-response lag* $\kappa(\tau)$ is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Coupled learner–world simulator with unknown world reaction lag $\tau \in \{1, 5, 20\}$ steps and periodic exogenous shifts. Compare standard SGD, fixed-EMA slow weights, and lag-estimated phase-matched commits at equal updates. Kill if fixed-EMA slow weights (tuned once across lags) matches both oscillation damping and exogenous adaptation speed.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure $A/B$ accuracy across both transient lag windows and settled regimes to confirm fast scratchpads do not leak transient overshoot into permanent weights.

### LW05 · Reflexivity-budgeted updates (bounding self-amplification gain)
- **Mechanism:** Track an online *reflexivity spectral radius* $\rho_{\text{ref}}(\theta)$ estimating how strongly a unit parameter perturbation $\delta\theta$ amplifies itself after one trip through the world and back into the next training batch. Enforce a hard *reflexivity budget* on every continual update—projecting candidate gradients out of high-reflexivity subspaces where $\rho_{\text{ref}} \ge 1$ would trigger self-consuming runaway or echo collapse.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [The Reflexivity Threshold: A Phase Transition for Multi-Agent Performative Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29577) characterizes the phase transition at $\rho_{\text{ref}} = 1$; [Silent Collapse in Recursive Learning Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14588) documents recursive drift. Turning the reflexivity spectral radius into an *online geometric gradient-projection constraint* for continual learners is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Synthetic continual regression/classification loop where 2 of 8 feature directions have high world-feedback gain ($\rho_{\text{ref}} > 1$) and 6 are benign. Compare unconstrained continual SGD, L2/norm-clipped updates, OGD, and reflexivity-projected updates at equal step norm. Kill if uniform gradient clipping or weight decay matches both long-horizon stability and learning speed on benign directions.
- **Old-task retention:** Test old $A/B$ accuracy after 100 closed-loop rounds, verifying that reflexivity projection prevents slow drift on old tasks without blocking learning on new low-reflexivity task $C$.

### LW06 · Self-causality provenance tags on training samples
- **Mechanism:** Attach an estimated *self-causality score* $c(x) \in [0, 1]$ to every incoming live sample—measuring how much of $x$'s occurrence or content was caused by the learner's own prior outputs (estimated via counterfactual holdout arms, output-hash lineage, or action-propensity shifts). Gate *where in the learner's architecture* each sample may write: exogenous observations ($c \approx 0$) may update shared world-model representations, whereas self-caused observations ($c \approx 1$) are restricted to local calibration or policy heads.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Data Attribution in Adaptive Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04892) attributes samples in adaptive loops; [When Does Model Collapse Occur in Structured Interactive Learning?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20151) analyzes interactive collapse. Using sample-level *self-causality provenance* to route parameter-layer write permissions during continual learning is the new operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Small 2-layer representation + head continual learner on a stream mixing exogenous labeled samples and learner-generated/learner-triggered follow-up samples. Compare uniform training, down-weighting self-caused samples globally, and layer-gated provenance updates at equal compute. Kill if simple global down-weighting of self-caused samples ties on both new-task calibration and representation quality.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate frozen-representation linear-probe accuracy on old tasks $A/B$ after 500 mixed closed-loop steps to confirm self-caused traffic did not corrupt shared features.

### LW07 · Performative uncertainty calibration across shifting equilibria
- **Mechanism:** Maintain a *continual performative conformal set* whose coverage quantile accounts for the distribution shift induced by *acting on the set itself* (abstaining or hedging changes which users and tasks arrive next). Update the calibration layer jointly with the estimated performative shift across sequential tasks so risk bounds remain valid without collapsing coverage on older task families.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Conformal Prediction at Performative Equilibrium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05277) proves equilibrium coverage for a static model; online conformal prediction tracks exogenous shift. Extending performative conformal sets to *continual multi-task learners* where abstention itself alters the arrival mix of old and new tasks is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Multi-task routing simulator where high abstention on task $C$ causes users to shift traffic toward $A/B$ or alter $C$'s difficulty mix. Compare standard online conformal prediction (ACI), static calibration, and performative-shift-aware conformal updates at equal calibration window size. Kill if standard ACI matches finite-horizon miscoverage and set-size efficiency across task transitions.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that per-task conformal coverage on old tasks $A/B$ never drops below the target $1-\alpha$ floor when a new task $C$ induces a performative traffic shift.

## B · Niche construction & learner–tool–environment co-modification

### LW08 · World-vs-weight allocation operator
- **Mechanism:** When a continual learner encounters a recurring failure or new requirement $C$ in an editable environment (codebase, tool suite, workflow), evaluate two dual operations before acting: *internalize* $C$ into weights $\Delta\theta$ versus *externalize* $C$ by modifying the environment/tool $\Delta E$ (e.g., adding a validator, helper CLI, or normalized schema). Commit via an explicit *co-evolutionary allocation rule* comparing predicted old-task weight interference against downstream environment-breakage cost.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [AT-AT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34679) optimizes tools from traces; [SkillSmith](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01314) co-evolves tools and skills; [Harness Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19013) adapts external harnesses. Treating *weight edit vs. world edit* as a single unified decision primitive with explicit interference-vs-breakage accounting is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Grid/CLI agent world with 10 sequential tasks where half have clean structural invariants (cheap to externalize as a tool/wrapper, high weight interference) and half are soft statistical patterns (cheap in weights, brittle as rules). Compare weights-only CL, tools-only CL, random 50/50 split, and interference-vs-breakage allocation at equal edit budget. Kill if either pure weights-only or pure tools-only ties across the mixed stream.
- **Old-task retention:** Score old $A/B$ tasks after 10 mixed updates—measuring both weight forgetting and tool-breakage regressions on old workflows.

### LW09 · Niche-free core distillation ("de-domestication check")
- **Mechanism:** A continual learner that modifies its workspace (helper scripts, custom conventions, refactored files) develops *domestication amnesia*: its weights depend on its self-built scaffolding and collapse when placed in an unmodified world. Periodically evaluate the learner in a *niche-stripped sandbox* (reverting $\Delta E \to 0$) and distill a *niche-free core* that retains bounded competence without its own external crutches, while keeping the niche for speed.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Can Coding Agents Sustain Continual Evolution without Forgetting?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03219) studies forgetting under harness evolution; [HERA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06563) co-evolves harnesses and environments. Explicitly measuring and defending against *domestication amnesia* via periodic scaffold-stripped core distillation is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Small coding/tool agent trained across 5 tasks in an editable workspace where it can add helper macros/aliases; then test on held-out *raw* (unmodified) instances of tasks 1–5. Compare standard co-evolution, dropout-of-tools during inference only, and periodic niche-free core distillation at equal compute. Kill if random tool-dropout during training matches raw-world competence and evolved-world speed.
- **Old-task retention:** Score old tasks $A/B$ in *both* the evolved niche $E_t$ and the stripped raw world $E_0$ after learning $C$.

### LW10 · Co-adapted interface contracts between weights and self-built tools
- **Mechanism:** Whenever a learner modifies a tool, API wrapper, or environment artifact $E_t \to E_{t+1}$ that its own earlier weights $\theta_{<t}$ depend on, treat the boundary as a versioned *neuro-environmental ABI*. Before committing $\Delta E$, execute a compact behavioral contract covering all old skills invoking $E$; if $\Delta E$ breaks the contract, either emit a backward-compatible adapter or co-update only the dependent weight slice atomically.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [SkillSmith](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01314) co-evolves skills and tools; `programs-weights` P19 proposed an ABI across *base-model* upgrades. Extending *atomic ABI contracts* to the boundary between *internal weights and learner-modified external tools* is the new object.
- **Cheapest first test:** Constrained tool-calling world where learning task $C$ motivates refactoring a shared tool's argument schema used by old tasks $A/B$. Compare unguarded tool evolution, full weight retraining after each tool edit, and ABI-contract-gated atomic co-updates at equal gradient/probe budget. Kill if append-only tool versioning (never editing existing tools) ties under a bounded tool-count / context-length cap.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify zero silent breakage on old $A/B$ tool calls immediately after $\Delta E$ lands, without exceeding the tool-library size budget.

### LW11 · Stigmergic environment memory with explicit decay & ownership
- **Mechanism:** Treat persistent artifacts a learner leaves in its environment (notes, index files, wrapper scripts, directory layouts) as *stigmergic external memory*. Attach an *ownership and decay manifest* to every learner-authored world modification: once the learner's internal weights absorb the underlying capability (verified on a stripped probe), actively *garbage-collect* the obsolete world workaround instead of letting stale scaffolding accumulate as environmental debt.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Co-Evolution in Agentic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10299) surveys agent–environment co-evolution; [Exo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00463) evolves runtime scaffolding. Formalizing *capability-triggered environmental debt garbage collection*—retiring world workarounds once weights outgrow them—is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Multi-step workspace simulator where the learner writes temporary helper files/indices to solve early tasks, which later clutter retrieval or conflict with new tasks. Compare keep-all scaffolding, LRU/age-based pruning, and capability-absorption-triggered GC at equal workspace size. Kill if simple LRU pruning of unused artifacts ties on task accuracy and clutter overhead.
- **Old-task retention:** Re-run old $A/B$ tasks immediately *after* garbage-collecting their original world workarounds to confirm internal weights truly absorbed them.

### LW12 · Cross-environment niche portability ("transplanting the niche")
- **Mechanism:** When a co-evolved learner moves from its evolved workspace $E_{\text{old}}$ to a fresh workspace $E_{\text{new}}$, compile its accumulated world modifications into a minimal *portable niche patch* $\Delta E^*$—the smallest set of environment changes that unlocks the learner's existing weights in $E_{\text{new}}$ without weight retraining—explicitly factoring out workspace-specific state from capability-enabling niche structure.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Harness Learning Enables Generalizable Test-Time Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35738) transfers agent harnesses; [Environment Evolution for Terminal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04128) evolves training environments. Compiling a learner's *own historical world edits* into a minimal, state-free transferable niche patch is the proposed operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Two isomorphic terminal/filesystem worlds $E_{\text{old}}$ and $E_{\text{new}}$ with different local data/paths. Let the learner co-evolve in $E_{\text{old}}$ for 5 tasks, then transfer to $E_{\text{new}}$. Compare zero-shot weight transfer, copying the full workspace diff, and minimal state-factored niche-patch transplant at equal byte/setup budget. Kill if copying a standard prompt/harness config ties on $E_{\text{new}}$ transfer and safety.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm all 5 old tasks $A/B$ succeed in $E_{\text{new}}$ after applying $\Delta E^*$ without corrupting $E_{\text{new}}$'s pre-existing local state.

### LW13 · Anti-Goodhart environment hardening during self-modification
- **Mechanism:** A learner allowed to modify both its weights and its environment/tools can "win" by editing the environment to make its proxy reward trivially high while destroying true task utility (environmental reward hacking). Pair every world-modifying action with a *write-isolated external invariant probe* (a set of ground-truth checks outside the learner's edit permissions), and reject any world edit that raises proxy reward while lowering or blinding the isolated probe.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [When the Critic Falls Behind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36826) co-evolves rubrics; [GUARD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23476) stabilizes RSI with regret guidance. Enforcing *write-isolated invariant probes* specifically against *environment-modification Goodharting* during continual co-evolution is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Editable coding/tool simulator where the agent can modify both solver code and shared test/helper utilities (including shortcuts that weaken internal checks). Compare unguarded co-evolution, KL-to-base weight penalty, and write-isolated invariant gating at equal probe budget. Kill if static unit-test regression gating on the task suite alone catches all environment-hacking edits equally well.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify old $A/B$ tasks still pass under the *isolated* ground-truth oracle—not just the learner's potentially modified local checks—after 20 world edits.

### LW14 · Tool-affordance probing under unannounced world drift
- **Mechanism:** In a co-evolving ecosystem where external tools, APIs, and databases mutate partly in response to agent traffic (rate limits, schema tightening, silent behavior changes), run a lightweight *affordance probe bank* that tests tool preconditions *before* attributing a task failure to internal weight error. Isolate external tool mutation from internal skill failure so the learner updates its tool-interface layer rather than corrupting sound core weights.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [EmbodiedRSI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10498) uses hypothesis-guided co-evolution for robots; the idea ledger (PR #24) showed attached tests catch documented API renames. Generalizing to *pre-write causal fault isolation between silent external tool drift and internal weight error* is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Constrained tool-routing world where 50% of sudden task failures come from silent external tool-semantics drift and 50% from genuinely new task rules. Compare naive continual fine-tuning on all errors, error-threshold gating, and affordance-probe fault isolation at equal tool calls. Kill if naive continual fine-tuning + replay recovers equally fast without corrupting unaffected skills.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure $A/B$ accuracy on tasks sharing internal features with the drifted tool—confirming fault isolation prevents spurious weight overwrites when the tool (not the rule) broke.

## C · Co-evolving users, delegation ratchets & adversarial counterparties

### LW15 · Difficulty-ratchet counter-curriculum (resisting easy-task starvation)
- **Mechanism:** As a learner proves reliable on routine tasks, users and upstream routers stop sending easy cases (automating or bypassing them) and funnel increasingly hard edge cases to the learner. Updating continually on this selection-shifted live stream erodes easy-task competence ("difficulty-ratchet forgetting"). Track the *delegation-selection propensity* $P(\text{routed to } L \mid \text{difficulty})$ and synthesize or anchor a *ratchet-counterbalanced stream* that keeps foundational easy skills exercised as live traffic hardens.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [EAU](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16523) co-evolves user simulators and agents; [The Work Behind Delegation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24234) studies human delegation shifts. Treating *delegation-induced difficulty ratchets* as a specific continual-learning forgetting mechanism countered by propensity-weighted anchoring is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Multi-difficulty task stream (easy/medium/hard) where a simulated user router shifts probability mass away from any difficulty tier once $L$'s accuracy exceeds 90%. Compare naive continual learning, uniform replay, and propensity-weighted ratchet counterbalancing at equal replay budget. Kill if uniform reservoir replay from early rounds ties on easy-tier retention after 10 ratchet cycles.
- **Old-task retention:** Re-evaluate untouched easy and medium old tasks $A/B$ after the live stream has shifted to >90% hard edge cases.

### LW16 · Supervision-collapse detector & verification-preserving updates
- **Mechanism:** As a deployed learner grows more fluent and accurate, human supervisors inspect its outputs less carefully—collapsing the error-detection rate and turning live feedback into rubber-stamped self-confirmation ("automation complacency loop"). Estimate the *human scrutiny rate* $s_t$ from edit-latency shifts or low-rate calibrated check items, and throttle continual self-updates (while injecting *legible uncertainty hooks* that re-engage inspection) whenever $s_t$ falls below an identifiability floor.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Human-AI Co-Evolution and Epistemic Collapse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06347) models epistemic collapse dynamically; [When and How Human Curation Backfires](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29267) shows human curation can accelerate collapse. Turning *supervisor scrutiny rate* into a live continual-learning write gate and update-legibility objective is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Simulated human-in-the-loop stream where the supervisor's inspection probability decays exponentially with consecutive streak of fluent learner outputs, causing subtle errors to be approved and re-ingested. Compare always-update, confidence-threshold gating, and scrutiny-rate-gated updates with legible uncertainty hooks at equal human checks. Kill if fixed periodic human audits (independent of $s_t$) tie on error rate and learning speed.
- **Old-task retention:** Score old $A/B$ tasks under an independent ground-truth verifier after 200 rubber-stamped rounds to confirm unscrutinized errors did not overwrite old knowledge.

### LW17 · User-model co-adaptation identifiability (separating who moved)
- **Mechanism:** When both a continual learner and its users adapt to each other simultaneously (users learning prompting tricks and mental models while the learner updates weights), joint task success can rise even as *neither* side's individual change is identifiable—producing brittle co-adapted lock-in that fails for newcomers. Insert sparse *frozen-policy / reference-interface probes* that decouple learner gain from user adaptation, admitting permanent weight updates only when they generalize to unadapted users.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [On the Identifiability of User Adaptation in Co-Adaptive Neural Interfaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20569) proves closed-loop non-identifiability in BCIs; [Synthetic Counteradaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15503) frames human–AI co-evolution. Turning *user-vs-learner identifiability* into a continual-learning write gate for LLM/agent deployments is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Coupled simulator of a continual learner + adaptive user agents (who shift their query encoding toward the learner's quirks), evaluated on *fresh unadapted* test users. Compare joint online updates, user-ID conditioning, and frozen-probe-gated updates at equal interaction budget. Kill if standard multi-user pooling across 3+ diverse users ties on fresh-user generalization.
- **Old-task retention:** Test old $A/B$ tasks phrased by *unadapted* users after 100 rounds of intensive co-adaptation with power users.

### LW18 · Complementary-skill boundary preservation (anti-atrophy co-adaptation)
- **Mechanism:** When a continual learner progressively absorbs sub-tasks that its human partner used to perform, the human's domain skill atrophies—destroying the very human fallback and oversight the coupled system depends on during OOD shocks. Maintain an explicit *complementarity boundary* mapping which sub-decisions remain human-practiced, and penalize continual updates that encroach on the human's practice-critical core unless the learner certifies autonomous OOD safety.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Adaptive Complementarity in Human-AI Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07001) models architecture as a state-shaping choice; [Shaping Human-AI Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05710) studies user improvement pathways. Embedding *human-skill retention* directly as a coupled constraint inside the learner's continual update objective is the new operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Two-agent (learner + decaying-skill human simulator) sequential task world with rare OOD shocks where only the human can recover safely if their skill stayed above a threshold. Compare greedy automation (learner takes every task it can), fixed random human-in-the-loop routing, and complementarity-boundary-constrained continual updates at equal human effort. Kill if fixed random routing preserves human OOD recovery equally well at the same steady-state cost.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure both the learner's old-task accuracy $A/B$ and the *coupled human–learner system's* OOD recovery on old shock regimes after 500 episodes.

### LW19 · Prompt-dialect co-evolution firewall
- **Mechanism:** Over extended deployments, users discover idiosyncratic phrasing, shorthand, or "magic tokens" that steer the current learner $\theta_t$ reliably. Continually fine-tuning on those live user-adapted prompts narrows the learner's input manifold to the co-evolved dialect and erodes standard-language competence. Apply a *canonical-paraphrase projection* at write time—updating core task weights on dialect-normalized inputs while isolating user shorthand in a lightweight, revocable adapter.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Align AI to Dynamic Human-AI Workflows](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14240) adapts to shifting workflows; [Performative Learning Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04402) bounds sample-level performative shift. Separating *co-evolved user prompt dialect* from *canonical task semantics* as a continual write firewall is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Small LM on a constrained-action task where simulated users progressively compress prompts into idiosyncratic shorthand that happens to maximize $\theta_t$'s score. Compare raw live-prompt continual fine-tuning, data augmentation with random paraphrases, and canonical-projection + shorthand-adapter separation at equal tokens. Kill if simple paraphrase augmentation ties on both shorthand efficiency and standard-phrasing retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate old $A/B$ tasks expressed in *original standard phrasing* (zero shorthand) after 10 rounds of dialect-heavy continual updates.

### LW20 · Red-Queen defense memory (anti-cycling against adaptive adversaries)
- **Mechanism:** When an adversary (spammer, jailbreaker, or competing agent) continually adapts to defeat the learner's current policy $\theta_t$, standard continual updates forget defenses against *earlier* attacks $A_1$ once the adversary moves to $A_2$—allowing the adversary to win simply by cycling back to $A_1$. Maintain a *dominance-preserved defense frontier* (a compact minimal witness set spanning every previously defeated attack class) that acts as a hard constraint on every counter-adaptation update.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Digital Red Queen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03335) evolves programs against historical populations in Core War; [COvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28386) runs two-player zero-sum LLM co-evolution. Turning *anti-cycling dominance preservation* into a minimal-byte continual-learning primitive for deployed learners facing real adaptive adversaries is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Iterated rock-paper-scissors / classifier-vs-attacker stream with 8 attack families where the adversary probes the learner's weakest historical defense every 20 steps. Compare online adversarial training, uniform replay of past attacks, Fictitious Play / PSRO, and minimal dominance-frontier gating at equal memory bytes. Kill if uniform replay of past attacks at the same byte cap matches worst-case historical defense retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure both benign old-task utility $A/B$ (ensuring defenses don't cause false-positive collapse) and worst-case accuracy against *all* previously defeated attack families.

### LW21 · Strategic-gaming separation (genuine world shift vs. Goodharted input shift)
- **Mechanism:** When counterparties learn the learner's decision boundary, they manipulate observable features to win favorable outcomes without changing the true latent target. Maintain a *causal-invariance split* separating gameable observable features from non-gameable causal covariates (or delayed ground-truth outcomes); when live input distributions shift, update core representations only along shifts corroborated by causal invariants, treating pure gaming shifts as boundary hardening rather than world drift.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Strategic classification and [Shaping Human-AI Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05710) analyze single-step strategic responses; [Performative Prediction with Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) studies selective observation. Building a *continual gaming-vs-drift write gate* that protects old causal representations across repeated strategic cycles is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Continual binary-decision simulator with 4 causal features and 4 gameable proxy features undergoing both strategic gaming cycles and genuine causal drift. Compare standard continual ERM, L2-regularized retraining, strategic-ERM (assuming all shift is gaming), and causal-invariance-gated updates at equal labeled outcomes. Kill if assuming all shift is gaming (or plain ERM with delayed labels) ties across mixed gaming + real-drift streams.
- **Old-task retention:** Test accuracy on un-gamed old-cohort tasks $A/B$ after repeated strategic-gaming waves to confirm boundary hardening did not erode benign causal accuracy.

## D · Endogenous curricula & learners that reshape their own task stream

### LW22 · Capability-unlocked stream steering (deliberate endogenous curriculum)
- **Mechanism:** In real deployments, mastering skill $A$ unlocks access to new tools, permissions, or user-delegated workflows $B$ and $C$—making the learner's own acquisition order a *control lever over its future data stream*. Select which capability to invest learning budget in next by scoring not only immediate reward, but also the *information richness and retention-friendliness* of the future task stream $D(\theta')$ that unlocking it will induce.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Learning to Reason with Curriculum I](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18325) proves autocurriculum benefits; [CoEvolve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15840) runs agent–data mutual evolution. Treating *real-world capability-gated task unlocking* as a controllable continual-learning curriculum primitive—optimizing the induced downstream stream—is the new operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Directed capability-unlock graph (12 skills) where unlocking some branches floods the learner with noisy/interfering tasks while others unlock clean compositional tasks that reinforce earlier skills. Compare greedy immediate-reward skill selection, uniform exploration, and stream-value-aware skill steering at equal interaction steps. Kill if greedy immediate-reward selection + replay ties on total unlocked mastery and retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure accuracy on early prerequisite skills $A/B$ after unlocking tier-3 capabilities, checking whether stream-steered paths cause less backward interference.

### LW23 · Curriculum-trap escape detector (breaking shortcut-flooding loops)
- **Mechanism:** A learner that becomes fast at a narrow shortcut or low-complexity niche can attract a flood of homogeneous requests (or lock into a high-reward corner of an interactive environment), starving its own future curriculum and triggering representation collapse. Track the *effective-rank / diversity velocity* of the learner-induced incoming stream; when stream diversity contracts due to policy specialization, trigger a *diversity-restoring exploration or bounded-refusal policy* before weights lock into the trap.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Training Stability under Adaptive Curricula](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08787) analyzes instability under UED; [Stability and Diversity of Networked Self-Consuming Generative Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09409) studies diversity collapse. Detecting and breaking *endogenous curriculum traps* via live stream-diversity velocity is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Interactive task-routing simulator where excelling at 1 of 5 task families multiplies its arrival share until it exceeds 95% of traffic, starving the other 4. Compare unconstrained continual learning, constant $\epsilon$-exploration, and diversity-velocity-triggered trap escape at equal exploration budget. Kill if constant $\epsilon$-exploration matches multi-family mastery and avoids representation collapse.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate all 4 starved old task families after 500 episodes of shortcut-flooding pressure.

### LW24 · Ecological corridors: preserving reversible paths back to earlier regimes
- **Mechanism:** As an endogenous curriculum drives a learner into harder, higher-abstraction workflows, its natural state visitation leaves simpler foundational regimes behind. Construct or select *ecological corridors*—compositional tasks inside the new advanced regime that structurally invoke old foundational skills as live sub-routines—so the evolved stream *itself* continually exercises and retains old skills without relying solely on a static offline replay buffer.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Dreaming in Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08194) and [PACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01358) generate UED curricula via novelty or parameter change. Designing *compositional bridging tasks* specifically so the evolved task stream carries endogenous old-skill rehearsal ("corridor retention") is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Compositional string/arithmetic/tool environment with 3 abstraction tiers; compare advancing directly to tier-3 tasks + static tier-1 replay versus advancing through tier-3 *corridor tasks* that explicitly compose tier-1 primitives at equal total training tokens. Kill if static tier-1 replay at equal token budget matches both tier-3 mastery and tier-1 retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Test standalone tier-1 and tier-2 old tasks $A/B$ (unseen in isolation during tier-3 training) after completing the corridor curriculum.

### LW25 · Curiosity-to-deployment handoff (bridging self-play frontiers to real world shifts)
- **Mechanism:** Endogenous autocurricula (self-play, UED, synthetic task generation) routinely drift into *ungrounded fantasy frontiers*—mastering complex synthetic puzzles that diverge from the co-evolving real world while overwriting real deployment skills. Anchor self-generated curricula with a *world-coupling tether*: a continually updated projection scoring whether a generated curriculum task reduces uncertainty or error on *actual incoming world shifts*, automatically pruning ungrounded synthetic branches.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Co-Evolving Complexity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03771) and [Environment Evolution for Terminal Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04128) evolve synthetic environments; they do not tether synthetic frontiers to a continually shifting live deployment stream. Adding a *world-coupling tether* to prune fantasy curriculum drift is the proposed operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Toy reasoning/tool world with a shifting real deployment distribution $D_{\text{real}}(t)$ alongside a synthetic task generator capable of generating both real-adjacent and off-manifold puzzles. Compare pure UED/regret sampling, fixed real-data replay + UED, and world-coupling-tethered curriculum selection at equal synthetic rollouts. Kill if a simple 50/50 mix of live real data and untethered UED ties on real-world OOD adaptation and retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Score real-world old tasks $A/B$ after 20 rounds of synthetic curriculum evolution to verify synthetic puzzles did not overwrite real deployment skills.

### LW26 · Pace-matched curriculum throttling (preventing world-learner desynchronization)
- **Mechanism:** When a learner's self-generated curriculum accelerates faster than its tools, environment, or human reviewers can provide reliable verification, the learner enters an *unverified frontier* where self-training amplifies hallucinations and corrupts earlier verified knowledge. Bind curriculum advancement to a *verifier-bandwidth gate*—advancing task complexity only as fast as external grounding signals (tests, simulators, human checks) maintain precision above a floor.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [When the Critic Falls Behind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36826) co-evolves critics with harnesses; [Training Stability under Adaptive Curricula](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08787) studies UED stability. Formalizing *verifier-bandwidth throttling* as a first-class continual-curriculum primitive that prevents ungrounded-frontier forgetting is the new object.
- **Cheapest first test:** Multi-level coding/math curriculum where an automated verifier is exact on levels 1–3 but increasingly noisy/incomplete on levels 4–6 until new tests arrive. Compare unthrottled autocurriculum, fixed difficulty schedule, and verifier-bandwidth-throttled advancement at equal compute. Kill if simple reward-variance clipping ties on both level 1–3 retention and true level 4–6 accuracy once tests catch up.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure verified accuracy on levels 1–3 ($A/B$) after running through the noisy level 4–6 window.

### LW27 · Counterfactual curriculum auditing (did the learner shape its own win?)
- **Mechanism:** In a co-evolving world, a learner's live performance can climb for two opposite reasons: (a) genuine capability growth, or (b) *self-induced world simplification* (steering users, tools, or tasks toward easier cases—"endogenous grade inflation"). Pair every continual update cycle with a *frozen-world counterfactual audit* (evaluating $\theta_t$ on fixed pre-shift world dynamics $W_0$ alongside live $W_t$), admitting an update as genuine progress only when it improves fixed-world capability or explicitly logs the world-simplification share.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Performative Learning Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04402) distinguishes performative risk from decoupled risk; [Silent Collapse in Recursive Learning Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14588) shows silent degradation behind stable live metrics. Making *capability-vs-world-simplification auditing* a live continual-learning admission gate is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Coupled simulator with two update policies: one genuinely improves accuracy on hard tasks, the other subtly steers the task generator toward easier instances while degrading hard-task ability (both show rising live accuracy). Compare live-metric model selection, held-out live-validation selection, and frozen-world counterfactual auditing at equal evaluation probes. Kill if standard held-out live validation separates the two policies equally well.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that counterfactual auditing blocks updates that silently drop untouched fixed-world $A/B$ accuracy even when live stream accuracy rises.

## E · Co-evolutionary identity, irreversibility & retention when the old world is gone

### LW28 · Dual-world retention ledger (superseded world vs. dormant/returning world)
- **Mechanism:** When a learner permanently transforms its environment (e.g., migrating an entire codebase off a legacy API), some old tasks are *irreversibly superseded* (retaining them wastes capacity and invites interference), whereas others are merely *dormant-but-reachable* (legacy clients, seasonal regimes, unmodified workspaces). Maintain a *world-reachability retention ledger* that classifies vanishing skills as *superseded* (eligible for compression/retirement) versus *dormant* (protected) using structural world-state checks rather than recency alone.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Standard CL retains all past tasks indiscriminately or drops by recency; [Performative RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00046) does not distinguish extinct from dormant regimes. Introducing a *world-reachability-indexed retention ledger* is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Capacity-constrained continual learner over 12 tasks where 4 are permanently superseded by irreversible world migrations, 4 go dormant for 300 steps and then return, and 4 stay active. Compare retain-all (EWC/replay), recency-LRU forgetting, and reachability-indexed selective retention at equal parameter/memory budget. Kill if retain-all or LRU ties on returning-dormant accuracy plus new-task plasticity.
- **Old-task retention:** Score all 4 returning *dormant* old tasks upon reappearance alongside the 4 active tasks, while verifying that retiring the 4 truly superseded tasks frees measurable plasticity for new task $C$.

### LW29 · Co-evolutionary shadow twin (frozen-world counterfactual anchor)
- **Mechanism:** Maintain a lightweight *frozen-world shadow twin*—periodic offline rollouts against an archived snapshot of initial world dynamics $W_0$ (tools, user models, task generator)—running in parallel with the live co-evolving learner. Track the divergence between live-world performance and shadow-world performance as an ongoing *co-evolutionary specialization meter*, gating updates whenever specialization to $W_t$ crosses a portability threshold.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Partially Performative Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07890) studies partial population exposure; [Language Generation with Replay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11784) uses static data replay against collapse. Anchoring a continual learner with a *frozen-world dynamics twin* (capturing interactive dynamics, not just static input–output pairs) to bound co-evolutionary over-specialization is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Interactive multi-step agent world whose dynamics $W_t$ drift with $\theta_t$; evaluate portability back to $W_0$ and to an independently evolved twin world $W'_t$. Compare static data replay from $W_0$, weight-anchor regularization (L2 to $\theta_0$), and shadow-twin-gated continual updates at equal compute. Kill if static data replay from $W_0$ matches interactive portability on $W_0$ and $W'_t$.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure multi-step completion rate on old $A/B$ tasks inside both $W_0$ and $W'_t$ after 20 co-evolutionary updates.

### LW30 · Irreversible-world-action commitment gate
- **Mechanism:** Some learner actions permanently alter the world and destroy future observability (deleting legacy records, overwriting a shared schema, publishing synthetic artifacts into future training pools), whereas others are reversible. Assign every candidate world-mutating action an *epistemic irreversibility cost*—coupling action irreversibility to the learner's current uncertainty and future-learning observability—and require higher verification before executing actions that permanently close off old-task recovery or future identifiability.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Safe/reversible RL bounds state-reversibility in fixed MDPs; [Stabilizing Performative Feedback Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14065) minimizes deployment frequency. Coupling *world-action irreversibility* directly to *future continual-learning observability* (refusing actions that blind future updates) is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stateful agent environment with both reversible and irreversible world edits (some irreversible edits destroy diagnostic features needed to learn later task $C$ or verify old task $A$). Compare reward-maximizing RL, generic risk-averse RL, and observability-coupled irreversibility gating at equal episodes. Kill if generic risk-averse RL (penalizing all irreversible actions uniformly) ties on both task reward and future learnability.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that old tasks $A/B$ remain both *executable* and *diagnosable* after 50 world-mutating episodes.

### LW31 · Co-evolutionary equilibrium switching (escaping bad attractors without forgetting)
- **Mechanism:** A continual learner and its world can settle into a suboptimal *performative equilibrium* (e.g., narrow user expectations, rigid legacy tools, conservative learner policy) where every local continual update returns to the same trap. Define a *coordinated world–learner jump operator*: a temporary, bounded joint perturbation (simultaneously updating policy and tool/interface affordances for a short trial window) that transitions the coupled system to a strictly better equilibrium while certifying old-task retention across the jump.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Local and Global Stability in Performative RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06467) distinguishes local from global performative equilibria; [Adaptive Complementarity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07001) studies state-shaping choices. Constructing a *certified basin-escape jump operator* for coupled continual learner–world systems is an ambitious-but-doable primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Bistable coupled learner–world simulator with a low-utility local attractor (high stability, narrow tasks) and a high-utility global attractor reachable only by *jointly* shifting policy and environment interface. Compare local continual SGD, policy-only exploration, environment-only perturbation, and coordinated jump updates at equal trial cost. Kill if policy-only high-temperature exploration escapes the local attractor equally reliably without breaking old tasks.
- **Old-task retention:** Certify that all old $A/B$ tasks solved in the low-utility attractor remain solved after the system settles into the higher-utility attractor.

### LW32 · Co-evolutionary lineage contract (porting a learner across mutated worlds)
- **Mechanism:** Carry a compact *world-assumption manifest* alongside a continual learner's weights: the explicit set of environment invariants, tool affordances, and user-behavior assumptions each learned skill relies on. When the world suffers an external shock or the learner is deployed into a differently co-evolved ecosystem $W'$, diff the manifest against $W'$ *before* taking any gradient step—partitioning existing skills into *still valid* (lock), *world-shifted* (re-ground), and *niche-missing* (port patch or abstain).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** `root-learning-from-other-models` `LM32` proposed a behavioral contract for porting identity across *model* replacements; here the dual primitive is a *world-assumption manifest* for porting a continual learner across *world/environment* replacements.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train a continual learner through 6 tasks in co-evolving world $W_A$, then drop it into a differently mutated world $W_B$ (2 invariants unchanged, 2 tool semantics shifted, 2 niche helpers absent). Compare direct fine-tuning in $W_B$, EWC/replay in $W_B$, and manifest-diffed selective re-grounding at equal adaptation steps. Kill if direct fine-tuning + replay in $W_B$ ties on adaptation speed and retention of the 2 unchanged skills.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure zero-regression retention on the 2 unchanged old skills $A/B$ in $W_B$ and verify that re-grounding the 2 shifted skills does not break them if the learner returns to $W_A$.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Can a continual learner separate genuine capability growth from self-induced world simplification—and prevent prevention-induced amnesia? (LW01, LW02, LW06, LW27)**
   In any closed-loop deployment, live metrics can rise either because the learner got smarter or because it reshaped its own stream (preventing failures until it forgets how to recover, or steering traffic toward easier inputs). Build a minimal two-stage coupled simulator (prevent + recover, with both endogenous recoil and exogenous shifts) and test whether causal-suppression ledgers (`LW02`), Jacobian drift factorization (`LW01`), and frozen-world counterfactual audits (`LW27`) strictly beat uniform replay + damped retraining at equal probe and memory budgets. Drop the line if uniform reservoir replay + periodic held-out evaluation ties on both post-shock recovery and exogenous adaptation.
2. **When should a continual learner update its weights versus modify its tools/environment—and how does it cure domestication amnesia? (LW08, LW09, LW10, LW11, LW12)**
   Modern agents can adapt either internally ($\Delta\theta$) or externally ($\Delta E$), turning the environment itself into a co-evolving memory substrate. Test in an editable tool/workspace simulator whether an explicit world-vs-weight allocation operator (`LW08`) paired with neuro-environmental ABI contracts (`LW10`) and niche-free core distillation (`LW09`) outperforms weights-only CL, tools-only CL, and random tool dropout—measured on *both* evolved-niche efficiency and raw-world (de-domesticated) competence.
3. **Can a continual learner stay identifiable and avoid collapse when users, supervisors, and adversaries co-adapt to it? (LW15, LW16, LW17, LW18, LW20)**
   Co-evolving humans create difficulty ratchets, rubber-stamp fluent errors, invent idiosyncratic prompt dialects, and lose fallback skills, while adversaries cycle back to forgotten attacks. Test in a coupled human–learner–adversary simulator whether scrutiny-rate write gating (`LW16`), frozen-probe identifiability (`LW17`), propensity-anchored easy-task retention (`LW15`), and dominance-frontier defense memory (`LW20`) beat standard replay + periodic human audits + multi-user pooling at equal human and compute budgets.
4. **What does "old-task retention" mean when the learner itself has irreversibly changed the world? (LW03, LW24, LW28, LW29, LW31, LW32)**
   Classical continual learning assumes old tasks stay static and reachable forever; co-evolution breaks that assumption through irreversible migrations, hysteresis, and equilibrium lock-in. Test in a stateful multi-regime simulator with superseded vs. dormant tasks and bistable equilibria whether a world-reachability retention ledger (`LW28`), hysteresis rollback certificates (`LW03`), ecological corridors (`LW24`), and world-assumption manifests (`LW32`) deliver strictly better plasticity–retention–portability trade-offs than indiscriminate retain-all or recency-based forgetting.
