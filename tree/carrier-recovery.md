# Carrier recovery without rollback

**Status:** Ideas only; no experiments, probe code, or GPU work.

**Parent:** [Cross-pool round 45](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/b2e20f6efee3abcd9b46231b9503380bbe7664a3/ideas/flood/cross-pool-45.md) ([PR #36](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/36)). **Review seeds:** [Flood triage, row 287](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/77f6bb2847e1efc4b511f25baaa7c483b600851b/reviews/flood-triage.md#L287) scores WCS1 (today's repair prepares optimizer state the next carrier binds in time) and WCS7 (recover the carrier, not the checkpoint) at 3; neither merge has yet beaten its best parts. **Ledger read first:** [Idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/77f6bb2847e1efc4b511f25baaa7c483b600851b/reviews/idea-ledger.md). In particular, return-point adapters tied save/restore by construction, tagged checkpoints failed against stored deltas and hurt retention, and readiness needs to beat simple matched controls rather than just predict a planted threshold.

## What would be recovered?

The object is a **recoverable training transition**, not a former policy: a paid, forward-only route from the current learner to a host that runs the same frozen witness and preserves obligations acquired in the meantime. Two carriers may agree on outputs today and still disagree about whether the next unchanged edit can be repaired in time. Conversely, successful recovery need not match the ancestor's parameters, logits, or moments: it must preserve the *current* installed behavior at handoff and support the witness's declared future use. Running a different fitted repair to get an equivalent endpoint is a baseline, not carrier recovery.

The ingredients are not new. [Witness carriers (PR #177)][carriers] already propose self-seeding and source-free regeneration; [optimizer-slot splits (PR #178)][splits] give matched immediate updates with delayed divergence; [split-timed deadlines (PR #180)][deadlines] distinguish last safe launch from visible failure; [training readers (PR #161)][readers] already count training-state dependencies. [Optimizer-state transfer][transfer] and [delayed optimizer-state transport][transport] show why the next updates can depend on moments, while [Net2Net][net] preserves current function and [ReDo][redo] and [continual backprop][cbp] renew capacity. **Verdict:** a candidate primitive only if a lawful repair measurably pays for restoring its own callable transition under later obligations and deadlines, beyond the best stacked parts. Otherwise keep the better renewal or verifier as an improvement.

### Contract for the first tests

- **Freeze what runs and what arrives.** Choose the witness before freeze; publish its payload, allowable evidence, optimizer and seed protocol, fixed addressing/binding rule, and entry checks. Independently freeze later edit bytes, intended utility and release times before carrier selection; conceal the jobs until release. A type declared in advance may guide a choice, but adapting the payload or binding to a hidden answer is not allowed. No retired teacher or checkpoint access in the source-free arm; bill any permitted summaries.
- **Show spend, return, and reuse.** Establish that an unchanged remaining job previously served by the witness no longer checks or fits the budget after a genuine spend, not merely that a moment changed. Record the learner state immediately before the return. Check exact installed outputs on declared finite domains at handoff, then actually run the unchanged witness after the return on an independent delayed edit. Count restored service only when the edit has useful measured effect and both old and intervening tasks still pass. A snapshot may be used by the rollback comparator, not by the candidate. A donor must be repaid by its own declared maturity before calling repeated service a cycle; track displaced options as well as bytes.
- **Separate clocks and interventions.** Compute the feasible launch set under a frozen legal action/observation menu, accounting for bridge and checker latency. Record last safe launch, carrier entry, and witness completion separately. Do not assume a connected window or equate verifier refusal with dynamical failure. On matched-history twins, hold weights, clocks, remaining trainer state and the complete immediate update vector on a specified common probe fixed; later differences can still be probe-specific. Exchange candidate slots in both directions and include a same-cost sham in shadow. Such exchanges are causal diagnostics, not service; the candidate must reproduce the needed relation by a legal continuation. Where natural matched twins are unavailable, report that separately from injected controls.
- **Make retention a service floor.** In every card, **A** is ordinary original tasks (including rare held-out inputs); **C** is *every* still-installed skill acquired since the ancestor carrier, including the spend and each subsequent acquisition. Score each against its own acquisition-anchored floor at every deployed prefix and after an unassisted suffix. Neither mean A/C nor an end-of-run replay can hide a broken prefix. Report refusal separately from failure and from unknown; no-write and refuses-all cannot earn useful admissions. A known incompatible new job can be refused without silently superseding C.
- **Price the best alternative.** Start from matched utility/margins and equal legal information and moves. Compare WCS1 with #177 card 01, #178 card 03, their best deadline-aware stack, ReDo/continual backprop, and Adam reset at the window. Compare WCS7 with bare rollback (failure control), rollback plus replay of C, exact transplant with paid source storage, #177 card 20, Net2Net plus renewal, and sham exchanges. Give exact reach-avoid planning the identical move menu on small instances. Equalize or show a frontier over useful progress, peak and persistent bytes, samples, repair/verification work, completion time, and donor obligations; charge rejected attempts too. A full-state exact solver is a ceiling on that menu, not a speed baseline with free model access.

**Paper sanity checks, not results.** PR #178's scalar Adam construction has two moment tuples giving the same first update of `1/4`; a later pulse drives one, but not the other, past a gate. It demonstrates a diagnostic, not a legally reachable carrier. Separately, consider a finite gate with installed outputs `(A,C) = (b + g·h, q·g)`. Before the spend `(b,g,h,q)=(0,1,1,0)`; after it `(1,1,0,1)`. A return to `(0,1,1,1)` leaves both current outputs `(1,1)` unchanged, while ancestor rollback loses C. If the frozen edit removes `b`, keeping `g·h=1` now matters. This construction supplies a handoff target, not a training recipe: prescribing the return tuple or the gate by fiat does not demonstrate lawful reachability, slot mediation, or renewal.

## Idea cards

### 01. Repair into a timed binding basin — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Among truthful recipes with equivalent immediate repair, choose one whose delayed slot trajectory enters a predeclared binding set before the next edit's latest safe launch. Bind only when the receiving route and donor are ready. **Novelty / control.** [Delayed transport][transport] scores future response and [#177][carriers] seeds a carrier; the candidate is the conjunction of witness-specific bindability, paid donor transfer and a later blind deadline. Against their time-aware stack, compare equal-weights/equal-update twins and sham binding. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a gated two-head Adam learner, two truthful current-job batch orders and a fixed foreign edit revealed before, inside and after the feasible window. Require feedback from actual later service, not basin classification. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C through the waiting interval and again after the first delayed pulse.

### 02. Bank the second moment without banking a second model — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Select current-job examples with opposed gradient directions whose squared contributions leave a reusable variance profile while their signed response largely cancels on protected directions. Transfer only via the already-declared binding. **Novelty / control.** [Matched Specialization, Different Adaptation][matched] already changes second moments and later adaptation. The added claim is a transferable damping profile that keeps the *same* witness useful in a blind window; compare a tuned scalar schedule and per-coordinate variance reset. **Cheapest first test.** Two-feature Adam regression with naturally reached matched histories; do `m`, `v`, both and sham shadow swaps, then bill a legal example-only reproduction. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C for tasks sensitive to each feature separately; a preserved mean may hide cancellation errors.

### 03. Make a later skill the counterweight — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Grow a recipient by learning a new C skill so its shared feature absorbs the carrier's displaced computation; the return uses that new feature rather than resetting it. **Novelty / control.** [Net2Net][net] plus distillation can preserve current outputs; the candidate is that acquired C structure lowers the cost of restoring the same executable transition. Compare distill-then-renew and #177 regeneration. **Cheapest first test.** Two dependent heads share a targeted gate in a tiny ReLU network; ablate the C-induced feature with a utility-matched separately routed C fit before the same recovery attempt. **Old-task retention.** Check rare C inputs that genuinely use the old gate, not just the average on inputs served by the new path.

### 04. Recover the optimizer read, not the optimizer tuple — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** For each allowed witness suffix, target a bounded projection of the slot-dependent response rather than exact `(m,v,k)`, then synthesize a lawful route into that quotient. **Novelty / control.** [Training readers][readers] and #177 card 04 already identify relevant reads; the added test is prospective synthesis from the current state with no source, rather than read transport from the ancestor. Compare exact transplant and projection matching from a stored source summary at equal bytes. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate short two-dimensional SGD trajectories with momentum where distinct tuples are valid, plus one twin that aliases until step three. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/C on the alias-sensitive suffix; matching the final witness loss alone does not count.

### 05. Make the shared update carry two obligations — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Pick a legal repair update that simultaneously establishes a future witness read and reduces the steps needed to discharge an existing donor obligation, rather than scheduling preparation and repayment separately. **Novelty / control.** [Pacti][pacti] can compose guarantees; the candidate gain is a single trained transition that reduces total steps for both promises. Compare separate repair and repayment with a joint exact scheduler. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two donors, one fixed-width learner and three handoffs with a maturity constraint, including a case where two cheap local moves cannot both be taken. **Old-task retention.** Test the task displaced by the lent donor at each maturity checkpoint and after the final handoff.

### 06. Reconstruct a slot from learning already owed — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose the order of mandatory truthful C batches so recovery state emerges from useful work already on the schedule; no extra source samples or tailored recovery targets. **Novelty / control.** [MTT][mtt] matches training trajectories and [La-MAML][lamaml] uses future transfer; the claim is substitution of C training for a paid carrier bridge. Compare best look-ahead curriculum plus separate bridge at the same examples and latency. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate all orders of four mandatory batches between two spends; test the chosen order on independently drawn examples and a frozen release clock. **Old-task retention.** Measure all prior A/C and the newly owed skill before the second edit and after its witness runs.

### 07. Recover against a family of continuations, not one trace — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Fit a single return relation that covers two independent lawful schedule changes before binding; admit only jobs in the checked family. **Novelty / control.** [Reachability][reach] already reasons about disturbances and #178 bounds slot trajectories. The candidate is a learned common return that makes future witness binding cheaper without editor-specific tuning. Compare robust full-state reaching and best per-schedule fitting, with both given the same menu. **Cheapest first test.** Change batch timing versus batch sign in a finite switched optimizer; add a third family that must be refused. **Old-task retention.** Require A/C floors on every admitted suffix, not just the branch on which the return relation was fit.

### 08. Maintain a return certificate while C grows — improvement
**Mechanism.** Revalidate a return proof against only obligations whose live training or output read intersects the proposed bridge; new obligations without verified disjointness must force rechecking. **Novelty / control.** [Proof-carrying code][pcc] and [training readers][readers] already support proof interfaces and dependency lists; this is an incremental checker specialized to a moving obligation set. Compare full checks and generic dependency invalidation. **Cheapest first test.** A finite graph where C alternates between head-only additions and a shared normalization dependency; measure exact rechecks saved. **Old-task retention.** Independently audit the first C that depends on normalization, including after a later optimizer update.

### 09. Turn a bad slot into a legal transient — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Accept a recovered carrier whose initial slot vector is wrong, provided the first lawful steps of the unchanged witness bring it into a safe response relation before the edit needs that response. **Novelty / control.** [Delayed transport][transport] already values multistep effects; the stronger claim is intentionally using the frozen witness's transient as part of recovery, instead of paying for preconditioning before entry. Compare exact preconditioning and reset-plus-warmup. **Cheapest first test.** Two-step momentum witness with an irrelevant first read and a critical second one, plus a negative control that moves the critical read to step one. **Old-task retention.** Measure A/C after the transient step, not just after the dynamics converge.

### 10. Return a lease to the learner, not a reserved neuron — improvement
**Mechanism.** Use a temporarily shared donor only for the checkable life of a carrier; when later training needs it again, witness execution and mandatory repayment must both fit. **Novelty / control.** [Deadline contracts][deadlines] and [reusable resource matching][matching] already give expiry and loan accounting; the improvement uses actual slot-conditioned service times. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate staggered and bursty three-job arrivals with one donor and variable bridge duration; compare a fixed reserve and a scheduler using exact measured eligibility. **Old-task retention.** Include C that recurs exactly at donor return and test it again after another borrow.

### 11. Learn a source-free inverse of the spend, only on the witness interface — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Compile one bounded recovery policy that undoes the spend's loss of witness executability but explicitly leaves later C acquisitions alone; allow different weights and moments. **Novelty / control.** [Machine unlearning via SISA][sisa] retrains affected shards from earlier states; the target here is a recoverable *transition*, not a counterfactual model. Compare rollback with replay, #177 regeneration and retrained shards at equal storage. **Cheapest first test.** Hold out spend orders in a two-module regressor with C using a shared coordinate; apply the fixed source-free inverse to both orders and run the same witness. **Old-task retention.** Check each intervening C independently after each inverse step, including a skill learned before a second spend.

### 12. Keep a tiny sufficient residue instead of a return point — improvement
**Mechanism.** Before retirement save a budgeted summary that predicts a class of legal bridges, not a tuple to inject; recovery still uses only live training and the public summary. **Novelty / control.** [MTT][mtt] and #177 read-scoped transfer already compress update information; the improvement is a smaller residue for repeated forward-only return. Compare equal-byte replay, low-rank per-version deltas and exact transplant. **Cheapest first test.** Sweep summary budgets in a small recurrent edit stream; include a collision with identical summary but incompatible bridge demand. **Old-task retention.** Check C after using a colliding summary; require refusal rather than selecting whichever branch preserves the average.

### 13. Synchronize the optimizer clocks without touching C — improvement
**Mechanism.** Bind only after lawful scheduling aligns response on required coordinates, using separate per-group clocks where the already-frozen implementation permits them; do not silently reset global bias correction. **Novelty / control.** [Optimizer resetting][reset] and [slot splits][splits] already expose hidden trainer counters. This is a targeted alignment check, not a new kind of state. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate Adam on two partitions with equal `(θ,m,v)` and differing counters, comparing aligned clocks, full reset and matched effective step schedules. **Old-task retention.** Track C on the nonbinding partition through the next ordinary update; a global clock change may first surface there.

### 14. Teach a return path through a different gauge — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use a declared function-preserving reparameterization to open a legal bridge that could not be reached in the original coordinates, then demonstrate same-witness service via the fixed binding. **Novelty / control.** [Net2Net][net] and the ledger's gauge preconditioning killed the raw rescaling claim. Compare optimizer-equivariant transport and matched effective learning rates; only a strict expansion of lawful recovery under both controls would count. **Cheapest first test.** A two-unit ReLU host with positive rescaling, momentum and an optimizer-equivariant null; test a differently scoped lawful move constraint. **Old-task retention.** Check exact current handoff outputs and old predictions after a common nonzero gradient, where gauge-induced LR changes would show.

### 15. Pass the carrier around a dependency cycle — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** During successive spends, recover the same witness through different donors in a ring, so each spend contributes to preparing the donor two jobs later rather than monopolizing one idle slot. **Novelty / control.** [Continual backprop][cbp] renews units and [self-replenishing capacity (PR #170)][renew] renews menus; the candidate is renewal of one identifiable transition with all C still in force. Compare their best stack and a fixed donor given identical width. **Cheapest first test.** Three donors, four blind spends, and one burst beyond the planned ring; include an alternate editor order never used to select the recipe. **Old-task retention.** Test A/C after the ring wraps, especially the first skill whose route is repurposed twice.

### 16. Make a return robust to being interrupted — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Teach a bridge whose deployed prefixes preserve A/C and can either finish recovery or return to productive current training if an unrelated job preempts it. Recovery may pause, but its latest safe launch must be recomputed. **Novelty / control.** [Reach-avoid planning][reach] already solves interruption as branching, and [deadline contracts][deadlines] price bridge completion. The candidate is one learned recoverable prefix reused across several unseen interruptions. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two preemption dates and a retry on a shared-slot host; compare atomic staging and same-menu contingent planning for storage and replay. **Old-task retention.** Audit preempted prefixes and the state after the unrelated acquisition, before any retry is allowed.

### 17. Learn a return relation that survives evidence quantization — improvement
**Mechanism.** Train towards an interior of a verified binding relation so low-bit receipts can check recovery without copying full slots; margin concerns witness execution, not current logits. **Novelty / control.** #177 card 09 already tolerates quantized transport and [proof-carrying code][pcc] validates small proof artifacts. The improvement is trading legal bridge work for fewer proof bytes. **Cheapest first test.** Finite fixed-point momentum with two quantizer grids and adversarial rounding near the entry boundary; compare high-precision receipts and exact per-entry checks. **Old-task retention.** Test A/C for every rounding extreme admitted by the verifier and under the next ordinary batch.

### 18. Repair the deadline by changing what a slot does later — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose a seed that keeps the response on the safe side for more of the same frozen release schedule, without changing edit bytes, its utility test or the verifier's definition of success. **Novelty / control.** [Slot splits][splits] and [split-timed deadlines][deadlines] locate windows. The proposed difference is a repair-produced cause of strictly larger lawful windows on blind editors, not a more optimistic forecast. Compare simple reset, slower dynamics matched for progress, and timed best-part stack. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate hard-gated, leaky, and reopening twins; measure actual feasible launch sets and bill bridge latency. **Old-task retention.** Check C after service at every claimed newly added launch, including the last one.

### 19. Let crossed demands expose a fake shared carrier — improvement (audit)
**Mechanism.** Recover for two held-out jobs separately, then cross their orders without refitting. A carrier promise must specify which ordered suffixes it serves; separate successful forks do not prove both jobs can be admitted on one live path. **Novelty / control.** [Pacti][pacti] and [witness carriers][carriers] already distinguish jointly feasible promises. The improvement is a cheap composition falsifier for hidden slot consumption. **Cheapest first test.** Two jobs that each spend the same momentum budget plus a disjoint-job null; compare endpoint and exact ordered checks. **Old-task retention.** The first repaired job becomes C for the second; test it even if both independent forks pass.

### 20. Derive an impossibility from the new skill, not from the ancestor — improvement
**Mechanism.** On a bounded action menu, find a minimal set of live C obligations whose simultaneous floors block all lawful returns; distinguish this from a missing probe or a weak proof. **Novelty / control.** [Reachability][reach] gives losing states and [Pacti][pacti] can diagnose incompatible contracts. The gain is a short independently checkable obstruction for refusal, not an impossibility theorem for learning. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a two-gate host with one genuinely conflicting C slice and a near-control with a dormant alternative; compare full-state and transcript-level solvers. **Old-task retention.** Retain all A/C under refusal; in the near-control, require restored witness service without relaxing the implicated C slice.

### 21. Earn future binding from a co-trained optimizer subspace — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** During witness selection maximize how many *legal* current-job batch sequences can finish in a common set of slot projections usable by several recipient bindings, then freeze one binding rule before new jobs arrive. **Novelty / control.** [GPM][gpm] projects against old gradients and [MTT][mtt] matches learned trajectories. The claim is a reusable set of terminal training transitions, not a larger null space or better one-step match. **Cheapest first test.** Small linear teachers sharing rank-one features; compare gradient projection, endpoint conditioning and best stacked repair before testing held-out recipients at fixed width. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C under each binding on protected and rare feature combinations, including after an interfering normal batch.

### 22. Have the first legal wake certify the second — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use facts already produced by running the recovered witness once to check the next binding, while testing separately whether that run actually prepared the required slots. No second teacher call and no credit for a cached check after its premises fail. **Novelty / control.** [Witness carriers][carriers] already self-seed, and [proof-carrying code][pcc] caches premises. The candidate is joint savings in legal reconstruction and independent checking, beyond their best stack. **Cheapest first test.** Three sequential blind edits with a stale-premise control: keep logs and immediate utility fixed while a lawful intervening batch changes a trailing read. Compare fresh checks, cached checks alone and self-seeding without receipts. **Old-task retention.** Audit A/C at both wakes and after the stale-premise run, which must either revalidate or refuse without disturbing installed skills.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Does today's lawful repair causally keep tomorrow's frozen edit repairable?** First demand equal immediate weights and update vectors and a sham binding; then require a source-free legal recipe to reproduce the diagnostic slot effect. A win means extra A/C-safe service or lower total cost versus #177 + #178 + #180 and timed reset on held-out streams, not just an oracle swap on a planted gate.
2. **What is the smallest recovery relation that preserves every intervening skill and really runs the same witness?** Seek divergent full states that pass the same exact bounded continuation checks and a near-miss that only differs on a late read. Compare source-free synthesis with rollback plus replay, Net2Net plus reset, and paid exact transplant; if the minimal artifact reconstructs the old learner, the claim has collapsed to checkpoint compression.
3. **Does the channel close at a real deadline or only at a checker or observation boundary?** Enumerate full-state, transcript-conditioned and certified launch sets; look for reopening, smooth loss of options, and examples where bridge duration, not a visible gate, makes the difference. If only proof acceptance moves, investigate cheaper verification rather than claiming restored plasticity.
4. **Can recovery pay its own donor and proof costs as commitments accumulate?** Run several blind admissions at fixed width with meaningful new C each round and no hidden donor bank. Compare net service, displaced options and outstanding debt at common retention floors. If gain dies under increasing C or full accounting, state the finite horizon instead of claiming a renewable primitive.
