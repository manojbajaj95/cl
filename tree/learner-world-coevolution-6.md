# Learner–world co-evolution (depth 5): strategic non-learning below guard cliffs & 1-step hibernating attractors

Child node (depth 5) of [PR #367](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/367) (`L5W10` + `L5W03`) · 26 bold candidate primitives · Ideas only; no experiments run

**Parent triage.** In `reviews/flood-triage.md` (row 508, PR #367 / `tree/learner-world-coevolution-5.md`), two score-3 primitives were kept:
1. **`L5W10` — stop learning just below the point where guards disappear (strategic non-learning):** estimate the prevention rate $p_{\text{crit}}$ at which downstream users and tools dismantle their checks, and cap the learner's competence on that failure just below it, so the world keeps its redundancy and the learner keeps its plasticity—making *deliberate non-learning* a first-class continual-learning decision.
2. **`L5W03` — keep a dormant skill one gradient step away while a guard covers it (hibernating attractors):** while the external guard covers $F$, add a cheap meta-learning regularizer during task $C$ that keeps weights within one gradient step of an $F$-safe point instead of freezing them, so $F$ can be re-entered in one step when the guard drops.

**Why this node pushes for bold primitives, not variants.** Both kept ideas open genuinely new territory outside standard continual learning:
- **`L5W10` turns *withholding competence* into an active design surface:** not just picking a scalar cap, but *sculpting which inputs fail* (zero-harm vigilance triggers), *band-pass vigilance* between complacency and alert fatigue, *unlearning-as-brake* against accidental positive transfer, *sacrificial canary failures* protecting shared guards, and *information-theoretic competence ceilings*.
- **`L5W03` turns *dormant wakeability* into a geometric primitive:** *zero-collateral orthogonal wake-ups*, *zero-label self-waking from a single live failure*, *representation-rotation-invariant hibernation*, *multi-skill orthogonal wake simplices*, and *first-order trust-region passive hibernation*.
- **Their coupling (`L5W10 × L5W03`) yields a striking synthesis (`L6W19` & `L6W25`):** deploy below $p_{\text{crit}}$ so the world keeps its external guards alive, while keeping **100% prevention 1 gradient step away** in a hibernating attractor as an instant safety net against exogenous guard outages—turning the co-evolving world itself into persistent external memory backed by 1-step emergency recall!

## Section A · Bold primitives for Strategic Non-Learning (`L6W01`–`L6W09`, deepening `L5W10`)

### L6W01 · Safe-failure sculpting (concentrating the $1 - p_F^*$ failure mass on zero-harm vigilance triggers)
- **Mechanism:** Capping prevention uniformly at $p_F^* < p_{\text{crit}}$ (`L5W10`) still leaks harmful failures on $(1 - p_F^*)$ of high-stakes inputs. Instead, *sculpt* the non-learned $(1 - p_F^*)$ mass: master 100% prevention on irreversible/high-severity $F$-inputs, while deliberately leaving *only* high-salience, low-harm, reversible $F$-inputs un-prevented so they reliably trip the external guard—keeping $g_t = 1$ at near-zero real-world harm!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Standard `L5W10` caps aggregate competence $p_F$; [Oversight Has a Capacity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.08919) calibrates alert volume without shaping *which* model failures remain unlearned. Sculpting non-learning onto benign, high-salience triggers breaks the harm-vs-vigilance tradeoff.
- **Cheapest first test:** Two-severity failure world (90% benign reversible triggers, 10% catastrophic triggers; guard disappears if total trigger rate drops below 5%). Compare uniform `L5W10` (90% cap), always-admit, `L4W15` binary, `GSX6`, Learning-to-Defer, and sculpted `L6W01` (100% on catastrophic, 94% on benign). Kill if uniform `L5W10` or L2D achieves equal catastrophic harm + guard survival + $C$-plasticity.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify zero catastrophic escapes, alive external guard ($g_t \approx 1$), zero pinning debt, and intact $A/B$ + $C_1 \dots C_{10}$.

### L6W02 · Unlearning-as-brake (blocking accidental positive transfer from crossing $p_{\text{crit}}$)
- **Mechanism:** Even if the learner explicitly stops training on $F$ at $p_F^* < p_{\text{crit}}$, learning new tasks $C, D$ can *accidentally generalize* to $F$ via positive transfer, silently pushing $p_F$ above $p_{\text{crit}}$ and killing the external guard! After every unrelated update $C, D$, audit live $p_F$; if positive transfer pushes $p_F > p_{\text{crit}}$, apply a surgical *anti-generalization brake* (logit softening or targeted micro-unlearning strictly on benign $F$-triggers) to hold expressed $p_F \le p_{\text{crit}}$.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Continual learning universally celebrates positive forward transfer; in a performative world with guard cliffs, *uncontrolled positive transfer into a guard-covered failure is a stealth ossification bug*.
- **Cheapest first test:** World where tasks $C_1, C_2$ share latent structure with $F$ so training on $C_1, C_2$ pushes $p_F$ from $0.88 \to 0.99$ (crossing $p_{\text{crit}} = 0.95$) and deletes the guard. Compare passive `L5W10` (stop training on $F$), always-admit, `L4W15`, `GSX6`, and unlearning-brake `L6W02`. Kill if passive `L5W10` keeps the guard alive without an active brake.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify $C_1, C_2$ retain full accuracy despite the surgical brake on benign $F$-triggers, guard stays alive, and $A/B$ hold.

### L6W03 · Sharp-cliff vs. smooth-slope guard-curvature classifier (when strategic non-learning works vs. fails)
- **Mechanism:** Row 508's sharpest stress test: strategic non-learning (`L5W10`) shines when guard removal has a *sharp cliff* ($\partial^2 g_t / \partial p_F^2 \ll 0$, e.g. guard stays at $1.0$ until $p_F = 0.95$ then drops to $0$), but *fails* under a smooth linear slope ($g_t = 1 - p_F$, where capping at $0.90$ leaves $g_t = 0.10$ and leaks harm!). Estimate local curvature $\hat{\kappa}_g = \partial^2 g_t / \partial p_F^2$ online: deploy `L5W10` capping *only* when $\hat{\kappa}_g$ certifies a concave cliff, and switch automatically to binary `L4W15` / L2D under smooth or convex decay.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [The Reflexivity Threshold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29577) studies sharp phase transitions; `L6W03` makes *guard-response curvature identification* an online continual-learning gate so `L5W10` never misfires in gradual-decay worlds.
- **Cheapest first test:** Dual world (half sharp-cliff guards, half linear-slope guards) over 10 tasks. Compare always-cap `L5W10`, binary `L4W15`, L2D, always-admit, and curvature-gated `L6W03`. Kill if always-cap `L5W10` beats binary `L4W15` even in the smooth-slope world, or if `L6W03` cannot separate cliff from slope within 20 probes.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify strictly lower cumulative harm + higher $C$-plasticity across *both* cliff and slope worlds, with intact $A/B$.

### L6W04 · Internal-mastery / external-valve decoupling (zero-representation-tax strategic non-learning)
- **Mechanism:** Capping weights directly to keep $p_F \le p_{\text{crit}}$ can cripple shared representations needed by high-value tasks $C, D$. Decouple *latent competence* from *expressed competence*: let the shared representation learn freely (even if internal features solve $F$ cleanly), and enforce the `L5W10` cap exclusively via a *lightweight output-level competence valve* (calibrated deferral/abstention on $(1 - p_F^*)$ of benign $F$-inputs) so the world observes $p_F^{\text{obs}} < p_{\text{crit}}$ while internal transfer to $C, D$ stays 100% unconstrained!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Separates [Performative Learning Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04402)'s *deployed action* from internal *representational learning*, removing the negative-transfer side effect of weight-level non-learning.
- **Cheapest first test:** World where $F$'s latent features are strongly helpful for new tasks $C_1 \dots C_5$, with a guard cliff at $p_{\text{crit}} = 0.95$. Compare weight-capped `L5W10`, binary `L4W15`, always-admit, L2D, and output-valve `L6W04`. Kill if weight-capped `L5W10` achieves equal forward transfer to $C_1 \dots C_5$.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify full forward transfer to $C_1 \dots C_5$, external guard survival ($g_t = 1$), and intact $A/B$.

### L6W05 · Band-pass vigilance control (sandwiched between complacency cliff and alert-fatigue cliff)
- **Mechanism:** Real human guards (`2604.00904`, `2606.08919`) fail at *both* ends of the competence spectrum: too high ($p_F > p_{\text{crit}}$) causes **complacency collapse**, while too low ($p_F < p_{\text{fatigue}}$) causes **alert-fatigue collapse** (humans rubber-stamp because there are too many alerts!). Replace one-sided `L5W10` capping with a *two-sided band-pass competence controller* holding $p_F \in [p_{\text{fatigue}}, p_{\text{crit}}]$—learning *just enough* to prevent alert fatigue, and *stopping* just before complacency!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Fatigue-Aware Learning to Defer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00904) caps alert load to prevent fatigue; `L5W10` caps competence to prevent complacency. Unifying both reveals that *sustained human oversight exists only in a bounded band-pass window* of continual-learning competence.
- **Cheapest first test:** Non-monotonic human-guard simulator where $g_t = 1$ iff alert rate $\in [2\%, 15\%]$ and drops to $0$ outside that band. Compare one-sided `L5W10`, binary `L4W15` (defer-all triggers fatigue!), always-admit (triggers complacency!), `GSX6`, and band-pass `L6W05`. Kill if binary `L4W15` or one-sided `L5W10` keeps $g_t = 1$ across both failure arrival rates.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify $g_t \approx 1$ across shifting failure arrival rates, minimal cumulative harm, and intact $C$ + $A/B$.

### L6W06 · Sacrificial-canary non-learning (master 9 failures, cap 1 benign failure to feed a shared guard)
- **Mechanism:** When a single external guard $G$ (e.g. a CI security scanner or human code-review checklist) monitors 10 related failure classes $F_1 \dots F_{10}$, applying `L5W10` to all 10 wastes utility on 9 of them! Master the 9 highest-harm failures $F_1 \dots F_9$ to 100%, and apply strategic non-learning *exclusively* to the single cheapest, most benign failure $F_{10}$—using $F_{10}$ as a *sacrificial vigilance feeder* that keeps $G$ alive and funded for all 10!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Turns multi-failure guard coupling (`L5W17`) from a liability into a *leverage primitive*: one benign uncapped skill subsidizes full mastery of nine high-value skills without killing their shared backup guard.
- **Cheapest first test:** 10-failure world sharing 1 external guard $G$ (which retires if total trigger rate across all 10 falls below $3\%$). Compare uniform `L5W10` across all 10, always-admit, `L4W15` binary, L2D, and sacrificial-canary `L6W06`. Kill if uniform `L5W10` or always-admit achieves equal joint harm reduction + guard survival + $C$-plasticity.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify 100% prevention on $F_1 \dots F_9$, guard $G$ stays alive via $F_{10}$, and $C_1 \dots C_{10}$ + $A/B$ retain full plasticity.

### L6W07 · Randomized Stackelberg non-learning (defeating unobserved human shirking)
- **Mechanism:** Under [Overcoming the Incentive Collapse Paradox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27049), if a learner maintains a *predictable* competence cap $p_F^*$, strategic human checkers learn *when* to shirk (ignoring routine batches) and vigilance still collapses! Use a *mixed/randomized Stackelberg strategy* (`2511.08347`): inject unpredictable micro-bursts of benign uncapped $F$-triggers across batches so rational checkers cannot predict safe-to-shirk windows, sustaining $g_t = 1$ at a *higher average competence* $\bar{p}_F > p_{\text{crit}}^{\text{det}}$ than any deterministic cap!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Overcoming the Incentive Collapse Paradox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27049) analyzes contract-level incentives; `L6W07` brings *randomized Stackelberg non-learning* directly into continual-learning deployment policies.
- **Cheapest first test:** Strategic-checker simulator where the human chooses checking effort $e_t \in [0, 1]$ per batch to maximize reward minus checking cost. Compare deterministic `L5W10`, always-admit, L2D, and randomized-Stackelberg `L6W07`. Kill if deterministic `L5W10` sustains equal human checking effort $e_t$ at the same average prevention rate $\bar{p}_F$.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify strictly higher equilibrium checking effort $e_t$ and higher $\bar{p}_F$, with untouched $C$ + $A/B$ plasticity.

### L6W08 · Information-bottleneck non-learning (making over-competence mathematically impossible)
- **Mechanism:** Soft loss caps (`L5W10`) can be silently broken by representation drift, scaling, or downstream fine-tuning. For critical invariants where external human judgment is structurally required (e.g., clinical final sign-off, production schema migrations), enforce strategic non-learning *architecturally*: apply an explicit *information bottleneck* that strips the single decisive context bit needed to cross $p_{\text{crit}}$ on $F$, making over-competence (and thus guard extinction) *information-theoretically impossible* across arbitrary future updates!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Distinct from weight regularization or deferral heads: uses *architectural input-channel blinding* to guarantee eternal non-mastery of a designated guard-owned boundary.
- **Cheapest first test:** Long 50-task continual stream with aggressive fine-tuning and positive transfer where soft-capped `L5W10` eventually leaks past $p_{\text{crit}}$. Compare soft `L5W10`, `L6W02` unlearning-brake, L2D, and information-bottleneck `L6W08`. Kill if soft `L5W10` + `L6W02` maintains $p_F < p_{\text{crit}}$ over 50 tasks at lower compute and equal $C_1 \dots C_{50}$ accuracy.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify permanent $p_F \le p_{\text{crit}}$ across 50 tasks with zero brake compute, alive guard, and intact $A/B$.

### L6W09 · Online guard-cliff radar (tracking non-stationary $p_{\text{crit}}(t)$ before crossing it)
- **Mechanism:** Organizational guard-removal thresholds $p_{\text{crit}}(t)$ drift over time (rising after outages, falling during staffing cuts). Rather than assuming a static $p_{\text{crit}}$, run an asymmetric *cliff radar*: apply tiny, reversible upward/downward nudges on benign $F$-triggers and monitor early-warning guard latency/skip-rate shifts (`2609.38459`), dynamically sliding $p_F^*(t)$ downward whenever $p_{\text{crit}}(t)$ tightens—*before* the guard dies.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Static `L5W10` fails silently if $p_{\text{crit}}(t)$ shifts from $0.95 \to 0.85$ mid-deployment; online cliff tracking makes strategic non-learning adaptive to shifting human institutions.
- **Cheapest first test:** Non-stationary cliff world where $p_{\text{crit}}(t)$ drifts randomly between $0.80$ and $0.96$ over 100 steps. Compare static-cap `L5W10`, binary `L4W15`, always-admit, `GSX6`, and cliff-radar `L6W09`. Kill if static `L5W10` with a conservative fixed margin ($0.75$) achieves equal cumulative utility + guard survival.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify zero guard extinctions across cliff shifts, higher average competence than a conservative static floor, and intact $C$ + $A/B$.

## Section B · Bold primitives for 1-Step Hibernating Attractors (`L6W10`–`L6W18`, deepening `L5W03`)

### L6W10 · Zero-collateral orthogonal-wake attractors (1-step $F$ wake-up guaranteed not to hurt $C$)
- **Mechanism:** Basic `L5W03` guarantees that 1 gradient step $\theta' = \theta_t - \alpha \nabla \mathcal{L}_F(\theta_t)$ restores $F$, *without controlling what that step destroys on task $C$*! Add an explicit *wake-orthogonality meta-term* during $C$-training: $\min_\theta \mathcal{L}_C(\theta) + \beta_1 \mathcal{L}_F(\theta - \alpha g_F) + \beta_2 \mathcal{L}_C(\theta - \alpha g_F)$, shaping $\theta_t$ so that the 1-step $F$-wake vector $g_F$ lands inside $C$'s flat valley—delivering a **zero-collateral-damage 1-step wake-up** when the guard drops!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** OML (`1905.12588`), ANML (`2002.09571`), La-MAML (`2007.13904`), and OSAKA (`2003.05856`) optimize fast adaptation *to* a task without constraining the 1-step adaptation vector to preserve the *currently active* task $C$.
- **Cheapest first test:** `0→1→0` guard cycle where $C$ rotates the representation during $g_t=1$ and shares steep curvature with $F$. Compare `L5W03` (without $\beta_2$), `L4W03`/`WPX2`, online EWC, OML/La-MAML, equal-compute replay, and orthogonal-wake `L6W10`. Kill if basic `L5W03` or La-MAML already preserves $C$ after 1-step $F$ wake-up.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify 100% $F$-recovery in 1 step *and* $\le 1\%$ $C$-drop upon wake-up, with $A/B$ untouched.

### L6W11 · Zero-buffer single-failure self-waking attractors (waking from 1 live negative signal)
- **Mechanism:** Standard `L5W03` and MAML/OSAKA assume a clean stored labeled support batch for $F$ is on hand to compute the 1-step wake gradient. Push bolder: meta-train the hibernating attractor so that a *single unlabeled live failure event* (or binary negative reward when an unguarded $F$ first occurs) produces a wake gradient aligned with the full $F$-prevention boundary—achieving **zero-replay-buffer self-waking** on the very first recurrence!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Eliminates replay-buffer storage and trigger-witness staleness completely; the dormant attractor acts like an *immune memory cell* triggered by a single live antigen.
- **Cheapest first test:** `0→1→0` cycle where stored $F$-data is deleted during $g_t=1$ and the learner must wake $F$ from 1 live failure example when $g_t \to 0$. Compare `L5W03` (needs stored batch), `L4W03`/`WPX2`, online EWC, OML, and 1-shot self-waking `L6W11`. Kill if standard 1-step SGD on the 1 live failure from an OML-trained checkpoint matches full-boundary $F$-recovery.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify 1-event wake-up generalizes to the full $F$-distribution (not just the single trigger) while preserving $C$ and $A/B$.

### L6W12 · Co-rotated representation-invariant hibernation (surviving $90^\circ$ feature rotation)
- **Mechanism:** Row 508's hardest stress test: during $g_t=1$, task $C$ heavily rotates hidden representations $\phi_{\theta_t}(x)$, breaking fixed-encoder OML (`1905.12588`) and fixed-anchor EWC. Instead of freezing $\phi$ during $C$, maintain a *co-rotating linear probe* $w_F(t)$ tracked cheaply alongside $\phi_{\theta_t}$ and apply the `L5W03` 1-step hibernation regularizer *through the live rotated features* $\phi_{\theta_t}$—letting $C$ freely rotate representations while keeping $F$ linearly 1-step wakeable in the new basis!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** OML/ANML achieve retention by *constraining* representation drift; `L6W12` decouples representation freedom for $C$ from 1-step wakeability of $F$.
- **Cheapest first test:** `0→1→0` cycle explicitly forcing a $90^\circ$ orthogonal rotation of hidden layers during $C$-training. Compare OML/ANML, fixed/online EWC, `L4W03`/`WPX2`, plain `L5W03`, and co-rotated `L6W12`. Kill if plain `L5W03` or `WPX2` survives $90^\circ$ representation rotation equally well.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify unconstrained $C$-accuracy under full feature rotation, 1-step $F$-recovery on $g_t \to 0$, and intact $A/B$.

### L6W13 · Mutually orthogonal $K$-skill wake simplex (parallel 1-step wake-up without collision)
- **Mechanism:** Keeping 1 dormant skill 1 step away is local; keeping $K=15$ guard-covered skills simultaneously 1 step away risks catastrophic interference if 5 guards drop in the same shock (their 1-step wake gradients cancel or blow up!). Regularize the $K$ dormant wake gradients $\{g_{F_1}, \dots, g_{F_K}\}$ at $\theta_t$ toward *mutual orthogonality* ($\sum_{i \neq j} \langle \hat{g}_{F_i}, \hat{g}_{F_j} \rangle^2$), constructing a *wake simplex* where any subset of dormant skills can wake in 1 parallel gradient step!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Extends `L5W03` from single-skill hibernation to *multi-skill concurrent emergency wake-up*, solving a failure mode that MAML/OSAKA never face (simultaneous multi-task re-entry).
- **Cheapest first test:** 10-guard-covered world where 5 guards drop simultaneously after $C$-training. Compare independent `L5W03`, La-MAML, online EWC, `WPX2`, equal-compute replay, and wake-simplex `L6W13`. Kill if independent `L5W03` wakes all 5 skills simultaneously in 1 step with equal $F_1$–$F_5$ and $C$ retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify simultaneous 1-step recovery of all 5 skills, $\le 2\%$ $C$-drop, and intact $A/B$.

### L6W14 · Reversible wake-and-re-hibernate (elastic snap-back across repeated guard outages)
- **Mechanism:** When a guard drops temporarily (`1→0→1`), waking a `L5W03` attractor moves $\theta_t \to \theta_t + \Delta_F$. If the guard comes back online 10 steps later, leaving $\Delta_F$ baked into weights re-introduces plasticity loss for future tasks $D, E$. Track $\Delta_F$ as a *reversible elastic excursion*: once decoy heartbeats confirm $g_t(F) \to 1$, subtract $\Delta_F$ (re-hibernating $F$ back into a 1-step dormant attractor) while carrying forward any orthogonal $C/D$ progress made during the outage!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Turns `L5W03` into a *strictly reversible elastic cycle* across arbitrary numbers of `1→0→1→0` outages with zero cumulative ossification.
- **Cheapest first test:** 5-cycle alternating-guard stream (`1→0→1→0→...`) with 10 sequential tasks $C_1 \dots C_{10}$. Compare non-reversible `L5W03`, `L4W03`/`WPX2`, online EWC, and elastic-hibernation `L6W14`. Kill if non-reversible `L5W03` achieves equal task-10 plasticity across 5 outage cycles.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify 1-step $F$-prevention during every outage, zero cumulative plasticity drag across 5 cycles, and intact $A/B$.

### L6W15 · First-order trust-region passive hibernation (zero inner-loop compute)
- **Mechanism:** Explicit MAML-style inner loops (`L5W03`) require second-order gradients that scale poorly to large models and many dormant skills. Exploit [Trust Region Continual Learning as an Implicit Meta-Learner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02417): replace explicit 1-step unrolling with a *first-order Reptile/gradient-alignment proxy* (maximizing $\langle \nabla \mathcal{L}_C(\theta), \nabla \mathcal{L}_F(\theta) \rangle_{\text{clip}}$ or bounding local curvature along $g_F$) so $C$-updates passively preserve 1-step $F$-wakeability at $O(1)$ compute overhead!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Directly addresses the compute bottleneck of `L5W03` using the implicit meta-learning geometry of `2602.02417`.
- **Cheapest first test:** Equal-FLOPs comparison on a `0→1→0` representation-rotating cycle: second-order `L5W03`, first-order passive `L6W15`, La-MAML, online EWC, and equal-compute plain rehearsal. Kill if equal-FLOPs plain rehearsal beats first-order `L6W15` or if `L6W15` fails 1-step wake-up.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify 1-step $F$-recovery matching second-order `L5W03` at $\ge 3\times$ lower FLOPs, with intact $C$ and $A/B$.

### L6W16 · Deepest-separable-layer wake switch (surgical layer placement for hibernation)
- **Mechanism:** Applying `L5W03` across *all* network layers wastes capacity and slows $C$-learning in early feature extractors; applying it *only* to the final linear head fails if $C$ erases $F$-features in mid-layers. Dynamically locate the *deepest layer* $\ell^*(F)$ where $F$ remains 1-step separable and confine the hibernating attractor's wake switch to layers $\ge \ell^*(F)$ (only pulling $\ell^*(F)$ earlier if separability degrades)—maximizing backbone plasticity for $C$.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Combines layer-wise representation geometry with `L5W03`, avoiding both whole-network meta-regularization tax and brittle head-only adaptation.
- **Cheapest first test:** 6-layer network on a `0→1→0` cycle where $C$ alters layers 1–3 heavily. Compare whole-network `L5W03`, head-only `L5W03`, OML, `WPX2`, and deepest-layer `L6W16`. Kill if whole-network `L5W03` or head-only `L5W03` matches both $C$-speed and 1-step $F$-recovery.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify fast $C$-learning in early layers, 1-step $F$-wake-up at $\ell^*(F)$, and intact $A/B$.

### L6W17 · Distributionally-robust wake attractors (1-step wake-up under drifted live triggers)
- **Mechanism:** When an external guard finally drops after 50 steps, the live recurrence $x'_F$ rarely matches historical triggers $x_F$ identically. Meta-train the `L5W03` hibernating attractor against a *worst-case perturbation ball* $\max_{\|\delta\| \le \rho} \mathcal{L}_F(\theta - \alpha \nabla \mathcal{L}_F(\theta; x_F + \delta))$, certifying that even a *shifted or novel* recurrence of $F$ snaps the full prevention boundary back in 1 step!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Bridges `L5W03` (1-step wake-up) with real-world distribution drift across guard vacations (`L5W04`).

### L6W18 · Hibernation-load saturation meter (when to evict a dormant skill from 1-step to cold storage)

Use `L5W03` as a *reversible probe*: temporarily wake the 1-step attractor on a small canary slice to test higher competence $p_F$, measure guard-readiness elasticity (`2609.38459`), and *immediately re-hibernate* (`L6W14`) back below $p_{\text{crit}}$ the instant decay registers—measuring the cliff without ever falling off it!

### L6W22 · Two-gear turbulent-risk autonomy (strategic non-learning in calm regimes, 1-step wake in bursts)
