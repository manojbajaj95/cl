# Test-time weights II: can a live write earn the right to stay?

**Status:** Ideas only; no implementation or learner runs. **Parent:** [Test-time weights](root-test-time-weights.md) ([PR #311](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/311)). **Ledger read first:** [Idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795531716-reviewer-verdicts/reviews/idea-ledger.md). **Review seeds:** [Flood triage, rows 426–429](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795531716-reviewer-verdicts/reviews/flood-triage.md#L426-L429) on [PR #6](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/6); row 429 gives score 3 to [#311#07](root-test-time-weights.md#07-falsifiable-writes-p) (a forecast judged by natural future traffic) and [#311#01](root-test-time-weights.md#01-authorship-masked-consequence-learning-p) (the world supplies the targets; the model's own tokens get zero direct loss). Neighboring rows review other roots, not additional seeds.

## What might be new?

A tool may genuinely send back a result the agent made inevitable. A user may repeat words the model prompted them to say. A pending write can therefore predict the next observation correctly, improve its own loss, and still be bad at the task. Conversely, a good write can see no eligible evidence before expiry. The bet is that a write should declare **what would count against it, who can supply that evidence, and which part of the write it would invalidate**. Authorship controls target eligibility; it does not by itself establish independence or benefit.

## Literature check and controls

Targeted alphaXiv searches covered delayed verdicts on test-time edits, authorship and frozen generators, and action-dependent or missing outcomes. The comparisons below are based on retrieved abstracts and method summaries, not claims of exhaustive priority.

| Prior art checked | What it rules out claiming |
| --- | --- |
| [Self-Generated Feedback Destabilizes Test-Time Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076); [TTT-E2E](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23675) | Next-token updates during inference are established. Fixed Generation and Recorded Replay already diagnose the generation/update loop; Settlement checks the candidate on independent real text **before** retaining it. Post-activation adjudication on delayed task outcomes must beat Settlement, not just all-token TTT. |
| [ASCENT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05303); [Delayed Supervision for Test-Time Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32312) | ASCENT already adapts across a one-pass task stream from verifier-accepted experience; it distills a hindsight-conditioned frozen teacher into student reasoning/action predictions. Delayed Supervision already uses delayed grounded QA on disposable branches during post-training. Neither is a consequence-only live target with a delayed verdict on an exposed edit. |
| [WeightLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07319); [HypoLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23718); [Recursive Self-Editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29513) | Feedback-driven weight edits and validation before deployment already exist. WeightLoop fits and validates patches on experimental feedback, HypoLoop revises editing plans across scientific rounds, and Recursive Self-Editing merges patches that win on a hidden gate. |
| [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917); [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685); [CoTTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13591) | Typed, evidence-gated, exactly withdrawable edits under a scoped architecture are already known; so are cheap low-rank edit carriers and random restoration toward initial weights. None of those alone earns novelty. |
| [Performative Prediction with Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272); [Actions Have Consequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26908); [Off-policy evaluation for slate recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04812) | Deployed policies change outcomes and which labels become visible; randomized intervention can detect an outcome shift; logged-policy evaluation requires support. The open question is how these distinctions change the fate of individual writes under passive traffic. |
| [Temporal Algebra for Delayed Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21914); [Time-uniform CLT and asymptotic confidence sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06476) | Separating prediction and label-observation time and allowing repeated inspection of accumulating evidence are established. Asymptotic intervals are not exact small-sample guarantees. |

- **Host and evidence.** Start with scripted, recurring tool requests and tickets, a linear or tiny MLP predictor controlling the same agent that learns, two persistent drifts, transient anomalies, echoed replies and delayed or selectively missing outcomes. The method never sees simulator truth, counterfactual actions or a curated old suite. Log request, action, source, observable exposure, event and arrival time separately. Tests that replay one fixed log test prediction, not a policy effect. If a card uses already-existing policy variation, log its propensity; report overlap and effective sample size, and abstain without support. No card gets extra diagnostic calls or solicits confirmation.
- **Write contract.** At proposal time pin a write ID, its scope, a comparator, an outcome and eligible event rule, an expiry and an exposure budget. Freeze any data used to fit the write out of its verdict. Use distinct outcomes for forecast accuracy and task utility; inspect full-state service under provisional exposure and after any removal. No evidence before expiry means *unresolved*, not disconfirmed. Use frozen snapshots or exact small-model replay as references, but price their storage and compute and don't count exact restoration alone as success.
- **Matched controls.** Inherit [the parent controls](root-test-time-weights.md#contract-and-controls-every-card). For #07 compare keep-all, matched time-decay/expiry, interleaved self-holdout, and pre-commit Settlement at matched evidence and scoring budget; at least one stream must confirm a harmful write's forecast. Designed-feedback loops are separate arms: price any extra queries, rather than supplying free evidence. For #01 compare all-token TTT, Fixed Generation, world-model-only (frozen acting policy, adapted predictor), and count- and gradient-norm-matched random masks, including one where a user echoes the model. For combined cards use the strongest feasible stack of the parent mask, the parent verdict, and each card's simple local control. Match candidate attempts, tokens, update/scoring work, retained bytes and intervention budget; when exposure necessarily differs, report the latency–harm frontier rather than equating those risks.
- **Readout.** Per-stream paired intervals over at least ten seeds before taking a surprise seriously; disclose reliability of sparse verdicts, coverage, swaps per write, time-to-benefit and cumulative regret during exposure, not just the final checkpoint. Old QA, tool calls and old simulation episodes are evaluator-only: score them before, during and after adaptation/removal and include the worst old family. A tie at matched budget or a violation of the predeclared old-task tolerance kills the candidate claim; unsupported cases count toward lost coverage. **P** = candidate primitive because it offers a reusable operation; **I** = improvement/control, not a new primitive.

## A. Falsifiable writes when the world supplies the verdict (#311#07)

### 01. Make a write predict how it would lose (P)
- **Mechanism:** Lock a disagreement event on which the write and its frozen predecessor bet on opposite outcomes. Only those opportunities settle the write; an expiry with no such event abstains. The reusable object is a forecast contrast attached to a particular change, not a certificate that something went well.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** Settlement in [2610.05076](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076) scores independent text before commitment. Here already-active writes face pinned opponent forecasts on future ordinary tasks; compare against ordinary paired prequential loss on all events to isolate the benefit of the disagreement rule.
- **Cheapest first test:** Feed an eight-key linear tool predictor logs where almost all calls are easy and both states predict success, but rare natural calls separate a true drift from an anomaly. Kill if equally budgeted paired loss matches false survivors at the same time-to-benefit; include a stream with zero disagreements.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score old keys inside and outside the declared region during exposure, not just after a removal.

### 02. Separate a wrong forecast from an unobservable one (I)
- **Mechanism:** Track obligations in three bins: observed and wrong, observed and consistent, and not observable. Expire the last group to the base without marking it as failed evidence for related writes.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Temporal Algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21914) already separates label coverage and timing from predictive error; the improvement is applying that split to write decisions and subsequent proposals.
- **Cheapest first test:** Delay ordinary close notices in a 200-ticket simulation, with each arm seeing the same notices. Compare timeout-as-failure and delay-model abstention at matched provisional lifetimes; kill if a delay-only rule matches reuse and regret.
- **Old-task retention check:** Include previously learned slow-closing ticket families and score them after unrelated writes time out.

### 03. Give confirmed writes a utility veto (P)
- **Mechanism:** A write carries a prediction witness and a separate task-utility veto. A true receipt forecast cannot outvote a later reopened-ticket increase. The two-outcome verdict allows the predictor to survive while the policy-facing edit is removed, if the two parts were separable before exposure.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [WeightLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07319) and [HypoLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23718) validate candidate corrections; the proposed operation splits fate after a genuine predictive win harmful to the task, not merely adding another validation metric.
- **Cheapest first test:** In a scripted desk, short replies cause immediate acknowledgments and later reopens. Compare a single adapter with a two-metric keep/drop gate, two independently gated adapters, and the split contract at equal rank. Kill if the best independent gate retains as much predictive gain at matched reopen rate.
- **Old-task retention check:** Check normal old tickets where brevity still helps and old tickets whose resolution historically needs a clarification.

### 04. Put an observation witness beside the outcome witness (P)
- **Mechanism:** Before exposure, pin which ordinary opportunities would still be logged without the write. If activation stops such opportunities from yielding labels, revoke the *global* claim and, at most, retain the edit on the subset with observed overlap.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) already models policy-dependent observation. The candidate operation is shrinking a live edit's certified scope when exposure invalidates its evidence, while allowing a supported subregion to remain.
- **Cheapest first test:** A three-route tool stops returning receipt labels for one route when a patch reroutes difficult requests. Compare no correction, fixed scope, and a clipped propensity gate at matched overlap. Kill if scope contraction adds no supported gain, or if known propensities alone recover the same frontier.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score old requests to the bypassed route, including those now absent from the receipt log.

### 05. Keep verdicts invariant to a harmless action rename (P)
- **Mechanism:** Attach the forecast to an outcome with a pinned map across API action aliases, rather than to the output token that names the action. Count equivalence failures as out-of-scope evidence, not confirming votes.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [TTT-E2E](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23675) predicts tokens; [Actions Have Consequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26908) tests distributional differences under intervention. The proposed operation is reusing a write verdict across naturally recurring equivalent actions without intervention.
- **Cheapest first test:** Cycle two documented call aliases and a deceptively similar third with different semantics. Compare alias-normalized features alone and plus verdict transfer at equal calls. Kill if transfer endorses the bad alias, or if feature normalization alone settles just as quickly.
- **Old-task retention check:** Evaluate all old aliases separately, including held-out combinations of arguments that were not renamed.

### 06. Require a witness that discriminates between co-active writes (P)
- **Mechanism:** Two edits share positive outcomes but disagree on which ordinary future requests would fail. Score both against the same pending event and withhold individual promotion until there is discriminating evidence; settle the pair only as a pair otherwise.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [HypoLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23718) compares editing policies via validation; [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917) scopes gated patches. The proposed unit is an explicit ambiguity set of co-active edits that passive evidence cannot yet distinguish.
- **Cheapest first test:** On a two-feature MLP with two pending rank-one deltas, initially correlate the features, then naturally uncouple them. Compare separate validation, leave-one-out shadow scoring and the ambiguity set under the same scoring budget. Kill if cheaper leave-one-out always assigns credit correctly.
- **Old-task retention check:** Include old tasks engaging each edit alone, and one old task engaging both, before and after splitting the pair.

### 07. Forecast a boundary, not just an expiry date (P)
- **Mechanism:** Give a candidate a predicted first ordinary event type after which it expects to stop helping, such as the return of an old protocol marker. Score its before/after effects without letting the learner choose the boundary retroactively.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Temporal Algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21914) separates label delay from shift; [CoTTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13591) restores weights stochastically. The candidate object is a write with a prospective falsifiable end condition, not an age-based reset.
- **Cheapest first test:** Simulate recurring API mode changes with imperfect markers observable at request time. Compare decay, marker-only change detection plus a reset, and persistent separate mode adapters. Kill if the boundary contract does not save earlier useful exposure at equal old-mode errors.
- **Old-task retention check:** Test the old mode both when interleaved during the drift and on its return, with labels held out from the learner.

### 08. Settle records that arrive out of order without reviving a dead write (P)
- **Mechanism:** A verdict is stamped with the exposure interval it adjudicates. Late positive evidence can authorize a *new* candidate but cannot reanimate a removed delta whose dependencies or current scope have changed.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Temporal Algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21914) already distinguishes event and arrival time. The proposed operation attaches verdict authority to the executed edit version and makes explicit when evidence must be revalidated.
- **Cheapest first test:** Hand-enumerate a three-version linear host and two delivery orders with identical eventual labels. Compare event-time ordering plus fixed TTL, no resurrection, and version-aware reproposal under one update per round. Kill if reproposal cannot recover useful late evidence without reviving stale behavior.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score original skills and the currently accepted new skill after every delivery, especially the late positive one.

### 09. Isolate the side effect a withdrawn write already taught (P)
- **Mechanism:** If a pending write changed what later writers saw, drop or refit only descendants whose training provenance crosses that exposure. Keep unrelated writes; do not pretend subtracting the initial adapter erases its effects on observed data.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917) gives exact withdrawal in an isolated typed setting. [Recorded Replay](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076) separates reading damage from writing damage. The candidate operation is dependency-scoped withdrawal across sequential learning, not guaranteed counterfactual recovery.
- **Cheapest first test:** Simulate one bad redirect that changes a later label and a good update to a disjoint tool. Compare delta subtraction, full observed-log refit, and no-exposure oracle (ceiling only). Kill if provenance-based refit offers no utility/compute gain over the best feasible baseline or does not abstain where the needed label is missing.
- **Old-task retention check:** Check targeted old calls and unaffected old calls; require the unrelated new behavior to survive too.

### 10. Spend exposure only where a verdict can be earned (I)
- **Mechanism:** Route a provisional edit only on naturally arriving request types likely to resolve before expiry, under a fixed harm budget. Report outcomes on the full request stream, including the requests it never serves.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) already relates deployment to observation coverage. Routing by prospective adjudication is an improvement and not evidence from support that was never exposed.
- **Cheapest first test:** Use common fast receipts, rare slow receipts and an easy-only exploit. Compare random exposure and a frequency-based gate at equal number of exposed requests; kill if any apparent advantage disappears off the routed subset.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score rare old task families at their original frequencies and separately as a worst-family slice.

## B. Consequence learning when provenance is not independence (#311#01)

### 11. Define an evidence atom that survives forwarding (P)
- **Mechanism:** A structured external field carries the identity of its original observed event; relays and rephrasings do not create more gradient mass. An independently produced correction creates a new atom even when its text matches an old relay.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [ASCENT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05303) gates completed trajectories and distills a hindsight teacher, while [TTT-E2E](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23675) consumes a token sequence. The proposed operation is one consequence write per recoverable origin, not string deduplication.
- **Cheapest first test:** Simulate a desk that forwards one erroneous receipt through several channels and separately samples identical true receipts. Compare string dedup and count-matched random masks with origin collapse. Kill if the origin method offers no improvement on equal-byte receipts, or claims benefit when the origin is hidden and not identifiable.
- **Old-task retention check:** Keep old cases where high support really does consist of independent identical receipts, as well as those with only relays.

### 12. Quarantine an external echo without banning dialogue (P)
- **Mechanism:** Keep externally grounded outcomes as targets but deny credit to a user reply that only repeats a proposed value, unless a separate observed consequence grounds it. Self text can remain context; neither source strings nor echo similarity alone authorize a target.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Fixed Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076) breaks the changing-generator loop, not the user's propensity to echo the generator. [Actions Have Consequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26908) establishes dependence can be detected when interventions are possible; this mask instead uses available provenance or abstains.
- **Cheapest first test:** A toy support desk returns either a restatement or an independently grounded correction, at matched length and unigram frequency. Compare the authorship mask, lexical dedup, and their stack. Kill if observed provenance adds no gain or the method suppresses grounded paraphrases at the same rate as echoes.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score both old dialogue with legitimate confirmations and old tasks requiring independent corrections.

### 13. Stop copying a command back into itself (I)
- **Mechanism:** Parse a tool result into returned request fields and genuinely computed fields. Mask echoed arguments or deterministic renderings of them even when the tool authored the response. Keep the rest as consequence targets.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** The paired-update conflict in [2610.05076](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076) concerns generated-text updates; this is a structured refinement of #311#01, not a new learning operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Add long argument echoes to 3 drifting tools on an otherwise unchanged stream. Compare mask-by-source, fixed-position field masking and a count- and norm-matched random mask. Kill if removing copy fields adds no held-out consequence gain at equal useful signal.
- **Old-task retention check:** Include an old tool where stable echo syntax is useful context for interpreting the computed result; context remains visible in all relevant arms.

### 14. Train a consequence contrast only after a natural matched pair (P)
- **Mechanism:** When normal traffic already supplies two actions with overlap for the same context, remember a signed difference in outcomes rather than the responses themselves. Do not generate a probe or treat two arbitrarily matched users as counterfactuals.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Off-policy evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04812) already estimates policy value from supported logged actions; #311#02 actively randomizes. The candidate operation is a bounded delayed pair that serves as a weight-update target when an existing logging policy furnishes usable variation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Simulate an existing policy that naturally chooses one of two equivalent-cost delivery routes. Compare per-outcome gradients and standard propensity-weighted learning at matched labels. Kill if the pair adds no benefit at matched buffer size, or acts when one route has no support.
- **Old-task retention check:** Hold out old routes with narrow support and measure whether their action preferences change during pair accumulation.

### 15. Freeze the action representation, not the acting policy (P)
- **Mechanism:** When predicting external consequences, detach the earlier action encoder from the gradient and keep its parameters disjoint from the adapted predictor; allow the downstream prediction update to change later action choice. This blocks rewriting the internal action code to make its own consequences look easier without freezing behavior end to end.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Fixed Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076) freezes the generator; [ASCENT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05303) freezes the hindsight teacher. The proposed boundary stops a consequence loss from changing how its own earlier action is represented, while permitting the new forecast to inform the next decision.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train a two-head MLP that can collapse two action embeddings and lower average prediction loss on imbalanced traffic. Compare detached actions, frozen policy, separate predictor and unmasked gradients. Kill if a separate predictor plus planner matches gain and old-task behavior at equal width and work.
- **Old-task retention check:** Test old tasks using both actions, with special attention to the rare action whose embedding was easy to collapse.

### 16. Learn from external residuals that change the choice (P)
- **Mechanism:** For supported recurrent actions in one context, subtract an action-common baseline from the consequence target. Permit a policy-facing write only on the residual that changes the comparison; action-invariant residuals may still train a prediction-only head.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Off-policy evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04812) compares policies from logged outcomes. The new candidate is a gate deciding *where* a consequence gradient may land, not using a control variate as if it were new.
- **Cheapest first test:** Combine a shared change in reply formatting, which dominates token loss, with a small reversal of one action preference. Compare action centering plus a separate predictor, the uncentered mask and the residual write gate; kill if centering alone yields the same separation.
- **Old-task retention check:** Measure old policy choices under cosmetic response changes and score old consequence prediction calibration separately.

### 17. Preserve source disagreements until reality distinguishes them (P)
- **Mechanism:** When two external sources disagree, maintain two provenance-indexed candidate deltas but do not pool them into a consensus target. Only a later ordinary downstream outcome can choose between them; if no such outcome separates them, keep the claim scoped or unresolved.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917) already represents private scoped exceptions; [HypoLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23718) revises competing editing plans from feedback. Here naturally delayed consequences arbitrate conflicting target provenance before the sources may update shared weights.
- **Cheapest first test:** Two logs report opposite statuses; sometimes they agree on every downstream outcome despite differing in text. Compare per-source adapters, learned source reliability and vote weighting plus the parent verdict. Kill if the bundle cannot resolve the cases with discriminating downstream consequences without falsely resolving the others.
- **Old-task retention check:** Test old tasks where each source used to be authoritative and one where their reports are harmless aliases.

### 18. Let repeated silence train only when delivery was possible (I)
- **Mechanism:** An ordinary absence can contribute a survival/censoring loss only for an independently enumerated opportunity with a known observation window. Never train on absent opportunities, and distinguish on-time closure with no reopen from an unobservable reopen.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Temporal Algebra](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21914) already separates delay and observation coverage, and [Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) models missing-by-policy data. This is an improvement to #311#01 by using known at-risk time without treating missing data as a successful outcome.
- **Cheapest first test:** Compare explicit-event-only learning, naive no-news-is-good-news, and a simple censored outcome model with the same open-ticket windows; kill if there is no benefit over explicit events where logging availability is reliable.
- **Old-task retention check:** Include old sparse-report channels where no reopen was never a reliable success indicator.

## C. Let evidence control both the update and the verdict (#311#01 × #311#07)

### 19. Split the event into a target and an independent referee (P)
- **Mechanism:** An ordinary exchange may contain immediate tool output and later independent resolution. Let the first train, reserve the second solely to adjudicate; prohibit a forwarded copy or mechanically derived downstream field from serving both roles. Independence is about production, not different field names.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [ASCENT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05303) uses verification to select learning material; [Settlement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05076) tests before retaining. The candidate operation is an origin-disjoint separation *within* a natural causal episode, while the new write is active.
- **Cheapest first test:** In a toy desk, vary whether close notices independently check resolution or merely copy the receipt. Compare random holdouts, episode-disjoint holdouts and the strongest parent stack on identical notices. Kill if the separation either has no additional value or helps only with simulator-only origin tags unavailable to the learner.
- **Old-task retention check:** Include old tasks where receipts and closure normally agree and rare old ones where the early receipt is misleading.

### 20. Withdraw the gradient's authority when its source recants (P)
- **Mechanism:** Treat an ordinary correction with a stable record ID as withdrawing a premise, not as another disagreeing sample. Inspect the dependency from that premise to provisional writes; refit affected writes from still-valid targets or retire them, without withdrawing independent verdicts.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917) withdraws edits at the owner boundary, and [Error Correction in Trained TTT Layers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16268) studies how a weight update overwrites an association. The proposed operation revokes the authority of an external *target* across multiple writes, not owner-level removal or simple overwrite.
- **Cheapest first test:** A corrected receipt had supported two updates; only one also has independent support. Compare recency, inverse-gradient removal, per-ID latest-label replay and dependency refits. Kill if the last baseline alone gives the same surviving behavior per byte.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score old neighboring associations before and after the correction and after an unrelated update; require the independently supported new behavior to survive.

### 21. Admit the part of a write shared by rival explanations (P)
- **Mechanism:** Under plausible causes (real drift versus persistent echo), isolate the partial update that improves the supported observations under both explanations without exceeding a disagreement budget on ordinary unaffected traffic. Keep that part active while the disputed remainder awaits a natural separator; the held-out old-task suite still decides whether that was safe.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [HypoLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23718) selects weight-editing plans by validation and [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917) already scopes a typed edit. The candidate operation is a partial commit indexed by unresolved observational equivalence rather than a winner or a uniform downweighting.
- **Cheapest first test:** Enumerate a two-coordinate linear host with one invariant improvement and one echo-sensitive coordinate. Compare norm-matched shrinkage, independent coordinate gates and full wait. Kill if an independent gate can match early gain and bad-spillover rate, or if changing coordinates changes the proposed invariant unpredictably; score predictions in function space.
- **Old-task retention check:** Include old prompts spanning both coordinates; test counterfactual subsets only in the evaluator and score the actual partial model too.

### 22. Recognize when a write disarms its own test (P)
- **Mechanism:** A forecast declares a minimum rate of opportunities to *observe* a counterexample on a normally served slice. If the edit makes counterexamples unobservable, downgrade the claim instead of rewarding perfect predictive accuracy. Distinguish fewer failures at fixed opportunities from loss of opportunity.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) identifies selection effects and [Actions Have Consequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26908) uses interventions to detect outcome performativity. This is an edit-level constraint on continuing to make a *negative* evidence path observable without extra probes. Unlike card 04's scope shrinkage, loss of this path can invalidate the entire claim even when overall receipt coverage grows.
- **Cheapest first test:** A toy policy funnels ambiguous requests into a route with success-only notices while total log volume rises. Compare ordinary coverage counts, a fixed support gate and the counterexample-path rule using the same visible audit events. Kill if a support gate matches useful exposure at equal hidden failures, or if the negative path is not observable.
- **Old-task retention check:** Independently score old ambiguous requests that formerly reached a fallback path, counting wrong acceptance as failure even if no complaint is logged.

### 23. Give intersecting writes one joint chance to be wrong (P)
- **Mechanism:** Two individually confirmed deltas may form a different policy when combined. At composition, pin a forecast for the interaction on the intersection of their scopes; contrast the combined state with both singleton states and the base on the same supported opportunities. Strip joint authority rather than blaming a singleton by default.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) gives low-rank carriers, and [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917) gets locality by restricting edits to subjects. The candidate operation is a separate falsifiable joint commitment on an overlap whose behavior is not implied by its parts.
- **Cheapest first test:** Two rank-one writes push an old class past a nonlinear decision boundary only together. Compare the stacked singleton gates, a matched score-threshold check of the full composite and interaction-only adjudication. Kill if scoring the composite is enough to attribute the failure and preserve more benefit at equal scoring cost.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score old single-scope tasks and old crossover tasks under base, both singleton and full states, including after a joint refusal.

### 24. Reserve future referee power across competing edits (P)
- **Mechanism:** If two provisional writes need the same rare ordinary event for adjudication and alter how it is produced, admit only the write that leaves a usable referee path for the other. Opt out when no admissible order exists instead of counting one contaminated event twice.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Time-uniform confidence sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.06476) address repeated inspection, not mutually changing evidence; [HypoLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23718) revises a plan after experiments. The candidate operation treats the ability to adjudicate a competing live write as a resource an exposed edit can spend.
- **Cheapest first test:** Enumerate two writes and 20 ordinary event slots: one order preserves two distinguishable verdicts, the other erases one. Compare first-come, most-informative-first, static workload caps and a tiny exact scheduler on the same arrival law. Kill if a static ordering matches time-to-benefit and wrong survival when drift order is permuted.
- **Old-task retention check:** Test old tasks whose normal receipt would be lost, and unaffected old tasks, during both feasible orders and any abstention.

### 25. Let evidence cross versions only if the rival would still disagree (P)
- **Mechanism:** During consolidation, move a live verdict obligation to a successor only after checking that both old forecast and registered rival retain their contrast on the original admissible event family. Otherwise restart its evidence clock without importing votes. This is transfer before the verdict, not late resurrection after withdrawal (card 08).
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [WeightLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07319) accumulates feedback into persistent updates and [Owned Edits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15917) supplies a stable typed declaration. The candidate operation is preserving the meaning of a still-pending falsifier through a change of parameterization.
- **Cheapest first test:** In a low-rank linear predictor, alternate semantics-preserving changes of basis and merges that preserve aggregate loss but erase a rare forecast contrast. Compare unconditional carryover, always reset, and checking the candidate alone (without rival). Kill if checking the rival never saves a wrong carryover at matched work.
- **Old-task retention check:** Test old in-scope and unrelated inputs at each merge; require old behavior on inputs missed by the merge examples.

### 26. Prove when two causal stories cannot choose a write (I; audit)
- **Mechanism:** Construct an echo world and an independent-drift world with identical observable logs, including dates, source tags and all labels under the executed policy, but opposite value for keeping the write on an unserved route. Count refusal as the correct action for that unsupported claim, not as successful learning.
- **Novelty vs nearest prior art:** [Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) already exposes incomplete feedback and [Actions Have Consequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26908) derives intervention-based distinguishability limits. This is a negative control for the child node: any apparent passive disambiguation on identical logs is leakage.
- **Cheapest first test:** Write two finite transition tables, replay identical logs, then reveal one already-occurring supported observation that separates them in a second pair. Kill a claimed primitive if it distinguishes the indistinguishable pair, or if it never acts even after the separator is present.
- **Old-task retention check:** Include one untouched old route and one old route affected only under the unserved action. Score both in the evaluator so a method cannot call the latter safe on the basis of missing feedback.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Can natural traffic falsify an edit that correctly predicts its own outcomes?** Start with cards 01, 03 and 22 and the impossibility pair in 26. Require a gain over Settlement plus interleaved holdout and an independent utility gate, at equal observed evidence and exposure, with no excess old-task loss. The twin-world negative control precedes any learner run: if logs do not separate the two worlds, narrow the claim to the supported slice or abstain.
2. **Can a learner keep useful external gradients without letting the evidence launder its own content?** Cross cards 11–13 with 19 in a single desk stream where verified relays coexist with independent paraphrases. Compare the strongest stack of source masking, deduplication and episode holdout, plus Fixed Generation; reject if the gain is really fewer tokens or if the masked writer loses old dialogue skills.
3. **Can withdrawals remove damage that already shaped the next training example?** Combine 09 and 20 on bounded logs; compare exact observed-log refitting, subtraction and dependency-scoped removal. A win must preserve unrelated new and old behavior and explicitly refuse where the no-exposure label is unobserved.
4. **Can a pending verdict survive interaction or consolidation without relabeling the claim?** Pair 06, 23 and 25 in an exact two-edit MLP. Compare best singleton gates plus composite prequential scoring before attempting larger hosts. Stop if there is no affordable way to detect that a previously useful falsifier no longer distinguishes the live states.
