# Momentum-sealed park entry

**Status:** Ideas only; no learner training or GPU work. **Parent:** [Momentum-landed gates](momentum-landing.md) ([PR #242][parent]). **Review seed:** [Flood triage, row 363][triage] (score 3), [round 55 PKR2][pkr2]. **Read first:** [Idea ledger][ledger].

## Can a truthful batch earn a park that lasts through the build?

**Verdict: candidate primitive, not a win.** The parent lands a gate and budgets later excursions. PKR2 instead asks for one public entry word of truthful C batches whose image, under the native optimizer, lies inside the set of states safe for *every* admitted suffix and due read of a particular build. The park can be a shared observable relation (such as a shut nuisance path), not identical weights or moments. Inward first moment can make a small opener sufficient; Adam's second moment can absorb enough of that push to make an apparently quiet entry unsafe two batches later. The exact check below separates these cases, not the best lawful stack.

**Nearest prior art.** [Momentum landing][parent] exploits inherited velocity; [sealed gates][gates] certify the optimizer coast; [parked replacements][parks] already combine truthful entry, relational parks and completed-set envelopes; [recovery basins][basins] already track second-moment differences through live learning. [Momentum steering][steering] turns or refills the slot, not a finite-build contract. [Delayed optimizer-state transport][transport] and [AdamW minibatch input–output dynamics][adamio] account for delayed effects, and [history-aware barriers][barriers] and [robust MPC][mpc] are close to the reachability machinery. None of that machinery is novel here. The testable claim is that choosing entry for the *specific promised build*, using carry and preconditioning together, obtains more timely useful builds per unit of truthful teaching or live capacity than the strongest combination of those pieces. These are nearest documented comparators, not a priority claim.

## Contract and deciding controls

- Freeze the lawful starting histories and observations, truthful entry pool, exposures and multiplicities, due readers, A and installed-C floors, B milestones and frozen final witness, and the native optimizer (including clock, schedule, clipping, decay and precision). The incoming C opener must do independently checkable C work. Publish what “parked” serves and when it expires; do not count shutdown of an old service as retention.
- Given public observation o, let F(o) contain every lawful compatible state, with weights, first and second moments, clock and any other optimizer state paired as reached. Choose one public entry word E for all of F(o). No batch search or dispatch by hidden alias, slot reset, synthetic steering, checkpoint, extra carrier or fresh capacity in the proposed arm. Apply actual gradients at each reached state, not a single nominal gradient. Protect due reads during entry too.
- Work backwards from the frozen B witness and, if promised, a common executable clear on existing capacity. At each completed set U, retain only states where due reads hold and **every** legal next transition and any due reads inside it obey the contract and continue in the next set. Include announced arrival/clock state if U alone is insufficient. The entry is admitted only when its full image is contained in the resulting initial seal. Equivalently, propagate a sound forward envelope and prove each edge. No re-parking or changing the menu mid-build to hide a failing order. Omission or duplication requires its own declared grammar; proof timeout means unknown, not impossible.
- Compare each part alone and the best lawful stack: independently tuned momentum landing + Adam-aware gate seal + parked rebatching, with joint verification where the stack already supports it. Include the same word after a reset (keep the clock fixed to isolate slots), stronger SGD entry matched on C utility and billed examples, and accumulation or fixed order at the very same exposure and deadlines. Freezing plus best replay and a shadow copy are resource controls, not free capacity. Report timely B witness coverage at matched A/installed-C floors and incoming-C gain; bill every gradient, proof and preflight observation, live byte, stall and withdrawal. If the jointly verified stack ties, call this an improvement or retire it.

## Exact paper check: a quiet entry with a late Adam failure

Use scalar nuisance q, atomic Adam updates with no decay, learning rate 1/2, both betas 1/2 and epsilon 1/4 outside the square root. With t the number of completed updates, each truthful half-squared regression record on q with target y takes g=q−y, m′=(m+g)/2, v′=(v+g²)/2, and q′=q−m′/[2c(√(v′/c)+1/4)] with c=1−2^(−(t+1)). Start the public entry at q=−1/40, t=2, m=1/4. Two possible histories differ only in the resulting v: 1/4 or 11/4. To realize them from zero slots, use lawful private prefix gradients (1,0) or (3,−1), choosing each starting q by subtracting the corresponding cumulative Adam displacement from −1/40. Each prefix record can have the fixed label “preceding q minus the stated gradient” on its declared context (freeze the two starts and four labels before comparing); no slot is assigned. The history space is just these two declared alternatives, with neither label sequence nor starting q exposed at entry. The fixture's retention contract starts at entry; it makes no claim about serving readers during the private prefix.

The one-record entry E has label −1/2. P and Q have labels 3/4 and −1/10. The old A alarm must stay off (q≤0); a distinct installed-C read has lower floor q≥−2/5. The entry decreases the incoming-C squared error to −1/2 on both histories. To check real B deadlines in the toy, give P and Q each their own live scalar z initially zero, with zero moments. Their losses also include the corresponding half-square to label 1. Adam acts coordinatewise; each B piece must have z≥1/4 from its delivery onward (and both at completion). At its first update, with global clock 4 or 5, z steps by (1/2)r/(√r+1/4), r=(1/2)/(1−2^(−t))>1/2. This expression is increasing in r and already exceeds 1/4 at r=1/2. Any remaining zero-gradient tick increases z further. No extra coordinates are allocated by entry; the z coordinates are the live build carrier.

| Starting slots and build | q after E | q after first B batch | q after second B batch |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| m=1/4, v=1/4; P → Q | −0.294 | −0.127 | −0.014 |
| m=1/4, v=1/4; Q → P | −0.294 | −0.365 | −0.122 |
| m=1/4, v=11/4; P → Q | −0.158 | −0.051 | **+0.005** |
| m=1/4, v=11/4; Q → P | −0.158 | −0.229 | −0.050 |
| Both slots reset, same clock; P → Q | −0.248 | −0.049 | **+0.059** |

Entries are rounded for display; the signs and floors can be certified with rational interval arithmetic: at each square root bound each rational endpoint by consecutive multiples of 10⁻¹² whose squares enclose it, then evaluate the recurrence with outward intervals. The crucial late endpoints lie in (−14/1000,−13/1000) and (5/1000,6/1000), respectively. With no entry, even the low-v history crosses q=0 on P first (q>13/100). The low-v-only contract works through both orders; the mixed fiber must refuse. Checking entry or only the first two reads would miss the high-v failure. Reset is a control, not a repair for this word; this does not rule out a better entry under reset or SGD or prove anything past the final read.

## Idea cards

### A. Choose entry for the promised suffix

### 01. Pull back the build before choosing the opener — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Compute the all-order viable set for the frozen build and select a useful entry word whose joint image lands inside it, rather than maximizing immediate gate slack. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Robust MPC computes predecessors and [parked replacements][parks] certify a given entry; the claimed operation is an entry chosen for the suffix that uses live Adam carry without a control input beyond truthful records. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two openers and two suffixes where the deeper immediate landing loses on the later batch; compare the best independently tuned stack. **Old-task retention check.** Require A and installed C at every entry and build read, including the losing late branch.

### 02. Make one observation reserve exactly the build it supports — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use the declared public log to derive a conservative starting fiber; issue a certificate for the specific later build at that exposure. A different workload must get a new proof, not inherit the old promise. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** The parent's settlement window is a time bound; here changing a future *batch* at the same horizon can remove the right to park. **Cheapest first test.** Offer two two-batch builds with the same clocks, one benign and one with the fixture's P→Q wake; require a different admission without any hidden read. **Old-task retention check.** Replay both workloads offline and measure worst held-out old alarms, not just the certified one.

### 03. Enter through two disjoint safe pockets — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Let one word land different histories in disconnected parts of a relational park, then keep proving both branches with no branch-specific actions. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Disjunctive verification is standard; the hypothesis is that a useful common entry realizes both pockets and removes a build-order restriction that a single park point imposes. **Cheapest first test.** Two old reader paths with opposite feature polarity; compare an exact disjunctive proof, one convex envelope and one point park. **Old-task retention check.** Exercise both old paths at all due reads and directly test a lost reader at a point between the pockets.

### 04. Buy only the horizon the old reader needs — improvement
**Mechanism.** Negotiate a fixed expiry at the final due parked read, with a checked successor if one is promised; compare words for the finite suffix and indefinite coast separately. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Switched-system dwell time and the parent's settlement horizon already use time; this makes the service lifetime explicit, not an optimizer primitive. **Cheapest first test.** A slot that is safe through three named reads but would wake on a fourth; add the fourth and require refusal. **Old-task retention check.** Test the last covered reader and the first successor read, billing any gap.

### 05. Pair opposing truthful records to land at the right Adam scale — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Search fixed legal sequences of useful C records whose signs partly cancel in m but whose squares leave the v required for the advertised suffix. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Unlike [momentum steering][steering], the goal is not a turn or refill; it is to create a denominator that helps later service under the unchanged Adam recurrence. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate reversible pairs plus a same-example accumulated batch; seek a split word that alone keeps both build orders safe at matched C gain. **Old-task retention check.** Read A and installed C between the two entry records, before their net first moments cancel.

### 06. Keep one truthful entry for two incompatible clocks — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose a shared E that lands under both lawful clock histories, even if their bias correction and learning-rate schedule differ. Prove the closure of each history's own trajectory, not a fictitious common clock. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Optimizer-state transport follows clock effects; here they constrain whether one teaching word grants public access under a documented clock ambiguity. **Cheapest first test.** Two reachable pre-entry clocks near a schedule change; compare clock-collapsed, worst-clock and paired reachability. **Old-task retention check.** Probe before and after the schedule change on both histories, including A behaviors absent from the opener.

### 07. Let the first real B batch spend the last of the inward carry — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose E so a threatened suffix is safe only because a useful B update arrives within its already lawful timing window; certify earliest and latest arrivals, including actual idle transitions. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Sealed gates][gates] bound coast; the extra claim is a precommitted, independently useful build edge that bounds the coast without a maintenance-only update. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate immediate and one-tick-late arrivals under both a true no-op and a native zero-gradient tick; identify when a promise must narrow. **Old-task retention check.** Read the rare old path at the last idle tick and after B, not merely at entry.

### 08. Distinguish a needed entry from a concealed forced opener — improvement
**Mechanism.** When a certificate needs one particular B batch first, export the failing legal order. Do not relabel that batch as C entry unless it is independently useful there and already lawfully available and billed. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Parked replacements][parks] already expose forced precedence; this is a guard against mistaking precedence for earned parking. **Cheapest first test.** Remove the required B batch from pre-entry exposure and re-enumerate both orders. **Old-task retention check.** Execute the reported bad order offline and name the first violated A or installed-C read.

### B. Certify the actual optimizer state, not its shadow

### 09. Match the next update and still refuse the park — improvement
**Mechanism.** Generate lawful aliases with equal q and equal effective entry displacement but different m, v or clock; search for divergence at a later due read. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [AdamW input–output dynamics][adamio] predict such divergence; this is a decisive negative control for an entry receipt based only on the first update. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate tiny prefix pools for two aliases sharing a rounded public entry step and check the next two native transitions. **Old-task retention check.** Hold out the old reader whose threshold lies between the two late trajectories.

### 10. Publish a joint ratio bound that survives the next loss — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Carry an outward-rounded paired enclosure of m, v and q along the threatened face, splitting only on a bound for a future legal gradient; keep correlations lost by independently bounding numerator and denominator. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Relational abstract interpretation and [momentum steering][steering] already pair aliases; the candidate receipt summarizes the pairs specifically across changing Adam ratios and all due suffixes. **Cheapest first test.** Compare independent intervals, paired ratio enclosures and exact enumeration on a two-coordinate, three-batch host. **Old-task retention check.** Verify the old reader coupling both coordinates, counting any false acceptance as failure.

### 11. Find the history with the worst *intermediate* denominator — improvement
**Mechanism.** Optimize for minimum service margin over history and read jointly, not biggest initial v. A large denominator can slow inward entry yet restrain an outward build step. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Reachability already searches adversaries; this stress-checks initial-v ranking as a shortcut. **Cheapest first test.** Sweep reachable prefix pools for a middle-v or later rank reversal and compare with tests of only extreme v. **Old-task retention check.** Score the first and worst old read separately and require both to pass on the withheld history.

### 12. Estimate joint slots using useful work but never branch on their estimate — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use costly public evidence only to bound the *starting* fiber before commitment; for example an already-due old validation read after ordinary useful work may eliminate impossible histories. Then run one fixed E for all remaining histories. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Active diagnosis adapts to observations; here observations buy a tighter admission proof, never a different action for indistinguishable states or a probe inside the build. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust a two-prefix host with and without one allowed noisy read, using an explicit error enclosure. **Old-task retention check.** Count old-read latency and test A and installed C on every alias consistent with the noise, not just the most likely one.

### 13. Let one host move the scale away from a sensitive reader — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Train a shared low-dimensional representation offline on real tasks so the residual directions rescaled by v stay tangential to the old reader across the actual build; freeze it before entry. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Recovery basins][basins] target a reader-null subspace; here the proposed test is whether co-design with a finite entry and held-out suffix buys more service than basin training and park certification run independently. **Cheapest first test.** On a two-feature linear host compare diagonal and rotated features, then hold out one suffix that turns the reader normal. **Old-task retention check.** Probe old tasks relying on each feature and the combined direction after the hardest rotation.

### 14. Preserve a quiet read inside a non-atomic update — improvement
**Mechanism.** When serving can observe an update between native writes, lift the seal to those actual instruction boundaries. An atomically safe endpoint is not a certificate for that setting. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Transactional publication and robust invariants already protect observers; this pins the cost of claiming a native live park without an atomic snapshot. **Cheapest first test.** One two-coordinate update with safe endpoints but an unsafe intermediate mixed pair; compare an atomic reader and a real interleaving. **Old-task retention check.** Exercise the old reader at each allowed preemption, logging waits as well as errors.

### 15. Prove that apparent safety came from rounding — improvement
**Mechanism.** Bound the actual reducer precision and epsilon placement separately from ideal-real Adam; refuse borderline words when the implementation can exceed the reader floor. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Numerical verification is established; this tests whether a purported admission margin survives execution without silently changing the optimizer. **Cheapest first test.** Perturb the q threshold around the fixture's late read and compare rational bounds with float32 and a deliberately coarse format. **Old-task retention check.** Read A and installed C under each allowed precision and repeat the most sensitive B milestone read.

### C. Earn useful build freedom or report why not

### 16. Offer a late reader without renegotiating the entry — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Before commitment, include one bounded family of late due reads in the seal. Sell that family as part of admission only if the same E and every legal suffix remain sound. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Parked replacements][parks] check named readers; the candidate is a budgeted right to activate one of several legitimate old reads later without a second park or advance knowledge of which one. **Cheapest first test.** Two possible late old readers near opposite faces; compare precoverage against rescheduling each reader when announced. **Old-task retention check.** Fire each reader at its earliest and latest due point in both batch orders and measure offered service.

### 17. Co-design the entry and the fixed partition without peeking — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Search over the same public C opener pool and permitted partitions of already available B records, jointly minimizing teaching cost subject to all-order closure. Freeze both choices at publication. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Parked replacements][parks] already co-design park and partition; novelty would be a truthful opener selected for the carry–denominator interaction that cuts cost versus their approach with Adam-aware landing bolted on. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate three equal partitions of four B records and two C openers; require the winning pair to fail when either part is replaced by its independently optimal choice, then compare the best verified stack. **Old-task retention check.** Include both rare old readers and the delivered B piece after the first batch, not just B at completion.

### 18. Diagnose which promise consumed the seal — improvement
**Mechanism.** Separate a failing entry, a specific suffix wake, and a failed common clear; produce a reachable counterexample with public scope or mark the proof unknown. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Round 55 PKR9][pkr2] already asks for stage attribution; this makes a denominator-based witness an explicit output of the PKR2 verifier. **Cheapest first test.** Plant one failure at each stage and compare counterexamples from exact search with a verifier using a loose envelope. **Old-task retention check.** Replay each reachable witness through A, installed C and B deadlines so a clear failure is not misreported as an old-task wake.

### 19. Keep a shared proof of the old reader after clear — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** From all final build states, certify one public successor policy through a short real continuation; refuse if equality of the visible cleared value hides a later harmful optimizer step. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Parked replacements][parks] already demand common clear; the candidate is a successor right conditional on the exact native slot coupling the entry and build create, rather than a post-clear reset. **Cheapest first test.** Two orders with similar final outputs and different v; apply one distinguishing ordinary record immediately after clear and compare delay plus matched replay. **Old-task retention check.** Protect both pre-clear readers and the first real old reader after continuation, including one that straddles the handoff if promised.

### 20. Find the no-teaching region for a whole public fiber — improvement
**Mechanism.** Certify that every available useful C word within budget either misses the build seal or violates a reader floor before reaching it. Distinguish that bounded obstruction from failure to search. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** The parent has no-refill obstructions; here impossibility depends on the future offered suffix and native denominator, not merely shortage of inward gradient. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust words of length at most two for the mixed fixture, then add one verifiable C example or remove a specified offer and see whether the obstruction disappears. **Old-task retention check.** Name the old reader that blocks each otherwise useful word and distinguish it from insufficient C or B gain.

### 21. Test whether inherited carry and Adam scale are genuinely complementary — improvement
**Mechanism.** Offline only, cross preserving versus removing m with preserving versus equalizing v, while leaving the clock fixed. Contrast with legal naturally reachable alias pairs and the best matched lawful stack. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Optimizer-state ablations are standard; the interaction being tested is in *timely admitted builds*, not one-step loss. **Cheapest first test.** Cross the four slot controls on one two-coordinate host, keep the public menu fixed, and compare with stronger truthful SGD entry and accumulated gradients. **Old-task retention check.** Match worst old A and installed-C loss at each due read and charge refusals before comparing B coverage.

### 22. Bound a reusable entry interface for many successor builds — moonshot, primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Compile a public, bounded-size receipt of the set of suffix grammars for which the same truthful word and live states are certified; a receiver can choose a promised member without learning an alias or running a new optimizer-state audit. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Compositional verification already reuses contracts; the bet is a small, selective interface tied to executed learning and safe across unseen legal compositions, without a hidden full optimizer copy. **Cheapest first test.** Tabulate exact certificates for three short grammars with a common prefix and one late counterexample; this only tests compressibility. Unlock a real attempt only if one receipt stays sound on held-out compositions and beats a table of per-build proofs at matched bytes. **Old-task retention check.** Check the complete A/installed-C reader suite on each held-out composition, including the rare reader that triggers rejection.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Is there an all-in advantage over the strongest lawful stack, not just the same word after reset?** Start with 01, 17 and 21 on an exhaustible Adam host. Demand more on-time B builds or strictly lower teaching/peak live cost at matched incoming-C gain and A/installed-C retention, with at least one lawful denominator twin that would falsely pass a first-read seal. If the tuned stack or stronger SGD ties, retire the primitive claim.
2. **Can realistic public observations describe a fiber that is neither unsafe nor so wide that it refuses nearly everything?** Compare exact prefix enumeration, independent ranges and paired certificates (06, 10–12) on withheld histories. Report false acceptance, conservative refusal and proof cost separately. If only private moments or exhaustive checkpoints make admission work, the proposed interface fails.
3. **Is the useful contract a genuinely order-free finite park, or a disguised scheduled opener?** Vary due reads and lawful idle ticks without changing truthful exposure (07, 08, 14, 16). Require every advertised order to deliver B on time and preserve old service; report the first update forcing precedence if it cannot.
4. **Do the entry's savings survive clear and the first useful continuation?** Exercise 19 and 22 with an old read spanning the handoff, a distinguishing legal batch and a real next build on released capacity. If clear needs re-parking, reset, a hidden state receipt or delayed old service, count those costs before claiming reusable capacity.
