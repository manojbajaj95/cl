# Test-time continual learners (depth 4): Common-support concept mitosis & the Polanyi verbalization-stability boundary

Depth 4 child of [PR #377](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/377) (`tree/test-time-learners-4.md`, deepening `T4-04` and `T4-05`, and folding in cross-pool round 109 `TCX2` and `TCX3`, stacked on [PR #376](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/376), [PR #374](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/374), and [PR #373](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/373)) · 30 candidate primitives (`T5-01`–`T5-30`) · All tagged **Ambitious but doable (P)** · Ideas only; no experiments run

**Thesis.** PR #377 narrowed self-organizing tacit↔explicit continual learning to two complementary structural laws:
1. **(a) Common-support contradiction-driven concept mitosis (`T4-04`):** Split a tacit habit that draws contradictory exceptions into two context-scoped explicit rules—*strictly requiring the contradiction on commonly shown items*, not merely exposure/covariate differences across contexts. Without common-support verification, any continual learner splits spuriously whenever context $C_1$ and context $C_2$ happen to see different input slices.
2. **(b) Verbalization-stability / anti-crystallization immunity (`T4-05`):** Formalize Polanyi's Paradox (*"we know more than we can tell"*) as an online stability law—measure text-edit stability on first promotion, return lossy/oscillating directions (calibration, continuous thresholds, stylistic cadence) cleanly back to weights, and protect them from wasteful re-promotion while still letting them adapt non-verbally.

The unifying insight of this node is the **Polanyi Phase Diagram (`T5-22`)**: when a first promotion *oscillates* under text edits (fixing $x_1$ breaks $x_2$, fixing $x_2$ breaks $x_1$), there are **two fundamentally opposite root causes**—(a) a *hidden context bifurcation* on commonly shown items (solvable by **concept mitosis** into two crisp context-scoped rules) vs. (b) *irreducible tacitness* within a single context (unsolvable in prose—must **return to protected weights** with continuous non-verbal adaptation). The 30 mechanisms below dissect exposure-free common-support mitosis (Section A), recursive taxonomy dynamics vs. adapter routing (Section B), verbalization-stability laws and non-verbal weight adaptation (Section C), and the unified Polanyi Phase Diagram plus cross-pool round-109 `TCX2` (forecast-gated correction merging vs. keeping local or splitting by context) and `TCX3` (short interaction note in context retired on interaction absorption at matched context tokens) (Section D).

**Mandatory controls across every section:**
- **Mitosis cards (Sections A–B & D):** single promotion (one unsplit explicit rule), no promotion (keep tacit), routed per-context adapters (separate LoRA per context), and gated patches—scored on **common-item accuracy per context**, **false-split rate on exposure-only shifts**, **novel context-composition transfer**, and **old-task retention**.

## Section A — Common-support contradiction discovery & causal concept mitosis (mechanisms for (a))

### T5-01 · Matched-item counterfactual intersection for exposure-free mitosis — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Naive gradient-opposition splitters trigger false mitoses whenever context $C_1$ and context $C_2$ merely have different item distributions (exposure bias). Gate mitosis on an explicit **common-support intersection**: evaluate suspected opposing exception gradients *strictly* on matched items $x \in \mathcal{X}_{C_1} \cap \mathcal{X}_{C_2}$ (either naturally recurring items or content-identical/context-swapped counterfactual pairs $(x, C_1)$ vs. $(x, C_2)$), splitting the tacit habit into two context-scoped explicit rules *only* when the user's exception reverses sign on the *same* underlying item.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Beyond Preference Following](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39550) benchmarks profile-query conflict statically; enforcing a *common-support matched-item contradiction test* before splitting a tacit continual-learning habit into two explicit rules is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 tacit habits: 4 with true context-scoped contradictions on shared items, and 4 *exposure-shift decoys* where $C_1$ and $C_2$ have disjoint item sets with no true contradiction. Compare matched-item mitosis vs. naive gradient-opposition splitting, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches on true-split precision/recall and common-item accuracy. Kill if naive gradient-opposition splitting achieves $<5\%$ false splits on exposure-shift decoys.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) before and after mitosis to verify zero interference.

### T5-02 · Doubly-robust propensity-matched contradiction estimator — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** In real streams, exact identical items rarely appear in both $C_1$ and $C_2$ organically. Treat context assignment $C \in \{C_1, C_2\}$ as a causal treatment on item representation $h(x)$: estimate the **context-conditional exception effect** strictly on the high-propensity overlap region ($0.1 < P(C_1 \mid h(x)) < 0.9$) via doubly-robust nearest-neighbor matching in representation space, triggering mitosis only when the overlap-region sign reversal is statistically significant.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Doubly-robust causal estimators come from observational causal inference; embedding a *common-support propensity-overlap test* as the trigger for symbolic concept mitosis in continual learning is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 habits with sparse natural overlap (only 15% near-duplicate items across $C_1, C_2$) plus 4 disjoint-support exposure decoys; compare propensity-matched mitosis vs. unadjusted splitting, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches. Kill if unadjusted clustering or routed per-context adapters match false-split rejection and common-item accuracy.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ remain unaffected across overlap estimation and splitting.

### T5-03 · Bifurcation vs. global-drift temporal disambiguation — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** A contradiction on commonly shown items has two structurally opposite explanations: **(i) context bifurcation** ($C_1$ and $C_2$ remain *simultaneously* valid) vs. **(ii) global temporal drift** (the user simply changed their mind everywhere). Before committing a permanent split, run a lightweight **interleaved revisit test**: probe 1–2 common items back in $C_1$ after seeing the opposing exception in $C_2$. If $C_1$ reverts to the old rule → **mitosis**; if $C_1$ adopts the new rule too → **global in-place update** (no split).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Are Self-Evolving Agents Continual Learners?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25858) highlights non-stationarity without separating permanent context bifurcation from global preference drift; *interleaved-revisit disambiguation between mitosis and overwrite* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 true context-bifurcation habits + 6 global temporal-drift habits on identical items; compare revisit-disambiguated mitosis vs. always-split, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches on post-drift accuracy and spurious-rule count. Kill if routed per-context adapters or always-split recover cleanly on global-drift items without revisit checking.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ across both bifurcation and global-drift episodes.

### T5-04 · Shared-trunk / bifurcated-branch mitosis — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Even when a tacit habit contradicts across $C_1$ and $C_2$ on commonly shown items, ~80% of the habit (parsing, domain conventions, calibration) is still *identical* across both contexts. Instead of duplicating the entire habit into two bloated text rules (or two redundant adapters), keep the uncontested **shared trunk** compiled in tacit weights and promote *only the minimal contradictory decision fork* into two compact context-scoped explicit clauses.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Routed Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38796) and [CaLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36994) route full per-context adapters, duplicating shared knowledge; *factoring mitosis into a weight-resident shared trunk + minimal explicit bifurcated fork* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 complex habits whose shared core is 150 tokens and contradictory fork is 15 tokens; compare shared-trunk mitosis vs. full-duplication mitosis, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches on context length, parameter bytes, and common-item accuracy. Kill if full-duplication mitosis or routed per-context adapters match accuracy at equal memory/context.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ and verify the shared tacit trunk preserves base-domain calibration across both branches.

### T5-05 · Minimal causal context-predicate induction — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Once common-item contradiction is confirmed, the learner must name *what distinguishes $C_1$ from $C_2$*. Naively conditioning on raw session metadata or surface prompt phrasing fails on the next session. Induce the **minimal causal context predicate** by ablating context attributes one at a time on the shared items until the minimal attribute set that flips the user's exception is isolated—turning an opaque context boundary into a clean, generalizable symbolic guard.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Beyond Preference Following](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39550) assumes explicit profile tags are given; *causal attribute-ablation induction of the minimal context predicate* from common-item contradictions is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 contradicting context pairs embedded in noisy prompts with 5 distractor metadata fields (timestamp, channel, user greeting, etc.), evaluated on unseen contexts sharing only the true causal predicate; compare causal predicate induction vs. raw-context splitting, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches. Kill if routed per-context adapters generalize equally well to unseen contexts with swapped distractors.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ are unaffected by the induced context predicates.

### T5-06 · Active common-support bridge querying before mitosis — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** When a tacit habit draws suspiciously opposing exceptions across two contexts $C_1, C_2$ that currently share *zero* commonly shown items, do not guess—**actively synthesize 1–2 bridge items** lying in the semantic overlap of $C_1$ and $C_2$ and elicit user feedback (or oracle verification) on both contexts before committing mitosis, converting an unidentifiable exposure-confounded stream into an identifiable common-support test.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Active learning queries uncertain inputs; *targeted synthesis of common-support bridge items specifically to decide whether to split a tacit habit* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 habits where natural $C_1, C_2$ streams are disjoint (4 true contradictions, 4 exposure-only shifts), allowing a budget of 2 bridge queries per habit; compare active bridge-verified mitosis vs. passive splitting, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches. Kill if passive splitting without bridge queries achieves equal precision/recall.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm bridge probing leaves untouched old rules $A/B$ unchanged.

### T5-07 · Synchronized multi-habit regime discovery — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Contradictory exceptions on commonly shown items rarely belong to a single isolated habit—they usually reflect a *shared underlying mode* (e.g., "external client communication" vs. "internal engineering note") that flips 5 different tacit habits simultaneously. Detect correlated common-item sign reversals across multiple habits and promote **one shared latent regime switch** governing all co-contradicting habits, enabling zero-shot context scoping on the 5th habit the moment it first encounters the regime.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Per-task adapter routing ([CaLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36994)) learns isolated task routers; *cross-habit synchronized regime discovery from correlated common-item contradictions* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 10 tacit habits where 5 share one underlying 2-mode regime boundary; train on habits 1–3 and measure zero-shot/1-shot split accuracy on habits 4–5 vs. independent per-habit mitosis, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches. Kill if independent per-habit mitosis learns habits 4–5 equally fast.
- **Old-task retention:** Score the 5 unrelated habits and untouched old rules $A/B$ to confirm the discovered regime switch never fires outside its scope.

### T5-08 · Noise-invariance gate & automatic sibling recombination — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Even on identical items, noisy/inconsistent human feedback can mimic a contradiction. Require **context-predictable sign consistency** (mutual information $I(\text{exception sign}; C \mid x) > \tau$) above an empirical annotator-noise floor before permitting mitosis; and continuously monitor split siblings $(R_{C_1}, R_{C_2})$—if future user feedback converges to the same answer on common items, **automatically fuse the siblings back** into one rule (or re-submerge into weights).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [PID-Gated Continual Prompt Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16129) damps noisy updates; *mutual-information noise gating + reversible sibling recombination* for concept mitosis is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 true contradictions + 6 noisy-label decoys (20% random user flips on identical items) + 3 temporary splits that later converge; compare noise-gated reversible mitosis vs. unguarded mitosis, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches. Kill if unguarded mitosis achieves $<10\%$ false splits under 20% label noise.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm sibling fusion restores baseline accuracy and ECE on untouched old rules $A/B$.

## Section B — Recursive taxonomy growth & symbolic mitosis vs. adapter routing

### T5-09 · Self-splitting / self-merging hierarchical rule tree — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Real-world continual policies are rarely flat 2-way splits: child rule $R_{C_1}$ may later draw a *new* common-item contradiction along a second orthogonal context axis $D_1$ vs. $D_2$. Generalize binary mitosis into a **self-splitting, self-merging hierarchical rule tree** where each internal node is a common-support-verified context predicate and leaf nodes hold either explicit clauses or re-submerged tacit weights, with automatic collapse whenever sibling leaves converge.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Harness Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19013) maintains flat prompt/skill lists; a *common-support-verified hierarchical rule tree with reversible collapse* grown out of tacit weight residuals is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on a 2-level nested context hierarchy (4 leaf regimes formed over 120 queries); compare hierarchical mitosis vs. flat single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches on leaf accuracy, rule-tree depth, and context tokens. Kill if flat 4-way routed adapters or flat gated patches match accuracy and compositionality.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ across every tree split and collapse event.

### T5-10 · $k$-way spectral mitosis for multi-regime contradictions — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** When a tacit habit simultaneously draws 3+ incompatible exception regimes on commonly shown items (e.g., legal vs. marketing vs. engineering), sequential binary splits depend arbitrarily on arrival order and create asymmetric trees. Build a signed agreement graph over contexts on shared items ($+1$ same exception, $-1$ opposing exception) and run **signed spectral clustering** to perform clean $k$-way mitosis in one step.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Signed spectral clustering is classic in graph theory; using *common-item signed exception graphs* to perform order-invariant $k$-way concept mitosis is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 habits each having 3–4 mutually incompatible context regimes across randomized arrival orders; compare $k$-way spectral mitosis vs. greedy binary mitosis, single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches on order-invariance and regime accuracy. Kill if greedy binary mitosis matches accuracy and context compactness across arrival orders.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ retain baseline accuracy and ECE.

### T5-11 · Boundary-ambiguity abstention between split contexts — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** After mitosis splits a habit into $R_{C_1}$ and $R_{C_2}$, live queries can arrive with underspecified context (neither $C_1$ nor $C_2$ clearly active). Instead of silently guessing one branch (causing high-confidence errors), detect **split-branch disagreement** (when $R_{C_1}(x) \neq R_{C_2}(x)$ and context-predicate confidence is low) and either abstain or emit a 1-bit context clarification question.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Routed adapters ([Macaron-V1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09819)) softly average weights on ambiguous inputs, producing invalid blends between contradictory rules; *explicit branch-disagreement abstention/clarification* avoids blended nonsense.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 split habits tested with 30% underspecified-context queries; compare branch-disagreement abstention vs. soft adapter blending (routed per-context adapters), single promotion, no promotion, and gated patches on selective accuracy and ECE. Kill if soft routing produces valid calibrated outputs on ambiguous-context queries.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm unambiguous queries on untouched old rules $A/B$ never trigger false abstentions.

### T5-12 · Compositional transfer advantage of symbolic mitosis over routed adapters — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Directly test the deep architectural hypothesis behind (a): why promote contradictory habits into *context-scoped explicit rules* instead of just training *routed per-context LoRA adapters* ([Routed Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38796))? When two independently split habits ($C_1/C_2$ and $D_1/D_2$) must be applied *jointly* on an unseen context combination $(C_1, D_2)$, explicit context-scoped rules compose zero-shot in attention while isolated per-context adapters suffer routing interference or require combinatorial $O(|C|\cdot|D|)$ adapters.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Not All Experience Belongs in the Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01787) compares prompt vs. weight storage on single tasks; testing *zero-shot compositional transfer across novel context combinations* as the decisive separator between symbolic mitosis and routed adapters is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B splitting 4 independent habits along 2 orthogonal context axes (training on 3 of 4 quadrant combinations, testing zero-shot on the held-out 4th quadrant); compare symbolic mitosis vs. routed per-context adapters, single promotion, no promotion, and gated patches. Kill if routed per-context adapters (composed via Task Arithmetic/TIES) match symbolic mitosis on the held-out quadrant.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ across both seen and unseen context quadrants.

### T5-13 · `CommonMitosis-CL`: exposure-confound vs. true-contradiction benchmark — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** A CPU-runnable, multi-seed assay dedicated to (a) that mixes four regimes in one continual stream: (1) *true common-item contradictions*, (2) *disjoint-support exposure-shift decoys*, (3) *global temporal preference drift*, and (4) *20% random user noise on identical items*, plus held-out compositional quadrants—scoring split precision/recall, common-item accuracy, context cost, and old-task retention against single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** No existing continual-learning benchmark pairs true common-item contradictions with exposure-shift, temporal-drift, and noisy-label decoys to falsify spurious splitters.
- **Cheapest first test:** Run on Qwen2.5-0.5B (3 seeds) and verify it cleanly separates exposure-free mitosis from naive gradient splitters, routed per-context adapters, gated patches, single promotion, and no promotion. Kill if routed per-context adapters dominate all four regimes simultaneously.
- **Old-task retention:** Mandatory zero-reset $A/B$ accuracy and ECE floor logged across all four regimes.

## Section C — Verbalization-stability laws & anti-crystallization immunity (mechanisms for (b))

### T5-14 · Behavioral Lyapunov contraction test on first promotion — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Operationalize "cannot be stably worded" as a dynamical-systems property: during a direction's first trial promotion into text, track the behavioral error vector $e_k$ on a held-out probe set across consecutive user-driven text edits $s_0 \to s_1 \to s_2$. If text editing is **non-contractive / oscillatory** ($\|e_{k+1}\| \ge \gamma \|e_k\|$ or fixing $x_1$ flips previously passing $x_2$ back to failing), certify the direction **verbalization-unstable**, abort symbolic promotion, and return it to protected weights.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [STEVE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23716) and [GUARD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23476) regularize prompt optimization to stay inside text space; using a *Lyapunov non-contraction test on first promotion to eject unwordable directions back into protected weights* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 discrete symbolic rules + 6 continuous calibration/threshold/style traits undergoing 3 sequential user corrections; compare Lyapunov-gated return vs. always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage (`REX4`) on held-out accuracy, calibration ECE, and oscillation count. Kill if STEVE/GUARD-style damped text editing stabilizes all 6 continuous traits as cleanly as weight-space retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ suffer zero accuracy or ECE regression during trial promotion and return.

### T5-15 · Exact pre-promotion weight rollback (preventing verbalization-corruption bake-in) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Returning a failed promotion to weights by *distilling the unstable promoted text back into weights* permanently bakes prose distortion into the model's calibration and style. Before any trial promotion, snapshot the pre-promotion tacit weight delta $\Delta W_{\text{tacit}}^{\text{pre}}$; if first-promotion stability fails, execute an **exact weight-space rollback** to $\Delta W_{\text{tacit}}^{\text{pre}}$ and apply the user's corrections directly in weight space—guaranteeing zero residual corruption from the aborted text detour.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Faithful Activation Verbalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34033) notes verbalization hallucinations; *atomic pre-promotion snapshotting + exact rollback on stability failure* prevents lossy verbalization from poisoning weights.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 calibrated/stylistic traits with intentionally imperfect first-promotion verbalizations; compare exact pre-promotion rollback vs. re-distilling the aborted text, always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage on post-return ECE and style fidelity. Kill if re-distilling the aborted text preserves calibration ECE within $0.005$ of exact rollback.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm exact rollback leaves untouched old rules $A/B$ bit-for-bit invariant.

### T5-16 · Protected non-verbal continuous adaptation channel for immunized directions — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Protecting a lossy direction from re-promotion must *not* freeze it—users still expect continuous calibration, abstention thresholds, and stylistic tone to adapt to feedback! Route every immunized tacit direction to a dedicated **non-verbal continuous adaptation channel** (e.g., graded activation-steering dosing cf. [Persona Dosing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36388) or rank-1 preference nudging directly in weight space) that incorporates user corrections smoothly without ever translating through lossy English prose.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Verifiable, Articulable, and Tacit Components of Preference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03025) and [Persona Dosing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36388) study tacit preference and graded steering offline; pairing *re-promotion immunity* with a *live non-verbal continuous weight-adaptation channel* in continual learning is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 continuous calibration/threshold/style traits receiving 10 graded user corrections ("too confident", "slightly more concise", etc.); compare immunized continuous weight channel vs. always promote (text editing), never promote (frozen weights), fixed split, and per-region triage on smooth calibration ECE and style target tracking. Kill if text-space editing tracks continuous targets equally smoothly.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) after 10 continuous non-verbal updates.

### T5-17 · Round-trip verbalization-gap bit-loss certificate — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Before even waiting for multiple failed user edits, measure the **round-trip functional KL gap** $\Delta_{\text{KL}} = \min_{s \in \mathcal{S}_k} \text{KL}(f_{W+\Delta W_{\text{tacit}}} \,\|\, f_W(s; \cdot))$ across $k=3$ candidate short verbalizations of the direction. Directions with low $\Delta_{\text{KL}}$ (crisp symbolic rules) pass into editable text; directions with high irreducible $\Delta_{\text{KL}}$ (continuous calibration curves, multidimensional authorship style cf. [2609.04792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04792)) are immediately certified **irreducibly tacit** and protected in weights.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Verbalizing Subliminal Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16927) optimizes text to match weight effects offline; using the *irreducible residual KL across candidate verbalizations* as an online immunity certificate is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 symbolic rules + 6 multidimensional style/calibration traits; measure AUC of $\Delta_{\text{KL}}$ for predicting which directions will oscillate under text editing vs. always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage. Kill if $\Delta_{\text{KL}}$ fails to predict text-edit instability ($AUC < 0.75$).
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm probing $\Delta_{\text{KL}}$ is read-only with zero effect on untouched old rules $A/B$.

### T5-18 · Factorized partial return (keep discrete skeleton in text, return continuous envelope to weights) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Many real habits are *hybrids*: a discrete verbalizable core ("refuse medical diagnosis") wrapped in an unwordable continuous envelope (exact refusal confidence threshold + empathetic tone). When first-promotion edits stabilize on the discrete core while oscillating on threshold/tone, do not make an all-or-nothing choice—**split the promotion**: keep the stable boolean skeleton in editable text and return *only* the oscillating continuous envelope to protected weights (`T5-16`).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Verifiable, Articulable, and Tacit Components of Preference](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03025) decomposes preferences conceptually into articulable + tacit parts; *online stability-driven splitting of a trial promotion into text skeleton + protected weight envelope* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 hybrid rules (discrete decision + continuous confidence threshold + style) undergoing both symbolic exception edits and threshold/style tweaks; compare factorized partial return vs. all-or-nothing return, always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage. Kill if all-or-nothing return or fixed split matches hybrid accuracy and calibration ECE.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ across both symbolic edits and continuous envelope updates.

### T5-19 · Linguistic-paraphrase Jacobian fragility test — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** A rule that is genuinely wordable should behave *invariantly* under minor meaning-preserving paraphrases of its promoted text ("be concise" $\approx$ "keep it brief"). On first promotion, evaluate output divergence across 3 synonymous paraphrases of $s$; directions showing high **linguistic-perturbation fragility** (behavior swings wildly with trivial wording changes—a hallmark of continuous calibration/style traits) are returned to smooth weight space and protected from re-promotion.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Prompt sensitivity is widely documented as a nuisance; turning *synonym-paraphrase fragility* into a zero-user-cost online detector of irreducibly tacit weight directions is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 discrete symbolic rules + 6 calibration/threshold/style traits; compare paraphrase-fragility gating vs. always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage on false-promotion rate, calibration ECE, and user-visible errors. Kill if discrete symbolic rules exhibit equal paraphrase fragility to continuous calibration/style traits.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify read-only paraphrase probing leaves untouched old rules $A/B$ invariant.

### T5-20 · Conditional immunity certificate with discontinuity-triggered expiration — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Permanent immunity is too rigid: a continuous threshold that is irreducibly tacit today may later acquire a crisp, legally mandated symbolic cutoff (e.g., "flag any score $> 0.85$"). Protect returned directions with a **conditional immunity certificate** that blocks routine re-promotion on smooth/graded feedback yet **automatically expires** if incoming user corrections suddenly exhibit a sharp step-function discontinuity along an identifiable input feature.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** No existing continual learner models *regime transitions from tacit to articulable* via conditional immunity certificates with discontinuity-triggered expiration.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 immunized continuous traits where 4 later receive a sudden crisp symbolic policy cutoff and 4 remain purely continuous; compare discontinuity-expiring immunity vs. permanent immunity (never re-promote), always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage. Kill if permanent immunity + continuous weight updates learn the crisp cutoff equally accurately in $\le 3$ steps.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm expired-and-promoted directions do not disturb untouched old rules $A/B$.

### T5-21 · Meta-learned tacitness prior across tasks — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Every failed first promotion costs user-visible oscillation. Record the weight/activation geometry (effective rank, spectral flatness, token-position spread, discourse dispersion) of every direction that passes vs. fails first-promotion stability, and train a tiny **meta-tacitness predictor** that predicts *a priori* which new residual directions belong to calibration/threshold/style manifolds—routing them straight to protected non-verbal adaptation (`T5-16`) without even suffering a failed first promotion.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Activation Steering for Authorship Style](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04792) shows style is high-rank/multidimensional; *meta-learning a cross-task geometric predictor of verbalization instability* to skip doomed trial promotions is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B across 20 tasks (10 symbolic, 10 calibration/style/threshold; train predictor on first 10, test on last 10); compare meta-prior-gated promotion vs. trial-and-error promotion, always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage on failed-promotion count and final ECE/accuracy. Kill if geometric features fail to beat chance ($AUC < 0.65$) on unseen tasks.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ across all 20 sequential tasks.

## Section D — The Polanyi Phase Diagram: unifying (a) and (b)

### T5-22 · Polanyi Phase Diagram: disambiguating text-edit oscillation into context mitosis (a) vs. irreducible tacitness (b) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** The centerpiece unification of (a) and (b): when a first promotion *oscillates* under text edits (fixing $x_1$ breaks $x_2$), run a **diagnostic common-support context test** before deciding its fate.
  - **Phase I (Stable text):** text edits contract monotonically → *keep promoted in text*.
  - **Phase II (Oscillating across contexts on common items):** oscillation correlates with context $C_1$ vs. $C_2$ on shared items → *hidden bifurcation; trigger **concept mitosis (a)** into two crisp context-scoped rules*.
  - **Phase III (Oscillating *within* a single context):** oscillation persists inside a single context along continuous calibration/threshold/style axes → *irreducibly tacit; **rollback to protected weights (b)** + non-verbal continuous channel*.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Neither prompt-stabilization work ([STEVE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23716), [GUARD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23476)) nor tacit-preference work ([2610.03025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03025)) recognizes that *text-edit oscillation has two orthogonal physical causes* requiring opposite structural actions (splitting in text vs. returning to weights).
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 12 oscillating first promotions (4 Phase I stable, 4 Phase II hidden context contradictions on common items, 4 Phase III irreducibly tacit calibration/style traits); compare Polanyi Phase Diagram routing vs. always-mitosis, always-return, always promote, never promote, fixed split, routed per-context adapters, and per-region triage. Kill if per-region triage (`REX4`) or always-return achieves equal accuracy and ECE across all three phases.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) across all 12 trajectories.

### T5-23 · Context-scoped continuous dosing over a split symbolic skeleton — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Directly composes (a) and (b) on the *same* habit: after common-support mitosis splits a habit into context-scoped explicit rules $R_{C_1}$ and $R_{C_2}$, each context often *also* carries its own unwordable continuous calibration/tone (e.g., cautious calibrated hedging in $C_1$ vs. crisp assertive tone in $C_2$). Attach a lightweight **context-conditioned continuous dose** in protected weights (`T5-16`, cf. [Persona Dosing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36388)) keyed to the active symbolic branch—keeping each branch's text minimal while matching per-context calibration and style.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Persona Dosing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36388) applies global trait doses; *keying protected continuous weight doses to the symbolic branches produced by concept mitosis* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 habits that differ across $C_1, C_2$ in *both* a discrete rule and a continuous calibration/style target; compare symbolic-split + context-conditioned weight dosing vs. text-only mitosis, routed per-context adapters, fixed split, and per-region triage. Kill if text-only mitosis matches per-context calibration ECE and style fidelity.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ receive zero dosing spillover.

### T5-24 · Weight-vs-text monotonic-improvement tournament — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Replace heuristic thresholds with a direct empirical **medium tournament**: on the first 2 user corrections after a tacit direction is flagged, run 2 shadow steps of text-space editing in parallel with 2 shadow steps of non-verbal weight-space steering on the exact same corrections, and score both on a held-out mini-validation slice. Commit whichever medium achieves strictly higher **out-of-sample monotonic improvement** (and lower regression on prior passing items).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Not All Experience Belongs in the Weights](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01787) routes components via static hand-designed rules; an *online shadow tournament between text editing and continuous weight steering* on live user corrections is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 12 mixed tasks (6 symbolic, 6 calibration/threshold/style); compare shadow tournament vs. always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage on held-out accuracy, ECE, and zero-regression rate. Kill if `T5-17` (round-trip KL) alone matches tournament routing at lower compute.
- **Old-task retention:** Shadow candidates are evaluated read-only; verify committed winners preserve untouched old rules $A/B$.

### T5-25 · User-vocabulary-adaptive Polanyi boundary — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Whether a behavior is "stably wordable" depends not only on the model, but on the **user's shared vocabulary**: a radiologist can crisply verbalize a diagnostic boundary that a lay user can only correct by tacit examples. Adapt the immunity threshold to the *user's feedback modality*—when a user provides example-only / graded corrections, return the direction quickly to protected non-verbal weights; when an expert supplies crisp symbolic predicates that pass contraction (`T5-14`), allow promotion to explicit text.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Existing personalization work fixes one feedback modality; *adapting the tacit↔explicit immunity boundary to the user's own articulation precision* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 habits under two simulated user profiles (explicit-predicate expert vs. example-only tacit user); compare user-adaptive boundary vs. always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage on convergence speed and calibration ECE. Kill if a single fixed stability threshold works equally well for both user profiles.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ across both user profiles.

### T5-26 · Discourse-level side-effect audit on weight return — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** [When Steering Shifts Discourse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41661) shows that weight/activation interventions for style or calibration can silently shift discourse structure on unrelated prompts. Whenever an unstable promotion is returned to protected weights and updated via the non-verbal channel (`T5-16`), run a lightweight **discourse-invariance audit** on 8 unrelated neutral prompts, projecting any continuous weight update orthogonal to general discourse-drift directions.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [When Steering Shifts Discourse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41661) diagnoses discourse drift statically; *orthogonalizing non-verbal continuous weight updates against discourse-drift directions* protects continual generality when tacit traits live in weights.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B returning 8 stylistic/calibration traits to protected weights and applying 10 continuous updates; compare discourse-audited updates vs. unaudited continuous weight updates, always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage on neutral-prompt discourse stability and target trait fidelity. Kill if unaudited rank-1 continuous updates already cause $<1\%$ discourse drift.
- **Old-task retention:** Explicitly enforces zero discourse or accuracy drift on untouched old rules $A/B$.

### T5-27 · Regret-bounded promotion, mitosis, and return controller — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Inspired by [GUARD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23476) and [PID-Gated Continual Prompt Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16129), wrap all three structural transitions—*promote to text*, *split via mitosis*, and *return to protected weights*—under one **cumulative-regret budget**. Every structural transition incurs an explicit switching cost; transitions fire only when expected cumulative error reduction over the horizon exceeds switching + oscillation regret, preventing thrashing under noisy real-world streams.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [GUARD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23476) bounds prompt-only self-improvement; extending *regret-bounded control across the full triad of promotion, common-support mitosis, and weight-return immunity* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on a 250-query noisy stream mixing true contradictions, exposure shifts, and continuous traits; compare regret-bounded triad controller vs. unconstrained transitions, always promote, never promote, fixed split, routed per-context adapters, and per-region triage on cumulative user-visible error and transition churn. Kill if simple hysteresis thresholds ($k=3$ exceptions) match regret-bounded control.
- **Old-task retention:** Track untouched old rules $A/B$ across all 250 queries to verify zero cumulative forgetting.

### T5-28 · `PolanyiMitosis-CL`: unified benchmark for common-support mitosis & verbalization-stability immunity — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** A single CPU-runnable, multi-seed continual-learning benchmark uniting (a) and (b): a 16-habit stream containing (i) 4 true common-item context contradictions (plus unseen compositional quadrants), (ii) 4 disjoint-support exposure-shift decoys, (iii) 2 global temporal preference drifts, and (iv) 6 irreducibly tacit calibration/threshold/style traits (2 of which later acquire a crisp symbolic cutoff). Evaluates every mandatory control for both (a) (single promotion, no promotion, routed per-context adapters, gated patches) and (b) (always promote, never promote, fixed split, per-region triage), with mandatory zero-reset old-task $A/B$ retention.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** No benchmark tests *common-support mitosis vs. exposure-shift decoys* alongside *first-promotion verbalization-stability rollback and non-verbal weight adaptation* in one unified continual stream.
- **Cheapest first test:** Run on Qwen2.5-0.5B (3 seeds) and verify that single promotion, routed adapters, always promote, never promote, fixed split, and per-region triage each fail at least one regime while Polanyi Phase Diagram (`T5-22`) + matched-item mitosis (`T5-01`) + protected continuous channel (`T5-16`) passes all four. Kill if any single baseline solves all four regimes simultaneously.
- **Old-task retention:** Mandatory zero-reset $A/B$ accuracy and ECE floor logged after every promotion, mitosis, rollback, and continuous update.

### T5-29 · Forecast-gated habit merging vs. local retention or context mitosis (`TCX2`) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Deepens cross-pool round 109's `TCX2` inside our common-support mitosis & sibling-recombination framework (`T5-01`, `T5-07`, `T5-08`): before merging multiple user corrections into one shared habit (or one shared meta-rule), require a **held-out predictive forecast test**—merge corrections into a shared habit *only if* the candidate merged habit accurately forecasts an *unseen* approved correction; if it fails to forecast the unseen correction, reject the merge and either keep the corrections **local** (if on disjoint supports) or **split by context via common-support mitosis** (if they contradict on shared items).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** `TCX2` (cross-pool round 109) and [Harness Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19013) merge or cluster rules by surface similarity; gating *every* habit merge on **out-of-sample forecasting of an unseen approved correction** (and routing failed merges directly to local retention vs. common-support mitosis) is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 12 correction clusters (4 truly shared cross-rule habits, 4 context-contradicting splits, 4 unrelated local quirks), holding out 1 approved correction per cluster as the forecast test; compare forecast-gated merging vs. always-merge (single promotion), never-merge (no promotion), routed per-context adapters, and gated patches on unseen-correction forecast accuracy, false-merge rate, and context compactness. Kill if similarity-based clustering matches forecast-gated merging on false-merge rejection and unseen transfer.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) after every accepted merge, local retention, or context split.

### T5-30 · Equal-token short interaction-note handoff vs. bilinear weights & routed LoRAs (`TCX3`) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Deepens cross-pool round 109's `TCX3` (from `T4-16`): once both rules $R_1$ and $R_2$ are absorbed individually in weights, evict their full definitions and retain *only a short interaction note* in context (describing solely how $R_1$ and $R_2$ compose or resolve precedence), retiring even that short note once the interaction itself is absorbed in weights. Explicitly evaluate at **equal total context tokens** against bilinear interaction weights (`T4-15`), routed/linear LoRAs, OPCD, and No-Context Anchoring (NCA)—testing whether a tiny transient symbolic interaction note beats purely parametric interaction composition during the absorption window.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** `TCX3` (cross-pool round 109), [OPCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12275), [NCA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11627), and [Routed Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38796) never benchmark a *transient, short interaction-only context note paired with solo-absorbed weights* at strictly equal context tokens against bilinear weights and routed/linear LoRAs.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 non-additive / non-commutative rule pairs where solo rules are already absorbed in weights; evaluate at matched context-token budget (20 tokens) across short interaction note (retired on absorption) vs. bilinear weights, routed/linear LoRAs, OPCD, NCA, and length-matched filler/entropy controls on two-rule accuracy, solo accuracy, and peak ECE across the retirement transition. Kill if routed/linear LoRAs or bilinear weights match two-rule accuracy and peak ECE immediately without needing the transient interaction note.
- **Old-task retention:** Score solo $R_1, R_2$ and untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) before, during, and after interaction-note retirement.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Does the Polanyi Phase Diagram (`T5-22`)—using a common-support context test to route oscillating first promotions into either concept mitosis (`T5-01`) or protected weight rollback + non-verbal adaptation (`T5-14`–`T5-16`)—strictly dominate always promote, never promote, fixed split, per-region triage, single promotion, routed per-context adapters, and gated patches on `PolanyiMitosis-CL` (`T5-28`)?**
   This is the sharpest falsifiable test of the entire node: it directly asks whether *text-edit oscillation* really splits cleanly into hidden context bifurcation vs. irreducible tacitness, and whether routing each to its opposite structural home beats every uniform baseline.

2. **How often do real continual-learning contradictions disappear once controlled for common-support overlap (`T5-01`, `T5-02`), temporal revisit (`T5-03`), and unseen-correction forecasting (`T5-29` / `TCX2`)—and when true common-item contradictions exist, does symbolic mitosis actually beat routed per-context adapters on unseen context compositions (`T5-12`)?**
   Directly tests whether common-support mitosis + forecast-gated merging solves a real failure mode (exposure-confounded over-splitting, spurious over-merging, and compositional failure of isolated per-context adapters) or whether non-parametric adapter routing already suffices.

3. **When an unstable promotion is returned to protected weights, does exact pre-promotion rollback (`T5-15`) + a non-verbal continuous weight-adaptation channel (`T5-16`) strictly beat damped prompt optimization (STEVE/GUARD) and per-region triage on continuous calibration ECE, thresholds, and multidimensional style—and at equal context tokens, does a transient short interaction note (`T5-30` / `TCX3`) beat bilinear weights, routed/linear LoRAs, OPCD, and NCA while interactions absorb?**
   Tests whether Polanyi's Paradox is a *fundamental medium limit* of natural-language rule editing in LLMs—or merely a prompt-optimization bug that better text-gradient damping can fix.

4. **Can a meta-learned geometric tacitness prior (`T5-21`) predict verbalization instability *before* incurring a failed first promotion—and can factorized partial return (`T5-18`) + context-scoped continuous dosing (`T5-23`) handle hybrid habits that are half symbolic, half tacit?**
   Most real deployment habits are neither 100% discrete nor 100% continuous; testing whether hybrid habits cleanly decompose into a split symbolic skeleton in text plus a protected, context-dosed continuous envelope in weights determines whether (a) and (b) scale to real-world tasks.
