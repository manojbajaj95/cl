# Successor-funded return paths

**Status:** Ideas only. All first tests below are proposals for CPU-scale enumeration or tiny learners; none was run.

**Parents:** [Cross-pool round 47][r47] and [round 48][r48] ([PR #36][pool]). The reviewer kept [RSL4 and RSL6][t47] and [OSR1 and OSR6][t48] at score 3: plausible mechanisms, no merge yet beats its best parts. The [idea ledger][ledger] rules out treating exact save/restore, replay ties, or by-construction readiness as wins. The closest nodes are [carrier recovery][cr], [successor recovery][sr], [safe order families][so], and [joint releases][jr]. In particular, [successor recovery][sr] already proposes a common read across hidden spends and repeated handoffs at fixed capacity. Neither, alone, is the novelty here.

**Verdict:** The candidate primitive is a return bridge whose cost or scheduling freedom is causally improved by learning a useful successor before the return demand is known. It must restore the unchanged repair across the admitted history/order pairs, keep installed skills live, and pay for actual joint use; a safe bridge that the successor did not make cheaper or more useful is not successor-funded.

## What counts as a return?

- **Spend, acquire, then return.** Start with ordinary old tasks **A** and a frozen callable repair **W** (payload, read/binding contract, permitted evidence and action menu). Install **B** by genuinely spending its carrier: demonstrate a fresh job type previously served by W now fails or cannot fit the budget. Acquire **C**, a legitimate successor, on truthful examples for C's own task. Freeze independent delayed jobs and release schedules before acquisition; hide them from both trainers. After C, attempt a verified output-neutral reroute to a surviving receiver, then run W as written on a fresh released job. No retired carrier, old label cache, or ancestor checkpoint in the candidate; bill all surviving summaries. A reroute that only improves current outputs cannot substitute for that delayed callable service.
- **One rule, not an oracle lookup.** Declare what the launcher can observe, the compatible history set, the admitted order family, and the lease (receiver, capacity, time interval, repayment deadline). Freeze one observation-based launch rule and lease before the hidden choices; on every compatible history and every admitted order they must preserve the installed outputs at handoff and finish W on time. Compare genuinely different feasible orders, not duplicates or a singleton winning trace. Independent history/order choices require the full product; any excluded pair needs a reachability proof fixed independently of its return outcome. A contingent response may use a lawful public event, never a hidden history ID or future batch order. Separate verified unsafe from not certified.
- **No double booking.** Name the obligations that can be called together, and check each reachable joint prefix, not just solo endpoints. Share a carrier for an actual joint return only with a verified simultaneous or ordered handoff, including peak scratch, read/write overlap, deadlines and donor repayment. For alternative paths issue contingent reservations, not two unconditional leases or two credits. Keep partner-specific joint-fold relief separate from freed receiver capacity.
- **Two proof objects.** Safety certifies the allowed coefficient set (or discrete menu), its domain, and which policies preserve installed behavior, bind W and respect hard leases/deadlines. Price certifies that a common policy from that safe relation remains affordable over a declared tariff region, including any claimed refund. A price change can invalidate price without invalidating safety; a change to reads, reachable states, hard deadlines or allowed moves is a scope change, not merely repricing. Keep a price proof only while its named policy and safety dependencies still hold. Deleting a search table is a win only after charging proof bytes and the cost of timelier lawful reacquisition when it was needed.
- **Compare the stacked parts.** Match the truthful C data pool, acquisition utility and margins, model width, permitted evidence, action menu and delayed job distribution. Start from shared C vs equally useful separate C, crossed with joint bridge synthesis vs the best stack of [successor recovery][sr], [safe order families][so], [joint releases][jr] and separate [deletion licenses][dl]. Also compare [Net2Net][net] or [GRACE][grace] where applicable, per-order recertification, exact conformant search on small menus, and paid rollback plus replay. Count useful calls, admitted order coverage, elapsed time, rejected attempts, proof/checker work, peak/persistent bytes and foregone uses. An exact solver with the same information and exhaustive work is a ceiling, not a claim that the candidate beats optimal planning.
- **Retention in every test.** **A/B/C** means ordinary held-out tasks for A, B, and *every* installed successor, including rare route-sensitive inputs. Record each against its acquisition floor at deployed prefixes, after W, and after the specified unassisted suffix; exact handoff equality is an additional requirement, not a replacement. Keep refusals separate and never count a no-write or refuses-all arm as having restored service.

**Two sanity checks for a first test (not results).** If legal bridges on equally observed histories are respectively `{p}` and `{q}`, and this discrepancy appears only on the last two orders, every history/order pair can be individually green while no common rule exists. A public bit can help only if received before the last safe launch. Likewise, two solo proofs with peak demand 1 do not prove a joint return with capacity 1: either show their live intervals can be made disjoint in all admitted schedules, or verify that the same live read serves both without a clobber. Neither constructed failure is evidence that learning C buys a fix.

## Idea cards

### 01. Train C to carry the complementary read — primitive candidate (RSL4)
**Mechanism.** Use a C feature needed for held-out C inputs to replace the computation lost at the spend. Move A's displaced read onto that feature via an exact compensating reroute while leaving C's pinned read intact; bind W at its declared interface. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Successor recovery][sr] already learns a replacement and [Net2Net][net] preserves outputs; the test is whether coupling acquisition to synthesis gives a cheaper *post-pin* route than composing those parts. **Cheapest first test.** Two-head rational ReLU host, matched shared/routed C, one attainable and one impossible pin. Turn the neutral reroute off in each arm; bill joint-fold credit only if the certified merge interaction falls. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/B/C on both sides of the shared activation kink and after the delayed W call.

### 02. Buy a family of orders, not a favourite order — primitive candidate (OSR1)
**Mechanism.** Select truthful examples for C that make two previously incompatible schedule frontiers accept the same receiver read. Compile one public launch rule and lease against the entire hidden-history/order product. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Safe order families][so] already teaches examples to unlock several orders; [successor recovery][sr] already funds a bridge. Look for a crossed effect: the useful C feature makes the *same paid bridge* robust to multiple orders where the best separate stack does not. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two matched-output momentum histories and two independent batch orders, including a failing cross corner; compare a cached winning order, per-order recheck and exact conformant search. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/B/C at both swapped prefixes and after the suffix under each admitted pair.

### 03. Sell one live read to two joint calls — primitive candidate (OSR6)
**Mechanism.** Use a read-only successor-produced representation once for two co-called repairs; isolate their writebacks, and hold the lease until both retire or have handed off. Issue conditional reservations when the calls are mutually exclusive instead. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Joint releases][jr] already shape capacity for co-demand; test whether C supplies a *live reusable read*, not just a joint release or a reused slot. Compare dedicated receivers and ordered reuse at equal work and width. **Cheapest first test.** Two witnesses, one scratch slot, two traces with identical solo checks but respectively commuting and destructive writebacks. Compare against exact joint enumeration and double booking. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B and both successors after each writeback, including the request that completes second.

### 04. Reprice without relitigating safety — improvement (RSL6)
**Mechanism.** Delete a search table after publishing a safe coefficient set with witness policies, and a separate region proving affordability for a particular common policy. At tariff-only expiry keep safety; if no alternative safe policy can be priced in time, refuse the call rather than revoke the safe set. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Deletion licenses][dl] plus [merge-weight ranges][mw] already separate sensitivity and coverage; demonstrate independent lifetimes with a paid common bridge, not a new theorem about parametric sensitivity. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate three coefficients and two policies, then cross a pure tariff threshold, change one pinned read, and introduce a late diagnostic. Compare a joint certificate, a same-byte table and full recertification. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/B/C at all retained coefficients after repricing and show the unsafe edit is not admitted even when cheap.

### 05. Buy a correction that works when history and order interact — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Fit C so the added read cancels an interaction residual in a bridge frontier, not either marginal drift. Expose the residual only to a checker with declared access; the launcher still uses a common public rule. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Merge-weight ranges][mw] already prices mixed residuals and [safe order families][so] checks products; the added claim is that lawful successor learning can *remove* a joint obstruction without a dedicated correction bank. **Cheapest first test.** Fit C in a two-feature linear-momentum learner with a crossed held-out history/order failure. Compare marginal-first fitting, direct interaction repair with equal bytes and the stack. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C on the failing corner as well as on the three passing ones after W.

### 06. Price the option set C leaves open — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use the incremental C budget to buy several return options with different permissible future joint calls; do not commit to any one reservation before lawful demand. Value C by the remaining feasible bundles, not by the sum of solo savings. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Joint releases][jr] handles demand-shaped capacity; robust scheduling already values options. The claim is a single trained read improving the feasible-bundle frontier without extra held capacity. **Cheapest first test.** Two optional jobs and one mandatory job, with equal solo return counts but distinct feasible joint sets. Compare expected-demand fitting, per-call reservations and exact contingent planning. **Old-task retention.** Test ordinary A/B/C after each bundle served, including the least frequent admitted pair.

### 07. Let a late signal split the lease, not choose the training examples — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Learn one C feature before the job is known, then shrink a conditional reservation on a verifiable demand event. Prior to that event use a common safe wait action that preserves both branches and fits both deadlines. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Contingent planning and [safe order families][so] already cover public modes; test whether a *shared C read* makes a formerly unaffordable common prefix viable. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two alternative calls with one public release bit arriving just before or just after the latest safe signal time; compare reserving both, committing early and exact contingent search. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C during the wait and after either branch; expired requests must not trigger an unsafe fallback.

### 08. Learn C against an adversarial pair of co-calls — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Train C on truthful examples picked to turn a witnessed joint conflict into a shared-read, separate-writeback factorization. Use only development co-call types for selection, not held-out job bytes. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Joint releases][jr] optimizes for co-demand; here C has to eliminate a capacity conflict at a writeback while still needing the new read itself. **Cheapest first test.** Three jobs with indistinguishable solo return costs and one clobbering pair; compare covariance-based example selection, dedicated receiver and the best stacked parts, then swap which pair co-occurs. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/B/C after both call orders, including C inputs dependent on both writeback coordinates.

### 09. Mint a refund only after the second return clears — improvement
**Mechanism.** Charge the full provisional receiver debt after the first call; issue a partner-indexed joint-fold refund only once the second completes and a separate merge check verifies the interaction reduction. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Fold-earned headroom][fh] already proves joint relief; this is an accounting improvement for prefix-safe use during a live handoff, not a new folding method. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a delayed second call, an aborted second call and a partner change. Compare immediate booked refunds, no credit and exact prefix accounting. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C during the unpaid interval and after a second-call abort, not only after a successful fold.

### 10. Charge the donor for what it can no longer serve — improvement
**Mechanism.** Give each lease an opportunity-cost counterfactual: which other callable repairs and merges would fit absent this commitment? Require the apparent C discount to survive charging the lost options. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Carrier recovery][cr] already records donor obligations and [deletion licenses][dl] prices policies; improve the baseline by using feasible alternative bundles rather than width alone. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate three callers and two same-size leases, one colliding with a high-value later merge. Compare byte-only, single-caller and exact bundle prices. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/B/C including obligations exercised on the donor route just before and after repayment.

### 11. Rebind the checker without paying for a second acquisition — improvement
**Mechanism.** Retain a dependency manifest per proof: relevant role, arithmetic, live frontier, lease and tariff terms. A receiver relocation that preserves the checked relation may reuse unaffected clauses; changed observations or rounding must not inherit them by name. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Safe order families][so] and [successor recovery][sr] already lift and revoke locally. The improvement is selective invalidation across both proof objects, including price's dependencies on the chosen policy. **Cheapest first test.** Cross a proved gauge permutation, quantization crossing, pure tariff increase and new reader; compare full and incremental checking at equal accepted calls. **Old-task retention.** Check rare A/B/C inputs near quantization boundaries after each admitted relocation.

### 12. Buy later starts, not faster inference — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** C's new read eliminates a preparation step at a binding frontier. Spend that latency gain on a later lawful observation or wider release window, rather than claiming speed on already-feasible calls. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Split-timed deadlines][sd] already distinguishes launch and visible failure; the claim is that learning C causally changes which blind calls can be admitted under a common lease. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate short handoff traces with equal W runtime but differing pre-bind steps; compare a faster checker, an earlier reservation, and a no-shared-feature C arm. Include a smooth cost curve without a timing cliff. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C during delayed launches and after the first time-critical W update.

### 13. Make the bridge portable across tariffs, not across states — improvement
**Mechanism.** Publish a safety certificate and a tiny manifest of named safe policies with resource vectors. An importer can reprice those vectors under a declared tariff family without receiving source history, but may not extend the history or coefficient scope. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Parametric sensitivity and [deletion licenses][dl] already compile price regions; test transfer-byte savings versus shipping the full decision table without silently giving up safety coverage. **Cheapest first test.** Use two importers with opposite byte/time prices and one with a different action menu. Compare a same-byte table cache, local recertification and a single fixed policy. **Old-task retention.** At each importer run A/B/C after using each affordable admitted policy; the changed-menu importer must fail closed.

### 14. Preserve the hole between two safe coefficient bands — improvement
**Mechanism.** Publish disconnected safe cells with separate affordable policies; admit a continuous transition only if all intermediate coefficients it actually uses are safe. Do not interpolate proof objects or combine each cell's cheapest policy into an unexecutable controller. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Merge-weight ranges][mw] already certifies holes; improve RSL6 by distinguishing safe endpoint choices from safe in-flight repricing in a live return. **Cheapest first test.** Hand-enumerate a two-band ReLU gate with a forbidden midpoint and prices crossing mid-return. Compare hull, atomic endpoint switch and exact safe-cell search. **Old-task retention.** Score A/B/C at every deployed coefficient, with a midpoint-sensitive holdout.

### 15. Retire a receiver on the last possible consumer, not the last observed one — improvement
**Mechanism.** Derive last use from all legal continuation orders and co-call statuses. A verified public release can retire a reservation early; a quiet prefix cannot. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Liveness analysis and [safe order families][so] already reason over remaining order ideals; apply them to paid receiver retirement and measure whether evidence buys any meaningful concurrency. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a three-event order family with one rare late reader; compare observed-trace liveness, union-of-orders reservation and exact conditional release. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/B/C after late-reader paths and after reusing the retired receiver on the common path.

### 16. Teach a C feature that pays back its own displaced pin — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Lend a read from B to train C; require C's acquired computation to repay the donor before B needs it, and only then treat the remaining complementary read as funding W. Do not count a repair that merely transfers the loss from A to B. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Carrier recovery][cr] prices donor repayment and [successor recovery][sr] trains counterweights; the claim is a learned read satisfying repayment and a blind multi-order return at lower combined cost than their stack. **Cheapest first test.** Two donor routes, two deadlines and one truthful C pool, including a path that can repay or return but not both. Compare permanent adapters and an exact scheduler. **Old-task retention.** Retest B just before, at and after donor maturity, with A/C after W.

### 17. Audit whether C actually funded anything — improvement (causal audit)
**Mechanism.** Replace the candidate C computation by an equivalently useful separately routed fit, or sham-permute a nonparticipating feature, before giving all arms the same bridge menu. Measure what disappears in *service*, not merely what changes in a fitted probe. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Successor recovery][sr] already audits sharing. Add a crossed price/order control so gauge choice, extra search and C utility do not masquerade as successor funding. **Cheapest first test.** A 2×2 of shared vs separate C and joint vs stacked synthesis, with a known unavoidable crossed conflict and a no-spend arm. **Old-task retention.** Match A/B/C through acquisition and W, including held-out C inputs actually using the candidate read.

### 18. Recover only what would change the return decision — improvement
**Mechanism.** When price-only expiry makes all compiled safe policies unaffordable, buy a legal observation only if some timely outcome could certify a cheaper safe alternative. Do not reacquire history just to reconstruct the old search table. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Decision-relevant queries][dq] and [deletion licenses][dl] essentially give the rule; the improvement is using the unchanged safety relation during price recovery and billing the observation against the return lease. **Cheapest first test.** One decisive cheap probe, an equally informative late probe and a cheap irrelevant probe; compare full reacquisition, no acquisition and optimal query choice on the same menu. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C on both the admitted reply and the unchanged waiting/refusal trajectory.

### 19. Ask the checker for a joint obstruction, not a new teacher — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Return the smallest set of reachable history/order/demand corners that no common affordable policy covers, with separate labels for physical, safety and tariff obstructions. Use the safety obstruction to select lawful examples for a *later independent* successor, not to tune on hidden evaluation jobs. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Counterexample-guided synthesis and [safe order families][so] already expose failing frontiers. The research bet is transferable example selection that removes an obstruction family rather than repairing the original trace. **Cheapest first test.** Fit on conflicts from two toy optimizer hosts, hold out the third and compare generic hard-example mining, single-corner repair and the exact full-menu oracle. **Old-task retention.** Retest A/B/C on the original obstructed pair and on held-out corners C training did not see.

### 20. Let a bounded chain repay bridge principal before borrowing again — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Repeat spend/acquire/return while carrying an audited balance of lease debt and joint refunds. Require each successor to retire or repay the previous obligation without a growing proof or adapter bank; a cheaper price certificate is not itself repayment. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Successor recovery][sr] already proposes repeated fixed-width handoffs. The additional bet is repeatability for multi-order joint calls *under a bounded proof and debt budget*, even when a credit expires. **Cheapest first test.** Three acquisitions and one withheld joint call per round; compare a rolling stacked controller, accumulating adapters, and no-refund planning; expire a price region mid-chain. **Old-task retention.** Score every previous skill separately after each spend and after the final blind suffix.

### 21. Learn a receiver that makes an actual jointly safe order insensitive — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Instead of requiring serialization, learn a C representation whose two W writebacks commute on the declared live reads while preserving C's own use of the representation. Prove this on reachable prefixes, including tails where either writeback is read again. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** Commutativity checks and [safe order families][so] already reduce order search; the hypothesis is that lawful C learning *creates* the commutation, rather than the prover detecting a symmetry already present. **Cheapest first test.** Rational two-coordinate updates with one pair commuting only after acquiring a shared C basis; compare scheduling, basis relabeling without learning, and separate receivers. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B and both C readouts after each prefix and after a third pulse that detects latent noncommutation.

### 22. Price the cost of promising all admitted orders — improvement (frontier audit)
**Mechanism.** Plot separate safety coverage and the least common-policy cost of each declared order family. Decide the family before release; count rejected order demand instead of calling a cheap reduced family universally safe and affordable. **Novelty vs nearest prior art.** [Safe order families][so] and parametric sensitivity already define the pieces. This audit distinguishes a genuine acquisition-funded frontier gain from discarding the hard orders or swapping policies by hidden labels. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate four extensions and two hidden histories; compare one-order, full-family, per-order oracle, and budgeted common-policy frontiers under shared and distinct C. **Old-task retention.** Run A/B/C on every accepted pair, including the most expensive valid extension, and check the unchanged state on refusal.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Can truthful C learning buy strictly more robust return service?** Prioritize the crossed test in 01/02/17: require the shared feature and the verified reroute together to improve useful common-policy calls or cost at matched A/B/C and C utility over the best stack. Stop the primitive claim if separate C, a cached single order with the same real coverage, or paid save/restore explains the gain.
2. **When is shared capacity real rather than two promises against the same slot?** Use 03/08/21 to test compatible and clobbering co-calls with identical solo traces. Demand a feasible joint execution or a properly conditional reservation, and charge donor holds and partner-specific credits. If only a serialized baseline fits, report that bound.
3. **Does independent repricing keep useful safety knowledge alive cheaply enough to matter?** In 04/13/18 compare pure tariff churn and actual scope changes with the best joint-proof and same-byte cache controls. Win only if separating proofs preserves useful calls or measurably cuts total check/reacquisition work without weakening admission safety.
4. **Can the gain survive repeated spends without hidden debt?** Use 12/16/20 to vary launch slack, donor maturities and refund expiry across three or more acquisitions. Require bounded total resident storage and verification work per cycle with retained A/B/C and continued blind service; a growing bank or a gradually shrinking admitted family is not renewal.
