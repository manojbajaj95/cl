# Momentum-steering records and weakest-alias refills

**Status:** Ideas only; no learner training or GPU work. **Parent:** [Momentum-landed gates](momentum-landing.md) ([PR #242][parent]), cards 12 and 16. **Review seed:** [Flood triage, row 355][triage] scored both ideas 3: rotate inherited tangential momentum toward the gate normal with a truthful record and without growing the slot, and choose the inward refill that raises the minimum certified allowance across lookalike states rather than the average. **Read first:** [Idea ledger][ledger].

## Can a truthful record redirect carry and buy room for the least-protected alias?

**Verdict: two separations, no established primitive.** A truthful gradient can redirect a decayed slot under a norm cap that rules out merely adding normal momentum. And with two binding gate faces, an inward refill with lower average allowance can be the only one that gives *every* alias room. Neither establishes a win over a reset plus a good truthful opener, or over an exact robust controller. The research bet is that the same public certificate can help select *useful* training records and authorize later corrections cheaply, without either a slot read or auxiliary control inputs.

**Nearest prior art.** [OGD][ogd], [GEM][gem] and [PCGrad][pcgrad] alter update directions; here the host takes an unmodified gradient from the declared pool, and the test is on the inherited state *after* the update. [Optimizer-state transport][transport], [AdamW input–output dynamics][adamwio] and [momentum fossils][fossils] make inherited carry an explicit baseline, not evidence for record steering. [Finish menus][menus] already reorient paired slots to preserve service; an ordinary record, not a slot operation, must do the work here. [Robust MPC][mpc] already reasons over uncertain states and [history-aware barrier functionals][barriers] already account for allowed excursions. Maximin choice alone is not novel: use exact robust search on the same pool and observations as the reference. The [ledger][ledger]'s plasticity-reserve and forecast-weighted-write failures are a warning to match **absolute** C gain and old retention before attributing any downstream advantage to a selector; the Noether result rules out treating a conserved toy norm as durable memory.

## Contract and falsifiers

- Freeze the old tasks A and B, installed C, an independent incoming-C witness and utility floor, gate thresholds, legal record pool, optimizer and schedule, and the public observation interface. A truthful record is an existing verified C example (or a batch made by the host's ordinary batching rule), not a made-up label, zero-gain recharge, or edited gradient. Bill each useful record once. All action choices are shared by aliases with identical public observations. No reset, projection, mask or slot edit in the proposed learner; use these only as named controls.
- Let `F` contain *all* optimizer states consistent with the observation and lawful history. In heavy-ball, `p = βm` is the no-record slot at the same step; `m′ = p + g`, `w′ = w − m′`. Use an outward gate normal `n`, so a larger `n·m′` helps move the weights inward. A qualifying turn increases `n·m′` without increasing `||m′||` relative to `||p||`, on every alias in scope. Comparing only to `||m||` mistakes ordinary decay for steering. Fix the norm before seeing the record; in Adam report both raw first-moment and effective displacement norms rather than treating them as interchangeable.
- Let `a(s)` be the worst slack over gate faces and the certified zero-gradient coast (including the live state); `A(F) = inf{s∈F} a(s)`. A refill must be certified inward, useful for incoming C, and raise `A` strictly. Select by the post-update minimum, not the expected slack or the slack of the alias that was worst *before* the step. Propagate every alias through the record; after a real public observation, condition only on states that could have produced it. A many-to-one update can also contract a fiber; neither case licenses discarding an inconvenient alias on a guess. Check all declared served prefixes and the new coast before admitting each debit.
- Compare equal all-in budgets: C records and absolute gain, gradients, observations, certificate cost and state bytes, live latency, and old-task floors. Steering controls are a normal-only landing, stronger truthful SGD, reset plus best truthful opener, direct slot write (illegal ceiling), and a purely tangential fiber with no rotating record. Refill controls are point-estimate, mean and exact worst-case search, reset, fixed envelope/impulse ledger, and a fiber where mean and minimum choose the same record. Count refusals and downtime separately from wake-ups; evaluate old-task behavior on held-out A/B and installed C, not just the gate proxy.

## Exact sanity checks

These are rational heavy-ball fixtures, not evidence that curated records are common. Use `β = 1/2` and step size 1. A linear singleton `(x,y)` has half-squared loss and gradient `(x·w−y)x`. For gate face `w_i ≤ −h`, its peak during zero-gradient coast is `max(w_i, w_i−m_i)`. Toy labels below are fixed before the comparison, with a separate fixed C witness; a deployment test must obtain labels from its actual stream.

**F1. A turn can land without adding slot norm.** At `w = (0,0)`, seal `w₁ ≤ −3/5`; take the public fiber `m = (0,2t)`, `1 ≤ t ≤ 5/4`. Record `x = (3/5,−1/5), y = −1` gives `g = (3/5,−1/5)`. The next slot is `(3/5,t−1/5)`; its squared norm changes from `t²` by `2(1−t)/5 ≤ 0`, the normal component increases from 0 to `3/5`, and the live and coast peak on axis 1 is `−3/5` for the whole fiber. At `t=1` the norm is exactly unchanged. A normal-only singleton giving `g = (3/5,0)` seals but grows squared norm by `9/25`. Purely tangential gradients cannot seal; adding the reversed slot `m = (0,−2)` to the fiber makes the same turn inadmissible (norm increase `4/5`). The incoming C witness `w₁` with target `−3/5` improves at the served update under both openers. The toy old checks `|w₂| ≤ 21/10` (A), `|w₁| ≤ 6/5` (B) and `|w₁+w₂| ≤ 33/10` (installed C) hold during the steered coast. **Negative control:** SGD or a reset can land with the same normal increment; F1 only separates the rotation from *norm-growing* carry, not from resetting it.

**F2. Mean-best spends the batch on the wrong face.** Seal both axes at `−1/2`. Start at `w = (−1/2,−1/2)` with aliases `U: m = (0,2)` and `V: m = (2,0)`; both have allowance 0. The same declared C pool contains record X (`x = (1,0), y = −3/2`) and record Y (`x = (1/4,1/4), y = −5/4`). They give `g_X = (1,0)` and `g_Y = (1/4,1/4)`. Both are componentwise inward and have equal one-record cost and identical immediate improvement on the independent witness `w₁+3w₂` with target `−6` (from `−2` to `−6` on U and `−4` on V with either refill).

| Refill | Coast allowance U | Coast allowance V | Uniform mean | Certified minimum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| X | 1 | 0 | 1/2 | 0 |
| Y | 1/4 | 1/4 | 1/4 | 1/4 |

The same later record `(x=(1,1), y=−19/8)` pushes outward. After Y, its gradient is `(−1/8,−1/8)` on both aliases and the certified allowance stays `1/4`; after X, the gradient is `(−5/8,−5/8)` and V has `w₂′ = 1/8` and coast allowance `−5/4`, so the request is refused there. The record is identical, not an equal-gradient debit across the two histories. For toy retention take A to require `w₁ ≥ −5` and no positive `w₂` nuisance, B to require `w₂ ≥ −5`, and installed C to require `w₁+w₂ ≥ −5`. They pass on both refill coasts and Y's debit coast; forcing the refused debit wakes A's nuisance. With only U in the fiber, mean and minimum both select X. An exact worst-case controller on the same two candidates selects Y: the fixture does not beat robust control.

## Idea cards

### A. Redirect the inherited slot

### 01. Spend tangential carry on the seal — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Among truthful records, choose one that increases normal carry and fits inside every alias's decayed-slot norm ball. **Novelty / control.** Restates [#242][parent] card 12; unlike [PCGrad][pcgrad], no projected vector is applied. Compare normal-only landing, stronger truthful SGD, reset, and a direct-write ceiling at matched incoming C service. **Cheapest first test.** F1, then remove the rotating record or admit the reversed alias; the method must refuse. **Old-task retention.** Read A's tangent-dependent output at entry, landing and the coast endpoint, as well as B and installed C.

### 02. Make batch composition close the rotation budget — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Batch two real records whose combined gradient passes every alias's norm cap and inward-angle test though neither alone passes both. **Novelty / control.** Not gradient averaging by itself: compare ordinary batch selection and [PCGrad][pcgrad] on exactly the same records and backward-pass budget. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate all legal size-two batches from four fixed labeled linear examples; look for a feasible pair but no feasible singleton. **Old-task retention.** Probe an old tangent-sensitive classifier after the pair and after each singleton in the sequential control.

### 03. Certify a shared turn from directional support — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** For a common gradient `g`, query the support of the decayed-slot fiber along `g`; the exact worst squared-norm change is `2 sup p·g + ||g||²`. Couple this with the gate bound to certify a record without enumerating aliases. **Novelty / control.** Standard support-function robustification; the conjecture is a reusable certificate for truthful records, not a new convex identity. Compare exact [robust search][mpc] and a componentwise interval check at equal bytes. **Cheapest first test.** Use a correlated rational slot polygon whose box contains a fictitious norm witness and compare the accepted pools. **Old-task retention.** Probe an old read aligned with the discarded correlation, including at the true worst vertex.

### 04. Turn in installments when no single record can — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Commit to a word of truthful records whose every prefix stays inside its decayed-slot norm cap and the old-task envelope; only the last record has to land the seal. **Novelty / control.** Compare [sealed gates][sealed]' word search without the angular target and exact finite-horizon robust search; no benefit if a single stronger record lands at the same accounting. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust length-two words on a rational 2-D pool and look for a strict feasibility split. **Old-task retention.** Query a live B task between the two records, where the gate is not yet landed; bill any withdrawal.

### 05. Route spare carry to a second gate — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Search for a turn that advances the first gate without spending the inherited component needed by a second gate at the next served update. **Novelty / control.** Compare [#242][parent] card 10's common inward velocity and a joint robust search: the separation would be from reuse of nonparallel carry, not two separate stronger records. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two-record plans with cooperative, perpendicular and opposed normals. **Old-task retention.** Probe A and B coupled to different gates during partial closure and installed C after both land.

### 06. Choose a safe side of a curved gate — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** A record can win on normal alignment and still coast across a curved face; choose by the full coast on the actual surface, breaking ties by leftover angle. **Novelty / control.** [Barrier methods][barriers] already handle nonlinear safety; test whether a small record certificate can replace full curvature-aware search without losing decisions. **Cheapest first test.** A parabola with two records tied on first-order normal gain and norm but different tangential coasts. **Old-task retention.** Query an old nonlinear reader at the worst interior point of the losing coast as well as the winner's endpoint.

### 07. Pass both the raw and effective turn tests in Adam — improvement
**Mechanism.** For each true record propagate `m`, `v` and step bias corrections; demand inward effective displacement and no increase in the declared raw and effective norms relative to the no-record step. **Novelty / control.** Extends [AdamW input–output analysis][adamwio] to an admission test; not a new optimizer. Compare a first-moment-only turn rule and best reset opener. **Cheapest first test.** Two aliases with the same `m` but swapped axis denominators; find a record whose apparent raw turn fails in effective coordinates. **Old-task retention.** Check the old task on the small-denominator axis where the raw-only rule overshoots.

### 08. Train the new task in a corridor the tangent used to protect — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Reserve only the part of tangent carry that keeps an old read in its service corridor, then let a truthful record redirect the remainder. **Novelty / control.** Unlike [OGD][ogd], the expensive quantity is loss of *future coast*, not just immediate first-order output change. Compare a controller enforcing the full corridor and a protected-Jacobian projected update. **Cheapest first test.** A 3-D linear reader where one tangent direction is needed only later; match immediate old logits between candidate and control. **Old-task retention.** Measure return below the old read's lower floor along the coast, including with the same continuation after reset.

### 09. Use the cap to prove a turn is impossible — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** If the certified decayed-slot fiber's convex hull contains zero, every nonzero common gradient violates the non-growth constraint for some alias. Return a supporting alias combination and withdraw rather than wasting a batch. **Novelty / control.** A convex feasibility witness, not new robust optimization; compare a crude radius rule and exhaustive pool scanning. **Cheapest first test.** Opposing slots and a nearly opposed pair whose hull excludes zero; the latter must not be refused by this witness alone. **Old-task retention.** Probe normal A/B service on the withdrawal or fallback route as well as on the near-opposed successful route.

### 10. Separate turn, brake and additive landing under a slot intervention — improvement
**Mechanism.** Replay the same legal record at identical weights with rotated, zeroed or reflected slots as offline controls; report the normal gain, retained norm and end-to-end service separately. **Novelty / control.** Builds on [momentum fossils][fossils]' slot/weight swap and [transport][transport], not a new update. **Cheapest first test.** F1 plus a pure braking record and an additive normal record; require the metrics to distinguish all three even when the live gate classification ties. **Old-task retention.** Compare matched immediate old logits and then held-out A/B service following one shared future C request.

### B. Refill for the least-protected alias

### 11. Raise the minimum, not the mean — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Score each useful inward record by the smallest post-record allowance across the propagated fiber. **Novelty / control.** Restates [#242][parent] card 16; maximin is ordinary robust control. Compare point-estimate, mean-best, reset and exact same-information [robust search][mpc]. **Cheapest first test.** F2, including the singleton-fiber control where mean and maximin agree. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B and installed C on both alias coasts before and after the debit, including the refused branch.

### 12. Refill the next bottleneck, not the currently worst alias — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Propagate enough candidates to detect bottleneck switches during the refill; rule out a batch that substantially helps today's worst alias but turns its neighbor into tomorrow's zero-allowance one. **Novelty / control.** Active-set tracking is standard in [robust MPC][mpc]; compare a greedy chooser using the current worst alias and full recomputation. **Cheapest first test.** Three rational aliases with one initially slack face becoming binding under the greedy refill. **Old-task retention.** Test both the original worst alias's old read and the read on the newly binding face.

### 13. Earn allowance by reducing policy-relevant uncertainty — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Pick a useful refill whose ordinary observable training result separates aliases by *future admissible corrections*; keep a robust bound before observing it, then recompute over each possible posterior fiber. **Novelty / control.** Dual control already values useful observations; compare a separate audit plus ordinary refill at equal observations, delay and records, and a robust controller with the same feedback. **Cheapest first test.** Three hidden slots and two useful records, with a public probe that splits only one record's images; then hide the probe as a negative control. **Old-task retention.** Serve held-out A without the probe and check A/B/installed C separately in each reachable observation branch.

### 14. Contract the slot fiber without reading it — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Legal heavy-ball refills contract raw slot differences by `β` at common weights, but move those differences into live weights. Seek a word with a strict contraction in *joint gate-relevant state* without conditioning on extra telemetry. **Novelty / control.** Set propagation and [optimizer response][adamwio] already give the state transition; compare equal-length coast, reset and exact robust propagation. **Cheapest first test.** Show that one linear step shrinks slot width but not uncertainty in the joint coast endpoint by the same factor; then test a real state-dependent second gradient for strict endpoint contraction. **Old-task retention.** Probe an old reader that sees the spread transferred into the weights, not only the better-behaved slot.

### 15. Bank headroom in the direction the next debit needs — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Publish which truthful outward requests a refill actually buys, rather than ranking refills solely by the smallest undirected slack across gate faces. **Novelty / control.** A discrete admission menu is close to robust backward reachability and [finish menus][menus]; compare that search, scalar maximin and the parent's impulse ledger. **Cheapest first test.** Two refills tied on scalar allowance but with disjoint safe subsets of three fixed debit records, then vary the declared request set. **Old-task retention.** Check the old task sensitive to each admitted debit direction; no credit for menu entries the old floor rules out.

### 16. Refill until the policy clears a discrete threshold — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Optimize the cost of the shortest useful refill word that permits a particular outward correction, rather than repeatedly maximizing slack one step at a time. **Novelty / control.** Compare exact shortest robust planning and a greedy maximin tank fill; need a cheaper certificate or a wider legal admission set at equal compute. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust two-refill words on a ReLU host where the greedy first refill changes the sign of the only useful second record. **Old-task retention.** Read A/B after each refill, not just after the successful debit.

### 17. Keep joint alias identities across faces — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Preserve the pairing of moments and weights within an alias when propagating refill outcomes, so the planner cannot assemble a fictional next state from unrelated worst coordinates. **Novelty / control.** Correlation-preserving reachable sets are standard [robust control][mpc]; compare exact enumeration and factorized interval ledgers, especially on coupled or multistep constraints where this matters. **Cheapest first test.** Two anti-correlated aliases and a subsequent record coupling the coordinates; test if the box rejects a correction which both real paths allow. **Old-task retention.** Check an old diagonal reader on both trajectories, then add a genuine joint worst alias and ensure it is protected.

### 18. Prove which historical aliases can be forgotten — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Replace the full history fiber in the refill certificate by representatives only when a sound dominance relation shows omitted aliases cannot bind for any declared next record. **Novelty / control.** Related to simulation relations and invariant sets in [robust MPC][mpc]; compare custom pruning against a fresh exact search and against dropping merely slack aliases. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust a two-step menu where currently slack aliases include one with a larger future outward response; the sound rule must keep it. **Old-task retention.** Probe the dominated and retained aliases separately, since gate dominance need not imply dominance for A/B.

### 19. Learn to ask for a refill without changing its label — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Send a public data request naming the under-supplied direction or task slice; an independent teacher answers with ordinary verifiable examples, and the gate only credits those that pass the fiber-wide refill test. **Novelty / control.** Compare active learning by uncertainty or absolute C progress and [robust control][mpc] on the same returned pool; the hypothesis is about acquiring *different* truthful candidates at equal label and search budget. **Cheapest first test.** CPU teacher with a fixed four-cell labeled table and one query left; include a slice with no lawful inward examples. **Old-task retention.** Compare held-out A/B and installed C after both selectors reach the same absolute C gain, charging any refusals and unfilled requests.

### C. Test the interaction, not just the two parts

### 20. Let one record turn the slot and raise the weakest allowance — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Intersect the all-alias norm cap, the all-alias inward-turn test and the strict minimum-allowance improvement test on a single useful record. **Novelty / control.** Neither [#242][parent] seed requires both in one step. Compare separate best turn and refill records, plus exact joint robust search, with equal service and accounting. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a 3-D, two-face pool containing a turn that lowers worst allowance and a refill that grows the slot; look for a third lawful record that meets both tests. **Old-task retention.** Check the old task coupled to tangent carry and the old task at the weakest face at the update and next admitted debit.

### 21. Refuse a beautiful turn that spends tomorrow's only truthful refill — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** If later truthful supply is finite, reject a qualifying turn when using that example now leaves no legal refill before a known outward request. **Novelty / control.** [Self-depleting supply][supply] already prices consuming records; compare its one-step accounting and full resource-constrained robust planning on the same declared requests. **Cheapest first test.** Two candidate turns and one reusable, one one-use record; compare a stream with no future debit to one requiring the one-use record to stay in reserve. **Old-task retention.** Test normal A/B service at the reserve deadline and during the conservative turn, not only when the future record is finally spent.

### 22. Find the boundary where both scores collapse to ordinary ranking — improvement
**Mechanism.** Enumerate small hosts varying fiber width, gate normals and truth-pool support; record where the norm cap removes no candidate or the mean and minimum never disagree. In a scalar affine gate, a common inward impulse improves all aliases monotonically, so a change of ranking needs a different constraint, response, or action. **Novelty / control.** A falsification map, not a new ranking; compare the [parent][parent]'s scalar impulse ledger and ordinary C-loss ranking. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust a one-face rational pool, then add a second binding face and a curved read; do not infer durability from a toy norm invariant. **Old-task retention.** Require the ranking split to survive matched absolute incoming C gain and old A/B/installed C floors, not merely show a wider raw score spread.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Do naturally available records give a norm-safe turn an advantage over reset plus the best truthful opener?** Start with 01–03, 08 and 10 on a shared-feature model with a fiber derived from public records. Require real old-task service at matched absolute C gain and compare against normal-only, strong SGD and exact robust search. If the slot-free baselines tie or truthful norm-safe turns are too rare at equal search cost, retire steering as a primitive.
2. **Can a reusable weakest-alias certificate buy safe corrections more cheaply than solving robust control again?** Start with 11–12 and 15–18. F2 isolates a different decision from mean and point selection, but exact robust search already makes it. Seek equal admissions at lower all-in cost, or more safe admissions at equal cost; kill the claim if the gain vanishes under retention matching or if one omission wakes a declared old task.
3. **Can the refill itself shrink uncertainty that matters to admissibility, without an auxiliary audit or moving hidden risk into the weights?** Use 13–14 and 19 to separate real posterior conditioning from deterministic contraction and from merely hiding an alias's bad outcome. Compare audit-then-refill at equal cost and information, and refuse on a public trace that cannot separate the relevant states.
4. **When do turn and refill compose into one useful record rather than fight over the same carry or data?** Use 04–07 and 20–22 on a multi-face gate and then Adam. Chart where the joint operation beats sequential actions at equal budget, where it reduces to an impulse ledger, and where the only safe policy withdraws. Report old-task service throughout the window in all three regimes.
