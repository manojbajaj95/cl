# Shared-part loan commits

**Status:** Ideas only; no training runs, implementation, or GPU work. **Parent:** [Confirmable margin loans](margin-loans.md) ([PR #233](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/233)), cards 05 and 13. **Review seed:** [Flood triage, row 345](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/ec716d67cef5349f3d783e2261308c958af296e6/reviews/flood-triage.md#L345) gives both ideas a 3: let the first example move the learner to a transient point shared by several useful continuations, then keep only what the independent confirmation also supports and repay the rest; hand the still-pending write to another lender only through overlapping old-safe reads, with at most one lender in debt and no extension of the original deadline. **Read first:** [Idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/ec716d67cef5349f3d783e2261308c958af296e6/reviews/idea-ledger.md): match margins, stack the strongest controls, reject wins built into the fixture, and test ordinary old tasks in every first test.

## Can a write keep its options while changing who pays for it?

**Verdict: two separate candidate operations; the merge has to earn its keep.** The first moves and serves provisionally without choosing a durable continuation; confirmation selects only the useful common direction and the host repays the rest. The second moves the live evidence through a point where both groups can read safely and neither is owed anything, before the next group lends its slack. Neither intersecting constraints nor switching between safe modes is new. [AND-mask][and] tests coordinate-wise gradient agreement, [TIES][ties] elects signs when merging updates, and [model soups][soups] average weights. Set-membership fitting can intersect evidence exactly. [Fast weights][fast] and buffered fitting preserve pending evidence; [Simplex][simplex] and [control barrier functions][cbf] cover safe switching. The distinguishing test is whether moving **the same live displacement** buys useful provisional service and later selective consolidation that the best budget-matched combination of those tools cannot reproduce.

### Contract for the first tests

- Freeze the host, ordinary phase maps, legal example/update menu, public clocks, lender groups, budgets and evaluation panels before drawing blind jobs. Let each view induce a set of **jointly realizable candidate changes** in a common function-space chart (signed, bounded changes on declared B reads); use their intersection, including magnitude and sign, not raw gradient support or intersection of unsigned spans. Require a nonzero, realizable and useful common change; empty, contradictory, or useless agreement means repay and abstain. Do not infer the sets from a supplied job ID, provenance bit, label oracle or future example. Fit rules on separate jobs; retire both examples and score held-out B contexts.
- Track the named lender's spent slack relative to a same-phase no-write orbit. Require repayment of the first before debiting the second, a reachable transfer state certified for the union of both old read obligations, and no overlap in debts at any exposed state. Two safe endpoints are not an overlap certificate; for continuously exposed reads, bound the path too. Fix a single absolute expiry at first admission; at the deadline repay before accepting any new confirmation. Charge any clock, proof, slot, pending statistic or read selector not already public. Compare early, boundary, just-before-expiry and just-after-expiry arrivals, including a disjoint-read-safe transfer control and a stalled handoff.
- **A** is ordinary old-task success; **C** is success on previously acquired jobs. Measure both every exposed phase, at confirmation or default, and after matched unrelated updates (returns 1, 8 and 64). Match starting per-item signed margins; require acquisition-anchored floors throughout and restoration of every declared old gap to its same-phase no-write counterpart after conversion/expiry. Check each true-vs-rival gap rather than just the mean or top label; use separate near-boundary held-out A/C panels and never hide a brief double spend in aggregate accuracy.
- Stack a useful provisional write and a byte-matched waiting buffer or fast trace, a phase-indexed [GEM][gem] or exact read-safety guard, and buffered exact intersection plus constrained fitting; give the stack the same read/write permissions, moves, lawful evidence, timing and compute. Also compare plain SGD, averaging, AND-mask, [PCGrad][pcgrad], #221 card 10 whole-write confirmation, #210 card 06 staggered loans, a fixed single-lender trace and expiry at first repayment. Use exhaustive same-information search as a ceiling. Match peak bytes, scans and probes, false confirmations, refusals, B coverage and latency; compare duplicates, truthful-but-wrong-job examples and norm/angle/timing-matched junk. Cut and transplant just the live displacement and compare late evidence after expiry with that evidence alone. Identical observable histories must yield identical decisions.

### W10 — a handoff can preserve a pending prediction without overlapping debts

Let the state be `(p,q;u,v,w)`, with pending coordinates `p,q ≥ 0` and durable coordinates initially zero. The same-phase no-write state is all zero. Old gaps for lenders A₁ and A₂ are `3 − max(p−q,0)` and `3 − max(q−p,0)` (floor 2); an ordinary old read C has gap 3 throughout. The first example sets `(p,q)=(1,0)`. The frozen phase maps next send `(p,q) → (p/2,q+p/2) → (0,p+q)`, then zero both coordinates at the deadline. Thus A₁/A₂ gaps are `(2,3) → (3,3) → (3,2) → (3,3)` with one debtor at most. The middle point preserves the provisional B read `p+q+u=1` and repays A₁ before A₂ spends anything. Along the linear interpolants the same floor and single-debtor property hold. Replacing the middle state with the no-write state removes the provisional read; the next ordinary map cannot recover it.

For the commit arithmetic, give a nonzero pending state the first menu `{(a,b,0): 0≤a,b≤1}` and a fresh confirming view the menu `{(a,0,c): 0≤a,c≤1}`, in durable coordinates. If the frozen utility ranks larger `u` first, the joint choice is `(1,0,0)`; for example, `(1,1,0)` is legal for the first view alone but not jointly. Clear `p,q` on return: the B read is still 1 but neither unsupported coordinate survives, and both old gaps return to 3. Restricting the second menu to `a=0` leaves only the useless zero change; default instead. The same confirming view at or after expiry cannot invoke this pending first menu. For a disjoint-safe-set control on the handoff, subtract `4 min(p,q)` from both old gaps. The endpoints are unchanged, but any continuous path keeping `p+q=1` must cross `p=q=1/2`, where both gaps are 1 and the floor is 2. There is no safe overlap on the carrier-preserving slice.

W10 checks consistency, not learning: it stipulates the phase maps and evidence decoder, its durable coordinates do not affect old reads, and an exact buffered solver given the same menus ties the commit. The next test must discover or verify a carrier in shared parameters on blind jobs, without putting the conclusion into the maps or giving only one arm the continuation menu.

## Idea cards

### 01. Open several continuations and close only their common direction — seed / primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** A truthful first update reaches one serving face with several feasible continuations; the next independent view chooses only the useful common direction, with the ordinary return removing first-view-specific movement. **Novelty / control.** Set-membership fitting already intersects constraints and [AND-mask][and] already gates on agreement; the candidate is a live, serving representation of the first menu, not the intersection rule. Compare exact joint fitting, whole-write latching, averaging and SGD. **Cheapest first test.** In a three-coordinate blind teacher, enumerate independent views with line, ray, empty and useless intersections; remove and transplant the serving displacement before confirmation. **Old-task retention.** A/C gaps on the serving face and after each commit, abstention and transplant.

### 02. Commit the same effect through different units — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Decode each view in a function-space chart, then use the pending state's current activation cell to realize their shared effect even when the two views require different raw parameter directions. **Novelty / control.** [OGD][ogd] protects output directions and [Git Re-Basin][rebasin] aligns redundant coordinates; the candidate makes shared evidence portable across physical realizations without an alignment copy. Compare aligned AND-mask and exact output fitting. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two legal ReLU realizations with disjoint supports for the same effect, then add a fresh read that makes them genuinely different. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C at the representation change and on the read that distinguishes the two realizations.

### 03. Jointly support gain, not just direction — improvement
**Mechanism.** Let a first-view face support a range of gains along several rays; commit only the gain certified by both views, and make excess provisional amplitude fund the return instead of leaving it in the optimizer. **Novelty / control.** Gradient clipping bounds a step and [TIES][ties] elects a sign; neither by itself assigns excess first-view movement to repayment. Compare magnitude-aware exact intersection plus an equally priced return. **Cheapest first test.** Use two same-sign views with overlapping and non-overlapping gain intervals at matched norms; detect whether the carrier contributes anything beyond a stored interval. **Old-task retention.** A/C at maximal provisional gain, at partial repayment, and after common later updates test for slot residue.

### 04. Preserve decisions shared by all branches, not their coordinates — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Treat a pending face as a family of B policies. Serve actions invariant across that family while waiting; on confirmation commit only the jointly supported change on a declared action panel, abstaining elsewhere. **Novelty / control.** Version-space unanimity is known; the candidate reuses the state that computes conservative service as the evidence carrier. Compare a version-space buffer that can also serve and a selective classifier at equal coverage. **Cheapest first test.** Four finite policies agree on one context and split on others; make the second view resolve only one split and measure selective error on held-out contexts. **Old-task retention.** Report normal A/C success and coverage separately so old abstentions cannot masquerade as retention.

### 05. Confirm without a gradient on the committed direction — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** A truthful second view excludes a false branch by failing one of its predictions, without directly training the surviving direction; let the live face supply that direction only if it is unique up to an old-safe equivalence. **Novelty / control.** Constraint elimination and co-training already use negative evidence; the candidate is consolidation through a live eliminator rather than a buffered refit. Compare a buffered version-space eliminator at equal labels. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate one negative response shared by two surviving branches and one that leaves exactly one; require strictly no commit in the ambiguous case. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C while the negative example is processed and after both the unique and ambiguous outcomes.

### 06. Make disagreement fund repayment — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Route the unsupported component of a corroborating update into a contracting direction that accelerates clearance of the corresponding pending freedom, without increasing the durable component or touching the deadline. **Novelty / control.** PCGrad removes conflicting gradient components and the parent returns whole loans; the candidate makes the removed component itself useful to the return. Compare PCGrad plus passive and actively fitted repayment. **Cheapest first test.** In a two-mode linear return map, enumerate bounded signed updates with the same durable gain and opposite effects on repayment; do not synthesize a special inverse example. **Old-task retention.** Read A/C at maximum contraction and through the worst lawful discrepancy that makes contraction unsafe.

### 07. Distill the common part into a smaller writable face — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** When multiple views justify a one-dimensional effect, refold the live broader face into the smallest realizable carrier whose next lawful common update is also preserved. Return only the freedom the refold actually releases. **Novelty / control.** Compression and low-rank adapters already shrink parameters; the candidate refunds a live write budget conditional on preserving the next admitted update, not just the current prediction. Compare an exact low-rank fit plus optimizer reset and a permanent wider carrier. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate rank-two faces with one- and two-dimensional corroborated effects, then deliver a normal third batch that distinguishes function-only refolding. **Old-task retention.** Measure A/C before the refold and after the next batch, including an old read sensitive to the lost mode.

### 08. Use a third view only when it can change the commit — improvement
**Mechanism.** Spend a fixed query only if some lawful answer changes a commit versus abstain decision before deadline; ask the question from the active face, then throw away unsupported degrees of freedom as usual. **Novelty / control.** Active learning and the parent's card 14 already select informative corroboration; this gate values the **decision over the common part**, not generic prediction disagreement. Compare buffered decision-theoretic querying. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate four unlabeled queries, one high-entropy but commit-irrelevant and one low-entropy that changes the legal gain; charge arrivals and misses. **Old-task retention.** A/C while waiting, at refusal and after each possible answer.

### 09. Assign the residue to observable future errors — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose among equivalent realizations of the jointly supported change so no unsupported movement is detectable under a frozen suite of lawful next updates, not merely on today's B panel. **Novelty / control.** The parent checks for latent residue and behavioral equivalence already handles future consumers; the proposed operation chooses the commitment representative using that future equivalence rather than auditing it afterward. Compare exact buffered output fitting with the same future constraints. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two slot states with the same outputs and a common next minibatch that unveils a discarded branch; fit on one minibatch suite and test on another. **Old-task retention.** At commit and after each intervening batch, require A/C gaps and C service for both representatives.

### 10. Learn a chart in which common evidence is not an axis accident — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** On offline jobs, learn one representation of serving faces such that independently held-out views predict common action changes under changes of nuisance basis. Freeze the chart before the blind stream and require each resulting commit to have a legal physical update. **Novelty / control.** Meta-learned representations and learned gradient alignment already improve transfer; the candidate chart supports both temporary reads and extraction of a mechanically realizable common commit. Compare a rotated AND-mask, exact function-space fitting and a learned fast buffer. **Cheapest first test.** Design on two latent teacher families and test on a third with a rotated nuisance basis and no common actuator for some pairs. **Old-task retention.** A/C under all bases and after rejecting the actuator-mismatch cases.

### 11. Transfer a pending write through a debt-free read overlap — seed / primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** The ordinary phase map first clears A₁'s debt at a state certified for both lender read sets, then carries the same pending displacement under A₂'s slack. Neither transfer nor new evidence moves the outer deadline. **Novelty / control.** The grandparent's staggering moves exposure and [Simplex][simplex] switches safe modes; the candidate moves the evidence and provisional service without an extra buffer. Compare a single lender, a first-example buffer, staggered independent loans and expiry at first repayment. **Cheapest first test.** Extend W10 with blind task amplitudes, early/late corroboration and a disjoint-overlap null; remove only the transported displacement. **Old-task retention.** Sample both lender groups and C before, at and after the handoff and verify the debt ledger itemwise.

### 12. Hand off a consequence instead of a coordinate — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Let A₂'s permitted weights look different from A₁'s, but transport the minimal measurable consequence that suffices for all admissible confirmations; reject a handoff that preserves present B service yet loses a distinguishing response. **Novelty / control.** The parent's representation changes and standard sufficient statistics cover encoding; the candidate certifies successor confirmations on a shared-read transfer without an extra statistic store. Compare a minimal buffered sufficient statistic using the same guard. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two first views with the same provisional read but different valid corroborators and search for a lawful encoding that separates them. **Old-task retention.** A₁/A₂/C at both representational boundaries and after the adversarial corroborator.

### 13. Build a reusable neutral exchange state — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Design one debt-free family of states shared by several lender pairs, carrying different pending views without keeping a per-pair transition cache; the next lender still has to be reachable in time through its own certified overlap. **Novelty / control.** Hybrid invariants and shared switching guards are known; the operation tries to reuse one information-preserving guard across blind jobs and lender pairs. Compare pair-specific reachability plus a byte-matched courier. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate three lender maps, two blinded pending states and one pair with no common route; compare certificate bytes and admissible transfers. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C for every incident route, particularly the lender absent from the chosen pair.

### 14. Verify the observable updates as well as the reads at the overlap — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Require equivalent old-safe readouts **and** equivalent confirmation decisions for a corroborator exposed at the exact transfer boundary, independent of whether the lawful scheduler presents it before or after the map. Refuse transfers for which the two cannot be reconciled. **Novelty / control.** Hybrid confluence and the parent's repayment priority cover event ordering; here the boundary must preserve both evidence semantics and the old-safe guard. Compare fixed-priority scheduling with an explicitly paid queue. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate both orders on one boundary pair with identical reads but different optimizer responses and another pair where the two orders commute. **Old-task retention.** Check A₁/A₂/C for both orders, not just for the order that commits.

### 15. Surrender a route before it needs two debtors — improvement
**Mechanism.** Certify a latest safe exit from each pending phase under admissible read arrivals, and trigger natural repayment if waiting longer could force a double debit before the next certified overlap. The fixed outer deadline is still an upper bound, not a promise of service until then. **Novelty / control.** Viability kernels and switched-system guards already encode safe exits; the candidate couples an exit to continued access to corroboration, not only to keeping an old gap above a floor. Compare exact reach-avoid scheduling with the same observation budget. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate three asynchronous arrivals and a case where two safe endpoints hide an unsafe intermediate exposed state. **Old-task retention.** Check all lender gaps at the earliest forced exit and after a rejected late read.

### 16. Let useful old work carry the handoff — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use a required, ordinary old-task update as the transport step when it both restores the first lender and preserves the current pending evidence; don't insert an artificial rescue batch. **Novelty / control.** Replay and the parent's ordinary map already supply recovery; the candidate makes a mandatory, matched old work item sufficient to move the evidence with no extra transfer call. Compare replay followed by a separately paid handoff and replay plus a waiting buffer. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate authentic old minibatches with matched old gain but different transport, including a batch that erases the pending view and must cause default. **Old-task retention.** A₁/A₂/C gain before and after every minibatch, with separate accounting for replayed versus held-out old items.

### 17. Handoff under lender dropout without resetting the clock — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose a debt-free exchange state from which the same pending job can switch to either of two certified lenders if one withdraws, using only observable dropout and never charging both. **Novelty / control.** Robust switching and staggered schedules already provide backup modes; this route preserves unfinished evidence under one uniform policy rather than just keeping old tasks safe. Compare a robust rotating buffer, deterministic schedules, and a hindsight planner as an upper bound. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate one dropout before, during and after overlap with a single public-history policy and report the cases where default is unavoidable. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C for all dropout orders, including the withdrawing lender's last permitted read.

### 18. Carry across clock jitter without minting time — improvement
**Mechanism.** Certify the set of phase offsets compatible with public timing and hand off only if every compatible state reaches a joint guard and repays by the same original expiry. **Novelty / control.** The grandparent handles jitter in staggered loans and robust reachability already checks uncertain clocks; the extra object is a transported evidence class that remains interpretable across the allowed offsets. Compare a timestamped buffer and a worst-case guard with no transport. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a one-tick ambiguity where every visible read agrees but one offset would interpret the pending view incorrectly; charge any new clock observation. **Old-task retention.** A₁/A₂/C on the full offset set, including the earliest possible expiry.

### 19. Make commitment commute with changing lenders — primitive candidate (merge)
**Mechanism.** Compare confirming the shared part just before versus just after the overlap: require the same realizable durable B effect and complete repayment, while allowing temporary coordinates and itemwise paths to differ legally. **Novelty / control.** Commuting diagrams and order consistency are standard; the candidate is a carrier whose noncommuting *physical* moves still commute on joint evidence and future behavior. Compare independent implementations of cards 01 and 11 stacked, including exact buffered fitting across a guard. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a two-lender rotation, a matching pair of commits and a rotation with a planted false positive that preserves read safety but changes the common direction. **Old-task retention.** A/C throughout both paths and after a common unrelated update that would amplify unmatched residue.

### 20. Split what must travel from what must be repaid locally — primitive candidate (merge)
**Mechanism.** Factor a pending state into a sufficient carrier for all still-admissible corroborators and a disposable, locally useful component; repay the latter before crossing the joint guard. Do not discard distinctions just because they are absent from the current B prediction. **Novelty / control.** Minimal sufficient statistics and the parent's partial commit cover the pieces; the candidate buys a previously unreachable handoff by clearing exactly what the far side cannot carry without changing valid commitments. Compare the best compressed buffer plus exact local repayment and whole-state transfer. **Cheapest first test.** Use an overlap with capacity for one bit less than the full provisional state; test both a redundant coordinate and a hidden bit needed by a rare corroborator. **Old-task retention.** A/C during local clearance, throughout transport and when the rare corroborator forces refusal.

### 21. Close the overlap with the commit itself — primitive candidate (merge)
**Mechanism.** If a legal independent confirmation arrives at the debt-free overlap, let its common effect use the same legal actuator that would otherwise open A₂'s loan. Commit with full repayment and skip the second draw rather than borrowing and instantly returning it. **Novelty / control.** Short-circuit scheduling is known; the claim is one actual update buys both a useful permanent effect and avoidance of new borrowing, where a separate confirm-and-return sequence cannot fit the read schedule. Compare early exact buffered commit plus ordinary guard and the two seeds stacked. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate overlap-only actuation with one available update, plus a generous schedule where sequential execution should tie. **Old-task retention.** Measure both lenders and C at the common actuator and later returns, showing A₂ never incurs debt in the claimed shortcut.

### 22. Reopen a resolved face without reviving the repaid alternatives — primitive candidate (merge)
**Mechanism.** Let a committed B skill become a protected C reader for the next loan; certify that reopening a shared host for a new job cannot let a repaid first-view component stand in for new evidence. **Novelty / control.** The parent asks whether commits can become protected readers; the stricter operation admits recursive reuse only if future pending choices cannot recover a discarded continuation. Compare independent per-job adapters, a bounded rotating buffer plus constrained fitting and a fresh disjoint face. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two orders of three blind jobs with one matched alias between an old discarded branch and a new first view; count peak bytes and false confirmations. **Old-task retention.** Check A and each previously confirmed B as C throughout the later handoffs, including late and mismatched corroboration.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Is a live shared face needed, or does a statistic explain everything?** Start with 01, 02, 09 and 10. Remove and restore only the displacement, then compare the strongest stack at matched bytes, exposure, false confirms, coverage and latency. Require both useful in-window service and useful held-out B after full repayment with A/C retained. An exact buffered tie is a null, not a weaker version of the claim.
2. **Can the host hand off the evidence, not just produce two safe endpoints?** Start with 11–14 and 16. Require one reachable debt-free intersection, both lender read obligations checked together, and equivalent lawful decisions at the overlap and immediately before expiry. Sweep a valid overlap into a disjoint one and compare expiry-at-first-repayment and buffer controls.
3. **Does selective repayment make an otherwise impossible handoff possible?** Start with 19–21. Test an overlap too small for the full provisional state, including a rare corroborator that needs the supposedly disposable coordinate. Require a matched-regime gain over the stacked seeds or report two separate ideas.
4. **Can the mechanism retire examples and keep serving through many jobs without hidden storage or revived residue?** Start with 07, 17, 18 and 22. Count every carry byte and every default, audit common later updates, and retire committed skills only from the evidence store, not from A/C evaluation.

These are nearest comparisons from the ledger, parent, and a targeted paper check, not exhaustive novelty claims. The first gate is a finite CPU test on blind jobs with a non-oracular carrier and an exact buffered counterfactual; W10 is only a hand check.
