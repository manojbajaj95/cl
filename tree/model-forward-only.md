# Can a model keep learning from forward passes alone?

**Yes, in important restricted regimes. The missing primitive is a way to turn forward evidence into reusable, retention-aware weight writes for capabilities the model does not already expose.** MeZO estimates an update from perturbation scores; ES parallelizes that trade; weight-space programming supplies a strong prior over promising edits. None of those observations establishes compute-competitive, open-ended acquisition *and* retention in the same model. More A/B samples address noisy comparisons, not missing supervision or the choice of what to perturb.

Here, *score-only* means a loss/reward or a single preference bit, with ordinary inference and weight mutation. *Forward-observable* also allows reading activations, contrasting traces, and solving local algebraic updates. Forward-mode automatic differentiation (JVPs) and analytic local derivatives avoid reverse-mode backprop but are not ordinary score-only inference. A pretrained external editor is allowed in the second regime; account for its inference cost and say if its offline training used backprop.

## Root cause: the comparison measures success but does not locate the write

1. **Credit bandwidth, not parameter count alone.** Locally, for an isotropic Gaussian direction `z` and gradient `g`, an antithetic loss difference estimates `g·z`. Re-expanding a single measurement gives `ĝ = (g·z)z`: unbiased in the linear limit, with covariance `||g||² I + ggᵀ` by the Gaussian fourth moment. Averaging `q` independent directions divides that variance by `q`; increasing rollouts for the *same direction* reduces reward noise, but does not supply more independent directions. The variance in Euclidean weight space grows with dimension, whereas the cost to the objective depends on where curvature lies: expected quadratic cost contains `||g||² tr(H)`, not just `D`. This explains why billions of mutable weights can coexist with useful small populations on aligned tasks. Neither the Hessian's effective rank nor a perturbation's matrix rank tells us how many directions a **stream** of novel tasks needs or how to find those directions.
2. **Preservation requires a separate signal.** A current-only score can't tell whether progress on B is caused by a clean write or by overwriting something A needed. Where B's gradient is in A's nullspace, random perturbations can create forgetting that exact descent would avoid. Where B's gradient points through particularly sharp old directions, the reverse can hold: [randomized shaping (2605.10658), Theorem 4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.10658v1) shows that **norm-matched local** ZO reduces expected quadratic old damage iff old curvature along the new gradient exceeds its directional mean. That's not a guarantee at matched B gain. The relevant object is a direction's **joint** effect on acquisition and retention, not a universal “ZO forgets more” claim.
3. **A fixed question set is itself an information bottleneck.** Each noiseless binary preference contributes at most one bit *conditional on the learner's prior observations and chosen query* (ties either discarded or encoded separately). A real-valued loss isn't “one bit”: with finite precision it can encode magnitude and a vector of per-question or per-token scores can provide many channels. With deterministic binary preferences, a million pairs can yield up to a million judge bits, but not necessarily independent or useful ones. Any two desired behaviors that induce identical judge transcripts for every permitted query are unidentifiable to the score-only learner, even with adaptive queries and infinite repeats. A benchmark can still identify a compositional rule if its questions separate the plausible alternatives; truly unseen arbitrary facts cannot be inferred by repeatedly judging unrelated outputs. Supplying a label, a teacher demonstration, or a diagnostic intervention changes the information available and should be counted, not called free.

**Working hypothesis:** amortize *where and how to write*, and use forward tests to identify and verify the write rather than reconstructing a dense update from terminal rewards. Measure **new-rule acquisition at fixed retention per generated token, FLOP, and byte of stored state**, not peak gate gain. This hypothesis would lose force if tuned dense ES matched structured or forward-observable methods on recurring, high-rank streams at matched total compute and identical information, or if a transferable write interface failed to beat a static basis after accounting for its training cost.

## CPU probe: isolate task rank, leakage, and the value of a scalar

Run `python tree/probes/model-forward-only/probe.py` (NumPy; ~1 second on CPU). A frozen ReLU feature layer and its trained linear head reduce to quadratic regression on one-hot questions in a question-aligned basis. The 256-dimensional shared head first fits A exactly (32 independent features), then learns B on disjoint features with 8, 32 or 128 equally curved directions and an independently sampled unit-norm target. B sees the same fixed questions during all updates. Dense scalar MeZO-style and sign-only antithetic search use the same perturbation stream; a full-batch exact-gradient step is the reference. Every ZO step costs **two full B evaluations** (each evaluation covers all B questions); no generation noise, batching, or ES parallelism is modeled.

All rows stop at the first step that halves B loss from 0.50 to at most 0.25; for old loss only, interpolate that last step offline to match exactly 0.25. Means and seed-bootstrap 95% intervals over 128 paired instances:

| B rank | Scalar steps | One-bit steps | Scalar old loss increase | One-bit old loss increase |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 8 | 7.18 [6.59, 7.79] | 9.57 [8.66, 10.62] | 0.702 [0.647, 0.757] | 1.401 [1.248, 1.581] |
| 32 | 24.02 [22.91, 25.21] | 38.05 [36.00, 40.23] | 0.226 [0.215, 0.238] | 0.368 [0.343, 0.394] |
| 128 | 90.71 [88.59, 93.02] | 147.88 [144.34, 151.79] | 0.062 [0.059, 0.065] | 0.093 [0.088, 0.098] |

Exact descent took one update with zero A damage. **Disconfirmation check:** zero the old coordinates of each random direction, keeping everything else (including B's perturbations and step sizes) equal. If A damage were necessary for that B gain, the projected arm would lose B progress; B took exactly the same number of steps on every seed and A damage fell to zero. The mask is an oracle; B's scores cannot recover it. The quadratic geometry and update rules establish the causal chain **in this probe**: B loss cannot see A coordinates, yet the dense estimator writes to them using B's score, moving A off its optimum. This is not confirmation of that mechanism in a transformer.

Rank 8 → 128 costs ~12.6× more scalar updates at fixed ambient dimension. Binary is ~1.63× slower at rank 128: it preserves direction sign but discards magnitude. In this noiseless Gaussian limit the signed measurement has expected alignment `√(2/π) g/||g||`, so a bit is useful; the experiment measures progress per comparison, **not** mutual information in a floating-point loss. The rank-aware step sizes shrink with rank, and dense old damage actually falls as B rank grows. Do not extrapolate the old-loss trend or sign/scalar ratio to tuned ES, shared features, noisy language rewards, alternative step schedules, or learned representations. The design tests identifiability and leakage in a deliberately solvable geometry, not generalization, quantized inference, or the relative returns of a million LLM rollouts.

Covariance-based capacity prediction lost to a constant and Gramian plasticity spread collapsed after retention matching; don't infer future plasticity from those summaries. Sparse-write certificates need to beat masked Jacobian SVD/OGD; coded merge audits need to beat BOCS and direct same-budget searches. ## Raw ideas for the reviewer

These are hypotheses, not verified novelty claims. Each cheapest test is a first distinguishing measurement, not a claim that a toy result will transfer. “Primitive” names a proposed reusable object or operation; “improvement” changes the efficiency of an existing method. Match total inference work (including the editor and instrumentation), judge/exemplar access, and persistent state unless the comparison explicitly varies one.

1. **Counterfactual write compiler — primitive (intervention → write).** Fork a success trace with oracle context and a failure trace without it. Transplant intermediate states, then have a larger editor pick the smallest *causally sufficient* intervention and compile it into a conditional low-rank write. **New vs WSP writes ICL / Self-WSP:** compile intervention effects across prompts, not residual shifts or raw tensor edits. **First test:** nonce arithmetic in a tiny transformer; hold out equivalent prompt formats, and compare activation-mean addition, scripted causal localization and matched random tensor edits. **Risk:** a single activation patch may not have a realizable weight equivalent.
2. **Credit-address book — primitive (portable supervision-to-write addresses).** Learn reusable keys mapping error signatures to small, conditional weight operators using forward edit outcomes; allow the larger editor to mint or redirect addresses when it demonstrates a new failure class. **New vs WIS-MeZO and learned weight ISA:** the object is a cross-task *credit routing map*, not an operator vocabulary or a task-conditioned sparse mask. **First test:** fit addresses on random permutation and composition tasks, hold out operator combinations; compare PCA and static learned edit bases at matched state. **Risk:** learns benchmarks' surface tags.
3. **Orthogonal question barcodes — primitive (multiplexed intervention score).** In a single perturbed model, independently gate several edits by question and use balanced codes across a batch to decode directional effects while holding token budget fixed. **New vs SPSA / BOCS:** question-conditional execution, not simultaneous global weight perturbations or sparse pairwise merge recovery. **First test:** two nearby edits affecting overlapping synthetic tasks; compare separately evaluated candidates and Hadamard global perturbations at equal tokens. **Risk:** gating helps by changing the model and loses the effects when gates are removed.
4. **Write-nullspace negotiation — primitive (two-party edit constraints).** Give a new-task proposer explicit reject/accept constraints from hidden old behaviors. Infer local separating directions from signed counterproposals rather than accessing old labels or gradients; refine the constraints after each accepted update. **New vs OGD / behavioral trust regions:** the protected subsystem sends forward counteroffers conditioned on the exact proposal, not a fixed Jacobian or pass/fail threshold. **First test:** give the probe a rotated hidden old subspace and a comparison-only old oracle; compare random projected ZO and behavior-gated ZO at identical old-query budgets, and benchmark oracle OGD. **Risk:** noisy accept bits may take more queries than retaining replay.
5. **Compositional A/B codebook — primitive (learnable hypothesis basis).** Treat a signed result as a constraint over alternative *program edits*. Pick paired candidates that split the current hypothesis posterior; learn new codewords when intersections consistently fail to explain outcomes. **New vs one-bit compressed sensing / sparse MEMIT syndromes:** code distinct executable joint rewrites and adapt the alphabet, rather than assume fact damage is sparse. **First test:** one million cheap pairs on rotated sparse and deliberately dense symbolic worlds; compare coordinate sign search, Thompson sampling and one-bit compressed sensing on the same library. **Risk:** behaviorally equivalent candidates and interaction effects collapse the codebook.
6. **Patch homomorphism — primitive (behavior/weight composition contract).** Find edits whose composition implements the composition of *new* rules. Use commutator tests on forward traces to separate commuting updates from order-sensitive ones and compile only the latter with joint operators. **New vs Task Arithmetic / LoRA merging / the PR #39 linker:** target a predictable homomorphism between behavior composition and weight edits, not merged endpoint scores. **First test:** learn permutations separately, predict unseen products including reversed order; compare TIES, scripted adapters and direct BOCS on equal audit counts. **Risk:** functional composition may require input-conditioned activation routing rather than a static sum.
7. **Local public proofs of correction — primitive (executable credit obligations).** A teacher expresses a task as independent intermediate assertions; blocks receive forward-testable witness failures, and the terminal A/B grader audits whether passing the assertions actually transfers. **New vs Forward-Forward / SOMA:** factor the *same* example's task semantics across shared layers with externally checkable obligations, rather than fixed goodness or separate corpora. **First test:** a toy virtual machine learned from I/O, with optional intermediate register checks; compare layer-local cross-entropy and supervised traces with equal labels/compute. **Risk:** teacher-provided decompositions solve the hard part and may not exist.
8. **Versioned inverse writes — primitive (behavior-conditional reversible update).** Store a compact inverse together with every accepted edit, then trigger a local inverse when old-context evidence contradicts the new rule, even after subsequent unrelated writes. **New vs task-vector negation / EWC:** inverse is tested after composition and versioned on the behavior it reverses, not subtraction of a static delta. **First test:** A→B→C→A synonym remapping with a forced B rollback; compare adapter deactivation, original-vector subtraction and model snapshot restore, equal bytes. **Risk:** keyed adapters may already solve it by construction.
9. **Acquisition-aware perturbation generators — primitive (activity → exploration distribution).** Failed and successful traces seed a small generator of edit distributions. It learns from pairs where the *same* external editor's suggested structure succeeds or fails in future contexts, not from the editor's natural-language confidence. **New vs learned optimizers / HiZOO:** emit testable counterfactual weight families conditioned on episode traces, with provenance masks controlling which observations may influence which write. **First test:** train on low-rank tasks, measure useful-proposal rate on held-out high-rank switching tasks versus a learned optimizer, CMA-ES and frozen-editor proposals at equal compute. **Risk:** turns dense offline backprop into a hidden subsidy.
10. **Calibrated disagreement teacher — primitive (diagnostic contrast generator).** The larger teacher does not propose any weights; it generates pairs of examples that distinguish the failure hypotheses attached to candidate edits, and an independent verifier labels them. Charge new diagnostics as supervision. **New vs active learning / iterative WSP:** choose examples by their ability to separate *edit-induced* functional signatures while simultaneously testing protected skills. **First test:** nonce translation whose original question set cannot distinguish grammar alternatives; compare random paraphrases and max-disagreement active learning. **Risk:** equivalent to ordinary active learning over a fixed committee.
11. **Layer-local eligibility escrow — primitive (persistent delayed-credit write token).** Before the judge fires, candidate layers issue compressed activation-linked tokens representing tentative writes; later outcomes redeem only the subset whose local counterfactual replay predicts the terminal result. **New vs e-prop/eligibility traces:** replay-verifiable tokens carry a reusable intervention and can be redeemed after intervening writes, not just weighted by a global reward. **First test:** delayed reward on a small stack-machine model with concurrent episodes, compare e-prop, SPSA and storing whole trajectories at equal bytes. **Risk:** stale tokens after representation drift.
12. **Representation checkpoint distillation without reverse mode — primitive (carryable write-space state).** Build a temporary feature for a novel skill; migrate it into a shared block by streaming feature cross-covariances and forward-intervention targets, then delete the temporary module. **New vs FoRo's recursive head / model merging:** transfer and verify *internal nonlinear features* into reused layers, not just a head or a fixed adapter bank. **First test:** teach two new operators to disjoint auxiliary modules and delete them after consolidation; compare recursive readout, activation regression and task-vector merge at the same persistent parameter cap. **Risk:** covariance fits the teacher manifold but not its causal function.
13. **Counterfactual latent routing annealer — primitive (edits that introduce a new routing distinction).** When two desired behaviors collide in the same representation, A/B test a reversible split of their shared feature before moving any output weights, then absorb the split into a fixed-width block. **New vs progressive networks / continual MoE:** promote a new routing feature only after a forward conflict intervention, and pay for it by reusing capacity. **First test:** alternate two tasks that agree on every training input except diagnostic pairs; compare standard MoE routing, a fixed capacity expansion and a random split. **Risk:** splits become task IDs and fail on unseen mixtures.
14. **Score-vector tomography — improvement (use outputs already computed).** Retain per-question or per-token score differences instead of their average, fit a low-rank matrix of behavioral changes online and test whether its rows support distinct edits. **New vs mean-reward ES / sparse MeZO:** spend existing judge resolution on separating behavioral gradients, not only reducing estimator variance. **First test:** the probe with noisy questions and known families; compare equal-budget stratified ES and multitask ZO with independent losses. **Risk:** correlated rows provide no additional useful rank; token scores may be unavailable.
15. **One-bit adaptive microscope — improvement (allocate repetitions and probes jointly).** Start a fixed question-set A/B campaign with shared decode randomness and few repeats across diverse directions, then assign repeats only to comparisons likely to flip a write decision. Keep an untouched test split and one frozen judge. **New vs racing / Dueling Bandits:** budget jointly for directional identification, judge reliability and old-task vetoes, not just identifying the winning arm. **First test:** run million-pair synthetic judges with controlled flip rates; compare uniform repeats, successive halving and an information-directed dueling-bandit control. **Risk:** circular calibration makes a cheap scheme confidently wrong.
16. **Zero-read data-transfer edits — primitive (fact-valued message becomes a weight operation).** Encode a stream of secret-seeded random associations as local vector targets transmitted by forward demonstrations; verify that an edit decodes facts on new prompts even if the editor sees no parameters, and that a later corrector can change one association without refreshing the rest. **New vs ROME / FoRo:** decoder addresses are learned and verifiably portable across learning episodes and different backbone bases, without layer-specific fact editing. **First test:** identical one-hot dictionaries under independently rotated bases; compare recursive least squares, MEMIT-style key/value updates and small lookup tables at equal bytes. **Risk:** the proposal is simply a more expensive associative memory.
17. **Writable counterfactual shadow model — primitive (distill the search process into the weights).** Run a detached micro-model that predicts the sign and leakage of full-model interventions from two sentinel passes, update it using pair outcomes, and distill its best *abstention/diagnosis behavior* into the full model's future edit proposals. **New vs surrogate-assisted ES / SelfWrite:** train and test whether the solver becomes a better prospective edit auditor across checkpoints, not whether a surrogate picks today’s winners. **First test:** hide synthetic interference geometries across checkpoints and compare a frozen surrogate, classifier uncertainty, and an independently updated editor. **Risk:** self-prediction overfits the editor's own proposal distribution.
18. **Streaming constraint exchange — primitive (continually replaceable sufficient statistics for safe writes).** When storage is full, use new conflicting tasks to challenge which compressed old constraints earn their slot; replace constraints only when an adversarially searched *held-out intervention* cannot distinguish old and new summaries. **New vs coresets / behavioral trust regions:** compress the ability to veto edits, not the ability to reproduce past logits. **First test:** a rotated version of the CPU probe with both sparse and dense interference; compare same-byte raw replay, dense random sketch with least squares, and masked-Jacobian SVD. **Risk:** the adversary and old-query budget can dominate; the ledger's dense-damage failure rules out assuming sparse repair.

## Ranked deeper nodes

1. **Can counterfactual traces compile into a reusable write that survives paraphrase and composition?** Candidate primitive: a causal trace-to-weight compiler. Start with nonce rules known to be absent from the base; ask whether a larger editor beats activation addition, same-budget random edits, and scripted localization. Highest upside because it replaces direction search with a structured measurement.
2. **Can old behaviors negotiate an edit constraint without replay or an old-model gradient?** Candidate primitive: comparison-only safe-write interface. Hide a rotated retention subspace and measure how many vetoes are needed to beat both replay and a same-budget random constraint; this turns the probe's oracle into a learnable interface.
3. **Can a learned credit-address book grow with genuinely new skills rather than reusing a static low-rank basis?** Candidate primitive: allocatable, portable write addresses. Test on successive held-out compositions and independent rules; a static learned basis at equal state and compute is the control.
4. **Can intervention-coded questions supply many separable credit channels in the same forward batch?** Candidate primitive: multiplexed experimental access to a shared model. Test dense interference as well as the favorable case and charge all extra tokens; decisive if it improves directions identified per token without changing what the final learned model can run.

The common decisive test is a sealed stream with new, secret-seeded facts **and** new compositional rules, periodic returns to old tasks, untouched evaluation paraphrases/compositions, and no retrieved answer or demonstration in the evaluation context. Include the unmodified base, retrieval/readout and WSP baselines where applicable, tuned ES/MeZO, and backprop with comparable replay/state budgets. Report both accuracy gained and old capabilities lost at matched plasticity, plus enough cost accounting to tell whether more rollouts simply bought a more expensive optimizer.
