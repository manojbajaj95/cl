# Effect-level loan commits

**Status:** Ideas only; no implementation, training runs, or GPU work. **Parent:** [Shared-part loan commits](loan-commits.md) ([PR #241](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/241)), cards 02 and 16. **Review seed:** [Flood triage, row 354](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/2cc957179840ef132915bc0d69e104feabc3c494/reviews/flood-triage.md#L354) advances these score-3 ideas: commit a shared output effect through the currently active ReLUs even if the two views need different raw directions; make a required ordinary old-task update repay the first lender and carry the pending write to the next, with no inserted rescue batch. **Read first:** [Idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/2cc957179840ef132915bc0d69e104feabc3c494/reviews/idea-ledger.md).

## Agree on the effect, not the actuator; let old work carry it

**Verdict: two candidate operations, not yet one mechanism.** The first intersects signed, jointly achievable output changes and re-realizes a useful common change in the active units of the pending model. The second uses the actual old-task update as the evidence transport, not a separate update run at the same time. The most interesting joint claim is that mandatory old work can make a formerly unreachable agreement writable, without destroying the evidence needed to confirm it. That claim must beat output fitting and ordinary replay with a waiting buffer used together.

**Nearest comparisons (2026-11-25).** An abstract-level arXiv/HF Papers check used the phrases “model editing output constraints activation regions ReLU continual learning,” “function space model merging activation alignment,” and “experience replay gradient interference minibatch selection continual learning.” [OGD][ogd] protects old outputs by projected gradients, [ROME][rome] edits a localized feed-forward map, and [Functional Dual Anchors][fda] represent task shifts with inputs whose gradients align with task vectors. [Activation-Informed Merging][aim] uses activations to prioritize weights when merging checkpoints. Those comparisons make “use activations,” “edit outputs,” and “work in function space” insufficient novelty claims. [MER][mer] trains for gradient transfer, [MIR][mir] chooses past samples based on predicted interference, and [Replay Scheduling][rs] decides when and what old work to replay. Choosing a helpful old batch is not itself new. The narrower bets are corroboration-dependent realization in a *live* active cell, and transporting still-actionable evidence through an update whose presence and old-task purpose do not depend on the pending write. The ledger's effect-class distillation (PR #13) lost to equal-budget execution-reward replay; this is not a relaunch of that proposal.

### First-test contract

- Freeze the legal examples, optimizer and step options, ordinary-work schedule, expiry, read permissions, and effect chart before blind jobs. For each independently arriving view, decode a set of **jointly** achievable signed changes on the same declared B outputs (e.g., a vector of true-vs-rival logit gaps on common semantic queries, at a frozen scale). Preserve magnitudes and correlations; a common unsigned span or coordinate-wise overlap is not consent. Agreement must be useful beyond those queries, on held-out B after repayment and example retirement.
- At confirmation, fit a physically legal update to the pending model's **current** activation cell for the declared reads. With a frozen feature body or one writable affine layer and fixed gates, the selected outputs are affine in the writable parameters; use exact constrained fitting in the toy case. With several writable layers, fixed gates do not make the parameter map affine; verify the finite update and exposed path, not just a Jacobian prediction. If the only useful realization needs an inactive unit, a gate crossing, or an old-gap violation, abstain and repay. A gate crossed by earlier mandatory old work is allowed if its own old reads were certified; it is not a license to cross a gate during the commit.
- A required old-task batch has a predeclared old objective and due slot. The no-write arm runs it too, with the same event and optimizer rule. If choosing among truthful due items is legal, freeze the menu and selection permissions and charge scans; also test a singleton menu. No synthetic targets, reordering a fixed queue, extra passes, rescue batches, manual slot writes, or expiry resets. At the boundary, repay A₁ to its same-phase no-write read gaps, certify both lender read sets together, and only then let A₂ spend. Require provisional service **and** preservation of the lawful confirmation distinctions, not just equality of one current output. If a required batch erases evidence or cannot safely transfer it, do the old work and default by a lawful return; a host without that safe default is inadmissible.
- Measure ordinary old tasks A₁/A₂ and previously acquired skills C on separately held-out, starting-margin-matched panels (including near-boundary items). Track itemwise gaps and success at every exposed read, during confirmation/default, and after matched unrelated updates at returns 1, 8 and 64. Separate old gain on due items from generalization to old held-out tasks; require repayment at resolution, no double debit, and acquisition-anchored floors throughout. Check interpolants only where reads are continuously exposed; do not infer path safety from endpoints.
- Shared controls: the parent's raw common-direction rule, AND-mask, PCGrad, averaging, OGD, and exact output fitting with the same constraints; ordinary-map handoff, expiry at first repayment, staggered loans, and a separately charged rescue step. The strongest stack gets the same legitimate provisional write, due old update, bounded waiting buffer, safety guard, and exact effect-intersection fit in the post-update cell. Match peak bytes (including slots and evidence), examples, probes, old gain, prequential B service, confirmation latency, coverage, and false confirmations. Give the exact solver the same observations, not oracle view menus; report a separate full-information ceiling. Remove or swap only the carrier on matched states to distinguish transport from an update that simply improves B on its own. A tie with the stack is a null.

### W11 — two arithmetic checks, not a learning result

**Different actuators, same effect.** Let a scalar readout have frozen ReLU features. Write `e_i` for a coordinate unit vector. On a canonical B query, the first and second view have active feature rows `e_1` and `e_2`; their legal view-specific write menus are `t e_1` and `t e_2`, respectively, for `0 ≤ t ≤ 1`. The raw menus intersect only at zero but both output menus are `[0,1]`. In the pending model the B row is `e_3`, and held old A and C rows are `e_1+e_2` and `e_4`. The legal head commit `e_3` realizes +1 on B and 0 on each old row; averaging the two view writes at `t=1` gives 0 on B and +1 on A. If the current B row is zero, or an old row is also `e_3` and must be unchanged, the same consensus has no admissible nonzero realization. This is an exact frozen-feature solve; a buffer and the same solver tie when given the same evidence.

**One useful old step, no rescue.** Let two head weights be `(p,q)`, initially `(0,0)` in the no-write arm and `(1,0)` after a provisional write. At the fixed old-work slot, both arms take the same squared-loss SGD rule on the old feature row `(1,1)`, target −2, step size 1/2, loss `(p+q+2)^2/2`. The no-write arm lands at `(-1,-1)` and the live arm at `(-1/2,-3/2)`. The old loss improves from 2 and 9/2, respectively, to 0; the relative displacement becomes `(1/2,-1/2)`. A₁'s gap `4-(p+q)` goes from one below its paired gap to equal. The provisional B contrast `p-q` remains 1 in the live arm and 0 in the no-write arm. At the overlap, let the A₂ gap be `3-max(-q-3/2,0)`; both arms read 3, also along the paired straight-line prefixes, and an independent C gap stays 3. At the next scheduled A₂ exposure with gap `3-max(-q-5/4,0)`, the same states yield 11/4 versus 3 (floor 2), with A₁ fully repaid. Now the receiver can read the carrier from `-2(q+1)`, with no intervening weight update. As an erasure control, a due two-item identity-feature regression step of size 1 on summed half-squared loss, with targets `(-1,-1)`, lands both arms at `(-1,-1)`: it restores A₁ but loses B. The chart, read schedule, and single evidence bit are stipulated here; this checks neither a general evidence decoder nor deadline repayment, which the blind tests must establish.

## Idea cards

### 01. Commit the effect on a third actuator — seed / primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Read support in output space from the two views, then choose only an active realization in the pending model, possibly on a unit neither view could use. **Novelty / control.** The parent's common raw direction and AND-mask can reject view directions with disjoint support; OGD and exact fitting already find local protected realizations. The candidate is corroboration gating a *live* write, not the solver. **Cheapest first test.** Replace W11's supplied amplitudes with independent partial observations of blind signed amplitudes, and include a zero B row and a B/A collision. Run the exact buffered fit. **Old-task retention.** Score A/C on accepted and refused jobs immediately and after a shared later step.

### 02. Require an agreement no marginal could fake — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Carry dependencies between outputs as part of a view's effect menu and search the joint intersection; do not match action deltas one by one. **Novelty / control.** Set-membership fitting already intersects correlated sets; the candidate is a compact live code whose contrast survives mandatory old work better than a byte-matched description of the set. **Cheapest first test.** Compare full joint fitting to marginal intersection on the menus `{(0,0),(1,1)}` and `{(0,1),(1,0)}`, then add a genuinely shared pair. **Old-task retention.** Check itemwise A/C gaps when false marginal agreement would otherwise trigger a write.

### 03. Confirm the decision, forbid any logit shortcut — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Let views endorse the same signed pairwise gap changes while nuisance logit shifts vary. Search for a representative that realizes the agreed contrast and does not alter any unsupported declared contrast. **Novelty / control.** Softmax shift invariance and the parent's shared decisions already give the quotient; the proposed operation enforces what remains *unchanged* after selecting a live representative. Compare exact contrast fitting with identical invariances. **Cheapest first test.** Three-class head with offset agreement but a contradictory target-rival sign; reject that pair and a norm-matched wrong-label view. **Old-task retention.** Audit all old true-vs-rival gaps, including swaps among nonwinning labels revealed by a subsequent batch.

### 04. Spend three active units to cancel old effects — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Fit a distributed current-cell realization when each usable unit alone changes an old gap; use paired cancellations across active units rather than waiting for an idle unit. **Novelty / control.** OGD and exact output-nullspace fitting already compute cancellations. The question is whether an independently confirmable effect can be routed to a *different* cancellation circuit after an ordinary update without saving the original circuit. **Cheapest first test.** Use a three-column head with two independent old constraints; rotate the columns with an ordinary map and enumerate exact certificates, including rank loss. **Old-task retention.** Check both old constraints, unsupervised old neighbors, and the same constraints after one old update breaks a cancellation.

### 05. Refuse a commit whose only realization touches a kink — improvement
**Mechanism.** Commit only at a positive gate margin that can withstand the next permitted numerical perturbation; report robustness separately from mere reachability. **Novelty / control.** [Reluplex][reluplex] verifies ReLU properties, and constrained optimization can impose slack. This is a guard around card 01, not a new operation. **Cheapest first test.** Sweep a one-unit preactivation from safely positive to zero and compare exact guarded fit, endpoint-only fit, and a deliberately blocked instance. **Old-task retention.** Probe the boundary-near A/C items before and after perturbing a successful and a refused commit.

### 06. Fit a shared effect just once across all candidate cells — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** If the public reads cannot distinguish current gate states, find one legal update that realizes the same effect in every compatible cell, or abstain. **Novelty / control.** Robust optimization already handles hidden state; the stricter promise here is simultaneous preservation of the consented B effect as well as old gaps. Compare an exact worst-case fit with a charged gate probe. **Cheapest first test.** Two masks give identical reads but flip which nuisance direction is safe; include one case with a universal actuator and one requiring the probe. **Old-task retention.** Exercise each hidden cell, including the one an optimistic point estimate would harm.

### 07. Confirm a sector rather than a point — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Keep a view-supported cone clipped by signed lower and upper effect bounds, then commit the largest useful shared fraction that fits the current cell, without renormalizing weak evidence into a full write. **Novelty / control.** Constrained trust regions and the parent's partial commit already scale updates. The candidate is a reusable certificate of consent over magnitudes after the actuator changes. **Cheapest first test.** Two partially overlapping effect intervals and a bounded receiver; sweep into zero-only overlap, compare buffered interval fitting, and test whether the weak commit still generalizes on B. **Old-task retention.** Measure near-boundary A/C at maximum legal scale and after the clipped remainder is repaid.

### 08. Remove a borrowing-induced false agreement — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Decode the second view's endorsement from a fixed chart and its observed target, not from the live residual, so a provisional prediction cannot masquerade as independent support. Under a carrier swap, the second view's menu must stay fixed even if the intersection changes. **Novelty / control.** A fixed teacher already removes moving references. The question is whether a target-based chart can separate legitimate corroboration from self-confirmation without retaining a per-job pre-write prediction. **Cheapest first test.** Hold a confirming example fixed, swap two equally useful provisional carriers, and include a view whose apparent sign flips only because the pending read moved. Compare a buffered pre-write prediction. **Old-task retention.** A/C on both swaps and on the default after apparent agreement.

### 09. Certify effect equality on a patch, not just anchors — improvement
**Mechanism.** In a declared polytope with verified fixed masks and a writable head, specify agreement at vertices so the effect is equal on the entire patch; hold out nearby cells to measure scope. **Novelty / control.** Affine interpolation and ReLU verification already give the certificate; the gain would be a cheaper confirmation panel, not a new learner. **Cheapest first test.** Triangle with matching center reads but divergent corners; compare exact vertex and sampled-anchor fitting. **Old-task retention.** Check old vertices and interior reads, plus old examples on an adjacent uncertified cell.

### 10. Carry a test that can still tell two effects apart — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Admit a first view only if a lawful future corroborator could distinguish the surviving candidate commitments after the known mandatory update. **Novelty / control.** Observability and the parent's consequence handoff already demand preserved distinctions; this version tests them in reachable *effect* space, not by requiring the full trace to survive. Compare a minimal buffered sufficient statistic under the same read budget. **Cheapest first test.** One scalar provisional service, two opposite valid future effect choices; map different raw traces to the same post-update state in the destructive control. **Old-task retention.** Compare A/C under refusal, under a lawful admission, and at the ensuing required step.

### 11. Make a due old update the only handoff step — seed / primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use the required optimizer step itself to repay A₁ and move the pending evidence into A₂'s eligible read region, without a separate weight or slot write. **Novelty / control.** Replay and the parent's ordinary-map handoff already recover old service and move evidence respectively; the candidate is one otherwise-required update doing both. Compare mandatory replay plus a waiting buffer and a separately paid rescue. **Cheapest first test.** Extend W11 with a singleton due menu and one matched-old-gain eraser; compare its exact counterfactual endpoint to the live one with no extra call, then test late confirmation. **Old-task retention.** A₁ held-out recovery, A₂ first exposure, C and no double debit, not just loss on the training batch.

### 12. Let an old no-op carry only the live difference — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Schedule an independently required maintenance batch whose optimizer update is zero on the reference orbit but nonzero on a pending departure, redirecting only the latter. **Novelty / control.** Ordinary SGD contracts old residuals and an already-fit batch has zero gradient; the candidate uses that difference as a selective courier while leaving the reference service untouched. Compare running the batch with a waiting buffer and its same-cost replay alternative. **Cheapest first test.** Use a fitted singleton in W11 with target 0, alternate it with a non-fitted due item, and compare a batch that annihilates the difference. **Old-task retention.** Verify old outputs on both arms and C after default, including old items absent from the fitted batch.

### 13. Make collateral old learning do the routing — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** If two truthful due minibatches have the same measured old gain, favor the one whose natural cross-feature update preserves the needed distinction; refuse if neither does. **Novelty / control.** MIR and replay scheduling already select samples and timing. Require preservation measured by legitimate later confirmations, beyond instantaneous loss or gradient alignment, and compare those selectors on the same due menu. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two equal-gain items, including a tie on gradient angle with different carrier transport; include a singleton policy control. **Old-task retention.** Check matched held-out gain per lender and C, not just gains on the selected items.

### 14. Route without observing who's borrowing — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Find a fixed old update rule that transports multiple distinct blind first views without querying the pending job or altering the due example. **Novelty / control.** MER learns generic future gradient alignment; the candidate needs a single map to preserve several separable consent classes through a debt-free overlap. Compare a frozen MER-style map and a byte-matched waiting buffer. **Cheapest first test.** Three blinded amplitudes, one fixed due batch, and a many-to-one negative map that preserves average provisional reward. **Old-task retention.** Measure old gain and A/C floors for every latent job including no write.

### 15. Exchange a trace carried by the actual optimizer slot — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Permit the due batch to move usable evidence from weights into a pre-existing momentum component only if the next ordinary forward/update sequence can decode it without a direct slot read or edit. **Novelty / control.** Fast weights and optimizer state already carry history. The candidate is a legally readable, debt-free transfer without reserving a new trace; charge the slot and compare a byte-matched buffer. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate scalar momentum SGD for two equal-weight post-batch states with different slots; try a lawfully distinguishing next event and a forced slot reset. **Old-task retention.** Audit A/C before and after the reveal or reset; reject delayed slot-induced debt.

### 16. Make the learning rate an old-task mandate, not a hidden message — improvement
**Mechanism.** For a public old-task step-size policy, derive the range that repays A₁ and preserves confirmation distinctions; only test transport at rates independently admissible for old work. **Novelty / control.** Line search and robust reachability already bound updates. The result is a falsifiable transport window, not a new learning rate rule. **Cheapest first test.** Sweep fixed rates around W11's 1/2, blinding the optimizer to the job, then compare to a fixed-rate buffer. **Old-task retention.** Report gain, debt and A/C floors for the full sweep, including overshoot and under-repayment.

### 17. Let the old batch open the receiver's ReLU — primitive candidate (merge)
**Mechanism.** A due old update changes which units are active, and transports enough corroboration to realize the shared effect in the new cell without a rescue call. The commit itself stays in that new cell. **Novelty / control.** Replay can change gates and exact fitting exploits active units already. A joint win needs the particular paid step to preserve evidence and open actuation that neither a no-gate-change carrier nor the equally equipped buffer stack can use as well. **Cheapest first test.** Factorial: receiver opens or stays closed, carrier survives or is erased, including buffered fits in all four cells of the experiment. **Old-task retention.** Check the gate crossing on every exposed old read and repayment after both successful and blocked commits.

### 18. Hand off through the old step's null direction, not its gradient — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Put first-view distinctions in components the due batch updates least, but allow the batch to remove the part that borrowed A₁'s slack. Demand that the surviving components support a different A₂ read, rather than staying merely dormant. **Novelty / control.** OGD/nullspaces and replay already separate directions; the shared novelty claim is a component serving as a successor's live confirmation evidence, not an invariant. **Cheapest first test.** Sweep the rank of a due least-squares batch while holding old gain fixed, comparing exact projected writes and a waiting buffer. **Old-task retention.** Check old gaps on retained and erased modes; stress a later old gradient transverse to the survivor.

### 19. Queue no rescue even when two due updates swap order — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** With a legal order ambiguity, let either first required batch pay A₁ before the same pending job can reach A₂; certify each order or expose mandatory default. **Novelty / control.** Replay scheduling and the parent's boundary confluence already handle order. The candidate is an evidence representation transportable by either *actual* old update, with no extra scheduling call. **Cheapest first test.** Two noncommuting old SGD maps, two due orders, and an order-dependent eraser; compare a lawful scheduler and a waiting buffer. **Old-task retention.** Score A₁/A₂/C for both orders at each exposure, even if only one order can confirm.

### 20. Pay for two jobs with one old step, without mixing consent — primitive candidate (merge)
**Mechanism.** If the host can already serve two pending jobs within one lender's slack, use the same required batch to transport two separable effect menus through the overlap; do not turn a match for one into consent for the other. **Novelty / control.** Batched replay and multi-edit fitting already amortize updates. The proposed shared transport must beat serial loans and a two-record buffer at identical total old work and peak bytes. **Cheapest first test.** Two blind effects with shared nuisance coordinates; exchange corroborators and include a rank-one receiver that can forward only one job. **Old-task retention.** Check aggregate and itemwise debt and A/C before clearing either job, after one defaults, and after both retire.

### 21. Distinguish a transported receipt from fresh old-task teaching — improvement
**Mechanism.** Cross two matched carriers with the same due batch and independently withheld corroborators; attribute later B only to the part that follows carrier identity rather than old-task content. **Novelty / control.** This is a counterfactual ablation of the parent's carrier claim, not a new update mechanism. Compare no departure, swapped departure and exact buffered evidence. **Cheapest first test.** Include a due batch that teaches B directly and a matched batch that only rotates the pending contrast. **Old-task retention.** Require matched A/C exposure and old gain across swaps before interpreting B differences.

### 22. Seal the effect before the next mandatory old batch can undo it — primitive candidate (merge)
**Mechanism.** Among current-cell realizations of the confirmed effect, choose one whose effect survives the next admissible ordinary old update while discarded alternatives stay non-actionable. **Novelty / control.** The parent tests future-error residue; exact multi-step fitting can already select stable representatives. The candidate has to exploit a carrier-dependent choice of stable actuator rather than mere forecast-weighted write selection, which already failed its ledger gate. **Cheapest first test.** Two currently equal fits with different effects after a held-out due batch, plus a case where all fits collapse; compare exact buffered fitting with the same public future menu. **Old-task retention.** Score A/C at both commits, after the unseen due batch, and through a subsequent unrelated loan.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Can old work make an agreed effect writable without destroying what made it agreed?** Run card 17's factorial with the two seeds (01 and 11) separately and stacked, including the exact buffered fit *after the same required batch*. Require a joint advantage at matched service, old gain, budgets, coverage and false confirmation. If an opened cell explains the result by itself, keep the ideas separate.
2. **What does the live state preserve that a sufficient statistic would not?** Use 02, 08, 10 and 14 on blinded first-view pairs with equal provisional service but different valid corroborators. Swap or cut the carrier, compare the minimum buffered statistic, and require useful held-out B after full repayment. If only more bytes distinguish the treatments, report the byte tradeoff, not a new kind of evidence.
3. **How often is any agreed effect realizable under real read obligations?** Use 04–07 and 09 to sweep active rank, old-gap headroom and hidden gate ambiguity. Separate genuine inter-view disagreement, current-cell infeasibility, and failed generalization. Report refusals and A/C floors as well as successes; a solver that works only by selecting easy high-margin jobs is not a win.
4. **Does the handoff hold up under mandatory work it did not choose?** Use 11, 13, 15, 19 and 22 with singleton/flexible due menus, erasers, boundary arrivals, and later mandatory updates. Track subgroup old gain, original expiry, and restoration even when default is required. If only a handpicked batch or a stale slot does the routing, the supposed no-rescue mechanism has not generalized.
