# Synchronizing training batches

**Status:** Ideas only; no training runs, code, or GPU work. **Parent:** [Fixed-budget repeated handoffs](fixed-budget-handoffs.md) ([PR #203][parent]), cards 06 and 19. **Review seed:** [Flood triage, row 312](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/64c7d14e06b652ec2de8fff3cf7f26af4544db4f/reviews/flood-triage.md#L312) scores both 3: merge an alias class at the witness interface by ordinary C training, and restore witness service after unlearning without restoring access to erased data. **Read first:** [Idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/64c7d14e06b652ec2de8fff3cf7f26af4544db4f/reviews/idea-ledger.md).

## Can an ordinary course do the work of a reset word?

**Verdict: two candidate primitives, neither established.** A short open-loop curriculum is interesting if it uniformly restores a frozen witness, with one constant binding and no extra acquisition cost that makes it worse than replay or per-state repair. A privacy-preserving recovery is interesting if it delivers a service that deletion alone doesn't, without increasing access to deleted records. The second claim does not follow from the first: agreement on the witness can coexist with identity leakage elsewhere. Conversely an erasure-safe binding need not require any special batch order.

The nearest art is substantial. [Synchronizing words][sync] merge states under a fixed word; [iterative machine teaching][teaching] and [multi-learner teaching][multi] already choose examples to shape multiple learners; [shuffling SGD][shuffle] makes batch order consequential; common-minimizer contraction is not a discovery. [#185][order] is the order-only key control, [#196][classes] the direct observation-class map, and [#202][cycles] already studies useful behavior of switched training. The distinct bet is **a specified and usable witness read**, uniform over a prospectively declared alias class, using only truthful successor training and one legal binding. On privacy, [SISA][sisa] and [certified removal][guo] are baselines, not evidence that a recovered service is safe; [benign relearning][jog] and [recoverability of private information][recoverability] motivate testing beyond the first post-deletion snapshot. A quick HF Papers search surfaced these teaching, shuffling and relearning neighbors; it did not establish novelty. If multi-learner teaching or an exact search of the abstract batch automaton ties, credit the existing method and keep only the CL feasibility result.

## Contract for the first tests

- **Setup.** A = original tasks; B = prior useful admissions; C = installed and incoming successor skills. Freeze the witness, its required read or tolerance, legal bindings, A/B floors, protected C domains and incoming C's utility floor before any spend. Declare lawful observations `O`, the reachable full learner states (weights, optimizer slots and any data cursor), and the ordinary batch pool and training rule first. One word per `O`-class, with no within-word observation or branching, and one binding for the whole class. An exhaustive tiny menu can certify all its members; a fitted finite abstraction cannot certify untested members without a sound lifting check.
- **Success and cost.** For every reachable state in the class, the word must make the predeclared witness read agree (within the declared numerical tolerance) and the shared binding must actually launch the frozen witness. An agreeing wrong read, a new witness trained to agree, or a binding selected after inspecting individual states is a failure. Labels are the real C labels (or the ordinary C self-supervised objective); no witness examples, pseudo-targets, identity tags, edited labels, resets or hidden teacher at deployment. Planning can use the public frozen interface and a declared model of the state set, but charge the search, candidate descriptions, proofs, side information and any acquisition premium. Freeze the pool before witness-based word selection and check it on held-out spends; a curriculum carrying privileged pre-erasure information is not ordinary C data. Match step/example counts, optimizer, pool, persistent bytes and peak working space; report C utility at every prefix and A/B plus every installed C after the binding and a normal continuation. The repeated-handoff claim additionally requires at least three spends at the same global cap.
- **Controls in every comparison.** Use plain C replay/reshuffling and a random-pool null with matching C utility, plus best same-budget C-loss teaching. Also report the direct class map, exact reset-word search on the tiny abstract automaton (verify on concrete states), identifying-then-recovering with a paid probe and an oracle label, and direct reset. Projected GD and the parent's one-step projected Newton on the **witness loss** are privileged controls: if they tie, the only surviving advantage can be the absence of their witness loss, target or state access, or a measured budget advantage. Include an alias class no legal word can merge. The cards add controls to these defaults; a diagnostic or a tied improvement is not a pilot nomination.
- **Unlearning fork.** Compare a non-deleted control, deletion only, retraining from scratch **without** the target records (with and without recovery), SISA (with and without recovery), and rollback plus replay. Include a per-spend map carrying identity and a witness whose legitimate read itself reconstructs erased data as positive controls for failure. On the same held-out deletion cohorts and threat model, measure membership inference (advantage and TPR at a fixed low FPR) and reconstruction success (exact match and a predeclared task-appropriate near-match). Match access, side information, query and continuation budgets against the immediate post-erasure baseline, including for attacks refit after recovery. Audit published words, certificates, bindings, logs and observable prefixes as well as final outputs or weights when released. Require upper confidence bounds on the paired attack increases below declared tolerances, not a nonsignificant difference; show comparisons to retraining as well, since a leaky post-erasure baseline is not acceptable evidence of removal. A finite audit is not a certificate. When a certified guarantee is claimed, state its reference distribution, release boundary and composition assumptions. A/B/C retention excludes the records and behaviors explicitly covered by the erasure request, not the rest of the task that contained them.

## An exact sanity check, not evidence of usable recovery

For squared-loss linear updates, let `P_b = I − η_b X_bᵀX_b`, with the loss normalized so its Hessian is `X_bᵀX_b`. On an alias difference subspace `D`, exact agreement at a linear read `rᵀ` means `rᵀ P_last … P_first D = 0`. Labels cancel in the difference but not in the actual endpoint read. Invertible updates cannot merge full states or wipe a full-dimensional alias class at a nonzero linear read; they **can** align a lower-dimensional class with the read's kernel. A reachable difference fixed by every legal batch and seen by the witness is a no-word certificate.

Take two truthful singleton squared losses with not-necessarily-zero labels from a fixed linear C teacher, inputs `x = (1,0)`, `y = (1,1)` and the **same** step size `1/4`. On an alias difference `d = (1,1)`, `x→y` sends it to `(5/16,9/16)`, while `y→x` sends it to `(3/8,1/2)`. At the fixed read `r = (9,−5)`, the differences are respectively `0` and `7/8`; singles leave `7/4` and `2`, and repeats leave `1/16` and `1`. The states still differ after the merging word. A third untouched coordinate can carry a protected old read for this sanity check, but that does not demonstrate retention on a shared representation. Neither the chosen read nor the agreement proves a legal binding, held-out synchronization, or a privacy gain.

## Idea cards

### 01. One truthful word for a whole alias class — primitive candidate (review seed)
**Mechanism.** Enumerate short words of ordinary C batches and publish a single open-loop word plus a fixed legal binding for all aliased spends. **Novelty / control.** A [reset word][sync] for a *read*, not all of state; compare multi-learner teaching, exact abstract search and equal-step plain C. **Cheapest first test.** Four spent states of a two-layer ReLU learner, four batches, length up to four; score shortest valid word, and include a class with a frozen witness-visible difference. **Old-task retention.** A/B and all installed C after launch, incoming C at every prefix.

### 02. Use batch order to rotate a difference out of view — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use overlapping, individually invertible batch updates to move alias differences into the witness kernel without collapsing them. **Novelty / control.** Noncommuting products and [shuffling SGD][shuffle] are known; unlike an [order-only key][order], the word removes read dependence on history rather than encoding a new key. Compare each permutation of the same multiset, singleton repeats and projected C-GD. **Cheapest first test.** Extend the two-coordinate check to a planted and a held-out witness drawn before defining the word; test whether any order works beyond the planted alignment. **Old-task retention.** Use old reads sharing both updated coordinates; reject if the word only works with untouched old features.

### 03. Let one normal continuation finish the merge — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Bring the alias class into a set whose witness read is guaranteed to agree after the next scheduled C batch, though the preparation word alone cannot deliver agreement. Count the continuation as part of the word and wait to launch until then. **Novelty / control.** Preimages of target sets in reachability are standard; test whether a *committed external continuation* makes an otherwise unaffordable handoff feasible. Compare best immediate repair and postponing repair, both given the same deadline and batches. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a two-phase schedule with one forced final batch and two candidate preparation batches; change that final batch as a negative control. **Old-task retention.** A/B and installed C both while waiting and at launch.

### 04. Merge through a ReLU gate, then reopen the capacity — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** The first C batch drives aliased states into the same inactive region for a nuisance path; a second trains a shared active path for service without reactivating the nuisance on the promised domain. **Novelty / control.** ReLU dead regions and the switched branches of [#202][cycles] are known; the claim is a legal shared route back to useful witness and C outputs, not zeroing a read. Compare one large C step, equal multiset shuffles, and a direct unit reset. **Cheapest first test.** Two gates, four alias states, sweep across the kink and include an unmergeable sign pattern. **Old-task retention.** Check rare A/B/C inputs that also used the gated path.

### 05. Cut through optimizer aliases without a slot reset — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Start from equal weights but different hidden moments. Sequence ordinary batches so the coupled weight/slot trajectory puts witness-visible differences into modes the witness and a declared continuation cannot see. **Novelty / control.** Momentum decay and the slot splices in [#178][slots] are known; the novelty would be a short truthful word that beats passive decay with no direct slot writes. Compare plain heavy-ball replay, explicit slot reset, and an oracle slot-specific step. **Cheapest first test.** Two weights, three moment states and a frozen learning rate; enumerate words and append two held-out legal batches. **Old-task retention.** Compare all A/B/C outputs at launch and after each appended batch.

### 06. Compose words by their input and output classes — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Give each word a contract over a reachable input set and the smaller output set it delivers; reuse it as a step in later synthesis only when the next word accepts the entire output set, including moments. **Novelty / control.** [Assume-guarantee contracts][pacti] and automaton word concatenation are known; the reusable object is a typed sequence of truthful training steps with preserved skill domains. Compare monolithic exact search and caching full per-class words at equal bytes. **Cheapest first test.** Two overlapping spend menus; include an output set that agrees at the read but violates the next contract. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C at the composition boundary and at launch.

### 07. Certify a word across unenumerated spend magnitudes — improvement
**Mechanism.** Propagate an interval or zonotope of hidden weights and optimizer slots through the word and bound the witness error and protected outputs; refuse where the abstraction cannot certify. **Novelty / control.** [Abstract interpretation of neural nets][deeppoly] is known; the improvement is one bound for a training trajectory rather than an inference network alone. Compare interval boxes, sampled pairs and exact piecewise-linear enumeration where tractable. **Cheapest first test.** A one-interval ReLU spend with one interior branch missed by endpoints. **Old-task retention.** Bound A/B and installed C on their full declared domains, including the missed branch.

### 08. Transfer the word as a relation between batches, not example IDs — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Publish a grammar such as “first a truthful batch in one declared feature region, then one in another.” Enumerate all instantiations when checking agreement so fresh independent C streams can execute the word without the original pool. **Novelty / control.** Machine teaching and curriculum transfer already reuse example selections; the added claim is uniform witness service under substitutions selected independently of spend history. Compare cached examples, C-loss-only batch selection and relearning the word per pool. **Cheapest first test.** Two disjoint C pools with the same declared feature regions; include one out-of-region replacement. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C under every accepted substitution, not just the best draw.

### 09. Allow a skipped delivery without branching on the learner — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Synthesize a short word such that deleting any one delivery in a declared flaky prefix still allows a fixed suffix to close the same read and use the same binding. **Novelty / control.** Fault-tolerant open-loop control is known; the claim is tolerance using only useful C batches and no retraining on a hidden history. Compare repetition, a coded fixed schedule, and no-fault optimal words padded to equal delivered budgets. **Cheapest first test.** Four batches and one skipped position; enumerate faults and include a critical-delivery counterexample. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C and incoming C utility for every fault, including the no-fault run.

### 10. Spend and renew one word slot three times — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Reuse a fixed-size word and binding slot once each successor acquisition has made the previous binding dispensable; require each next truthful pool to carry the next repair. **Novelty / control.** Inherits the parent's liveness and [PackNet][packnet]-style reuse; the distinct claim is that ordered training, not a spare adapter, makes the fixed budget repeatably feasible. Compare retiring into a capped adapter bank and equal-work ordinary C streams without feature sharing. **Cheapest first test.** Three spends with two truly aliased states per spend and one deliberately unshared C fork. **Old-task retention.** Re-run all earlier A/B/C at each spend, including consumers that would still read the retired slot.

### 11. Synchronize two witnesses without buying two words — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Find a word whose image lies in the intersection of two witness-equality sets, with separately fixed bindings and no need to inspect which read diverged. **Novelty / control.** Simultaneous control and multi-objective teaching give the ingredients; a gain needs a shared update that neither optimal single-witness word can replace. Compare both concatenation orders, the two independent class maps and exact joint search. **Cheapest first test.** Two intersecting read kernels in three dimensions, then an incompatible pair; compare the shortest useful joint word with singles. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B/C after both launches and verify neither binding invalidates the other.

### 12. Restore service, not the erased ancestor — primitive candidate (review seed)
**Mechanism.** After deleting a record, recover a frozen witness whose required output is supported by retained data, with an identity-independent word and binding; test both attack families on the whole release. **Novelty / control.** [SISA][sisa] and [certified removal][guo] address deletion, not a distinct service lost during deletion; compare retrain without target records, SISA, rollback plus replay and a per-spend identity-leaking map, all at matched service where feasible. **Cheapest first test.** Tiny logistic learners on paired datasets with different deleted points, with one public downstream feature and a positive-control witness that reproduces a deleted value. **Old-task retention.** A/B/C on retained examples and each full retained-task domain, alongside membership and reconstruction at the deletion-only level.

### 13. Make recovery certified post-processing — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Apply the same randomized or deterministic transformation to the certified post-deletion learner and its scratch-retrained reference, using only independently available C inputs and public metadata (or explicitly justified side information). Include the word choice and binding in that transformation. **Novelty / control.** Post-processing of a distributional guarantee is known; the research question is whether a useful witness fits inside that release boundary, rather than requiring a private pre-spend snapshot. Compare uniform recovery with recovery using a pre-spend cache and with retrain plus recovery. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a finite randomized learner's release distribution, measuring both directions of the claimed deletion bound; make one cache bit violate the premise. **Old-task retention.** Verify A/B/C and launch in every accepted outcome, not only on average. The guarantee is relative to the transformed retraining reference, not proof that attacks on the original and transformed releases have identical power.

### 14. Audit the word publisher, not just the trained learner — improvement
**Mechanism.** Publish only a word chosen from a declared public pool using admissible post-deletion inputs; treat length, refusal, receipt and timing as outputs. **Novelty / control.** Side-channel analysis and post-processing already supply the logic; measure extra leakage from publishing a policy even when the terminal learner is identical. Compare a fixed shared word with a per-request selector and no-publication recovery. **Cheapest first test.** Paired deletion worlds with identical final reads but different valid-word lengths, scored by both attacks with and without metadata. **Old-task retention.** Show identical service and A/B/C so any inferred difference cannot be explained by utility.

### 15. Refuse a witness that is itself a reconstruction channel — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Bound the best reconstruction available through required witness answers under the declared query budget; if every service-correct binding exceeds the post-erasure tolerance, issue a refusal rather than hide a leak in the binding. **Novelty / control.** Information-flow auditing is known; the useful output is a service-vs-deletion incompatibility certificate, not a weaker attack on one selected binding. Compare an output-only empirical screen and exact enumeration of legal bindings. **Cheapest first test.** Target bit erased with equal prior odds; compare a frozen witness that must return that bit with one that must return an independent retained statistic. **Old-task retention.** Both successful service and refusal leave retained A/B/C intact.

### 16. Check the binding against auxiliary information — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Require a witness and its release to remain compatible with erasure conditional on declared auxiliary data, not just when target records are sampled marginally. Include provenance checks on C when it is correlated with the deleted record. **Novelty / control.** Conditional privacy is standard; the distinct question is whether a synchronizing curriculum can avoid conditioning on the very correlation that enables the restored witness. Compare selection from a public independent pool, marginal-only screening, and scratch retraining with the same auxiliary view. **Cheapest first test.** A target bit and a publicly available correlated feature; vary the correlation and compare attacks with and without that feature. **Old-task retention.** Verify A/B/C on the correlated slice separately rather than dropping the whole slice.

### 17. Keep the erased record erased after benign continuation — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose a word whose output is safe under a declared family of later ordinary C continuations, not only when queried immediately. Certify the family by common post-processing where valid; otherwise bound empirical attack increases at each horizon. **Novelty / control.** [Benign relearning][jog] already demonstrates why one snapshot is weak; the claim is recovery designed to survive the same clean continuations as deletion-only and retraining baselines. **Cheapest first test.** Two recovery orders with the same launch, followed by the same two retained-data curriculum steps; refit membership and reconstruction attacks at each horizon. **Old-task retention.** Check retained A/B/C and witness launch at all documented horizons.

### 18. Recover after two deletions without laundering the first — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Replace the previous word/binding using only the second post-deletion release and admissible retained data; audit the entire published transcript for each record and for their union. **Novelty / control.** Sequential erasure and adaptive privacy composition are known; the claim is two useful handoffs that neither re-expose an earlier record nor need a growing recovery store. Compare scratch retraining without both records, per-deletion independent repair, and rollback-plus-replay. **Cheapest first test.** Two tiny deletions in both orders, one pair sharing a latent feature; score attacks immediately and jointly across releases. **Old-task retention.** Check retained A/B/C after each deletion and each recovery, not just the last.

### 19. Share a repair plan across held-out deletion cohorts — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Commit the selection rule and all constants before testing on fresh erasure requests. Select a word using only a public transition model and a public cohort descriptor that is identical across the evaluated aliases. **Novelty / control.** Cohort holdouts and multi-learner teaching are known; the claim is that one repertoire can serve unseen deletions without smuggling cohort identity through recovery parameters. Compare per-cohort tuned repair and a single global replay rule at equal stored bytes and examples. **Cheapest first test.** Fit on two synthetic spend cohorts, reserve two with distinct record identities and swapped correlations, and test both attack families. **Old-task retention.** Report worst held-out cohort A/B/C, and require matched incoming-C utility.

### 20. Randomize over safe words rather than hide a bad deterministic one — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Commit a public mixture over words that each satisfy read and retention requirements for the whole class; sample using independence from the erased record conditional on allowed inputs. Audit the joint release including the chosen word if visible. **Novelty / control.** Randomized post-processing is standard; the bet is that several legitimate state-dependent training maps can reduce excess leakage relative to single good words under the same interface. Compare the best deterministic word, random-order replay, and an identity-conditioned mixture as a failure control. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two safe orders and their release distributions; report attacks when the draw is public and when it is not. **Old-task retention.** Every supported word must retain A/B/C and launch; averaging retention is not permitted.

### 21. Serve a frozen statistic while removing individual attribution — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Bind a witness to a statistic learnable from multiple disjoint sets of retained C examples, without changing the witness's interface; require agreement in counterfactual erasure worlds with the same retained statistic. **Novelty / control.** Sufficient statistics and privacy-preserving aggregation predate this; the candidate operation is substituting an independently learned support for a frozen service while holding the deletion release guarantee fixed. Compare an explicit retained-data estimator, scratch retraining and the original binding with its provenance exposed. **Cheapest first test.** A frozen threshold on a group-level rate, with redundant vs singleton support; do not tune the threshold to the erasure. **Old-task retention.** A/B/C, especially rare retained groups; require both attack measures not to rise.

### 22. Find a common invariant that proves no legal word works — improvement
**Mechanism.** Search for a witness-visible alias difference preserved by every legal batch (or an invariant set of differences bounded away from the witness kernel). Report a certificate only for the declared pool and training rule. **Novelty / control.** Standard switched-system invariants and non-synchronizability certificates; the improvement is refusing an infeasible handoff without blaming the word search. Compare bounded exact enumeration and a generic contraction test, which can miss invariant directions. **Cheapest first test.** Tiny linear pool with one common invisible-to-C direction, then add a truthful batch that acts on it and check the old certificate breaks. **Old-task retention.** Refusal makes no writes; check A/B/C for any word found in the feasible fork.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Is there a held-out read that a short truthful word can restore, rather than merely equalize?** Start with 01/02/04. Freeze everything before the spend, match the exact batch multiset and C utility, and require one binding to launch in all states including new admissible spend magnitudes. Stop if plain replay or multi-learner teaching ties, if projected GD/Newton ties without extra access, or if the common endpoint only works after post hoc rebinding.
2. **Which witness services are compatible with the post-erasure release boundary?** Pair 12/13/15. Find one witness supported by retained data and one that necessarily reveals a deleted value. Show attack non-increase versus deletion-only and compare to retrain-without-targets and SISA at matched service; a certificate, if any, must cover the word and binding as well as the learner. A service that only works by consulting pre-erasure data fails.
3. **Can synchronization survive the optimizer's hidden state and a normal training suffix?** Try 05/06/17 with same-weight, different-slot histories; a one-step endpoint that immediately splits on lawful continuation is not reusable recovery. Compare direct slot reset as a privileged control and plain C replay at equal steps.
4. **Does the word actually renew a fixed-budget handoff?** Run 08/10/18 across at least three spends without raising the global cap. Charge proofs, cached pools and retired-but-still-readable storage; compare the capped bank and hold out a successor with no shared feature. A first forced refusal is useful evidence; it is not indefinite renewal.
