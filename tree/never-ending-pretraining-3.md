# Never-ending pretraining 3 · Change what a weight write means

Child of [Never-ending pretraining 2](never-ending-pretraining-2.md) ([PR #390](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/390)). The [review](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/2ee73316580303a019dd854e009d76ff935b14b6/reviews/flood-triage.md) kept NP2-01 (portable byte-event effects) and NP2-21 (withdraw one independent source, not its copies) at 3 and found no 4. Here a card must propose a different writable object or operation, not a better projection, a broader withdrawal benchmark, or a longer migration test. The cited non-neural operations are prior art, not novelty claims; the question is whether the learner can execute their semantics as weight writes. Ideas only; no experiments run.

**Common comparison.** Begin with a finite model over short terminated byte strings in two split/merge charts, or a tiny fact-and-rule model with counterfactual source histories. Byte writes must survive the change of chart and read out cold, with no tokenizer-path annotations at inference. Against migration alone, use [exact byte pushforward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09303), matched embedding-transfer row deltas ([FOCUS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14481)/[WECHSEL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.06598)), [AlphaEdit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02355), [MEMIT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07229), a budget-matched list of NP2-01 point effects, and raw-span retraining. For withdrawal use deduplication, frequency weighting, [source-tagged training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01019), per-copy gradient ascent/[NPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05868), and NP2-21 with a supplied root graph. Start with known provenance; where structure is uncertain, expose that uncertainty rather than assuming identity is recoverable from text. Use no-root retraining as the oracle when the requested semantics are removal; use a corrected-graph replay oracle otherwise. Charge provenance, staging, auxiliary state, and compute to each method. External symbolic or decoding solutions are informative ceilings, not evidence that the operation lives in the weights. Hold out raw contexts or queries, not merely spellings already supplied to the writer.

## A · Operate on a probability law, not on a list of scores

### NP3-01 · Rewrite an event by moving its probability mass
- **Mechanism.** Store a reversible, byte-defined substitution on a selected language of complete strings. The write pushes probability mass from each selected string to its image while fixing the rest. On a finite fixture, transfer paired masses exactly; retain a rule for the substitution, not the original probabilities or a table of edited strings.
- **Nearest art.** [Token-to-character conversion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03719) changes the representation of the same law; [Grammar-Aligned Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.21047) conditions a law at inference. Here the law changes by pairwise conservation and the model emits the result cold.
- **First test / kill.** Swap two suffix slots under a tiny regular pattern, with unseen fillers. Compare a decoder-side substitution oracle and equal-budget point edits. Kill if the writer moves the total mass outside the selected language or the point edits transfer mass for held-out fillers just as well.
- **Retention.** Keep nonselected completions sharing the swapped suffix and the original relative odds inside untouched subtrees.

### NP3-02 · Write dependence without writing prevalence
- **Mechanism.** Represent a lesson as a change to a conditional odds ratio, with both marginal laws fixed. A finite joint readout implements the writer by scaling rows and columns after changing its interaction term. Neither component fact is what gets learned; only their association changes.
- **Nearest art.** [I-divergence projections](https://doi.org/10.1214/aop/1176996454) already solve fixed-marginal projections, and [DPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) writes comparative preferences. The candidate is a reusable interaction write on byte-observable variables, not a new projection algorithm.
- **First test / kill.** Change the association of two two-valued properties over one set of prefixes. Compare DPO, direct point constraints and an external projected-joint oracle. Kill if either marginal moves materially or the control generalizes with the same number of supervised cells.
- **Retention.** Keep old single-property answers, including the rarer property, and the original association in another domain.

### NP3-03 · Bind a write to a variable rather than its spelling
- **Mechanism.** Use a byte grammar to bind argument roles, then write an interchange rule: swapping the two bound entities must swap which role supports the edited answer. The writer acts on the role-exchange representation, and prevents the edit from following the literal string outside that role.
- **Nearest art.** [Causal Abstraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04709) relates interchange interventions in models to interventions on abstract variables; [MQuAKE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14795) measures propagation of factual edits. Here a lesson is the action under a variable substitution, not a fact plus a test of its ripple.
- **First test / kill.** Introduce one new binding law in synthetic two-argument records. Test fresh entity pairs in both orders, including names with overlapping bytes. Kill if role-swap augmentation plus an ordinary editor matches it or the learned effect follows the name.
- **Retention.** Keep old properties of the same names and facts in an unmodified role template.

### NP3-04 · Replace when the model stops without editing what it would say next
- **Mechanism.** Factor generation at byte boundaries into a stop hazard and a next-byte law conditional on continuation. Write the hazard alone for a specified prefix rule; conserve the continuation law even at a location hidden inside a merged token. A finite decoder can allocate terminated and continuing mass separately and compile it back to tokens.
- **Nearest art.** [When EOS Tokens Disagree](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20511) merges functionally equivalent EOS tokens for distillation. That transfers stopping; it does not require changing the stop hazard at an interior byte boundary without changing the conditional law of non-stop outcomes.
- **First test / kill.** Teach a model to stop once a synthetic record is complete but preserve the old next-byte law conditional on continuing. Kill if pooled-EOS finetuning or a list of point effects preserves the same conditional on held-out record values.
- **Retention.** Keep longer old records and common continuation bytes at shared prefixes where the stop rule does not apply.

### NP3-05 · Hold an invariance across interchangeable names
- **Mechanism.** Install a finite symmetry action and average only the designated conditional readout over its orbits during the write. The learned fact is that substitutions of names in a specified slot do not affect this prediction, while their identity remains useful elsewhere. Implement it first with a scoped invariant component and an untouched identity component.
- **Nearest art.** [LEACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03819) removes linear access to a concept; interchange in [Causal Abstraction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04709) gives a stronger operational comparator. Erasure is too broad here: the write equates outcomes under one action only, without erasing the variable.
- **First test / kill.** Remove a name shortcut from one synthetic relation but make name identity indispensable for a second. Test substituted held-out names in both charts. Kill if scoped LEACE or substitution augmentation achieves the same invariance and identity retention.
- **Retention.** Keep identity retrieval and prior distinctions that explicitly bind to the substituted name.

### NP3-06 · Reweight a language without relearning its inside
- **Mechanism.** Use a finite byte automaton to define an event and install a single likelihood ratio between the event and its complement. The ideal write multiplies all complete-string probabilities inside each slice by one shared constant, preserving ratios within both slices. In the toy model, reweight two normalized automaton-state channels and absorb the result into the readout.
- **Nearest art.** [Tractable Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07438) uses a tractable model to guide language generation and [regular-language CRFs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07306) incorporate regular constraints in structured probabilities. This is a soft cold weight operation with prescribed preservation of both within-slice laws.
- **First test / kill.** Double the odds of one pattern without favoring particular surviving strings. Contrast balanced pattern training, NP2-01, and exact decoding reweighting as an oracle. Kill if the weight write changes within-slice odds or point effects generalize to unseen matches at equal cost.
- **Retention.** Keep rare within-slice continuations, including completions that only differ in their final byte.

### NP3-07 · Install a range of allowed laws after an ambiguous lesson
- **Mechanism.** Write a feasible interval instead of a guessed score when several byte-event distributions explain the evidence. In a finite chart, store two attainable endpoint witnesses and a shared constraint; answer numeric bounds as readouts and intersect the feasible set on the next discriminating example. The first fixture only needs one unresolved scalar, so interval composition is exact.
- **Nearest art.** [Balke–Pearl bounds](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1997.10474074) get sharp bounds by optimizing over feasible latent models; [Bayesian dropout](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02142) supplies model uncertainty. Neither is a claim that point editing can apply a later lesson as intersection with the original observable constraints.
- **First test / kill.** Give two conflicting base models identical ambiguous evidence, then add one resolving example. Compare soft-label CPT and a small ensemble using the same bytes and parameters. Kill if the interval excludes either attainable world, fails to narrow, or the controls are equally sharp without overconfidence.
- **Retention.** Keep previously identified probabilities exact and old ambiguities unresolved rather than replacing them with a global confidence offset.

### NP3-08 · Reconcile two directions of a fact by writing one joint
- **Mechanism.** Treat forward and reverse query forms as conditionalizations of a shared weight-resident joint over byte-defined values. A write to its joint statistics changes both views through normalization, without two templated edits. Start with tied embeddings and a finite normalized joint, where an impossible pair of conditional requirements can be rejected.
- **Nearest art.** [Bilinear relational editing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21993) already propagates single edits to reverse facts and logical dependents. The gap is normalization across unequal alternatives: a changed conditional is not simply a symmetric new fact, because the reverse answer must weight all causes by their priors.
- **First test / kill.** Edit one many-to-one relation with unequal source priors and read it both ways, holding out reverse prompts. Kill if a bilinear editor or forward-plus-reverse augmentation gives the same calibrated posteriors at equal state, or the model reports incompatible marginals across views.
- **Retention.** Keep other causes of the same output and unrelated relations sharing a surface value.

### NP3-09 · Write only an ordering, leaving magnitudes free
- **Mechanism.** Add a scoped partial order over byte-defined answers. Training inside its order cone may still move logits, including shrinking any surplus margin; subsequent writes can remove a declared comparison without restoring an older score vector. In the exact toy model, parameterize scores subject to the active inequalities.
- **Nearest art.** [DPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18290) uses preferences to optimize a policy and [HardNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10807) enforces constraints through architecture. Here a comparison has an identity and lifetime and governs future writes without carrying a reference policy.
- **First test / kill.** Write A above B, later teach facts moving both scores, then remove only A above B. Compare repeated DPO on live pairs and a constraint table outside the weights. Kill if the comparison cannot hold through the middle update without pinning either magnitude or the paired-control matches without extra state.
- **Retention.** Keep unconstrained old score differences and active comparisons that share either endpoint.

### NP3-10 · Define an edit on byte-equivalence classes of model states
- **Mechanism.** Declare two execution states equivalent only for a named byte-observable family. Learn a write that maps each whole equivalence class to a single output class, not just that makes the mean over paths correct. A minimal realization can tie a shared writable coefficient to the byte summary and leave path-specific residuals out of that coefficient.
- **Nearest art.** [The Tokens Remember](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29045) shows edits can fail to cover alternate segmentations; [byte-prefix marginalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22334) aggregates probability mass. This candidate is a quotient operation on the learned state, with per-state equality rather than an aggregate test.
- **First test / kill.** Use synthetic synonymous segmentations with one deliberately disagreeing hidden path. Compare all-path augmentation, a point-effect editor, and marginal matching. Kill if the write only fixes aggregate probabilities or augmentation transfers to held-out paths with the same data and state.
- **Retention.** Keep legitimate segmentation-state differences in readouts the equivalence declaration did not cover.

### NP3-11 · Move credit between byte cuts while preserving the endpoint lesson
- **Mechanism.** Attach a learned potential to interior byte states and add successive potential differences to local credit, with both endpoint potentials fixed at zero. The total scalar supervision for a complete span is unchanged, but a block-local or truncated updater now receives early credit. Define the potential over bytes so a segment spanning several cuts receives the telescoped difference.
- **Nearest art.** [RUDDER](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07857) redistributes delayed return; [tokenization-bias work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16829) distinguishes surface strings from token-conditioned effects. The bet is portable composition of local learning credit under different cuts, not novelty of reshaping a scalar reward.
- **First test / kill.** Train a finite block-local reader on a late-labelled span, then move a boundary across the revealing byte. Compare terminal supervision and token-local learned losses whose weights are tuned separately. Kill if the byte potential loses scalar conservation or buys no first-block learning beyond those controls.
- **Retention.** Keep an old task in which that early byte has the opposite association outside the target span type.

### NP3-12 · Refine a read into two branches that still sum to the old read
- **Mechanism.** Replace a learned event by two weight-resident child events with a shared total; a new write chooses the split, not the total. The previous query sums both children exactly. Unlike changing a tokenizer, the refinement partitions a learned claim by new context and can be applied even when the byte alphabet is unchanged.
- **Nearest art.** [Online hierarchical label expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14374) learns finer classes after coarse classes using hierarchy-aware pseudo-labels and rehearsal. The operation here is exact conservation of the existing parent law through repeated child writes, not label expansion itself; a frozen parent with an ordinary conditional head is a direct control.
- **First test / kill.** Refine a generic synthetic “accepted” answer by a newly observed subtype, then add a lesson that affects only one child. Kill if the parent moves or a frozen parent plus a separately fitted conditional head matches transfer and retention at equal state, including on repeated refinements.
- **Retention.** Keep the old unqualified answer and the odds of neighboring unrefined events.

## B · Rewire the grounds of a lesson, not the copies of a sentence

### NP3-13 · Subtract inherited evidence but keep a descendant's discovery
- **Mechanism.** Learn with a source report represented as inherited sufficient statistics plus its own innovation relative to its declared parents. Withdraw an ancestor by disabling the inherited channel throughout its descendants while retaining each independently witnessed residual. A linear model with a source DAG is the exact first realization.
- **Nearest art.** [Provenance semirings](https://doi.org/10.1145/1265530.1265535) track derivations; [OriginBlame](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13037) traces trainable records and tokens. A descendant need not be either a copy or an independent root: the operation separates the part of its training effect that depends on the ancestor.
- **First test / kill.** Use a report quoting an ancestor and adding a corroborated observation embedded in the same span. Compare token-level delete masks, a source-DAG symbolic oracle, and root-only withdrawal. Kill if the learned residual fails when both effects use the same bytes or masks already match at equal cost.
- **Retention.** Preserve the descendant's verified contribution and facts independently corroborated under the same wording.

### NP3-14 · Withdraw the measurement rule, not the measurement
- **Mechanism.** Give a source's observation and the operator that turns it into evidence different handles. On invalidating a calibration rule, subtract its downstream claim effects while retaining the reported measurements and the ability to compose them with a newly supplied calibration. Implement first as observations contracted with a replaceable typed linear map.
- **Nearest art.** [DBSP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.16684) updates derived views; [EVEDIT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11324) edits along deductive boundaries. The proposed weight operation replaces a source-conditioned *operator* and leaves its original observations available without their documents.
- **First test / kill.** Change the conversion rule for one of two sensors and read new derived answers for held-out entities with stored observations. Kill if fact-by-fact editing or input tagging matches without enumerating affected entities, or the old sensor readings disappear.
- **Retention.** Keep raw readings, other calibrations, and stable derived facts supported by the other sensor.

### NP3-15 · Revoke a defeater and admit the alternative it blocked
- **Mechanism.** Store support and attack as separate weight channels: a source may disable a named inference without itself asserting the opposite. Withdrawing that source re-enables the valid alternative, even if the removed text never mentioned the alternative's answer. Use a small acyclic argument graph and a differentiable readout with hard semantics on the fixture.
- **Nearest art.** [Dung's argumentation framework](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(94)00041-X) already defines attacks and acceptance, and [CoME](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15826) unlearns conflicting knowledge as part of editing. The new target is a distinct defeater write in the learner, not a sign-flipped factual label.
- **First test / kill.** Disable one inferential step with an independent report, then withdraw it. Compare tagged signed evidence, root counting and a truth-maintenance oracle. Kill if the cold learner merely grows uncertain rather than recovering the already-supported alternative, or signed evidence is sufficient.
- **Retention.** Keep rebuttals that still have live backing and unrelated conclusions from the withdrawn source's topic.

### NP3-16 · Withdraw the opportunity that gave silence its meaning
- **Mechanism.** Store evidence from a documented chance to observe separately from observed outcomes. A verified search with no hit contributes a likelihood factor for absence; revoking the search's coverage removes that factor without deleting real observations. Model an exposure gate and the outcome likelihood it licenses.
- **Nearest art.** [Balke–Pearl](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1997.10474074) distinguishes observable constraints from latent quantities; [Source-Aware Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01019) binds observed claims to document identities. The removable object is an observation opportunity, not a negative document or a citation.
- **First test / kill.** Give two labs identical empty reports but different search coverage, then invalidate one coverage record. Kill if tagging whether a report was empty achieves the same counterfactual on held-out coverage and true-positive queries, or if an actual observation gets erased.
- **Retention.** Keep recorded positives and negatives backed by still-valid covered searches.

### NP3-17 · Keep several possible independence partitions live
- **Mechanism.** When roots cannot be identified, hold a compact set of admissible report partitions. Compute upper and lower support over those partitions in a weight readout; a new provenance judgment removes partitions rather than adding a pseudo-observation. Use a few uncertain links or exact finite enumeration before any approximation.
- **Nearest art.** [Integrating Conflicting Data](https://doi.org/10.14778/1687627.1687690) models dependencies among sources; [Balke–Pearl bounds](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1997.10474074) give partial identification rather than a point estimate. This version would let ambiguous independence change learned bounds without changing any report's text or data.
- **First test / kill.** Give identical reports whose copying status is initially unknowable, then certify one link and retract a report. Compare hard dedup, a fitted copying prior, and exact partition enumeration. Kill if the learner's bound is invalid or no tighter than counting all reports versus counting one, even after the link is certified.
- **Retention.** Keep exact agreement for facts with certified independent sources and uncertainty for other unresolved groups.

### NP3-18 · Withdraw a common exposure without discarding complementary views
- **Mechanism.** Give reports that reuse the same experiment unit a shared latent unit factor and separate measurement likelihoods conditional on that unit. Revoke a measurement by removing one likelihood; revoke the unit by removing all of its evidence for prevalence. A repeated observation can improve knowledge of the unit without creating another independent trial.
- **Nearest art.** [Integrating Conflicting Data](https://doi.org/10.14778/1687627.1687690) uses source dependence to reconcile conflicts; [provenance semirings](https://doi.org/10.1145/1265530.1265535) represent shared contributors. Neither makes an overlapping experimental unit equivalent to duplicated content. The comparator is a hierarchical statistical ledger, not merely text dedup.
- **First test / kill.** Supply two noisy assays of one synthetic specimen and one assay of another, then withdraw one assay or one unit. Kill if binary root weighting handles both cases at equal retention or the estimated prevalence counts assay repeats as extra trials.
- **Retention.** Keep information from surviving assays and unchanged prevalence estimates in untouched cohorts.

### NP3-19 · Transfer the right to support a fact without changing the answer
- **Mechanism.** Exchange one live evidential basis for a newly certified independent one while holding today's answer fixed. The operation changes who can cause a future withdrawal and which dependent conclusions survive. In a tiny model, separate the aggregate read from its factorized support and conserve the aggregate exactly at exchange.
- **Nearest art.** [Truth maintenance](https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(79)90008-0) replaces justifications and [source-aware training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01019) teaches citation. Neither makes ordinary close-output editing a substitute for exchanging counterfactual withdrawal rights in the weights.
- **First test / kill.** Replace a sufficient witness, later revoke the old witness, then revoke the replacement. Compare tag swapping, NP2-21 with delete-plus-add and byte-matched external justification state. Kill if the exchange changes the current answer or a delete-plus-add transaction matches all future behaviors without more state or write cost.
- **Retention.** Keep facts that still rely on the old source for other reasons, including facts using the same answer string.

### NP3-20 · Withdraw a learned rule but keep independently learned instances
- **Mechanism.** Place generalization from a source in a revocable rule channel and direct evidence in independent instance channels. Cutting a rule erases its novel instantiations while retaining directly grounded outputs. In a two-layer fixture, a shared relation matrix supplies rule effects and independently learned residuals supply exceptions and memorized instances.
- **Nearest art.** [EVEDIT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.11324) accounts for deductive editing boundaries and [Corrective Machine Unlearning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.14015) targets broader effects than identified wrong examples. The unit withdrawn here is the learner's ability to make one substitution, not a list of its earlier conclusions.
- **First test / kill.** Teach a synthetic lexicon rule and independent attestations for a few applications; withdraw only the rule. Kill if training-data removal plus ordinary editing matches both attested and unseen outputs or if a cold query can no longer access the attested cases.
- **Retention.** Keep independently learned translations and unrelated lexical rules that share the same affix.

### NP3-21 · Remove a source from how future gradients act, not from present beliefs
- **Mechanism.** Give evidential status and optimizer calibration separate revocation handles. A source can be withdrawn from the preconditioner while keeping weights fixed, e.g. subtract its additive covariance sketch from a finite linear optimizer, rebuild the inverse and then apply the next unrelated gradient. Preserve factual evidence if the fault concerns gradient calibration only.
- **Nearest art.** [Sketched Metadifferentiation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15044) estimates the influence of training data; [Unmerge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38895) uses task arithmetic for approximate weight unlearning. Here only the next-step map changes, so prediction parity at revocation is necessary but not enough.
- **First test / kill.** Use an overrepresented source with valid labels and misleading gradient covariance. Revoke its calibration, then present the same fresh batch to the proposed learner, reset-and-warmup and an exact surviving-covariance oracle. Kill if its next update is no closer to the oracle than a matched optimizer reset.
- **Retention.** Require exact immediate prediction retention and preserve useful old curvature from unaffected sources on the following updates.

### NP3-22 · Distill the difference between having a source and not having it
- **Mechanism.** Package a self-distillation target as a stable teacher component plus a tagged counterfactual residual from one source channel. A later revocation disables just that residual, including on student examples with no visible source marker. Begin with a frozen teacher whose two outputs differ by one exactly enumerable channel.
- **Nearest art.** [Byte-Prefix Marginalization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22334) transfers a teacher distribution; [Counterfactual Training for Self-Attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35295) teaches whether context mattered. Neither makes the causally attributable component an independently retractable target after consolidation.
- **First test / kill.** Consolidate a rule into the same lineage through a toy distillation pass. Compare training-data lineage tags, short teacher-replay and an unmerge vector. Kill if the tagged residual leaves the same withdrawn behavior as the no-residual control or harms teacher effects common to both worlds.
- **Retention.** Keep the shared teacher competency on fresh inputs and independently established copies of some conclusions.

### NP3-23 · Revoke the rule that admitted a record, not a still-admissible record
- **Mechanism.** Treat admissibility as a proof with independently revocable certificates. Route a learned sufficient statistic through the disjunction of live admission proofs. Revoking a credential removes all effects licensed solely by it but does not erase records independently admitted under a second rule, or promote a replacement record by default.
- **Nearest art.** [Exact Unlearning After Data Curation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33426) repairs statistics when deletions change which records enter a fixed curation rule. [Provenance semirings](https://doi.org/10.1145/1265530.1265535) already express alternative proofs. The new target is withdrawing a proof rule as an operation on learning state, not deleting raw records under fixed rules.
- **First test / kill.** Admit overlapping records via two independent licenses and invalidate one license, with no records provided to the writer. Kill if a symbolic admission ledger plus inverse sufficient-statistic updates matches the weight-resident version on cost and retention, or multiply licensed records lose weight.
- **Retention.** Keep the shared records and unrelated cases admitted by the surviving rule.

### NP3-24 · Withdraw a guarantee without withdrawing the estimate
- **Mechanism.** Separate a factual estimate from the sampling evidence that licenses a claim of precision. Attach a live-source proof to the uncertainty radius, with an adaptive sampling protocol spelled out. Revoking a calibration certificate can widen an interval without moving its point estimate or deleting the evidence.
- **Nearest art.** [Confidence sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240) give time-uniform intervals under a specified process; [ConflictBank](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12076) studies conflicting knowledge in language models. The target is a weight write on the certification of a claim, not recalibration of all predictions.
- **First test / kill.** Invalidate the claimed independence of a group of equally agreeing sources, keeping their observations informative for the point estimate. Compare bootstrap after dedup and an external sufficient-statistic confidence ledger. Kill if interval coverage degrades on a planned sequence of looks or the control gives equal valid precision and state.
- **Retention.** Keep point estimates and protected confidence intervals for disconnected source groups.

### NP3-25 · Revoke a source's expertise without revoking its observations
- **Mechanism.** Give the learned reliability map for a source its own independent grounds. On revocation of the calibration grounds, fall back to the live prior or another certified map; do not remove the source's observations. A finite parametric aggregator can assign separate weight blocks to evidence and to estimated competence by domain.
- **Nearest art.** [Integrating Conflicting Data](https://doi.org/10.14778/1687627.1687690) estimates source copying and trustworthiness and is a strong non-neural control. The idea is that training can withdraw the provenance of a *reliability rule* independently of the claims it currently weights.
- **First test / kill.** Remove one lab's calibration witness in one domain while retaining its similar-looking observations there and in another domain. Kill if source-weighted refitting achieves the same cold transfer without report replay, or revocation changes the lab's unaffected domain.
- **Retention.** Keep independent observations and prior domain-specific competence unaffected by the invalidated calibration.

### NP3-26 · Make evidence for a coarse conclusion nonspendable for its details
- **Mechanism.** Give a source an observable sigma-algebra in the finite fixture: it can root writes only for predicates distinguishable by its measurements. A broadcast total can support a coarse fact but cannot keep either detailed assignment alive after the one resolving source is withdrawn. Compile per-source permissions onto the coefficients for coarse and residual effects.
- **Nearest art.** [Balke–Pearl bounds](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1997.10474074) distinguish identified and unidentified targets; [OriginBlame](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13037) locates evidence by author down to tokens. Neither a root count nor word-level attribution determines which predicate a coarse measurement actually identifies.
- **First test / kill.** Teach a joint distribution using a trusted marginal and a second source identifying its split. Withdraw the second source. Compare root-semiring deletion with typed predicates and an identified-set oracle. Kill if the learner retains either unsupported detail or the typed semiring already gives all the same cold effects at equal state.
- **Retention.** Preserve the supported marginal, independent old details, and the distinction between a reported fine value and a warranted one.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Can a cold weight write preserve a law, rather than a finite list of values?** Start with NP3-02 and NP3-06. Require independently checked marginals or within-slice odds on held-out raw strings in both charts. If projected point edits generalize just as well at the same budget, stop pursuing a new byte-law primitive.
2. **What is the smallest unit of evidence that can be withdrawn without destroying its descendant's own contribution?** Start with NP3-13, then test NP3-18 where two measurements share a unit but are not copies. Demand a repeated-write advantage over OriginBlame and a matched external dependence ledger, not merely better attribution.
3. **Does changing the grounds of a lesson require a write that changes no current answer?** Contrast NP3-19 and NP3-21: a witness exchange changes later revocation while a calibration revocation changes the next training step. Use two states with identical current reads but opposite next-operation outcomes; reject the proposal if ordinary transactional updates or optimizer reset match at equal cost.
4. **Can the learner conserve justified coarse knowledge after losing all fine evidence?** Pair NP3-12 with NP3-26: one operation introduces a refinement, the other removes permission to believe it. Require a correct coarse read after removing the last fine source, and no residual confidence in either unsupported detail. A byte-matched typed ledger and a jointly fitted hierarchical model are the first controls.
