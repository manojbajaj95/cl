# Root: Continual Learning from Non-Neural Learners

Root node (depth 0) · 36 candidate primitives · Ideas only; no experiments run

**Thesis.** Neural continual learning assumes a single centralized parameter vector updated by global scalar gradients, replay buffers, or explicit task routers. Non-neural biological systems—adaptive and innate immunity, acellular slime molds, sessile modular plants, prokaryotic gene-regulatory circuits, ecological communities, and multicellular tissues—learn continually across lifetimes and regime shifts without neurons, backpropagation, central clocks, or task IDs. Their computational primitives live in *where* memory sits (synonymous syntax, tube-diameter hierarchies, extracellular matrices, dormant seed banks, bioelectric setpoints) and *how* updates are authorized or bounded (catalytic eviction at write time, locus exhaustion, reciprocal partner sanctions, keystone suppression, and local coupling horizons). Importing these primitives verbatim—not as metaphors for replay, MoE routing, or weight regularization—opens a distinct design space for both neural models and tool-using agents.

**Scope.** Read the [idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795531716-reviewer-verdicts/reviews/idea-ledger.md) and [flood triage](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795531716-reviewer-verdicts/reviews/flood-triage.md) first. This node stays completely outside the five narrowing lines in flood triage: no optimizer-state inheritance or slot splits, no meters/observability or weight self-headers, no token retirement/deletion licenses/cancelling pairs, no teaching markets or learning-economics pricing, and no relearnability/savings-as-read-head channels. It also excludes mechanisms already proposed in the [neuro-bio flood (PR #46)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795538372-neuro-bio-idea-flood/ideas/flood/neuro-bio.md) (plant vernalization locks, microbial bet hedging, slime-mold ghost conduits, arthropod molts, autotomy, cofactor escrow, epitope masking, idiotypic anti-memory graphs, central tolerance nurseries, quorum write thresholds, complement cascades, genomic imprinting, plant grafting, endosymbiosis, fungal heterokaryons, waggle dance, niche construction) and the [immunology and development flood (PR #75)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795539211-immuno-dev-flood/ideas/flood/immuno-dev.md) (cross-presentation, missing-self invariants, partial-agonist spectra, response-class switches, active tolerance escrows, developmental organizers, morphogen gradients, and epithelial turnover).

**Literature check.** Comparisons use alphaXiv and web discovery against original abstracts and papers; they mark the closest existing mechanisms and the falsifiable gap to test, not priority claims. Classic bio-inspired ML already covers clonal and negative selection in [Artificial Immune Systems](https://dlnext.acm.org/doi/10.1016/j.tcs.2008.02.011) (and [continuous-learning AIS](https://seer.ufrgs.br/rita/article/view/RITA_VOL27_NR4_34), [Innate Immune Memory DCA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8852961/), and [Agent-Native Immune Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28270)); physical local learning in [Equilibrium Propagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.05179), [Coupled Learning](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.021045), [Learning without Neurons in Physical Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.05831), and [Physarum Lagrangian / network adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08531); diversity-preserving search in [MAP-Elites](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909); cellular regeneration in [Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/); and epigenetic/evolutionary adaptation in [ELENA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05735), [Dimensionless Controls of Plasticity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23889), [Training Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30109), and [Sandscapes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10903). Foundational non-neural biology anchors include [Baluška & Levin (2016)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00902/full), [Kramar & Alim (2021)](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.2007815118), [Biswas et al. (2021)](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(21)00099-7), [Kiers et al. (2011)](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.1208473), and [CRISPR array division of labor (2026)](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/10.64898/2026.09.22.753571). Renaming EWC, replay, MoE gating, or LoRA in biological vocabulary is not a primitive; every card below must beat its named non-biological control at equal bytes, calls, and compute.

**Shared CPU-first protocol.** Every first test runs on CPU using a small NumPy/PyTorch model, a finite-state/gridworld environment, or a constrained symbolic/tool-using agent (e.g., an A→B→C→D→A/B stream where B is rare and shifts arrive without task IDs). Give the strongest non-biological control the exact same persistent bytes, update steps, model/environment queries, and candidate pool; charge auxiliary fields, depots, and structural state against total memory. For any text-memory agent arm, include same-length filler and an entropy-matched temperature control (per the idea ledger's A3/PR #27 rule). Report adaptation at matched retention and retention at matched adaptation. Kill any card whose biological mechanism ties replay, flat retrieval, MoE load-balancing, or EWC under the same real budget.

**Old-task retention check for every card.** Evaluate a fixed, hidden held-out mix of ordinary A and rare B queries or episodes through the normal inference interface before learning C, immediately after C, after a second shift D, and upon returning to A/B. Report per-task accuracy and worst-task drop alongside pooled performance, compared against a no-new-learning floor. The learner never sees the retention probe set as a routing signal or write gate.

## A · Immune Systems Beyond Clonal Selection

### NNL01 · Synonymous mutability coding (AID somatic hypermutation hotspots)
- **Mechanism:** Represent a model's weights or an agent's executable rules in an over-parameterized or syntactically redundant coding where multiple distinct encodings compute the *exact same function* but differ sharply in their susceptibility to future edits (mirroring B-cell immunoglobulin genes packing AID hotspot motifs `WRCY` into variable CDR loops and coldspot synonymous codons into conserved framework regions). When an invariant is confirmed, compile it into mutation-cold syntax; keep uncertain interface clauses in mutation-hot syntax so a uniform, blind downstream editor automatically concentrates changes on hot regions with zero external mask table.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [EWC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00796) and [PackNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05769) protect parameters using separate importance arrays or binary masks; [ELENA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05735) attaches explicit mutation-resistance tags. The primitive encodes editability *inside the synonymous representation itself* so the protection travels with the object under blind editors and costs zero side-table bytes.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train a tiny symbolic rule/program agent (or discrete-code MLP) where each clause has functionally identical hot/cold encodings under a uniform mutator/editor on an A→B→C shift stream. Compare against an explicit freeze-mask / EWC control charged for mask storage and against uncoded uniform editing; verify that blind edits land on uncertain clauses without reading a mask.
- **Old-task retention:** Score held-out A and rare B rules after C and D shifts, including old invariants that share tokens/features with C and must stay unmutated.

### NNL02 · Chronological leader-insertion array with mismatch-primed neighbor capture (CRISPR-Cas)
- **Mechanism:** Maintain a bounded, ordered failure-signature array that inserts every newly acquired pattern strictly at the *leader* end (giving structural recency priority without learned routers or score tables, while trailer-end entries naturally erode). When a shifted environment produces a *partial mismatch* (near-hit) against an older entry, trigger *primed adaptation*: capture the adjacent co-occurring context features directly from the current episode and insert them at the leader end, rapidly tracking drifting threats without overwriting or re-scoring the older entry.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [CRISPR array division of labor (2026)](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/10.64898/2026.09.22.753571) models biological spacer dynamics; in ML, LRU caches and retrieval-augmented failure buffers rely on embedding similarity or explicit utility scores. The candidate primitive is *near-hit-triggered adjacency capture* coupled to positional expression decay, turning partial recognition failure into a targeted local write operator.
- **Cheapest first test:** Run a constrained tool-calling agent on a sequence of drifting error schemas (where each shift mutates one field of a prior failure pattern while keeping neighboring fields intact). Compare leader-insertion + primed adjacency capture against an equal-byte LRU cache, cosine-similarity RAG failure memory, and same-length filler.
- **Old-task retention:** Re-test unshifted A and rare B error schemas after 3–5 drift waves, specifically checking whether rare old entries pushed toward the trailer still trigger primed re-capture when they recur.

### NNL03 · Active catalytic eviction at write time (HLA-DM peptide editing)
- **Mechanism:** Route every candidate memory, rule, or adapter update through a bounded loading buffer equipped with an active *destabilizing catalyst*—a fixed-budget perturbation operator (calibrated input paraphrases, distractor injections, or local weight-noise kicks) that actively tries to eject candidates from the buffer. Only candidates whose behavioral binding survives repeated catalytic ejection attempts (low kinetic off-rate) are committed to long-term storage; fragile shortcuts are stripped out *before* they ever enter memory.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Agent-Native Immune System](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28270) and standard confidence/margin gating filter writes by passive scalar thresholds (which failed or tied simple baselines in ledger MX2). Here, admission is governed by *kinetic stability under active adversarial stripping*, not static loss or margin.
- **Cheapest first test:** On a synthetic rule-learning stream mixing true cross-context invariants with high-confidence spurious shortcuts, compare catalytic write-time editing against loss-thresholding, margin gating, and held-out validation filtering at identical perturbation/query budgets.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure A and rare B accuracy after admitting C rules, verifying that catalytic eviction blocks spurious C shortcuts that would otherwise overwrite or dilute A/B decision boundaries.

### NNL04 · Monotonic in-place locus exhaustion (B-cell receptor editing)
- **Mechanism:** Structure each modular policy head or agent skill slot as an ordered linear locus of discrete sub-component segments `(V_1..V_k, J_1..J_m)` with only the innermost active junction `(V_i, J_j)` expressed. When a live interference check flags a self-conflict against old invariants, do not tune weights or delete the module: excise the offending inner junction and splice the next flanking pair `(V_{i-1}, J_{j+1})` *in place*, preserving outer interfaces while monotonically consuming the finite segment bank until the slot either resolves the conflict or exhausts its locus and freezes.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Continuous-learning AIS](https://seer.ufrgs.br/rita/article/view/RITA_VOL27_NR4_34) uses random clonal mutation and negative selection (deleting bad detectors). The primitive is *ordered, in-place excisional splicing* with a built-in monotonic edit budget per slot, preventing unbounded oscillation or thrashing under conflicting shifts.
- **Cheapest first test:** Build a tiny modular classifier or symbolic routing agent facing sequential conflicting tasks where naive re-tuning oscillates. Compare monotonic in-place locus splicing against unconstrained random resampling, weight rollback, and gradient fine-tuning at equal candidate-segment count.
- **Old-task retention:** Check A and rare B behaviors after every splice event, including inputs routed through neighboring unedited loci that share the same outer interface.

### NNL05 · Mechanical tether-rupture extraction from sources (BCR force-calibrated affinity)
- **Mechanism:** When a learner ingests a candidate rule, demonstration, or distillation target from an external teacher or tool output, apply a calibrated *pulling tension*—simultaneously perturbing the candidate's fit to the learner's existing invariants and its dependence on the source's phrasing/context. Extract and internalize the candidate *only* if its binding to the learner's schema ruptures the source-context tether before breaking internal consistency; claims that are tightly glued to source quirks fail to extract, preventing teacher contamination without an oracle verifier.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Self-distillation and teacher-filtering methods (e.g., CGTR / OPSD in the ledger) gate on teacher confidence or student loss. The primitive tests the *relative mechanical break-point* between source-dependence and learner-invariance as a direct extraction gate.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stream mixed clean and context-spoofed rules from noisy teachers to a small NumPy/symbolic learner (no verifier oracle). Compare tether-rupture extraction against teacher-confidence gating, cross-entropy thresholding, and source-reputation tracking at equal perturbation queries.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate held-out A and rare B tasks after ingesting noisy C teachers, checking specifically for silent corruption from fluent but context-bound C claims.

### NNL06 · Non-degradative intact-exemplar surface cycling (FDC immune-complex depots)
- **Mechanism:** Maintain a tiny, strictly non-rewritable depot of *unparsed raw interaction objects* (exact input-environment-outcome tuples, never compressed into embeddings, summaries, or latent prototypes) that periodically cycle back to the learner's raw input boundary. Unlike generative or latent replay—which degrades as the learner's representation drifts—the depot never digests its contents, keeping long-term memory anchored to the unaltered external object across arbitrary internal representational shifts.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Standard episodic replay buffers and [Semi-parametric Memory Consolidation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14727) either replay raw data uniformly during training or store compressed latent embeddings/summaries. The narrow primitive is *periodic, non-training surface re-presentation of undigested raw objects* specifically to detect and re-anchor representational drift after major basis changes, tested against equal-byte latent/summary replay.
- **Cheapest first test:** Subject a small representation learner (or agent with evolving schema summaries) to four successive representation-rotating shifts A→B→C→D. Compare an equal-byte intact raw depot (fewer items, zero digestion) against a larger compressed-embedding replay buffer and a distilled-summary buffer.
- **Old-task retention:** Test A and rare B on original raw queries after shift D, measuring whether intact raw cycling prevents the cumulative drift collapse seen in digested replay.

## B · Slime Molds & Active-Matter Flow Networks

### NNL07 · Flow-advected softening-agent conductance hierarchy (Kramar–Alim tube memory)
- **Mechanism:** Model a learner's routing topology as a fluid-flow network whose edge capacities ("tube diameters") have no gradient updates. Encountering a reward or error event releases a conserved *softening signal* that is advected strictly by the network's own inference-time activation flows; an edge widens or narrows locally as a function of `advected_signal × local_flux`, encoding the spatial geometry of past tasks in the *relative hierarchy of edge capacities* across the whole graph.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Tero et al. (2010)](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-conmatphys-040821-115312) and [Cognition as Least Action: Physarum Lagrangian](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08531) solve static shortest-path/TSP optimization; [Kramar & Alim (2021)](https://www.pnas.org/doi/abs/10.1073/pnas.2007815118) showed *Physarum* encodes spatial memory in tube-diameter hierarchies via an advected softening agent. The CL primitive imports *flow-advected softening* as a gradient-free, multi-task routing memory for continual streams, distinct from NB28's dormant ghost conduits.
- **Cheapest first test:** Build a sparse 2D/graph routing learner navigating shifting source–sink tasks (A, rare B, C, D) on CPU. Compare flow-advected softening conductance against SGD-trained attention routing, REINFORCE edge gating, and static Physarum flow (without advected softening) at equal edge count and episodes.
- **Old-task retention:** Re-query A and rare B source–sink pairs after C and D shifts, testing whether the global tube-diameter hierarchy preserves B's branching bias without retraining.

### NNL08 · Pacemaker-free peristaltic phase-gradient memory (shuttle-streaming waves)
- **Mechanism:** Attach a lattice of locally coupled phase oscillators to a learner's module gates, with no central clock or task-ID input. When the environment undergoes recurring or quasi-periodic regime shifts, local sensory discrepancies entrain the oscillators into a *traveling phase-gradient wave* whose wavelength and reversal period store the temporal structure of past shifts—automatically pre-arming the right sub-circuit ahead of recurring transitions and smoothly de-entraining when periodicity breaks.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Learning without Neurons in Physical Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.05831) covers equilibrium/steady-state physical networks; neural continual learning uses explicit time embeddings, RNN hidden states, or HMM regime filters. The primitive stores *temporal shift memory in a distributed phase gradient* that generalizes to phase-shifted or skipped cycles without a global clock.
- **Cheapest first test:** Run a small continual bandit / sequence predictor under periodic and quasi-periodic regime alternations (including an unannounced pause where the shift temporarily stops, testing anticipatory recall). Compare coupled phase-oscillator gating against an equal-state GRU, Fourier time features, and a regime-switching HMM.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate held-out A and rare B regimes after a non-periodic C disruption, checking whether phase re-entrainment recovers A/B faster without corrupting their internal policies.

### NNL09 · Externalized decaying negative-footprint exclusion (extracellular slime trails)
- **Mechanism:** Instead of appending failed trajectories or dead-end warnings to an agent's internal memory (which causes context bloat and rule dilution, cf. PR #89/#103), allow the agent to write tiny, decaying *negative exclusion stamps* directly onto external environment objects (file headers, tool-endpoint metadata, state-hash tables). At read time, local repulsion from stamped states prevents re-entering dead ends with zero internal prompt tokens consumed.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Stigmergic memory (CL-bench B15) and niche construction (NB43) add *positive* discriminative landmarks; [Active Context Compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07190) compresses internal history. The primitive is an *externalized, decaying repulsive field* specifically for negative failure memory, keeping internal context bandwidth constant as failures accumulate.
- **Cheapest first test:** Place a constrained tool-using or gridworld agent in a multi-trap environment with shifting dead ends and 50+ accumulated failure modes. Compare external decaying exclusion stamps against an equal-byte internal failure-rule list retrieved into context, plus same-length filler and temperature-matched no-memory controls.
- **Old-task retention:** Re-run old A and rare B tasks after accumulating 40+ C failure stamps, measuring whether old-task rule compliance avoids the dilution drop seen when negative rules live in internal context.

### NNL10 · Localized sclerotium vitrification and cue-triggered rehydration (dormancy cysts)
- **Mechanism:** When an active subgraph experiences sustained high-conflict updates during a hostile shift, encapsulate that subgraph into a metabolically inert, read-only *vitrified cyst*—physically detached from both inference routing (zero FLOPs) and weight updates (zero gradient exposure)—paired with a lightweight environmental rehydration predicate. When the original regime's cue returns, the cyst rehydrates and splices back into the live graph intact.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [PackNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.05769) and [Progressive Nets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04671) freeze modules permanently on known task boundaries while keeping them in the active forward/routing graph. The primitive is *conflict-triggered detachment into zero-compute dormancy* with autonomous cue-based rehydration, without task IDs.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train a modular small MLP/agent on A→B→hostile-C→D→A/B where C strongly conflicts with A's features. Compare conflict-triggered vitrification + rehydration against EWC, replay, and permanent freeze-and-expand at equal peak storage and inference FLOPs.
- **Old-task retention:** Score A and rare B immediately upon regime return (measuring both rehydration latency and zero-shot post-rehydration accuracy) as well as during C (ensuring false rehydration does not poison C).

### NNL11 · Zero-gradient cytoplasmic anastomosis (fast-state mixing between specialists)
- **Mechanism:** Allow two independently adapted learners (models or agents) with compatible fast-state substrates—one habituated to a shifted nuisance or environment calibration, one naive—to establish a temporary *anastomosis bridge* that equilibrates only their *fast working-state concentrations* (e.g., normalization statistics, short-term activation biases, or working-memory key-value pools) without averaging long-term weights or exchanging raw training data, then disconnect.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Model soups, FedAvg, and weight-space merging combine *slow parameters*, often causing destructive interference across distinct tasks; distillation requires shared unlabeled data. Inspired by *Physarum* plasmodial fusion transferring habituation purely via cytoplasmic flow, this primitive transfers *calibrated fast states* across incompatible long-term specialists.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train two tiny specialist agents on disjoint tasks A and C; expose only the C specialist to a new sensory/tool calibration nuisance (e.g., systematic offset or latency shift). Fuse fast states briefly, disconnect, and compare transfer to the naive A specialist against FedAvg weight averaging, LoRA merging, and example distillation at equal communication bytes.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that both A and C specialists retain their distinct core task accuracies after anastomosis, with zero cross-task weight contamination.

### NNL12 · Viscoelastic wall-relaxation timescale ladder (Maxwell/Kelvin-Voigt parameter elements)
- **Mechanism:** Replace scalar parameters with mechanically coupled viscoelastic elements `(elastic_fast, plastic_slow)` governed by constitutive stress-relaxation dynamics (mirroring *Physarum* actomyosin tube walls): incoming updates deform only the elastic spring, which automatically rebounds toward zero when a transient anomaly ends; permanent remodeling transfers into the plastic dashpot *solely* as a nonlinear function of sustained strain duration × magnitude—separating brief shocks from persistent regime shifts without any learning-rate schedule or task detector.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Momentum SGD, Adam, and fast-slow weight EMA ([Sandscapes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10903) explores self-modifying landscapes; [Physarum smart network adaptation](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-conmatphys-040821-115312) reviews viscoelastic wall mechanics) linearly integrate all gradients over time, so repeated high-magnitude spikes still corrupt slow weights. The primitive introduces a *yield-stress / viscoelastic creep threshold* so transient high-loss bursts deform elastically and rebound completely while low-magnitude persistent drifts remodel plastically.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stream a small NumPy regression/classification learner through alternating long persistent regimes (A, B, C) punctuated by intense, brief outlier bursts. Compare viscoelastic spring-dashpot parameters (with yield-stress creep) against EMA fast-slow weights, AdamW, and gradient clipping at identical 2× parameter state and compute.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure A and rare B retention after both intense outlier bursts (must rebound to ~0% permanent drop) and genuine persistent C shifts.

## C · Plants & Modular Sessile Learners

### NNL13 · Polar flux-driven transport canalization (auxin PIN-efflux highway carving)
- **Mechanism:** Equip every node in an undifferentiated message-passing network or agent workflow with directional egress gains (`PIN` efflux weights) that amplify *strictly in proportion to net directed information/credit flux* along an edge and decay whenever bidirectional chatter yields zero net flux. Repeated task flow spontaneously carves sparse, self-reinforcing directed highways out of a uniform graph and smoothly re-canalizes around damaged or shifted nodes without a global router.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Attention and top-k MoE routing learn edge weights by global backpropagation; [Coupled Learning](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.11.021045) uses local contrastive energy differences on undirected edges. Auxin canalization is a *strictly directed, net-flux positive-feedback symmetry-breaking primitive* that suppresses bidirectional cycles and carves tree-like pipelines locally.
- **Cheapest first test:** Construct a 2D lattice of small message-passing units tasked with routing multi-step transformations A, B, and C from changing input ports to output ports, including mid-stream node knockouts. Compare polar flux canalization against end-to-end learned sparse attention and undirected coupled learning at equal parameter and FLOP budgets.
- **Old-task retention:** Re-test old A and rare B input-output ports after C carves a new highway and after a node knockout forces re-canalization, checking for cross-highway leakage.

### NNL14 · Out-of-band airborne volatile stress priming (HIPV non-local warning)
- **Mechanism:** Allow any module in a modular model or multi-tool agent that encounters a local distribution break to emit a tiny, domain-blind *volatile stress broadcast* out-of-band (bypassing the normal compute graph) to anatomically distant, non-adjacent modules. Receiving modules do *not* copy the sender's policy or data; instead, they locally shift their own read/write thresholds into a pre-armed defensive posture *before* the shift propagates to their inputs.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Global anomaly detectors centralize state; [NeuMoSync](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04358) learns end-to-end global neuromodulation. Inspired by plant herbivore-induced plant volatiles (HIPVs) warning distant branches across air gaps, this primitive uses *decentralized, content-free out-of-band broadcasts* to pre-arm downstream modules' local plasticity gates ahead of causal arrival.
- **Cheapest first test:** Build a multi-module agent pipeline where a schema/environment shift hits an upstream tool first and reaches downstream tools 5–20 episodes later. Compare out-of-band volatile priming against normal graph message passing, a centralized anomaly flag, and unprimed local adaptation at equal communication bits.
- **Old-task retention:** Check A and rare B tasks on primed downstream modules during false alarms (verifying pre-arming does not degrade normal A/B performance) and after genuine shifts arrive.

### NNL15 · Counter-current xylem/phloem bus separation (tension-pulled evidence vs. pressure-pushed allocation)
- **Mechanism:** Physically separate an agent's or layered model's inter-module communication into two non-mixing, opposite-direction buses: an upward *demand-pulled* bus ("xylem") carrying raw, unparsed anomalies and unmet constraints strictly from input/tool leaves toward core synthesis, and a downward *supply-pushed* bus ("phloem") carrying synthesized context, skill allocations, and capacity budgets from core to leaves. Neither bus can write onto the other's channel, preventing top-down priors from overwriting raw anomaly reports.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Standard transformers and hierarchical agents multiplex bottom-up observations and top-down beliefs inside one shared residual stream or message bus; predictive coding passes errors upward and predictions downward but couples them at every layer via shared linear weights. The primitive enforces *strict physical non-mixing between raw demand pull and synthesized supply push* across long-horizon agent workflows.
- **Cheapest first test:** Run a multi-layer symbolic/LLM-free planning agent under a subtle tool-spec shift where strong top-down priors normally rationalize away raw leaf errors. Compare physically separated counter-current buses against a single unified residual/message bus with typed headers at equal total channel bandwidth.
- **Old-task retention:** Score A and rare B workflows after C shifts, verifying that uncorrupted upward raw anomalies do not destabilize downward supply allocations for unaffected old leaves.

### NNL16 · Guard-cell hysteretic I/O aperture memory (stomatal drought memory)
- **Mechanism:** Equip every external input channel, sensor, or tool interface of an agent/model with a stateful *hysteretic aperture* (distinct closing vs. reopening thresholds, driven by past channel-failure stress). After a channel delivers poisoned, noisy, or shifted data, its aperture snaps partially shut and requires *sustained* clean evidence to gradually reopen—trading short-term throughput for protection of internal weights/memory without altering internal parameters.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Confidence gating and EMA trust weights are memoryless or symmetric in opening/closing; [Harness Continual Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19013) adapts prompts/memories rather than stateful asymmetric I/O valves. Stomatal guard-cell hysteresis provides a *two-threshold asymmetric valve primitive* that remembers past channel droughts at O(1) state per channel.
- **Cheapest first test:** Feed a continual learner multiple noisy/intermittently poisoned sensory or tool channels that alternate between clean and corrupted bursts. Compare hysteretic stomatal apertures against symmetric EMA trust gating, static confidence thresholding, and unweighted ingestion at equal per-channel state.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure A and rare B accuracy after intermittent poisoning on shared channels, checking both protection against corruption and recovery speed once clean A/B inputs resume.

### NNL17 · Apically-suppressed totipotent reserve buds (meristematic axillary dormancy)
- **Mechanism:** Attach tiny, zero-inference-cost *undifferentiated reserve buds* directly to intermediate structural nodes at every level of a hierarchical model or agent workflow. While the parent branch downstream of a node maintains low error, continuous "apical suppression" keeps the local bud inert; the moment a shift breaks a mid-level branch, suppression lifts locally and *that exact node's bud* sprouts into a fresh sub-branch reusing the intact upstream stem, leaving sibling branches untouched.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Growing Networks with Autonomous Pruning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19759) and [DEN](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.01547) expand width/experts globally or at task boundaries; continual backprop reinitializes dead units uniformly. The primitive is *local, hierarchically anchored reserve dormancy* released strictly by the *absence* of downstream apical health, localizing structural growth to the exact broken sub-tree.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train a small tree-structured hierarchical classifier/agent on A and B; break only one mid-level branch with shift C while keeping the root and sibling branches valid. Compare apically-suppressed local buds against top-level expert addition (Progressive Nets), global dead-unit reinitialization, and fine-tuning at equal total parameter budget.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify zero degradation on sibling A/B branches and test whether old queries routed through the intact upstream stem still resolve cleanly after bud outgrowth.

### NNL18 · Allelopathic write-zone exclusion (root-exudate suppression of rival modules)
- **Mechanism:** Once a learned module or agent skill achieves stable competence over a region of input space, let it deposit a lightweight *inhibitory exclusion field* that suppresses *only* competing write proposals (gradient steps or rule edits from rival modules) inside its established domain, while still allowing downstream modules to *read* its outputs freely. This prevents newly arriving tasks from colonizing already-solved representational niches.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Hard Voronoi/spatial partitioning blocks cross-module reading and sharing; global EWC regularizes all weights uniformly regardless of input-space overlap. Allelopathy decouples *read openness* from *rival-write exclusion* in input/feature space, letting modules share features while defending their write territory.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train a multi-module learner on overlapping regression/classification domains A, rare B, and C where C's training distribution partially encroaches on B's rare niche. Compare allelopathic write-exclusion fields against Voronoi hard routing, global EWC, and standard replay at equal memory bytes.

## E · Ecosystems & Community Dynamics

- **Nearest prior / gap:** Warmup/decay LR schedules and two-stage distillation try to transition *the same* model from plastic to stable or compress one finished policy into another. Ecological succession separates *substrate-transforming pioneers* (which are expected to be outcompeted and discarded) from *climax learners* that never touch raw unnormalized shifts.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stream a raw non-stationary feature shift (drifting scales, missing fields, noisy encodings) to a small continual learner. - **Old-task retention:** Test A and rare B climax modules after a new pioneer transforms shift C and retires, confirming that pioneer plasticity never touches old climax weights.

### NNL26 · Keystone-predator suppression of dominant territory expansion (competitive-exclusion prevention)
- **Mechanism:** Introduce an active *keystone suppressor* operator that monitors which learned skill, feature subspace, or expert is expanding its representational territory fastest during long stretches of frequent-task data, and selectively dampens *that dominant module's expansion* (not its existing accuracy). By preventing competitive exclusion from monopolizing shared capacity, rare specialists survive indefinitely without needing uniform input frequency or replay.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Switch-Transformer MoE load-balancing losses force *uniform routing frequency* across experts, which breaks when rare tasks genuinely have low input frequency; [Training Ecosystems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30109) studies learning in simulated ecosystems rather than keystone-regulated CL architectures. Keystone suppression caps *territory colonization* by dominant modules while respecting true skewed task frequencies.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train a shared-capacity modular network on a severely imbalanced stream (95% frequent task A/C, 5% rare task B, then a long stretch with 0% B). - **Old-task retention:** Evaluate rare B accuracy after 1,000+ steps of zero-B frequent C traffic, verifying that B's unvisited niche was not colonized by C.

### NNL27 · Cue-gated dormant seed-bank storage effect (Chesson temporal coexistence)

- **Nearest prior / gap:** Experience replay stores *data* rather than buffered *competitor policies*; [MAP-Elites](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909) maintains a static behavioral archive for offline illumination rather than cue-gated online germination under shifting environments. - **Cheapest first test:** Run a non-stationary multi-regime bandit / control stream where regimes vanish for long horizons and return unpredictably with partial environmental cues. - **Nearest prior / gap:** [Growing Neural Cellular Automata](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/) train a fixed local rule for one static target image; [Baluška & Levin (2016)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2016.00902/full) establish bioelectric `V_mem` patterns as rewritable anatomical memory in non-neural tissues.
