# Test-time continual learners (depth 3): Self-organizing tacit-to-explicit ontologies & compositional interaction-level handoff

Depth 3 child of [PR #376](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/376) (`tree/test-time-learners-3.md`, deepening `T3-24` and `T3-07`, and folding in cross-pool round 108 `REX2` and `REX4`, stacked on [PR #374](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/374) and [PR #373](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/373)) · 30 candidate primitives (`T4-01`–`T4-30`) · All tagged **Ambitious but doable (P)** · Ideas only; no experiments run

**Thesis.** PR #376 showed that micro-engineering tweaks to absorption meters or residual masks are implementation details, not new continual-learning mechanisms. What survived at tier 3 are two genuinely big architectural ideas:
1. **(a) Conflict-driven tacit-to-explicit promotion (`T3-24`):** A continual learner's boundary between *implicit/sub-symbolic weights* (System 1) and *explicit editable text* (System 2) should **move itself**—promoting a tacit residual direction into a first-class symbolic concept *only once it keeps drawing user exceptions*, while leaving uncontested habits fast and compiled in weights.
2. **(b) Compositional interaction-level handoff (`T3-07`):** Knowing rule $R_1$ and rule $R_2$ individually in weights does *not* imply their interaction $R_1 \wedge R_2$ is absorbed (the parametric two-hop/compositionality gap). A continual learner must therefore treat **interactions as first-class objects**—holding back handoff (or retaining minimal interaction contracts / allocating bilinear interaction weights or test-time recurrence) until the *interaction itself* is absorbed.

The deeper unification of this node is that **(a) and (b) are two faces of how a continual learner discovers missing structure**: *exception pressure* reveals when a tacit habit must become an explicit symbol, and *compositional failure* reveals when two solo weight deltas need an explicit interaction circuit (or when their colliding tacit residuals must be promoted into a cross-rule contract). The 30 primitives below push for **architectural mechanisms**, not hyperparameter choices: self-organizing System 1↔2 lifecycles (Section A), social/developmental promotion dynamics (Section B), compositional interaction circuits and handoff (Section C), and unified compositional-collision promotion, continual interaction hypergraphs, and cross-pool round-108 `REX2` (approved failure-witness memory gating context retirement & targeted micro-TTT) and `REX4` (per-region counterfactual residual triage: keep, adjust, or promote to text) (Section D).

## Section A — Self-organizing System 1 ↔ System 2 ontology (mechanisms for (a))

### T4-01 · Bidirectional System 1 ↔ System 2 lifecycle (promote on exceptions, re-submerge on stability) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** One-way promotion eventually bloats context with stale explicit rules. Make the boundary between tacit weights and explicit text *bidirectional*: a tacit residual direction promotes into an explicit editable rule once it accumulates $\ge k$ user exceptions, and once a promoted explicit rule runs for $M$ consecutive queries with zero exceptions, it **re-submerges back into tacit weights**—creating a self-regulating symbolic working memory whose context footprint stays bounded across lifelong streams.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Building Intelligent Agents with Neuro-Symbolic Concepts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06191) grows symbolic concept libraries monotonically; a *closed-loop bidirectional tacit ↔ explicit lifecycle* governed by exception rate is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on a 200-query stream with 12 rules where 4 tacit habits draw bursts of user exceptions and then stabilize for 80 queries; compare bidirectional lifecycle vs. fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on peak context length, exception accuracy, and stability-phase latency. Kill if monotonic promotion without re-submersion stays within the same context budget and accuracy.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) after both promotion and re-submersion transitions.

### T4-02 · Cross-rule latent habit factoring (one promoted meta-rule replacing $N$ rule patches) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** A single unverbalized tacit habit (e.g., an implicit tie-breaking bias, hedging style, or default ordering) often causes exceptions across *five different explicit rules*. Track shared activation/gradient directions across exceptions belonging to *distinct* rules; when one tacit direction explains exceptions across $\ge 3$ rules, promote it as **one global meta-rule** rather than patching each rule separately—compressing $N$ local exceptions into 1 factored concept.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [OntoAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21983) abstracts hypotheses from symbolic action logs; *discovering and promoting a shared sub-symbolic weight direction that causes exceptions across distinct rules* into a single meta-rule is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 10 distinct rules sharing 2 planted latent tacit biases (e.g., always rounding down / always deferring to alphabetical order) that users repeatedly correct across different rules; compare cross-rule meta-promotion vs. per-rule fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on zero-shot transfer of the fix to the *remaining uncorrected rules* sharing that habit. Kill if per-rule patching generalizes equally well to untouched rules sharing the habit.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ (which do not share the factored habit) suffer zero accuracy or ECE drop.

### T4-03 · Behavioral counterfactual concept naming (induce the rule by flipping the residual, not reading weights) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Decoding weights directly into text hallucinates because weight coordinates are opaque. Instead, when a tacit direction $v$ accumulates exceptions, perform **causal counterfactual intervention** ($+v$ vs. $-v$ on the exception-bearing inputs) to generate minimal behavioral contrast pairs $(x, y_{+v}, y_{-v})$, then let the model induce the symbolic rule *purely from the observed input-output difference*—grounding every promoted symbol in causal behavior rather than weight-space guesswork.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [PRISM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09563) and Weight Oracles decode activations/weights directly; *causal intervention contrast synthesis* to name a contested tacit direction without any weight-reader model is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 10 rules with planted tacit sub-behaviors drawing exceptions; compare counterfactual contrast naming vs. direct activation/weight decompilation, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on human/oracle faithfulness of the promoted rule and post-edit exception accuracy. Kill if direct decompilation achieves equal faithfulness and post-edit accuracy.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm intervention probing is read-only and leaves untouched old rules $A/B$ unchanged.

### T4-04 · Concept mitosis (bifurcating a contradictory tacit direction into two orthogonal symbols) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** When a single tacit residual direction draws *mutually contradictory* user exceptions in different contexts (e.g., "be terse" in alerts vs. "explain thoroughly" in audits), promoting it into one monolithic rule causes edit oscillation. Detect opposing exception gradients along the same residual direction and perform **concept mitosis**: split the single tacit direction into two context-disjoint explicit symbolic predicates $C_1, C_2$ so future edits target each regime independently.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Soft Ontology Priors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30391) uses a fixed concept set; *online concept mitosis* triggered by opposing user-exception gradients on one tacit weight direction is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 rules where 3 tacit directions draw opposing user exceptions across two latent context regimes; compare concept mitosis vs. single-rule promotion, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on regime-specific accuracy and edit oscillation count. Kill if a single promoted rule with an "if/else" clause stabilizes equally fast without mitosis.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ to verify mitosis does not fragment unrelated task representations.

### T4-05 · Anti-crystallization immunity (protecting irreducibly continuous tacit skills from verbalization damage) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Not every contested tacit direction *can* be expressed in words—forcing continuous calibration, probabilistic thresholds, or stylistic cadence into English prose causes lossy oscillation where every text edit breaks another case. Measure **verbalization stability** on the first promotion trial: if text-space editing fails to reduce exception rate across 2 consecutive corrections while continuous residual steering succeeds, tag that direction **irreducibly tacit** and immunize it from future symbolic promotion.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Every neuro-symbolic and decompilation framework assumes more explicit symbolic coverage is always better; *online detection and immunization of verbalization-lossy directions* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 symbolic tacit habits + 6 continuous calibration/threshold tacit habits, both drawing user corrections; compare immunity-gated promotion vs. always-promote, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on both symbolic accuracy and continuous calibration ECE. Kill if promoting continuous habits into prose matches continuous steering without oscillation.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ retain baseline accuracy and ECE across both symbolic and immunized updates.

### T4-06 · Online concept invention by exception compression — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Users rarely name the missing abstraction directly—they give 5 concrete exceptions ("not for item $X$", "not on Tuesday", ...). When a tacit residual direction accumulates $\ge 4$ concrete exceptions whose activations cluster along one latent axis, **invent a single higher-level symbolic predicate** (compressing the 4+ ad-hoc exceptions into 1 new concept) and replace the exception list with that concept, enabling zero-shot generalization to the 5th unseen instance of the same latent category.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Auto-Formalizing Neuro-Symbolic Predictors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01519) formalizes known rules; *compressing accumulated concrete user exceptions along a tacit residual axis into a newly invented latent concept* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 latent exception categories (giving 4 concrete examples per category, testing on 4 held-out instances); compare concept-invention promotion vs. fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on held-out 5th-instance generalization. Kill if storing the 4 raw exceptions in context or TTT generalizes equally well to the held-out instances.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ to verify the invented concept does not over-trigger on unrelated domains.

### T4-07 · Formal executable-guard promotion (tacit weight → verifiable symbolic predicate, not prose) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Natural-language prose is ambiguous and drifts on re-absorption. When a contested tacit direction governs deterministic/structural logic (bounds, types, precedence, set membership), promote it directly into a **verifiable executable guard** (a 3-line formal predicate or symbolic constraint evaluated alongside the model) instead of prose—eliminating linguistic ambiguity while still allowing human inspection and exact editing.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Learning Symbolic Constraint Representations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12267) learns constraints from scratch via queries; *promoting a contested neural weight residual directly into an executable formal guard* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 10 structured/numeric/schema rules with contested tacit edge cases; compare executable-guard promotion vs. prose promotion, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on exact constraint satisfaction and multi-edit drift. Kill if prose promotion matches exact constraint satisfaction across 3 sequential edits.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ suffer zero interference when executable guards are added or edited.

### T4-08 · Long-horizon upstream tacit credit assignment — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** In multi-step reasoning or tool-use chains, a user exception at step $T$ is often caused by an unverbalized tacit habit (e.g., an implicit framing or assumption) applied at step $t \ll T$. Use counterfactual replay on the trajectory's residual activations to identify *which earlier step's tacit direction* maximizes flip-probability of the step-$T$ error, and promote that **upstream tacit assumption** rather than patching the downstream step-$T$ output.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Standard model editing and decompilation operate on single-turn input→output pairs; *multi-step counterfactual credit assignment to promote upstream tacit habits* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 3-step reasoning chains where a planted step-1 tacit assumption causes step-3 user exceptions across 10 varied step-3 phrasings; compare upstream tacit promotion vs. step-3 patching, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on generalization to unseen step-3 phrasings. Kill if step-3 patching generalizes equally well.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old single-step and multi-step rules $A/B$ to confirm zero upstream disruption.

## Section B — Social, developmental & evolutionary dynamics of tacit promotion

### T4-09 · Per-user / per-domain ontology branching over a shared tacit substrate — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** In multi-user or multi-domain deployment, User A contests tacit habit $H_1$ while User B leaves $H_1$ uncontested and contests $H_2$. Keep one shared, fast tacit weight substrate and **branch the promoted explicit ontology per user/domain**—so User A's exception history promotes $H_1$ into User A's editable text layer without forcing User B to pay context cost or suffer behavioral drift on $H_1$.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Multi-tenant LoRA serving separates full adapters per user; *sharing one tacit weight substrate while branching only the exception-promoted symbolic ontology per user* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B serving 2 simulated users with 6 shared tacit habits where User A overrides habits 1–3 and User B overrides habits 4–6; compare branched-ontology promotion vs. global promotion, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on per-user accuracy, cross-user leakage, and total memory/context. Kill if separate per-user LoRAs match accuracy and zero leakage at equal bytes.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure shared untouched old rules $A/B$ across both users.

### T4-10 · Shadow evolutionary competition between tacit and promoted symbolic paths — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Treat every tacit-to-explicit promotion as a *hypothesis*, not a permanent decree. After promoting a contested tacit direction to explicit text $s$, run both the promoted symbolic path and the original tacit weight path in lightweight shadow evaluation on the next $M$ user queries; **commit the promotion only if its out-of-sample exception generalization strictly beats the tacit path**, else auto-demote and mark the verbalization failed.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** No existing continual decompilation loop validates promoted symbols via *online out-of-sample evolutionary selection* against the tacit representation they replaced.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 12 contested tacit directions (6 cleanly verbalizable, 6 subtly mis-verbalized by a noisy decoder); compare shadow-selected promotion vs. unconditional promotion, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on held-out post-promotion accuracy. Kill if unconditional promotion never hurts held-out accuracy on the 6 noisy cases.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ across both committed and auto-demoted trials.

### T4-11 · Sleep-phase adversarial dreaming for pre-emptive tacit crystallization — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Inspired by [Do Agents Need to Sleep?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02216), don't wait for 3 costly user failures in production: during idle/sleep windows, sample adversarial counterfactual queries around high-variance tacit residual directions and check whether the model's own explicit rule-checker contradicts its tacit weight output. Pre-crystallize *only* those tacit directions that trigger internal self-contradiction into editable candidate rules for fast one-click correction.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Do Agents Need to Sleep?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02216) motivates offline memory consolidation conceptually; *sleep-phase adversarial self-contradiction probing of tacit weight residuals to pre-promote latent failure cliffs* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B with 10 absorbed rules containing 4 hidden tacit contradictions; run a 50-query sleep dreaming pass before deployment and measure live user exception count before convergence vs. reactive-only promotion, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit. Kill if sleep dreaming produces $>50\%$ false-positive promotions or fails to reduce live user exceptions.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify sleep dreaming and pre-promotion leave untouched old rules $A/B$ intact.

### T4-12 · Two-stage promotion via a compact non-linguistic discrete code — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Inspired by [Learning a Non-Linguistic Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31192), full English prose is often too verbose and brittle for first-stage promotion. When a tacit residual first draws exceptions, promote it into a **1–4 token discrete symbolic code** (a learned latent tag in KV that is editable/switchable without full prose expansion), and expand that discrete code into full human-readable natural language *only* when a human explicitly requests inspection or writes a free-text override.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Learning a Non-Linguistic Code](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31192) learns compact rule codes from reward; using a *compact discrete code as an intermediate rung between tacit weights and full English text* in continual learning is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 12 contested tacit directions comparing direct English promotion vs. two-stage discrete-code → English promotion vs. fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on context tokens used, exception accuracy, and human-override latency. Kill if short English prose (10 tokens) matches discrete codes on both fidelity and context cost.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ to verify discrete code tokens do not collide with base vocabulary behavior.

### T4-13 · Causal-mechanism promotion (promoting *why* the habit fired, not just *what* it outputs) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Promoting a surface correlation ("for input $X$, output $Y$") breaks on the very next domain shift. When promoting a contested tacit direction, extract a minimal **2-node causal statement** ("because tacit detector $Z$ fired on attribute $A$, weight shifted toward $Y$") by ablating input attributes one by one—so the user can edit either the *trigger attribute $A$* or the *consequent $Y$* cleanly across unseen inputs.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Standard instruction extraction (PRISM) outputs flat behavioral descriptions; *attribute-ablation causal-link promotion* from a contested weight residual is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 10 rules where user exceptions target the *underlying cause* rather than one surface example; compare causal-link promotion vs. surface-rule promotion, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on OOD exception transfer. Kill if surface-rule promotion transfers equally well to shifted inputs.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ before and after causal-link edits.

### T4-14 · Homeostatic working-memory budget (fixed-capacity explicit rule competition) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Impose a strict biological-style working-memory cap of $B$ explicit promoted rules in context. Every new tacit direction that earns promotion under exception pressure must compete for a slot—forcing the learner to either **merge two overlapping explicit rules** or **re-submerge the lowest-exception explicit rule** back into tacit weights, guaranteeing $O(1)$ context cost over arbitrarily long continual lifetimes.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Existing agent memories grow unboundedly or evict by naive LRU; *exception-rate-driven homeostatic competition between symbolic promotion, rule merging, and tacit re-submersion* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on a 30-rule continual stream with a hard cap of $B=5$ explicit slots; compare homeostatic competition vs. LRU eviction, fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on cumulative exception accuracy and average context length. Kill if LRU eviction of explicit rules matches homeostatic competition.
- **Old-task retention:** Track untouched old rules $A/B$ across all 30 rules to verify re-submersion never erodes older tasks.

## Section C — Compositional interaction circuits & interaction-level handoff (mechanisms for (b))

### T4-15 · Bilinear cross-rule interaction fast weights ($\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Additive solo updates $\Delta W_1 + \Delta W_2$ are *structurally linear* and cannot represent non-additive rule interactions ($R_1 \wedge R_2 \neq R_1 + R_2$, e.g., XOR, override, or joint constraint). Introduce a dedicated **bilinear interaction fast-weight patch** $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2} \cdot g_1(h) g_2(h)$ that activates *strictly* when both rule-1 and rule-2 detectors co-fire, and hand off two-rule KV queries *only* once $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ absorbs the joint interaction.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Routing Is Not Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03150) and [LiST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22370) linearly combine solo LoRAs; allocating a *multiplicatively co-gated bilinear interaction weight* specifically to absorb non-additive two-rule interactions for handoff is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 non-additive rule pairs (override, exception-of-rule, joint-condition) where each rule passes solo in weights; compare bilinear $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ handoff vs. additive $\Delta W_1 + \Delta W_2$, single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on two-rule accuracy, solo accuracy, and peak ECE. Kill if additive solo adapters fine-tuned jointly match bilinear $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ without hurting solo accuracy.
- **Old-task retention:** Because $g_1(h) g_2(h) \approx 0$ on solo and old-task queries, verify zero accuracy or ECE loss on solo $R_1, R_2$ and untouched old rules $A/B$.

### T4-16 · Interaction-only KV stub (evict full solo rules, keep a 10-token interaction contract in KV) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Holding two full 500-token rules in context just because their *interaction* isn't absorbed wastes 95% of KV memory. Once $R_1$ and $R_2$ each pass solo absorption in weights, **evict both full rule definitions into weights** and leave *only a 10-token interaction-resolution stub* in KV (e.g., *"When $R_1$ and $R_2$ both match, apply $R_2$ first then $R_1$"*), releasing even that stub once the interaction weight absorbs it.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** All prior context-distillation methods (`OPCD`, `Cortex`, `NCA`) treat each rule as an atomic keep-or-evict span; *factoring KV eviction into solo-rule weights + minimal interaction-only KV stub* is a novel memory-compression primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 verbose (200-token) interacting rule pairs; compare interaction-only KV stub vs. keep-full-context, single-rule per-query handoff (evict both), global-factor, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on KV tokens saved, two-rule accuracy, and peak ECE. Kill if evicting both solo rules breaks the 10-token stub's ability to steer the interaction.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ and solo $R_1, R_2$ queries to confirm the stub never interferes outside joint matches.

### T4-17 · Sheaf / cocycle gluing-defect handoff condition — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** View each absorbed solo rule as a local section on its single-rule domain and each two-rule query as an overlap. Measure the **gluing defect**—the logit/output divergence between weights-only joint evaluation, sequential application $R_1 \circ R_2$ (and $R_2 \circ R_1$), and the KV reference—and permit two-rule handoff *only* when the gluing defect vanishes across the overlap, formalizing compositional absorption as a topological consistency condition.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Do World Models Learn Global Understanding?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34058) notes fragmented local-vs-global behavior in LLMs; using a *sheaf gluing-defect metric* as the live handoff gate for multi-rule queries is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 rule pairs (4 consistent/commutative, 4 order-dependent/conflicting); compare gluing-defect gating vs. single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on two-rule peak ECE and accuracy. Kill if solo absorption scores already predict gluing defect ($r > 0.9$).
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ remain invariant as overlaps are checked and handed off.

### T4-18 · Depth-staged compositional circuit verification (anti-superposition gate) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** [Why Knowing Both Hops Is Not Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07261) and [How Do LMs Compose Functions?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01685) show two-rule parametric composition succeeds only when rules organize sequentially across depth (early-layer $R_1$ writing an intermediate representation read by late-layer $R_2$) rather than colliding in the same layer. Measure cross-layer causal flow between $\Delta W_1$ and $\Delta W_2$—holding KV live while the two rules remain *superposed in conflicting layers* and handing off only once a depth-staged compositional circuit forms.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Why Knowing Both Hops Is Not Enough](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07261) diagnoses two-hop layer failure offline; *online depth-staging / anti-superposition verification as a continual handoff gate* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 two-hop / sequential rule pairs (comparing same-layer LoRA placement vs. depth-staged LoRA placement); evaluate two-rule accuracy and peak ECE against single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls. Kill if same-layer adapters compose as reliably as depth-staged circuits.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure untouched old rules $A/B$ across early and late layers to verify depth-staging reduces cross-task interference.

### T4-19 · Non-commutativity & precedence-order absorption gate — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** For non-commutative rule pairs ($R_1(R_2(x)) \neq R_2(R_1(x))$), weights often absorb both solo rules and output a *commutative blend* on joint queries, losing precedence. Gate two-rule handoff specifically on **asymmetry absorption**: weights must match KV's *order-sensitive* preference between $R_1 \circ R_2$ and $R_2 \circ R_1$ (measured on paired precedence probes) before KV attenuates.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Shattered Compositionality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22510) shows brittle order/structure failure in Transformers; *explicitly gating handoff on non-commutative precedence absorption* is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 non-commutative rule pairs (e.g., string transform + filter, discount + cap) tested in both orders; compare precedence-gated handoff vs. single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on precedence accuracy and peak ECE. Kill if single-rule per-query handoff preserves precedence equally well.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm solo accuracy on $R_1, R_2$ and untouched old rules $A/B$ stays at baseline.

### T4-20 · Compositional grokking phase-transition detector for KV release — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Implicit multi-rule reasoning in weights exhibits a sudden *grokking phase transition* ([Grokked Transformers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15071), [Is Grokking Worthwhile?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.09049)): a long plateau of 100% solo mastery + poor joint generalization followed by rapid circuit formation. Track representation-rank collapse / gradient-coherence jump across joint queries to detect the **end of the pre-grokking plateau**, holding KV throughout the plateau and releasing it abruptly only once the generalizing interaction circuit snaps into place.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Grokking detectors are studied on offline synthetic training curves; using an *online compositional grokking detector to time live KV-to-weight handoff* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 compositional rule families distilled over 50 steps, plotting solo accuracy, unseen joint accuracy, and representation-rank jump; compare grokking-gated release vs. single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls. Kill if joint accuracy rises smoothly with solo accuracy (no plateau/transition) on these rules.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ before and after the grokking transition to ensure circuit reorganization causes zero forgetting.

### T4-21 · Test-time recurrence as a compute bridge for unabsorbed interactions — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** What if a two-rule query arrives whose solo rules $R_1, R_2$ are already in weights, KV has been evicted for memory reasons, and the 1-step *interaction* is not yet absorbed? Bridge the gap with **test-time depth/recurrence**: execute a 2-step latent loop (step 1 runs with $\Delta W_1$ active to produce an intermediate state; step 2 feeds that state through $\Delta W_2$), trading *extra test-time compute* for *unabsorbed compositional weights* until the 1-step interaction circuit $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ finishes compiling!
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Bridging the Compositionality Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39016) uses architectural recurrence statically; *dynamically allocating a temporary 2-step recurrent bridge only while a rule pair's 1-step interaction is unabsorbed—then retiring the extra pass once compiled*—unifies thinking-vs-updating with compositional handoff.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 two-hop rule pairs with solo rules in weights and KV evicted; compare 2-step recurrent bridge (retired post-compilation) vs. 1-step weights-only, single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls at matched FLOPs. Kill if 2-step sequential application of $\Delta W_1 \to \Delta W_2$ fails to beat 1-step additive weights on unabsorbed pairs.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify solo queries and untouched old rules $A/B$ take the 1-step fast path with zero accuracy or latency penalty.

### T4-22 · Double causal-mediation check against compositional shortcuts — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Weights frequently pass two-rule probes via *single-rule shortcuts* (guessing the joint output from one salient keyword without actually composing both rules). Before certifying interaction absorption and releasing KV, run a **double causal-mediation check**: ablating *either* $\Delta W_1$ alone *or* $\Delta W_2$ alone must degrade the joint output in the same pattern as ablating $R_1$ or $R_2$ in KV—blocking premature handoff of brittle shortcut solutions.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Multi-Hop Composition is Bound by Pretraining Exposure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09338) exposes shortcut vs. true composition offline; *double causal-ablation verification as an online interaction-handoff gate* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 rule pairs containing 4 pairs with planted surface-cue shortcuts during training, then tested on cue-swapped OOD compositions; compare double-mediation gating vs. raw joint-accuracy gating, single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls. Kill if raw joint-accuracy gating already rejects the 4 shortcut pairs.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm mediation ablations are temporary/read-only with zero effect on untouched old rules $A/B$.

## Section D — Unified theory: compositional collisions driving tacit promotion & continual hypergraphs

### T4-23 · Compositional collision as the trigger for tacit-to-explicit promotion (unifying (a) + (b)) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** The deepest bridge between (a) and (b): often *neither* rule $R_1$ nor $R_2$ has a bug alone—their **unverbalized tacit residuals** $\Delta W_{\text{resid},1}$ and $\Delta W_{\text{resid},2}$ collide when a query activates both! When a two-rule query repeatedly fails interaction handoff or draws user exceptions despite solo mastery, **promote the colliding tacit interaction direction into an explicit cross-rule interaction contract**, let the user/model edit it in text, and compile the resolved contract into $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ (`T4-15`).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** No existing work connects *compositional interference between two absorbed rules* to *tacit-to-explicit symbolic promotion* of their hidden interaction boundary.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 rule pairs where solo rules pass 100% but their unstated tacit defaults collide on joint queries (drawing user exceptions); compare collision-triggered interaction promotion vs. fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, AlphaEdit, and single-rule per-query handoff. Kill if standard single-rule promotion or TTT overwrite resolves the tacit collision without hurting solo behavior.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure solo accuracy on $R_1, R_2$ and untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) after promoting and compiling the cross-rule contract.

### T4-24 · Sparse $k$-way continual interaction hypergraph (additive pairs vs. non-additive cliques) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Scaling (b) to 50+ continual rules cannot afford $O(N^2)$ interaction checks. Maintain a **sparse interaction hypergraph** over absorbed rules: pairs whose activation supports are disjoint or whose gluing defect is zero stay unlinked (*additive-independent*, safe for immediate solo handoff), while only pairs/triples with non-zero gluing defect form *hyperedges* that receive interaction-only KV stubs (`T4-16`) and bilinear interaction weights (`T4-15`).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Audit Before You Merge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10684) audits static adapter pairs post-hoc; an *online sparse interaction hypergraph* governing which rule combinations get immediate solo handoff vs. guarded interaction handoff is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 16 continual rules where only 6 of the $\binom{16}{2}=120$ pairs are genuinely non-additive; compare sparse hypergraph handoff vs. all-pairs tracking, single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on memory/compute scaling, interacting-pair accuracy, and peak ECE. Kill if single-rule per-query handoff handles the 6 interacting pairs or if $>50\%$ of pairs require hyperedges.
- **Old-task retention:** Score all 16 solo rules and untouched old rules $A/B$ across continual ingestion.

### T4-25 · Virtual co-occurrence dreaming at rule ingestion — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Two rules $R_1, R_2$ learned weeks apart may never co-occur until a high-stakes deployment query. Whenever a new rule $R_{\text{new}}$ enters, retrieve the top-3 existing absorbed rules with highest trigger-manifold overlap and synthesize 3 **virtual co-occurrence boundary queries**—immediately testing whether $(R_{\text{new}}, R_j)$ is additive or non-additive and pre-seeding an interaction stub (`T4-16`) or $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ before any live user hits the collision.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Knowledge Interaction in Hypernetwork CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15677) analyzes conflict on observed task mixtures; *ingestion-time virtual co-occurrence dreaming* to discover non-additive rule pairs before they ever co-occur in the wild is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B ingesting 12 rules sequentially where 4 interacting pairs never appear together until test time; compare virtual co-occurrence dreaming vs. reactive-only handoff, single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on first-encounter two-rule accuracy and peak ECE. Kill if virtual dreaming fails to discover $\ge 75\%$ of the 4 hidden interacting pairs.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm virtual dreaming updates only the interaction edge (or stub) and leaves all solo rules and untouched old rules $A/B$ intact.

### T4-26 · Emergent-capability-preserving continual handoff — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** [On Emergent Capabilities and Model Merging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24504) demonstrates that capabilities emerging from combining two skills can *vanish* when solo weights are merged additively. Probe for emergent two-rule behaviors present in KV (skills that neither $R_1$ nor $R_2$ produces alone) and **veto additive handoff** whenever an emergent capability drops below KV parity—forcing explicit interaction-weight compilation (`T4-15`) before KV release.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [On Emergent Capabilities and Model Merging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24504) measures emergent loss on offline static merges; *online emergent-capability veto gating during continual KV-to-weight handoff* is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 6 rule pairs exhibiting an emergent third behavior only when both rules co-apply; compare emergent-veto handoff vs. single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on emergent accuracy and peak ECE. Kill if solo handoff already preserves emergent two-rule behavior.
- **Old-task retention:** Score solo $R_1, R_2$ and untouched old rules $A/B$ to confirm veto-triggered interaction compilation causes zero regression.

### T4-27 · Zero-gradient interaction-weight hypernetwork (`(\Delta W_1, \Delta W_2, c_{12}) \mapsto \Delta W_{1\otimes 2}`) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Compiling $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ by gradient descent for every interacting pair is slow. Inspired by [Latent Merger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33048) and [Knowledge Interaction in Hypernetwork CL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15677), train a compact **meta-interaction hypernetwork** that takes two solo adapters $(\Delta W_1, \Delta W_2)$ plus a 1-line interaction contract $c_{12}$ and predicts the non-additive correction $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ in a *single forward pass*—enabling instant interaction-level handoff without test-time gradient loops.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Latent Merger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33048) merges document LoRAs in latent space; predicting an explicit *non-additive pairwise interaction delta* from two solo adapters + a 1-line interaction contract for live handoff is untested.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B training a small hypernetwork on 40 synthetic interacting rule pairs and testing zero-shot on 10 unseen rule pairs; compare hypernetwork $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ handoff vs. gradient-trained $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$, single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls. Kill if zero-shot hypernetwork predictions fail to beat additive $\Delta W_1 + \Delta W_2$ on unseen pairs.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify hypernetwork-synthesized $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$ has zero effect on solo rules and untouched old rules $A/B$.

### T4-28 · `TacitCompose-CL`: unified developmental benchmark for ontology growth & compositional handoff — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** A single CPU-runnable, multi-seed benchmark uniting (a) and (b): a 16-rule continual stream featuring (i) 4 cross-rule tacit habits (2 symbolic, 2 irreducibly continuous) that draw sequential user exceptions—including 1 contradictory regime requiring concept mitosis—and (ii) 6 non-additive / non-commutative rule pairs (including 2 tacit-residual collisions) whose solo mastery precedes joint mastery. Scores cumulative exception accuracy, context-budget efficiency, unseen-rule habit transfer, two-rule interaction accuracy, peak handoff ECE, and mandatory zero-reset old-task $A/B$ retention against all required controls for both (a) and (b).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** No continual-learning benchmark jointly evaluates *self-organizing tacit↔explicit ontology evolution* and *compositional interaction-level handoff* under strict zero-reset retention and matched controls.
- **Cheapest first test:** Run on Qwen2.5-0.5B across 3 seeds and verify that fixed-split/decompile-all/never-promote/AlphaEdit fail the exception-vs-context-vs-residual tradeoff while single-rule per-query handoff/global-factor/NCA fail non-additive two-rule accuracy and peak ECE. Kill if any single baseline solves both halves simultaneously.
- **Old-task retention:** Mandatory zero-reset $A/B$ accuracy and ECE floor logged after every promotion, re-submersion, and interaction handoff.

### T4-29 · Approved failure-witness memory for compositional handoff & targeted consolidation (`REX2`) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Deepens cross-pool round 108's `REX2` inside our compositional handoff architecture (`T4-15`–`T4-22`): instead of trusting aggregate absorption metrics, maintain a compact, persistent bank of **short approved failure witnesses**—minimal concrete inputs where partial absorption failed (paraphrases, two-rule compositions, and multi-step trajectories). Context (or the interaction-only KV stub `T4-16`) is **never retired until every active witness passes in weights-only mode**, and those exact witnesses serve as the surgical targets for micro-TTT / bilinear interaction compilation (`T4-15`), retiring each witness once absorbed and keeping a tiny regression subset as a permanent guard.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** `REX2` (cross-pool round 108), replay buffers, and [OPCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12275) replay raw training distributions without tying *individual approved failure witnesses* simultaneously to **context-retirement gating** and **targeted compositional micro-TTT**. Treating failure witnesses as executable acceptance contracts for KV retirement is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 8 interacting rule pairs + multi-step trajectory paraphrases where solo accuracy hits 95% while 6 concrete compositional/trajectory witnesses fail; compare witness-gated retirement + witness-targeted micro-TTT vs. single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls on witness pass rate, unseen compositional generalization, and peak ECE. Kill if random replay + global-factor retirement matches witness-gated retirement at equal gradient steps.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old rules $A/B$ (accuracy + ECE) and verify witness-targeted micro-TTT causes less forgetting than uniform replay.

### T4-30 · Per-region counterfactual residual triage: keep, adjust, or promote to text (`REX4`) — Ambitious but doable (P)
- **Mechanism:** Deepens cross-pool round 108's `REX4` inside our self-organizing System 1↔2 ontology (`T4-01`–`T4-08`): after any approved edit $s \to s'$, partition the input domain into local behavioral regions (base region, exception region, boundary/compositional overlap) and run **per-region counterfactual ablation tests** on the old tacit residual $\Delta W_{\text{resid}}$ to make a three-way structural decision *per region*: **(1) keep** $\Delta W_{\text{resid}}$ intact where it aids calibration/formatting without fighting $s'$; **(2) adjust** (rank-1 prune / re-anchor) where it mildly biases $s'$ on continuous dimensions; or **(3) promote to explicit text** where it causes discrete, recurring semantic conflict with $s'$—unifying residual preservation, surgical pruning, and tacit-to-explicit promotion into one region-aware decision law.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** `REX4` (cross-pool round 108), [AlphaEdit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02355), and whole-change decompilation apply one global policy across the entire input domain. *Per-region counterfactual three-way triage (keep / adjust / promote)* over a live residual after an approved edit is the candidate mechanism.
- **Cheapest first test:** Qwen2.5-0.5B on 10 rules where each rule's $\Delta W_{\text{resid}}$ behaves differently across 3 regions (benign calibration in region 1, mild threshold bias in region 2, hard semantic conflict in region 3) after an approved edit; compare per-region counterfactual triage vs. fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit on per-region accuracy, calibration ECE, and context cost. Kill if any single global policy (fixed split, decompile-all, or never-promote) matches per-region triage across all three regions.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify untouched old rules $A/B$ remain in the untouched "keep" regime with zero accuracy or ECE regression.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Does an exception-driven bidirectional System 1 ↔ System 2 lifecycle (`T4-01`, `T4-02`, `T4-04`, `T4-05`) coupled with per-region counterfactual residual triage (`T4-30` / `REX4`) outperform fixed split, smallest editable slice, decompile-all, never-promote, TTT overwrite, and AlphaEdit over a long continual stream?**
   This tests whether dynamic tacit-to-explicit promotion is a true continual-learning architecture rather than a heuristic: specifically whether per-region keep/adjust/promote triage (`REX4`), cross-rule habit factoring (`T4-02`), concept mitosis (`T4-04`), and anti-crystallization immunity (`T4-05`) preserve uncontested calibration while transferring fixes zero-shot and keeping context bounded via re-submersion (`T4-01`).

2. **Are additive solo weight deltas fundamentally unable to hand off non-additive/non-commutative two-rule queries—and do bilinear interaction weights (`T4-15`), interaction-only KV stubs (`T4-16`), and approved failure-witness gating (`T4-29` / `REX2`) strictly beat single-rule per-query handoff, global-factor, keep-in-context, NCA, and filler/entropy-matched controls at a fraction of full-context cost?**
   Tests the core structural thesis of (b): whether factoring two-rule memory into *evicted solo weights + 10-token interaction stub → bilinear $\Delta W_{1\otimes 2}$* captures keep-in-context accuracy at ~10% of the context cost where single-rule per-query handoff and NCA collapse.

3. **Can temporary test-time recurrence (`T4-21`) substitute for KV memory on unabsorbed two-rule interactions—and does retiring the extra recurrent pass once compositional grokking (`T4-20`) completes yield a clean compute-to-weights learning curve?**
   Directly connects continual learning to test-time scaling: instead of keeping rules in context forever or failing silently when evicted, the learner spends extra *depth/thinking* on novel rule combinations and amortizes that compute into 1-step interaction weights over time.

4. **Do compositional collisions between two solo-passing rules actually serve as the sharpest trigger for tacit-to-explicit promotion (`T4-23`)—and can a sparse interaction hypergraph (`T4-24`) + ingestion-time virtual dreaming (`T4-25`) catch non-additive pairs before live users hit them?**
   Tests the grand unification of (a) and (b): whether most real-world continual-learning failures are neither pure single-rule bugs nor pure forgetting, but *unverbalized pairwise interactions* that can be discovered proactively, promoted into explicit contracts, and compiled without touching old tasks $A/B$.
