# Successor-skill recovery

**Status:** Ideas only; no experiments, code, or GPU work. **Parent:** [Carrier recovery without rollback](carrier-recovery.md) ([PR #188](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/188)), cards 03 and 11. **Review seed:** [Flood triage, row 295](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/f81e66e0755e951fd1111bf9f0bf563ff5e6d2fc/reviews/flood-triage.md#L295) scored two directions 3: make the next skill the counterweight, and compile a source-free inverse of the spend on the witness interface alone. **Read first:** [Idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/f3dbc1cf3d7dc26e99fc657d0f2d19079e521dd6/reviews/idea-ledger.md).

## Can learning a skill replace a spent computation without buying back the past?

**Verdict: a candidate primitive only if learning the successor pays for a genuinely shared replacement and a bounded map restores useful witness service without changing any installed newer skill.** [Net2Net][net] and [distillation][kd] preserve or transfer functions; [progressive nets][pnn] reuse frozen features by adding capacity; [#177 regeneration][carriers] makes a fresh carrier. [GPM][gpm] protects activation subspaces, [ROME][rome] aims for edit locality, and [GRACE][grace] leaves base weights alone by routing through adapters. None is a novelty claim here. The distinct target is a successor that still needs the replacement for its **own** job, plus a map that restores the **same frozen witness** from the post-acquisition learner, without resurrecting the old carrier. The two bets can fail separately: sharing may help without certifiable recovery, or a map may work without the successor having paid for anything.

## Contract for the CPU first tests

- **Timeline and freeze.** A = original ordinary tasks; B = the useful admission that genuinely spends the carrier; C = every still-installed acquisition after B, including the successor under test. Protect B separately as well as A and C. Freeze witness payload, interface and legal bindings, finite input and continuation domains, utility criteria, and resource caps before B. Establish pre-spend useful service and post-spend failure at the same budget; then acquire the next truthful C, seal its behavior, and try recovery before revealing the delayed job. Require useful execution of the unchanged witness on that job, not just a repaired readout or good logits. No resetting parameters or optimizer, no ancestor checkpoint, teacher or old source examples in the candidate at recovery; any predeclared retained witness metadata is charged and cannot encode a restorable carrier.
- **Proof scope.** For post-acquisition state `s` and declared legal map `R`, certify `F_i(R(s), x) = F_i(s, x)` for every installed newer skill `i` and every input `x` in its promised domain. Compare full declared outputs, not just accuracy or training logits. For stateful skills use a paired transition invariant: equal observations and related next states for all declared events and horizons (or prove an inductive invariant). Extend the check to exposed intermediate writes, the witness's protected execution, and any ordinary suffix claimed in the proof; run the reference from `s` with the same external events and ordinary updates, but without recovery or witness writes. An atomic implementation must actually prevent visibility of the intermediate state. Proofs on finite domains or bounded suffixes say nothing about arbitrary unseen inputs or training. Also check acquisition-anchored A/B/all-C floors on held-out ordinary tasks at every deployed prefix and after an unassisted suffix; floors are not substitutes for exact C preservation.
- **Causal sharing.** Compare real and sham binding, and cross a shared successor with a utility-, margin-, data- and width-matched separately routed C fit. In shadow, intervene on just the nominated feature and look for loss of both C utility and restored witness service; test sufficiency with the declared binding and a feature-only substitution, not a whole-state swap. Shadow interventions are diagnostics, not legal deployed moves. Reject donors that were planted only for the witness, unused C branches, and wins explained by spare width or by matching C at lower utility.
- **Charge the stacked alternative.** Compare rollback plus replay of all later skills (full snapshots allowed only to that control), Net2Net + distillation + #177 card 20 regeneration, and a best applicable locality stack (projected updates, [SISA][sisa]-style shard retraining or GRACE re-insertion, and exact rechecking). Include no-write, separately routed C and exact joint search with the same legal information and moves on tiny instances. Report admissions, refusals, proof unknowns and true infeasibility separately. Charge peak and persistent bytes (including receipts, masks, transcripts and shadow copies), updates, examples, proof/check work, latency and displaced capacity; match acquisition utility and retention or show a frontier. No claims of learning from an enumeration whose moves plant the answer.
- **Ledger stop signs.** Exact restore and flat reacquisition are wins by construction; replay and save/restore get the same budget, not straw versions. The failed reserve/forecast pilots ([#9](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/9), [#21](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/21)) rule out selling a Gramian or spare-rank predictor alone. Functional syndromes ([#24](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/24)) found diffuse damage, so don't assume sparse interference to make a cheap inverse work. Self-quizzing replay ([#8](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/8)) gives no reason to credit teacher identity; effect-class distillation ([#13](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/13)) makes execution-reward replay a serious control, not a weaker label.

**Paper sanity check, not a training result.** In an exact finite linear host, suppose B leaves the old carrier erased and the successor learns a feature with two live contributions `u,v`. After sealing C, its output is `u+v`; the declared new binding reads `u` and the witness can launch only at `u=t`, with `t` fixed by the witness contract, not read from a snapshot. The candidate map moves by `(t-u,u-t)`: it leaves C exactly unchanged despite changing its actual read path and doesn't require historical weights or moments. A second installed skill reading `v` blocks the same move unless `u=t` already. This is a positive and a negative control for the checker. It neither proves the paired move is lawful nor shows that the witness's later updates preserve C, that A/B survive, or that C training saved work.

## Idea cards

### 01. Learn the next job with a shared bottleneck — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Train truthful C at fixed width so its predictive bottleneck can also take over the spent computation; bind the frozen witness only after sealing C's outputs. **Novelty / control.** [Progressive nets][pnn] and Net2Net + distillation transfer realized features; ask whether acquiring a genuinely useful new feature substitutes for paid regeneration, against their stack with #177 and a separately routed C. **Cheapest first test.** Fit a two-head ReLU learner to paired shared-factor versus independent-factor C tasks and reveal the witness's delayed job only after both reach equal utility. Ablate the shared bottleneck in shadow. **Old-task retention.** Check A/B and the rare C examples for which losing that bottleneck changes the output, before and after execution.

### 02. Invert the spend only at its live interface — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Synthesize a capped map from current state and the frozen interface predicate, factoring its writes through an exact C-preserving relation; don't reconstruct ancestor weights or moments. **Novelty / control.** SISA rebuilds affected training shards, GRACE retrieves an adapter, and GPM constrains updates; the extra deliverable is an executable witness and a preservation proof on a genuinely shared path. Compare those stacked with Net2Net, plus rollback/replay. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate legal low-rank paired moves in the linear sanity check, with a planted later C that reads the same coordinate and another that blocks the move; run a two-step witness, including a later-read trap. **Old-task retention.** Prove each newer skill invariant over its finite domain and verify A/B on the second step, not just the landing state.

### 03. Earn the replacement from disagreement examples — improvement
**Mechanism.** Train C on truthful pairs that hold the old shortcut fixed while varying the factor the witness needs; compared with ordinary C acquisition, spend no extra labels and use no witness targets. **Novelty / control.** [Machine teaching][teaching] already selects examples for their updates; the improvement is substituting ordinary feature identification for a second transfer fit. Compare its best curriculum plus regeneration with the same available examples. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate pools for binary C with correlated nuisance bits and one disagreement pair, then test witness service on an opposite-correlation split. **Old-task retention.** Include rare A/B cases using the shortcut legitimately and both C label classes on the held-out split.

### 04. Hand off a computation spread across two C features — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Require the licensed binding to read a combination of two features both needed by C, even though neither feature alone implements any useful carrier. No dedicated recovery unit may be created. **Novelty / control.** Net2Net factorization and task distillation can implement the combination; the claim is recovery paid by **joint** C feature use, beyond independent feature regeneration plus the best allowed linear readout. **Cheapest first test.** Fit a two-output C where an invertible mixture of two independently varied factors is required; test both singleton removals and a wrong-sign combination. **Old-task retention.** Test each C output separately alongside A/B and earlier C, including cases where the two factors cancel at the witness read.

### 05. Pass a replacement through a family of C-equivalent gauges — improvement
**Mechanism.** Express a candidate move as exact channel permutation or positive rescaling with inverse compensation at every C consumer; optimize only among such gauges for a runnable fixed witness interface. **Novelty / control.** [Git Re-Basin][rebasin] and Net2Net already cover symmetry alignment and function preservation. The narrower gain is choosing a gauge using delayed witness reads rather than matching weights. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate gauges of a two-layer ReLU host with a frozen optimizer-sensitive witness; include a gauge that matches all forward outputs but fails its second read. **Old-task retention.** Check all newer consumer heads and rare A/B inputs after the witness updates, when plain forward symmetry may no longer suffice.

### 06. Preserve a nonlinear shared path by region, not by Jacobian — improvement
**Mechanism.** Compile paired writes and verify affine output equality on every jointly reachable activation region of a small declared domain; reject any crossing not covered by the proof. **Novelty / control.** [ReLU verification][relu] can already check this property; the improvement is using region-aware synthesis to recover cases a first-order projection falsely accepts or unnecessarily refuses. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate kink intervals in a one-input ReLU model with a compensation valid in one interval but not the adjacent one. Compare GPM and full rechecking. **Old-task retention.** Include exact equality at region boundaries and held-out A/B/C cases on both sides of the kink.

### 07. Make successor learning buy a canceling lever — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Acquire C so two live pathways have different functional roles over its domain but admit a coordinated write preserving the full C function and moving the witness read. Choose the C fit for the feasible **operation**, not for nominal spare rank. **Novelty / control.** GPM protects subspaces and [La-MAML][lamaml] favors transfer; ask whether their stack fails to learn the paired intervention that enables witness service. **Cheapest first test.** Train two matched C fits, one with a legal coupled actuation and one with equal width but no such move; use exact search over both legal menus and bill any acquisition premium. **Old-task retention.** Include earlier C with sensitivity to each pathway; test for refusal when even one protected function destroys the cancellation.

### 08. Recover a stateful transition without recovering its latent state — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Bind the witness to a successor's recurrent feature and synthesize a relation between before/after C states that preserves observations and is closed under their event transitions. **Novelty / control.** [Bisimulation metrics][bisim] already abstract equivalent behavior; the candidate is a recovery operation exploiting equivalence while making a frozen training transition executable, versus exact C state restoration and bounded model checking alone. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a two-bit transducer with distinct latent encodings and a late event exposing a false relation. **Old-task retention.** Check old and newer task traces, including an installed C reachable only after a two-event prefix, and A/B after witness service.

### 09. Permit only endpoint-atomic recoveries with overlapping reads — improvement
**Mechanism.** Compose two paired writes in a bounded transaction; prove exact equality at the commit and deny all reads of the temporary buffer, otherwise require equality at both steps. **Novelty / control.** Atomic commit and [Pacti][pacti] contracts are known; measure whether genuine atomicity admits witness recovery unreachable by safe serial moves after paying for buffering. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two writes that must cancel on a C read; inject an intervening invocation and compare serial checking with a full shadow copy. **Old-task retention.** Invoke A/B and every C before and after each observable boundary, including an aborted transaction.

### 10. Teach a successor whose training covers two legal bindings — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Learn one C representation with two verified witness read realizations that use its prediction features differently; a fixed rule chooses between them based on lawful evidence after an intervening acquisition. **Novelty / control.** #177 regeneration and progressive nets can maintain multiple bindings; the proposed gain is **one** useful C acquisition paying for both, not an uncharged backup. **Cheapest first test.** Use a three-feature C task with two allowed reads and two later acquisitions, each invalidating a different read; compare two separately regenerated carriers at equal storage. **Old-task retention.** Check each branch's full installed C set, especially the intervening skill that invalidated the rejected route, as well as A/B.

### 11. Reuse a recovery map under new C only when the proof lifts — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Export a bounded operation and explicit preservation premises on consumer functions; at a subsequent acquisition, check whether the new consumer satisfies those premises without refitting the map. **Novelty / control.** Pacti and incremental verification supply reusable proofs; the added empirical claim is that successor-derived maps transfer across acquired skills with positive net service versus exact recertification plus cached per-skill adapters. **Cheapest first test.** Try a linear consumer in the proved invariant family and a second that agrees on training examples but violates the condition elsewhere. **Old-task retention.** Require unchanged outputs for the first on the entire declared domain and refuse the second without touching it or previously installed A/B/C.

### 12. Let C computation repay a previously displaced A read — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use a successor feature to take over both the frozen witness's missing read and an A computation temporarily served by costly fallback, so removing the fallback finances the binding. **Novelty / control.** Compression and multi-task distillation already share features; compare their best fixed-width fit plus #177 regeneration and count a win only if ordinary C acquisition replaces both jobs. **Cheapest first test.** Use three binary heads sharing a latent feature under a fixed operation budget and compare with a utility-matched C whose features do not serve A. **Old-task retention.** Compare A risk and latency at the predeclared floor before and after removing fallback, including its rare sole-consumer inputs; verify B and all C too.

### 13. Recover the read on its later clock — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use a C-trained feature that supplies the initial binding and whose lawful subsequent training induces the later witness read without resetting moments. Check the delayed read and C preservation together. **Novelty / control.** [Optimizer-slot splits][slots] and [split-timed deadlines][deadlines] already expose delayed read effects. The distinct claim is replacing paid slot preparation with a causally shared C feature; compare their timed stack plus Net2Net. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a tiny momentum host with matched initial reads and C utility, then remove or reroute the C feature in shadow before the second read. **Old-task retention.** Check early and late C outputs and A/B under the same ordinary suffix, with no slot surgery on the deployed path.

### 14. Spend less proof by checking a cut all C paths traverse — improvement
**Mechanism.** Identify a small proved feature-and-state cut separating the touched block from each installed C; show the entire cut invariant instead of checking every output independently. **Novelty / control.** Compositional verification and Pacti already exploit cuts; the gain is proof cost under a genuinely shared block, not a new guarantee. **Cheapest first test.** On a tiny multihead DAG compare cut checking to exact independent checks; add a bypass edge that breaks the cut without changing sampled outputs. **Old-task retention.** Cover every C consumer, including the bypass user, and run A/B after the witness; sample success cannot rescue a failed cut.

### 15. Recover with an equality object instead of a task-ID gate — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Accept a map only if it preserves the newer behavior on the union of legal inputs under the same deployed routing; it may not divert old calls through a witness-only identifier. **Novelty / control.** GRACE uses scoped routing and ROME tests locality; this asks if shared, untagged invocations can carry a recovery with exact protection, against adapter retrieval with the same routing information. **Cheapest first test.** Use overlapping input domains with no task ID and a planted boundary where a nearest-key adapter would redirect a legitimate C call. **Old-task retention.** Enumerate the overlap and its boundary for A/B/C, including identical raw inputs presented in different permitted histories.

### 16. Learn C jointly with an admissible recovery program grammar — improvement
**Mechanism.** During C acquisition bias the fit toward a small, frozen grammar of compensation writes; after sealing C, synthesize a short legal program and independently check it. Count the explicit training premium. **Novelty / control.** [Syntax-guided synthesis][sygus] and La-MAML already give search spaces and future-aware training. The improvement is fit/check cost at fixed coverage, not new expressivity. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate paired scalar moves versus a richer exact oracle, including a recoverable case outside the grammar and an infeasible case inside the same size limit. **Old-task retention.** Check all C after each program prefix and A/B after an ordinary update; distinguish grammar failure from forgetting or impossibility.

### 17. Prove an obstruction from several C skills jointly — improvement (audit)
**Mechanism.** Produce a small set of exact C obligations whose conjunction makes every bounded witness recovery impossible, even though each obligation separately permits one. **Novelty / control.** Unsatisfiable cores and exact reachability already do this. The benefit is distinguishing genuine incompatible commitments from a weak checker, not predicting capacity from rank. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two individually compensable linear C reads with conflicting joint constraints; compare an exact core with rank and no-write controls, and remove one constraint in a positive-control fork. **Old-task retention.** On refusal preserve the entire live suite, not just the core; on the reduced fork retain the independently installed A/B and remaining C.

### 18. Audit sharing under distribution-preserving feature swaps — improvement (audit)
**Mechanism.** In shadow compare paired swaps chosen to retain C's empirical utility while exchanging the purported causal feature with a nuisance feature of equal marginal statistics. If actual witness service tracks the nuisance or an address, reject the sharing claim. **Novelty / control.** Causal activation interventions are standard; the gain is an inexpensive falsifier that random unit ablation and a shared-versus-separate fit can both miss. **Cheapest first test.** Construct two equally frequent correlated factors with counterfactual pairs, and run feature, nuisance, and sham swaps before the same delayed job. **Old-task retention.** Recheck exact C equality on unswapped declared domains and held-out rare A/B/C; shadow-swap success does not authorize deployment.

### 19. Preserve C when the frozen witness changes a shared optimizer state — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Synthesize recovery against the composed transition of map **and** witness, requiring that each protected C trajectory remain related to an untouched reference through an ordinary update. The map alone may pass while the composition fails. **Novelty / control.** GPM and exact reachability already constrain updates; the proposed object is a bounded compensation for an immutable downstream update, not permission to project or retrain the witness itself. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate a shared-momentum two-head system where C outputs agree at commit but diverge after the next batch unless the map handles the coupled moment. **Old-task retention.** Check all C output traces and A/B at both the witness step and the ordinary batch; certifying the endpoint alone does not count.

### 20. Find the smallest amount of C learning that actually pays for recovery — improvement
**Mechanism.** Across prospectively matched C acquisition milestones, compare the recovery cost with and without causal access to the learned feature; require extra C progress to reduce total end-to-end work rather than merely raising match confidence. **Novelty / control.** Learning curves and curriculum selection are known; this is a break-even measurement, not another plasticity forecast. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate three labeled batch counts and two equal-utility C fits at each feasible count; compare best acquisition plus independent distillation and replay. **Old-task retention.** Anchor earlier C to its original outputs at all milestones, seal the successor when acquired, and check A/B/C under each accepted continuation.

### 21. Hand off again without an accumulating recovery bank — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** On the next genuine spend retire and reuse the prior replacement's storage; require each subsequent truthful C acquisition to provide the next binding under a fixed global budget, while keeping all previous functions protected. **Novelty / control.** Continual backpropagation renews capacity and #177 renews carriers; the narrower claim is that each new acquisition can replace the spent computation without accumulating adapters or proof logs. **Cheapest first test.** Three held-out demands in a small shared-feature stream, with a third C chosen to make the tested budget infeasible; compare capped GRACE + regeneration and rollback/replay. **Old-task retention.** Check every acquired C independently after each retirement and again after refusal; never count shelving a live C as freeing capacity.

### 22. Recover a common read when the learner cannot identify which spend occurred — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Compile one map per lawful observation class that restores the same witness and proves preservation for every current state consistent with that class; do not decode or recreate the erased ancestor. **Novelty / control.** [Partial-information games][games] already compute uniform policies and [#179][examples] buys revealing evidence. The proposed gain is exploiting a common C-derived replacement to avoid that purchase, versus exact belief search with the same observables. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two aliased histories with a common compensating move and a matched pair requiring incompatible moves; price a revealing probe. **Old-task retention.** Verify every installed A/B/C trace in both aliased states, not just the one supplying the positive witness run.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Did the successor's own learning pay for restored service?** Cross shared and separately routed C at equal utility with real and sham binding, then remove just the C feature in shadow. Look for useful delayed executions that depend on the shared feature and cost less end to end than Net2Net + distillation + #177 regeneration and rollback plus replay. If the unshared twin or a free spare slot ties, drop the counterweight claim.
2. **What is the smallest source-free map that preserves all newer skills on a path they actually share?** Start with exact enumeration, the planted overlapping C that allows compensation, and the second C that forbids it. Separate initial forward equivalence from witness and ordinary suffix equivalence. If only task-ID isolation or a compressed checkpoint passes, report that narrower result rather than claiming an interface inverse.
3. **Which refusals come from actual C commitments, and which from weak evidence or a weak proof language?** Compare full-state, lawful-transcript, and certificate-limited solvers on identical menus and deadlines. Make each rejected case a true obstruction, an information deficit, or a checker unknown before deciding whether to improve the map or buy evidence.
4. **Does the trade survive repeated spends with every newer skill still live?** Freeze a fixed-width and proof-byte budget across several blind successors. Include a late incompatible C and count retained service, cumulative work and unused capacity against the stacked controls. A finite useful horizon is a result; it is not indefinite renewal.
