# Learner–world co-evolution (depth 1): self-suppressed rehearsal & stateful rollback certificates

Child node (depth 1) of [PR #363](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/363) (`LW02` + `LW03`) · 28 candidate primitives · Ideas only; no experiments run

**Parent triage.** In `reviews/flood-triage.md`, PR #363 (`tree/root-learner-world-coevolution.md`) kept two score-3 primitives:
1. **`LW02` — rehearse what the learner's own success removed from its stream:** maintain a suppression record separating failure modes that vanished *because the learner now prevents them* from ones the world no longer produces, rehearsing recovery only for self-suppressed modes so an exogenous shock does not hit an amnesic learner.
2. **`LW03` — a rollback certificate for a world the learner changed:** pair each update with a compact certificate of the world state its predecessor checkpoint assumed, and on rollback test the restored checkpoint against the *current* world rather than assuming restoration—choosing between plain rollback, forward repair, and a world-state bridge when weight rollback and world change do not commute.

**Thesis.** Both surviving primitives expose the same structural blind spot of standard continual learning: *a learner's own competence alters what remains observable and what remains reversible.* In Section A (`L2W01`–`L2W10`) we deepen `LW02`—how to prove self-suppression without re-inducing live failures, decouple prevention from recovery, synthesize leak-through shocks from near-misses, and retire genuinely extinct modes. In Section B (`L2W11`–`L2W20`) we deepen `LW03`—how to measure the world–weight rollback commutator, extract minimal world-assumption witnesses, run three-way rollback triage, factor reversible vs. irreversible world edits, and keep rollbacks valid during probation windows. In Section C (`L2W21`–`L2W28`) we isolate genuine *interactions* between `LW02` and `LW03`—un-suppression shocks upon rollback, downstream guard removal (Peltzman world-pinning), and joint probe allocation—each required to beat the stacked union of `LW02` alone + `LW03` alone.

## A · Self-suppressed streams & prevention-induced amnesia (`LW02` deepened)

### L2W01 · Matched counterfactual-relaxation micro-probes (proving self-suppression)
- **Mechanism:** To prove that failure mode $F$ vanished because $\theta_t$ prevented it—without disabling prevention in live traffic—run read-only *shadow-relaxation probes*: replay recent live prefixes through the pre-improvement checkpoint $\theta_{\text{old}}$ (or a lightly perturbed $\theta_t - \epsilon$) in an offline/sandboxed transition step and estimate causal suppression $\Delta p_F = P(F \mid \theta_{\text{old}}, x_t) - P(F \mid \theta_t, x_t)$. Admit $F$ to the recovery-rehearsal ledger only when $\Delta p_F$ clears a statistical floor.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Performative Prediction with Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) corrects for policy-censored labels; [Gaussian Process Bandits with Selectively Verified Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17760) allocates expensive checks. Estimating *causal suppression* via read-only shadow-checkpoint replay on live prefixes avoids both live safety degradation and blind uniform rehearsal.
- **Cheapest first test:** Two-stage prevent/recover simulator with 8 failure modes (4 self-suppressed by $\theta_t$, 4 exogenously deprecated in the world). Compare uniform reservoir replay, recency replay, selective-labels IPW, and shadow-relaxation-gated rehearsal at equal buffer/probe budget. Kill if uniform reservoir replay or IPW matches post-shock recovery across the 4 self-suppressed modes without wasting capacity on the 4 extinct modes.
- **Old-task retention:** Score steady-state prevention on $A/B$ plus forced-recurrence recovery on all 4 self-suppressed modes after 500 live steps.

### L2W02 · Prevention–recovery gradient antagonism decoupling
- **Mechanism:** Rehearsing *recovery* from failure $F$ while simultaneously training *prevention* of $F$ can cause gradient interference in shared representations—leading the learner to fire recovery actions prematurely during normal operation or weaken prevention margins. Introduce an explicit *regime-switch gate* separating prevention weights from recovery weights on boundary states so recovery rehearsal updates only the post-failure branch.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [The Stability Gap in Continual RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26252) documents transient interference during continual updates; standard replay mixes prevention and recovery batches in one parameter pool. Isolating *prevention–recovery antagonism* via a boundary-gated modular split is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Small MLP/tabular-feature agent in a 2-stage MDP where optimal prevention and optimal recovery use opposite actions on visually similar states. Compare joint `LW02` rehearsal, OGD-projected rehearsal, and boundary-gated decoupled rehearsal at equal parameters and replay steps. Kill if OGD or standard joint `LW02` rehearsal shows no prevention-margin drop while matching recovery accuracy.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure both steady-state false-alarm rate on normal old tasks $A/B$ and post-shock recovery accuracy on $F$.

### L2W03 · Synthetic shock-boundary synthesis from near-miss prefixes
- **Mechanism:** Once prevention succeeds, true failure states vanish from the stream, but *near-misses* (states where $\theta_t$ barely prevented $F$, indicated by low action margin or high critic sensitivity) still arrive. Harvest near-miss prefixes and perturb *only the single prevented transition* into a counterfactual post-failure state, generating fresh, world-current recovery rehearsal without maintaining a stale static archive of old failures.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Rollback: Experience Backtracking in World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36068) backtracks inside world models for policy improvement; standard reservoir replay stores ancient raw episodes that go stale as $W_t$ drifts. Synthesizing *current-world recovery states directly from live near-miss prefixes* is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Prevent/recover simulator where the *world's non-failure context drifts* while failure $F$ is suppressed (so ancient stored failure episodes become out-of-distribution). Compare static reservoir replay from $t=0$, recency replay, and near-miss counterfactual shock synthesis at equal compute/bytes. Kill if static reservoir replay from $t=0$ matches post-shock recovery under drifted context.
- **Old-task retention:** Check both recovery accuracy on drifted-context forced shocks and untouched old tasks $A/B$.

### L2W04 · Suppression half-life & decay-indexed recovery budgets
- **Mechanism:** Self-suppressed failure modes do not erode equally fast: modes whose recovery features have high gradient overlap with new incoming tasks $C$ decay rapidly, while orthogonal modes survive passively. Maintain a per-mode *recovery-margin decay velocity* $v_F = -\Delta m_F / \Delta t$ measured via cheap 1-shot probe checks, and allocate finite rehearsal budget proportionally to `suppression_prob × decay_velocity`.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [When to Forget](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22897) scores context-block retention by risk; standard replay allocates fixed uniform or recency weights. Indexing recovery rehearsal by *measured forgetting velocity of self-suppressed modes* is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** 8 self-suppressed failure modes where 2 strongly collide in feature space with incoming task stream $C$ and 6 are nearly orthogonal. Compare uniform `LW02` rehearsal, reservoir replay, EWC + uniform replay, and decay-velocity-allocated rehearsal at a tight fixed replay budget (20% of uniform full coverage). Kill if uniform `LW02` rehearsal or EWC ties on worst-case recovery across all 8 modes.
- **Old-task retention:** Score worst-case recovery across all 8 suppressed modes plus normal accuracy on $A/B$ and $C$.

### L2W05 · Causal extinction witness (proving a failure truly disappeared)
- **Mechanism:** Rehearsing a failure mode that the world *genuinely stopped producing* wastes capacity and freezes obsolete features. Pair the suppression ledger with a *causal extinction witness*: a compact upstream precondition check proving that even the unadapted baseline $\theta_0$ cannot reach failure $F$'s basin under current world $W_t$, licensing safe retirement (or compression) of $F$'s recovery slot.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Towards Reversible Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18177) manages obsolete enterprise knowledge; [Target Recoverability under Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11063) studies when targets become unrecoverable. Formalizing a *falsifiable causal witness of exogenous extinction* to safely free capacity inside a suppression ledger is the new operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Capacity-limited continual learner facing 10 failure modes: 4 self-suppressed, 4 genuinely extinct (upstream trigger removed), and 2 dormant-then-returning. Compare retain-all `LW02`, recency-LRU drop, and extinction-witness-gated retirement at equal memory cap. Kill if recency-LRU or retain-all matches both new-task $C$ accuracy and returning-mode recovery.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify zero premature retirement on the 4 self-suppressed and 2 dormant-returning modes while measuring freed plasticity on $C$.

### L2W06 · Multi-stage cascade suppression (hidden downstream amnesia)
- **Mechanism:** In a multi-stage learner or agent pipeline ($M_1 \to M_2 \to M_3$), improving upstream stage $M_1$ (e.g., input sanitization or early planning) starves downstream stages $M_2, M_3$ of malformed or edge-case inputs—causing silent downstream amnesia even when $M_2$ never changed its own policy. Propagate an explicit *suppression-shadow token* from $M_1$ to downstream stages naming which input classes $M_1$ is now intercepting, triggering targeted downstream recovery drills.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Performative Prediction with Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) treats a single decision maker; multi-stage pipelines create *cross-module competence-induced starvation*. Passing a *suppression-shadow contract* across pipeline stages is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** 2-stage pipeline (validator $M_1$ + handler $M_2$) where $M_1$ learns to prevent 95% of malformed requests, followed by 300 updates of $M_2$ on clean traffic, then a sudden bypass shock hitting $M_2$ directly. Compare end-to-end uniform replay, isolated $M_2$ recency replay, and upstream-signaled suppression-shadow drills for $M_2$ at equal compute. Kill if end-to-end uniform replay matches $M_2$'s bypass-shock recovery.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate $M_1$ prevention, $M_2$ normal-traffic accuracy on $A/B$, and $M_2$ direct bypass-shock recovery.

### L2W07 · Competence-masked censoring: separating prevention from avoidance
- **Mechanism:** A learner can eliminate failure $F$ from its observed stream in two radically different ways: *causal prevention* (acting so $F$ never happens) or *avoidance censoring* (abstaining, delegating, or refusing inputs likely to trigger $F$, so $F$'s outcome is simply unobserved). Maintain a *prevention-vs-avoidance split* in the suppression ledger: avoidance triggers active selective-label exploration/probing, whereas prevention triggers recovery rehearsal.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Performative Prediction with Selective Labels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08272) and [Delay Complexity Dimension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18935) address selective/censored labels; `LW02` targets prevention. Explicitly separating *causal prevention* from *avoidance censoring* within one suppression ledger prevents misapplying recovery rehearsal to unlearned avoided regions.
- **Cheapest first test:** Constrained-action world where 3 risk slices disappear because $\theta_t$ actively prevents them and 3 disappear because $\theta_t$ routes/abstains around them. Compare pure selective-labels IPW, pure `LW02` recovery rehearsal, and split prevention/avoidance routing at equal probe budget. Kill if selective-labels IPW alone or `LW02` alone ties across both avoided-slice accuracy and prevented-slice shock recovery.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure accuracy on untouched normal tasks $A/B$, avoided slices (when abstention is disabled), and prevented slices (under forced shock).

### L2W08 · Adversarial leak-through drills (worst-case post-prevention shock)
- **Mechanism:** When an exogenous shock or edge case finally *bypasses* an effective preventer $\theta_t$, the leaked state is *not* drawn from the historical failure distribution $P_0(x \mid F)$—it is drawn from the *preventer-bypassing residual* $P(x \mid F, \theta_t \text{ failed})$, which is systematically harder. Bias recovery rehearsal toward *hard residual states* (constructed by finding minimal perturbations that slip past $\theta_t$'s preventer) rather than raw historical failures.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Counterfactual Online Conformal Prediction Under Adaptive Logging](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30811) accounts for policy-induced selection in calibration; standard `LW02` replays historical failure episodes. Targeting the *conditional bypass distribution* of a strong preventer is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Prevent/recover simulator where $\theta_t$ prevents 95% of easy failures, so real shocks that leak through lie strictly in the hard 5% tail. Compare uniform reservoir replay, unweighted `LW02` historical failure replay, and preventer-bypass-weighted recovery rehearsal at equal replay steps. Kill if unweighted `LW02` historical failure replay matches recovery accuracy on actual leaked shocks.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify recovery on both standard historical failures and hard preventer-bypassing shocks, plus steady-state $A/B$ accuracy.

### L2W09 · Shared-suppression compression (factoring overlapping failure recoveries)
- **Mechanism:** In long-lived agents, dozens of distinct failure modes become self-suppressed over time, making per-mode replay buffers infeasible. Factor the suppression ledger into a small set of *canonical recovery subroutines* plus compact *mode-to-subroutine trigger bindings*, rehearsing the shared basis and verifying each suppressed mode's trigger with a cheap 1-step check.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Coreset selection compresses static datasets; here the buffer must preserve *conditional emergency recovery* across many zero-arrival failure modes under tight byte budgets. Factored *subroutine + trigger-binding* compression of a suppression ledger is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** 20 self-suppressed failure modes generated from 4 underlying recovery mechanisms × 5 distinct surface triggers, under a tight replay-byte cap. Compare reservoir replay, per-mode equal-quota `LW02`, and factored basis+trigger rehearsal at identical bytes. Kill if per-mode equal-quota `LW02` or k-center coreset replay ties on worst-case recovery across all 20 modes.
- **Old-task retention:** Score recovery across all 20 modes upon forced shock plus untouched normal tasks $A/B$.

### L2W10 · Rehearsal-induced performative leakage guard
- **Mechanism:** If recovery rehearsal touches semi-live surfaces (or if rehearsed recovery habits bleed into live policy outputs), the rehearsal itself can perturb users or tools—accidentally re-inducing the failure mode or altering the world-response operator $J_W$. Attach a *live-isolation certificate* to every recovery-rehearsal pass verifying that rehearsed updates leave the live pre-failure action distribution invariant within a tight KL/TV bound.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Stabilizing Performative Feedback Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14065) bounds live deployment shifts; standard replay checks aggregate loss, not *performative spillover* of rehearsal into live actions. Enforcing a *live-action-invariance bound* specifically on recovery rehearsal is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Coupled simulator where even a 5% shift in normal-state action distribution causes users to panic/re-route and trigger new failures. Compare unconstrained `LW02` rehearsal, L2-regularized rehearsal, and live-action-KL-constrained recovery rehearsal at equal compute. Kill if plain L2 weight regularization prevents live performative spillover equally well while matching shock recovery.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure both live-world stability (zero induced failure spikes) and untouched $A/B$ accuracy alongside post-shock recovery.

## B · Stateful rollback certificates & non-commutative world–weight reversion (`LW03` deepened)

### L2W11 · Commutator audit: measuring $[\text{Revert}_\theta, \Delta W]$ before rollback
- **Mechanism:** Define the *world–weight rollback commutator* $\mathcal{C}(\theta_{\text{old}}, W_{\text{old}} \to W_{\text{now}}) = \mathcal{L}(\theta_{\text{old}}, W_{\text{now}}) - \mathcal{L}(\theta_{\text{old}}, W_{\text{old}})$, estimated via a compact set of read-only behavioral probes on current world $W_{\text{now}}$ *before* committing a rollback. Use $\mathcal{C}$ to certify whether rollback commutes ($\mathcal{C} \le \epsilon$, safe plain restore), is bridgeable ($\epsilon < \mathcal{C} \le \tau$), or is non-commuting ($\mathcal{C} > \tau$, blocking blind restore).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Rollback the World, Keep the Reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18304) and [AgentRewind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14380) rewind sandboxed trajectories; [Local and Global Stability in Performative RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06467) studies equilibrium stability without a rollback operator. Estimating the *non-commutativity gap* $\mathcal{C}$ prior to weight reversion is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stateful routing/tool MDP with 20 update cycles where half the world changes commute with rollback and half break $\theta_{\text{old}}$. Compare always-rollback, never-rollback (always forward fine-tune), and commutator-audited rollback at equal probe budget. Kill if a simple 1-step live canary test of $\theta_{\text{old}}$ on 1% traffic ties without incurring catastrophic live errors.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure accuracy on both $W_{\text{old}}$ unchanged tasks and $W_{\text{now}}$ shifted tasks immediately after every rollback event.

### L2W12 · Minimal world-assumption witness (what $\theta_{t-1}$ secretly relied on)
- **Mechanism:** Archiving full world state $W_{t-1}$ is intractable. At each update $\theta_{t-1} \to \theta_t$, extract a compact *world-assumption witness*—the minimal set of environment invariants, tool preconditions, and input-distribution moments whose perturbation flips $\theta_{t-1}$'s protected decisions—and store it as the rollback certificate's testable precondition (far smaller than a full `LW29` shadow twin).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [When Validation Stops Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10873) audits update admission on validation sets; `LW29` maintains a full frozen-world simulator. Compiling a *minimal decision-flipping world-assumption witness* per checkpoint is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stateful tool/schema environment with 30 environment variables, only 3 of which $\theta_{t-1}$ actually depends on. Compare full `LW29` shadow-twin replay, random subset of 3 state checks, and minimal world-assumption witness checks at equal probe bytes. Kill if checking the 3 most-changed world variables (regardless of $\theta_{t-1}$ sensitivity) predicts rollback safety equally well.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that passing the minimal witness guarantees old $A/B$ retention on $W_t$ after restore, with zero false-safe rollbacks.

### L2W13 · Three-way rollback triage (plain revert vs. world-shim vs. forward repair)
- **Mechanism:** Replace binary "keep $\theta_t$ or restore $\theta_{t-1}$" with a *three-way rollback operator*: when $\theta_t$ regresses in mutated world $W_t$, score (1) plain checkpoint restore $\theta_{t-1}$, (2) restored $\theta_{t-1}$ composed with a lightweight world-compensation shim $S_{W_t \to W_{t-1}}$, and (3) targeted forward repair from $\theta_t$, selecting the lowest-cost path that clears both old-task and mutated-world floors.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [SafeLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26313) triggers risk-aware trajectory rollback; performative-RL retraining always moves forward. Formalizing *three-way stateful recovery triage* (revert / shim / forward-repair) driven by the rollback certificate is the new operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stateful world with 3 regression types: clean revertible regression, schema-shifted regression (fixable by a 2-param input/output shim), and irreversibly shifted regression (requiring forward repair). Compare always-revert, always-forward-fine-tune, performative-RL retraining, and three-way certificate triage at equal recovery steps. Kill if always-forward-fine-tune ties across all 3 regression types.
- **Old-task retention:** Score retention of *both* pre-update tasks $A/B$ and post-mutation world tasks immediately after recovery.

### L2W14 · Reversible vs. irreversible world-diff factorization at commit time
- **Mechanism:** When a deployed update $\theta_t$ alters the world ($W_{t-1} \to W_t$), factor the world delta $\Delta W$ into a *world-reversible slice* (undoable via an atomic transactional log: tool wrappers, config files, routing rules) and a *world-irreversible slice* (user habit shifts, external API state, consumed quotas). On rollback, execute atomic undo on the reversible slice and build a compensation shim *only* for the irreversible residual.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [AgentRewind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14380) adds transactional undo for *reversible* agent tool calls; it cannot undo external/human state. Factoring $\Delta W$ into *undoable + shim-compensated* components bridges transactional rollback to open worlds.
- **Cheapest first test:** Hybrid environment with both local editable files (undoable) and an adaptive user simulator (non-undoable). Induce a bad update $\theta_t$ after 50 steps. Compare plain weight rollback, transactional-undo-only rollback, forward fine-tuning, and factored undo+shim rollback at equal steps. Kill if transactional-undo-only (ignoring user adaptation) ties on post-rollback performance.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate old $A/B$ workflows spanning both local tools and adapted users after rollback.

### L2W15 · Shadow-world pre-rollback dry run (verifying restore without live harm)
- **Mechanism:** Before switching live control back to $\theta_{t-1}$ inside mutated world $W_t$, execute a bounded *shadow dry run* feeding live $W_t$ observations to $\theta_{t-1}$ read-only and comparing its proposed actions against both $\theta_t$ and the world-assumption witness (`L2W12`). Block live cutover if $\theta_{t-1}$ triggers invariant violations or out-of-schema actions on $W_t$.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Safe Continual RL in Non-stationary Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19737) enforces safety during forward learning; standard shadow deployments evaluate *new* candidates, not *old checkpoints being rolled back into a mutated world*. Using read-only shadow dry runs specifically as a *rollback cutover gate* is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Constrained tool-use simulator where 30% of candidate rollbacks would emit invalid/harmful calls under mutated world $W_t$. Compare immediate checkpoint restore, 5% live canary rollback, and read-only shadow pre-rollback gating at equal latency budget. Kill if a 5% live canary rollback incurs negligible harm while matching detection accuracy.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify zero live safety violations on $W_t$ during rollback screening while retaining $A/B$ performance once safe cutover succeeds.

### L2W16 · World-compensation shim algebra (composing shims across multi-step rollbacks)
- **Mechanism:** When a regression is discovered only after $k \ge 2$ updates ($\theta_0 \to \theta_1 \to \dots \to \theta_k$ with world states $W_0 \to \dots \to W_k$), rolling back to $\theta_0$ requires compensating for a *chain* of world mutations. Represent per-step world shifts as composable typed shims $S_{t \to t-1}$ and verify *algebraic composability* ($S_{1\to 0} \circ \dots \circ S_{k\to k-1}$), flagging non-invertible bottlenecks where intermediate world edits erased information $\theta_0$ needs.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Travel Back To Move Forward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13058) backtracks multi-step agent trajectories in resettable environments; multi-step *world-shim composition* for non-resettable continual deployments is unstudied.
- **Cheapest first test:** 4-step sequential environment mutation chain (affine/schema transforms, with one lossy projection in half the runs). Compare plain $\theta_0$ restore, fresh shim fitting directly from $W_4 \to W_0$, and composed per-step shim chains with lossiness flagging at equal samples. Kill if fitting one direct $W_4 \to W_0$ shim from scratch ties on sample efficiency and non-invertibility detection.
- **Old-task retention:** Score $A/B$ accuracy from $\theta_0$ inside $W_4$ after composed shim application, and verify accurate abstention/forward-repair routing on lossy chains.

### L2W17 · Rollback-safe update regularization (two-phase world commit)
- **Mechanism:** Rather than only checking rollback *after* the world has already broken $\theta_{t-1}$, constrain forward updates during a *probation window*: penalize any world-mutating action by $\theta_t$ that would violate $\theta_{t-1}$'s world-assumption witness (`L2W12`) until $\theta_t$ clears held-out verification ("two-phase world commit"). Only once $\theta_t$ graduates may it make world changes incompatible with $\theta_{t-1}$.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Stabilizing Performative Feedback Loops](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14065) limits deployment frequency; [When Validation Stops Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10873) gates weight updates. Keeping the *world itself backward-compatible with $\theta_{t-1}$* during a probation window is the proposed primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stateful simulator where bad updates are detected after $M=50$ steps, and unconstrained $\theta_t$ makes an irreversible world change at step 10. Compare unconstrained deployment + forward repair, delayed deployment (waiting before acting), and two-phase rollback-safe probation at equal horizon. Kill if delayed deployment + forward repair ties on cumulative reward and safe recovery.
- **Old-task retention:** Confirm that rolling back during probation restores 100% of $\theta_{t-1}$'s $A/B$ performance without needing a shim, while graduated good updates suffer minimal probation tax.

### L2W18 · Selective-slice rollback under partial world mutation
- **Mechanism:** When $\theta_t$ regresses on task slice $C$ while the world has already co-evolved to rely on $\theta_t$'s improved behavior on slice $B$, a global rollback to $\theta_{t-1}$ breaks $B$ in $W_t$. Combine the rollback certificate with a *slice-scoped restore*—grafting $\theta_{t-1}$'s behavior back *only* on the regressed slice $C$ (verified against $W_t$) while keeping $\theta_t$ intact on world-coupled slice $B$.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Towards Reversible Forgetting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18177) reverses isolated knowledge updates in static enterprise settings; here slice-scoped rollback is specifically driven by *asymmetric world co-evolution* across slices.
- **Cheapest first test:** Multi-slice world where slice $B$ triggers immediate user/tool co-adaptation to $\theta_t$ while slice $C$ regresses. Compare global rollback to $\theta_{t-1}$, global forward fine-tuning on $C$, and certificate-guided slice-scoped rollback at equal compute. Kill if global forward fine-tuning on $C$ with $B$-replay ties on recovery speed and $B$ stability.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure *both* $C$ restoration and zero disruption on world-coupled slice $B$ plus untouched old slice $A$.

### L2W19 · Post-rollback world-rebound predictor ("rollback whiplash")
- **Mechanism:** Executing a rollback $\theta_t \to \theta_{t-1}$ triggers a *second performative wave* as users and external tools react to the abrupt behavioral reversal—creating temporary "rollback whiplash" that looks like a failed restore. Include a bounded *rebound-horizon estimate* in the rollback certificate so the monitor distinguishes transient post-rollback settling from genuine rollback failure and avoids rapid flip-flop oscillation.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [The Stability of Online Algorithms in Performative Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24207) studies oscillation under repeated retraining; it does not model *post-rollback transient whiplash* in stateful monitors. Adding a *rebound-horizon window* to the rollback certificate is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Delayed-response user/world simulator where reverting $\theta_t \to \theta_{t-1}$ causes a 10-step transient error spike before settling to optimal performance, mixed with truly broken rollbacks that stay bad. Compare instant-threshold re-rollback monitor, fixed-cooldown monitor, and certificate-predicted rebound-horizon monitor. Kill if a single hand-tuned fixed cooldown matches false-oscillation rate and broken-rollback detection time across varying world lags.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that settled post-rollback $A/B$ performance is reached without triggering spurious secondary updates that corrupt $\theta_{t-1}$.

### L2W20 · Rollback-preserving failure tombstones (don't re-learn the reverted mistake)
- **Mechanism:** Plain rollback $\theta_t \to \theta_{t-1}$ erases the learner's memory of *why* $\theta_t$ failed in $W_t$, causing the next continual-learning cycle on the same stream to immediately re-commit the identical bad update. Attach a compact *negative-rollback tombstone*—the minimal $(x, W_t, \Delta\theta)$ witness of the reverted failure—to $\theta_{t-1}$'s rollback certificate, acting as a targeted veto against re-entering that failure mode.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Rollback the World, Keep the Reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18304) keeps text reflections after *episode* rewind; here we keep a *weight/update-level negative tombstone* across *deployment rollbacks* in a mutated world (distinguished from ledger-killed self-sealing prohibitions by tying the veto to an explicit falsifiable $W_t$ condition).
- **Cheapest first test:** Continual stream with a deceptive high-reward shortcut that causes a delayed regression in $W_t$; run 5 update-and-rollback cycles. Compare plain rollback, rollback + frozen LR decay, and rollback + falsifiable failure tombstones at equal memory. Kill if simply freezing updates on the offending feature cluster ties without blocking legitimate later learning.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that tombstones block repeat regressions without causing false vetoes on legitimate new task $C$ or old tasks $A/B$.

## C · Joining self-suppressed rehearsal with stateful rollback (`LW02 × LW03` synthesis)

### L2W21 · Un-suppression shock check inside the rollback certificate
- **Mechanism:** Rolling back $\theta_t \to \theta_{t-1}$ can *instantly un-suppress* failure modes that $\theta_t$ had been preventing—exposing $\theta_{t-1}$ to a sudden wave of failures in mutated world $W_t$. Require every rollback certificate (`LW03`) to query the suppression ledger (`LW02`) before cutover and verify $\theta_{t-1}$'s recovery competence on every failure mode that rollback will re-enable.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Neither `LW02` alone (which only rehearses recovery for the live policy $\theta_t$) nor `LW03` alone (which checks $\theta_{t-1}$ on current *live* traffic $W_t$, where suppressed failures are absent!) can detect an *un-suppression shock* caused by rollback itself.
- **Cheapest first test:** Stateful simulator where $\theta_t$ prevents failure $F$ (so $F$ has 0% live rate in $W_t$) while regressing on $C$; rolling back to $\theta_{t-1}$ immediately un-suppresses $F$ in a mutated $W_t$ where $\theta_{t-1}$ fails catastrophically on $F$. Compare `LW02` alone, `LW03` alone, `LW02 + LW03` stacked independently, and the coupled un-suppression rollback check at equal probe budget. Kill if `LW03` alone (probing $\theta_{t-1}$ on live $W_t$) catches the shock equally well.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure post-rollback $C$ recovery *and* survival of the un-suppressed failure mode $F$ alongside untouched $A/B$.

### L2W22 · Prevention-induced world hysteresis (the "safety-crutch" / Peltzman trap)
- **Mechanism:** When $\theta_t$ reliably suppresses failure $F$, external users and downstream tools *remove their own manual guards* against $F$—mutating the world $W_t$ to depend structurally on $\theta_t$'s prevention. Detect *downstream guard removal* inside the suppression ledger and mark $\theta_t$'s prevention slice as *world-pinned* in the rollback certificate, forbidding rollback unless external guards are first restored or a prevention-preserving slice shim is attached.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [Partially Performative Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07890) and [Performative RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.00046) do not model *asymmetric external-guard erosion* induced by learner competence. Linking *suppression-induced guard removal* to *rollback pinning* is a genuine `LW02 × LW03` primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Coupled simulator where an external safety filter turns itself off after 50 failure-free steps under $\theta_t$; then $\theta_t$ regresses on $C$. Compare plain rollback, `LW02` alone, `LW03` alone, and world-pinned slice-preserving rollback at equal probes. Kill if plain `LW03` live probing detects the missing external filter before rollback causes failures.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify zero catastrophic failure spike on $F$ during $C$-regression recovery while retaining normal $A/B$ performance.

### L2W23 · World-current recovery rehearsal driven by rollback witnesses
- **Mechanism:** Rehearsing recovery for a self-suppressed failure mode $F$ using *old* trajectories from $W_0$ fails if the world has since mutated to $W_t$ (so old recovery actions no longer work in $W_t$). Pipe `LW03`'s world-assumption diff ($\Delta W = W_t \ominus W_0$) directly into `LW02`'s recovery rehearsal generator so every self-suppressed failure mode is rehearsed *inside the current mutated world semantics $W_t$*.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** `LW02` alone rehearses stale $W_0$ recoveries; `LW03` alone only uses $\Delta W$ at rollback time. Reusing `LW03`'s world-diff witness continuously to keep `LW02`'s suppressed-mode recovery *current with $W_t$* is the coupled operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** Prevent/recover simulator where failure $F$ is suppressed for 400 steps while tool schemas / world dynamics shift $W_0 \to W_t$; then force a shock of $F$ in $W_t$. Compare `LW02` alone (rehearsing $W_0$ failures), `LW02 + LW03` uncoupled, and world-diff-updated recovery rehearsal at equal compute. Kill if `LW02` alone + standard $W_t$ normal-task training generalizes recovery to $W_t$ automatically.
- **Old-task retention:** Score recovery on forced $F$ in $W_t$ alongside normal $A/B$ tasks in both $W_0$ and $W_t$.

### L2W24 · Joint suppression-and-rollback probe budget allocator
- **Mechanism:** Both `LW02` (probing suppressed failure modes) and `LW03` (maintaining live rollback certificates) consume a shared, finite probe/sandbox budget. Allocate probes dynamically by *marginal shock regret*: comparing `P(exogenous shock) × recovery_staleness` against `P(update regression) × rollback_commutator_growth`, shifting budget toward rollback certification right after risky updates and toward suppression rehearsal during quiet competence plateaus.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Static 50/50 splitting of `LW02` and `LW03` wastes probes during quiet regimes (on rollback) and during unstable regimes (on stale shocks). Risk-coupled *joint probe scheduling* across suppression and rollback is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Alternating-regime simulator (bursts of risky updates followed by long quiet prevention plateaus with late shocks) under a strict per-step probe cap. Compare fixed 50/50 `LW02 + LW03`, `LW02`-only, `LW03`-only, and regret-allocated joint probing. Kill if fixed 50/50 `LW02 + LW03` ties on combined post-regression and post-shock loss.
- **Old-task retention:** Evaluate old $A/B$ accuracy after both regression-rollback events and late-plateau forced shocks.

### L2W25 · Guided forward recovery when suppression debt blocks rollback
- **Mechanism:** When a newly deployed $\theta_t$ regresses on task $C$, *and* rolling back to $\theta_{t-1}$ is blocked by the coupled certificate (because $\theta_{t-1}$ cannot survive either $W_t$ or an un-suppressed failure mode $F$), execute *guided forward recovery*: distill $\theta_{t-1}$'s $C$-behavior into $\theta_t$ while anchoring $\theta_t$'s $W_t$-valid prevention and recovery—strictly outperforming both blocked rollback and blind forward fine-tuning.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Standard rollback is binary; standard forward fine-tuning discards $\theta_{t-1}$'s clean $C$ solution. Using the *blocked rollback certificate* to synthesize a targeted $\theta_{t-1} \to \theta_t$ forward distillation patch is the new operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** World where $\theta_t$ learned essential $W_t$ prevention of $F$ while breaking $C$, and $\theta_{t-1}$ knows $C$ but fails fatally on $F$ in $W_t$. Compare plain rollback, blind forward fine-tuning of $\theta_t$ on $C$, `LW03` shim on $\theta_{t-1}$, and certificate-guided forward distillation at equal gradient steps. Kill if blind forward fine-tuning of $\theta_t$ on $C$ + `LW02` replay ties on $C$ recovery speed and $F$ safety.
- **Old-task retention:** Score $A/B$ retention, $C$ restoration, and $F$ prevention/recovery in $W_t$ immediately after recovery.

### L2W26 · Suppression-aware probation windows (two-phase prevention rollout)
- **Mechanism:** Combine `L2W17`'s two-phase world commit with `LW02`'s suppression ledger: when a new update $\theta_t$ begins suppressing a failure mode $F$, keep *both* $\theta_{t-1}$'s rollback certificate *and* `LW02`'s recovery rehearsal hot for a probation window until $\theta_t$ proves both sustained prevention and clean post-leak recovery—preventing premature retirement of old recovery skills or irreversible world-pinning during an unproven rollout.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [When Validation Stops Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10873) gates update acceptance statically; here the probation window is explicitly triggered by *newly detected stream suppression* so competence-induced blind spots cannot lock in prematurely.
- **Cheapest first test:** Simulator where a flashy update $\theta_t$ suppresses failure $F$ for 80 steps (tempting premature retirement/world-pinning) before failing on a rare trigger at step 100. Compare immediate commit + `LW02`, immediate commit + `LW03`, and suppression-triggered probation windows at equal compute. Kill if a fixed-length probation window independent of suppression detection ties.
- **Old-task retention:** Measure $A/B$ accuracy and step-100 rare-trigger survival with and without rollback.

### L2W27 · Causal attribution of post-rollback failures (world hysteresis vs. suppression amnesia)
- **Mechanism:** If a rolled-back policy $\theta_{t-1}$ unexpectedly fails in $W_t$, two completely different mechanisms can produce identical error rates: (a) *world hysteresis* ($W_t \neq W_{t-1}$ broke $\theta_{t-1}$'s assumptions) or (b) *suppression amnesia* (a previously prevented failure recurred and $\theta_{t-1}$ lacks recovery). Run a 2-probe causal split—testing $\theta_{t-1}$ on $W_t$-shifted normal inputs versus $W_{t-1}$-style failure inputs—to route directly to a *world shim* (`LW03`) versus *recovery rehearsal* (`LW02`).
- **Nearest prior / gap:** Neither `LW02` nor `LW03` alone diagnoses *which* failure mode struck after a rollback; applying a world shim to suppression amnesia (or recovery rehearsal to world hysteresis) fails. This 2-probe causal router is the candidate primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Benchmark mixing 50% world-hysteresis rollback failures and 50% suppression-amnesia rollback failures with matched aggregate error drops. Compare always-shim, always-rehearse, random routing, and 2-probe causal attribution at equal recovery steps. Kill if generic forward fine-tuning on the failing rollback batch ties on recovery steps and old-task retention.
- **Old-task retention:** Verify that routed repair fixes the rollback failure without degrading untouched $A/B$ tasks in either $W_0$ or $W_t$.

### L2W28 · End-to-end co-evolutionary safety contract (unified `LW02 + LW03` admission gate)
- **Mechanism:** Unify `LW02` and `LW03` into a single *co-evolutionary update-admission contract*: a candidate update $\theta_{t-1} \to \theta_t$ is admitted to live deployment only if (i) every failure mode whose arrival rate $\theta_t$ is predicted to suppress is registered for world-current leak-through rehearsal, and (ii) a certified rollback, shim, or guided forward-repair path remains feasible in the induced world $W_t$.
- **Nearest prior / gap:** [When Validation Stops Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10873) and [Safe Continual RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19737) gate updates on static validation or fixed-MDP safety. Gating continual updates jointly on *future stream-suppression coverage* and *non-commutative rollback reachability* is the overarching primitive.
- **Cheapest first test:** Long-horizon (1,000-step) coupled simulator combining exogenous shocks, genuine extinctions, silent world mutations, and occasional bad updates. Compare standard CL + replay, `LW02` alone, `LW03` alone, naive stacked `LW02 + LW03`, and the unified admission contract at equal total probes and memory bytes. Kill if naive stacked `LW02 + LW03` ties on worst-case shock + regression regret.
- **Old-task retention:** Score untouched old tasks $A/B$ in both $W_0$ and $W_t$, forced-shock recovery on all self-suppressed modes, and post-rollback integrity across the full 1,000-step run.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Can read-only shadow-relaxation probes and near-miss shock synthesis (`L2W01`, `L2W03`, `L2W08`) detect self-suppressed failure modes and preserve recovery under drifted world context—without ever re-exposing the live world to failures?**
   Start with the two-stage prevent/recover simulator mixing self-suppressed failures, genuinely extinct failures, and drifted non-failure context. Drop the line if uniform reservoir replay or selective-labels IPW matches post-shock recovery on preventer-bypassing residual shocks at equal memory bytes and zero live safety degradation.
2. **When weight rollback and world mutation do not commute, does a minimal world-assumption witness + three-way rollback triage (`L2W11`–`L2W14`, `L2W17`) strictly beat plain rollback, forward fine-tuning, performative-RL retraining, and a full `LW29` shadow twin?**
   Test in a stateful environment mixing commuting world changes, reversible tool edits, and irreversible user-habit shifts under a fixed probe/byte cap. Drop the line if always-forward fine-tuning with replay (or a compressed `LW29` shadow twin at matched bytes) matches both recovery speed and dual-world ($W_0$ and $W_t$) old-task retention.
3. **Do genuine `LW02 × LW03` interactions—un-suppression shocks upon rollback (`L2W21`) and Peltzman downstream guard removal (`L2W22`)—break the independent union of `LW02` + `LW03`?**
   Construct the minimal coupled world where $\theta_t$ suppresses failure $F$, downstream guards turn off, and $\theta_t$ regresses on $C$. Test whether coupled un-suppression checks and world-pinned slice rollback prevent catastrophic rollback failures that both `LW02` alone and `LW03` alone miss. Drop the synthesis line if independent `LW02 + LW03` catches these failures at equal probe cost.
4. **How does prevention-induced recovery rehearsal behave when prevention and recovery share representations (`L2W02`) or span multi-stage pipelines (`L2W06`)?**
   Measure whether rehearsing recovery inside a single model degrades steady-state prevention margins (false-alarm spillover, `L2W10`), and test whether boundary-gated modular splits (`L2W02`) and upstream suppression-shadow signals (`L2W06`) eliminate that trade-off better than OGD or end-to-end replay.
