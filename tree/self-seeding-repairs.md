# Self-seeding repairs

**Status:** Ideas only; no experiments, code, or GPU work. **Parent:** [Renewable witness carriers](witness-carriers.md) ([PR #177](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/177)), cards 01 and 14. **Review seed:** [Flood triage, row 281](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/2f8d94132b1e46d3cb1cad5afc7bc6ac3617a457/reviews/flood-triage.md#L281) scored two ideas 3: “let the spend seed the next carrier” and “preserve diagnosis after picking the wrong repair.” The first asks for a truthful frozen batch whose ordinary repair both admits today's job and makes a feature that paid dormant capacity can bind; the second favors a renewal whose lawful probe port survives either of two equally useful repairs. **Read first:** [Idea ledger](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/23cfd2e9633460d9b6825fd7a01d539ccc383de3/reviews/idea-ledger.md): beat stacked controls, rule out wins by construction, and check ordinary old tasks even in the cheapest first test.

## Is a batch whose repair builds its next carrier a new continual-learning object?

**Verdict: only if the feature produced by ordinary repair does work that separate resupply cannot match.** [Iterative machine teaching][teaching] selects examples for the update they induce; [gradient matching][condense] and [trajectory matching][mtt] can target both prediction and post-update state. [GRACE][grace] already reuses repair adapters, [continual backpropagation][cbp] and [ReDo][redo] renew slots, and [Net2Net][net] can transport a realized feature. [Dual control][dual] and [partial-information games][games] already value branched access to information. Neither example selection, cycling, nor planning for a surviving probe is new by itself.

The candidate is a **witness whose normal execution prepares its own next checked binding**. The carrier is consumed by today's useful admission. Computing that very admission produces a feature from which the next carrier can be bound without collecting a new batch, calling a teacher, or fitting a second repair. The stronger claim is that the binding preserves a useful probe if *either* of two admitted repairs is taken, not merely if the planned one is taken. An exact planner with the same state, evidence, and moves already subsumes both claims. The empirical question is whether a fixed learned batch and a reusable binding check can replace part of its per-job work on unseen continuations.

[Correction classes (PR #169)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/169) asks which jobs can share a witness, not whether using that witness regenerates its physical binding. [Self-replenishing capacity (PR #170)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/170) restores a reserve, which need not contain the particular feature a witness reads. [Indistinguishable edits (PR #173)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/173) and [examples versus probes (PR #179)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/179) ask how a chosen reply or truthful examples affect sensing; here a port must survive a subsequent choice that may not be ours. These distinctions are not evidence of gains.

## What must survive a spend

- **Fix the operation before the stream.** Freeze the batch, the usual loss and optimizer, the recipe's admissible address map, and the utility test. Search over truthful batches before deployment; no invented feature targets, renewal loss, extra repair step, demand-specific binding, or hidden teacher at run time. An off-the-shelf constrained fit or an auxiliary seed head is a useful control, not the strong mechanism. The transfer may bind or move the produced feature into a dormant slot, but it may not learn the feature on the side.
- **Distinguish a carrier from a useful incidental feature.** Show a held-out job that was served by the old binding but cannot now be served at the same budget. Measure the feature before and after the spend; use the declared transfer, verify that it leaves installed outputs unchanged on declared domains, then run the identical recipe on a newly revealed job. Test several cycles after the original source and its backup are retired and their slots reused. Check the feature's necessity with removal or substitution, and sufficiency by restoring only that feature, not the host snapshot. Charge unavailable donor options and require the recurring-demand claim to balance its donor stock; cumulative new commitments have a separate bill.
- **Separate a surviving port from a surviving decision.** Freeze two genuinely competitive repairs that both meet the same current utility and retention floors (match cost, or report the premium for robustness). On clones, take each repair, then use the same declared lawful probe protocol with no retraining of the decoder. A fixed, predeclared repair-indexed interpretation may be used only if the actual repair index is lawful and charged. A port has survived if the transcript on **each** branch distinguishes hidden continuations requiring different verified affordable responses, relative to the best no-probe reply or abstention. Current repair identity alone is not such evidence. Include a case where a common safe reply makes sensing redundant.

### Shared contract for the CPU first tests

Start with exact enumeration of tiny finite menus and then try a trained linear or small ReLU host; the finite examples check the protocol, not a learning win. Freeze blind editors and held-out old inputs before choosing the batch. Compare (i) per-job repair fit, (ii) GRACE-style cached repair + ReDo/continual-backprop resupply + Net2Net binding + exact cost-sensitive diagnosis on the same actions and information, and (iii) the relevant strongest teaching or trajectory-matching objective with the same feature and data menu. Also include real versus sham transfer crossed with correct versus permuted binding, with matched current utility, A/C margins where relevant, and capacity. If exact joint planning ties, ask which evidence, fit, or certificate work can be avoided, not whether the compiled recipe can outplan its own oracle.

Score useful blind admissions over no-write, work per admission and decision cost **on each repair branch**, with separate rates for no port, non-actionable reads, and unknowns. Charge labels, searches, verifier calls, saved state (including probe logs/forks and decoder state), peak width, and donor debt; include setup cost rather than amortizing it over a horizon chosen after seeing results. **Retention notation:** `A` is the unchanged old-task suite with held-out rare cases; `C` is all prior edits not explicitly superseded, including the current repair after it lands. Every card requires acquisition-anchored A/C floors on all committed prefixes and exact declared-domain output equality across transfers. The extra checks below target different ways to get apparent renewal by losing an obligation.

## Idea cards

### 01. Teach on a tie between truthful repairs — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Choose a fixed ordinary repair batch from a set of truthful batches that tie on today's admitted job. Select the one whose gradient also grows the latent coordinate required by the next binding. Today's repair must spend the current carrier; moving the learned coordinate into a dormant slot is the only renewal step. **Novelty / control.** [Iterative machine teaching][teaching] already selects examples for their update; [trajectory matching][mtt] can aim at a successor state. The candidate is a batch that keeps creating the same checked precondition after use, not one lucky selection. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate balanced and skewed subsets of valid examples in two-feature regression; evaluate both on the same full current task. Freeze the best and check a third job after two bindings, against matched terminal-state teaching and external resupply. **Old-task retention.** Include old inputs aligned with the incidental feature, not just inputs orthogonal to it; match their margins before attributing future service to seeding.

### 02. Make the ordinary residual write tomorrow's gate — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Use a small shared trunk so a residual needed to correct today's predictions also moves a dormant route toward a bindable gate threshold. Designate the route before choosing the truthful batch and forbid extra gate targets. **Novelty / control.** [GradMax][gradmax] grows initially neutral units to improve future optimization; [gradient matching][condense] can target the repair vector. Here the current residual should create a specific callable entry state at fixed width, not only large useful gradients. **Cheapest first test.** On a two-layer scalar-input ReLU net, compare same-utility ordinary batches with aligned and opposed residuals; hold threshold and moves fixed. Require independently frozen follow-up jobs and a causal feature swap, not a win defined by gate crossing. **Old-task retention.** Sweep old points on both sides of the shifting ReLU boundary, with near-boundary cases held out from witness selection.

### 03. Transfer a feature statistic, not a feature copy — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Bind the next carrier using only a sufficient statistic of the ordinary repair's learned activation on a declared domain, e.g. a scale and sign relative to a fixed basis. Verify that the frozen recipe sees the same input and update over its covered domain. **Novelty / control.** [Net2Net][net] transports a function; [Pacti][pacti] composes entry assumptions. Candidate savings are in transmitting and checking the *update-relevant* statistic rather than cloning an activation or tracing the previous use. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate two bases for a linear head: one in which the statistic determines the next update and a mixed basis with a collision. Compare minimal checked statistics with exact cloning and fresh transfer verification. **Old-task retention.** Keep old cases whose outputs depend on omitted coordinates; perturb those coordinates to distinguish safe proof abstraction from hiding a failed task.

### 04. Let a source-free cycle repay its own donor — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Let the spend free the old scratch slot only after the update builds a feature in another slot. Bind there and use the released slot as the next round's donor, without ever using a slot still read by an old commitment. **Novelty / control.** [Continual backprop][cbp] and [ReDo][redo] already recycle capacity; [#170](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/170) checks reserve recovery. Here the next executable feature is a byproduct of admitted learning, and the renewal pays its own replacement bill. **Cheapest first test.** Two scratch slots, one previously installed head, alternating independent jobs over a bounded recurring demand set. Compare measured donor stock and useful admissions with reset-and-recover plus cache. Add a job that leaves a live reader of the spent slot and must break this cycle. **Old-task retention.** Execute past users of the reclaimed slot after each reuse, including an old case whose path differs from the common head.

### 05. Require the same port after either repair — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** During batch selection prefer features that bind a port through both of two cost- and utility-matched repairs. A branch may change probe values, but the declared interpretation must still select a useful verified reply when the hidden continuation changes. **Novelty / control.** [Safe exploration][safe] and [dual control][dual] plan for safe actions and information; parent CRR5 asks for a later distinguishing probe. Here test a port that can be inherited even if another editor chooses the repair. **Cheapest first test.** Plant two hidden continuations sharing current behavior and needing different next replies; cross them with both repairs. Give one fragile binding a cheaper cost than the robust one in a sensitivity arm. Compare CRR5 with chosen-branch sensing and exact contingent sensing with all the same moves. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C under both repairs before sensing and again after each possible verified reply, including continuations where the probe should be skipped.

### 06. Keep an invariant read through complementary overwrites — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Let either repair overwrite a different part of the seed, but design the ordinary repair batch to learn a relational read shared by both successors (e.g. the same contrast exposed in either surviving coordinate pair). Only one fixed small probe reads the contrast. **Novelty / control.** [Orthogonal gradient descent][ogd] protects selected outputs to first order; [#175](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/175) uses jointly silent slots. A finite repair-invariant diagnostic is stronger than reserving a null direction, but could reduce to exact invariant enforcement. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust a three-feature linear host with two finite coordinate rewrites and one bounded read. Compare best single reserved coordinate, two copies, and exact invariant search; include a rewrite where the two surviving read spaces lose their intersection. **Old-task retention.** Keep old readers of both overwritten coordinates so simply disabling either path cannot pass.

### 07. Bind a port to a symmetry orbit, not a unit index — improvement
**Mechanism.** Repairs can permute or rescale the units carrying a learned seed. Publish a canonical binding selected by lawful activation tests, with an explicit tie/unknown outcome when symmetry cannot be resolved. **Novelty / control.** [Git Re-Basin][rebasin] aligns units under permutations and [Net2Net][net] preserves functions. The improvement is cheaply resolving only the witness/probe addresses the next service actually needs; permutation symmetry itself is not new. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate signed permutations in a tiny host and add a collision whose aligned weights look plausible but whose probe meanings differ. Compare whole-network matching, fixed addresses, and the checked selective alignment. **Old-task retention.** Run old inputs that activate multiple aligned units to expose an address swap that only preserves single-unit probes.

### 08. Learn a reference that survives a probe sign flip — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Predeclare an equivariant port: either repair may flip a feature's sign, but the same lawful read pairs it with a repair-invariant reference so the decoded distinction is unchanged. Neither a new decoder fit nor a branch log is needed. **Novelty / control.** [Partial-information games][games] can condition on a trustworthy action log; [Net2Net][net] supplies function-preserving transformations. A reusable host-side reference counts only if it replaces a paid log or re-verification, not if the decoder is given the hidden branch for free. **Cheapest first test.** Compare two gradient-induced sign conventions with a reference-bit read, an equally sized transcript log, and fresh calibration; add a case where the reference vanishes. **Old-task retention.** Hold out old near-zero reference cases and verify both branches and abstention without weakening their floors.

### 09. Use an intervened clone to prove the seed matters — improvement (audit)
**Mechanism.** After the ordinary repair, overwrite the putative seed with a margin-matched decoy before transfer; in a rescue arm restore only the measured seed. Cross that with the matched sham move and binding permutation. **Novelty / control.** [Architecture-preserving provable repair][aprnn] supplies controls for keeping the installed behavior fixed, and parent #21 already proposes sham transfers. The addition is a causal mediation audit separating feature formation, move, and address. **Cheapest first test.** On a four-unit CPU host search for decoys equal on every installed test but different under the recipe; if none are legal report that the intervention does not identify the effect. Compare useful second and third admissions, not only next-gate activation. **Old-task retention.** Match A/C and old-output margins across ablation, rescue and untouched arms, including the recent repair.

### 10. Grow the next seed in the optimizer, not the forward pass — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** A fixed ordinary repair leaves a momentum component that is both spent by the next recipe and re-created by its next update. The carrier explicitly declares these training-time reads and binds slots and optimizer state together. **Novelty / control.** [Learning to Continually Learn][lamaml] already uses optimization dynamics to improve future adaptation; [#161](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/161) counts optimizer readers. Candidate novelty is source-free renewal of this specific checked transition, invisible to output-equivalent weight copies. **Cheapest first test.** In two-coordinate momentum SGD, compare full derived state, zeroed momentum, permuted momentum, and paid state copying on the same fixed batch and later jobs. **Old-task retention.** Test older tasks after the first update from the new binding, not just immediately after copying weights when all arms can look identical.

### 11. Close a period-two teaching orbit — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** A fixed witness uses two predeclared compatible addresses; normal execution at each writes the feature required at the other. No new batch enters at the switch. **Novelty / control.** [Trajectory matching][mtt] can target a finite sequence and [reachability][hj] already finds controlled cycles. The candidate is a learned, independently checkable period that survives new jobs, not a replay of one prescribed trajectory. **Cheapest first test.** Enumerate orders of two recurring job types in a tiny piecewise-linear host, then hold out a repeated-job run. Compare a cycle-targeted teaching control with exact planning and a single-address refill. Mark failed types unknown or unsupported rather than silently skipping them. **Old-task retention.** Keep tasks from both phases and evaluate off-phase and repeated-type prefixes to reveal alternating forgetting.

### 12. Pay off a delayed seed rather than resetting early — improvement
**Mechanism.** Publish a minimum feature-to-bind deadline. A partially built seed may mature over the remaining ordinary minibatches of the current repair, but cannot be counted as renewed early. Charge all time for which the previous carrier stays live. **Novelty / control.** [Reachability][hj] handles deadlines and the [split-timed deadlines node](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/180) treats expiry of entry rights. This is an overlap schedule for learned readiness, not a new time model. **Cheapest first test.** Use a two-step repair whose first step does not suffice and introduce a blind job between steps. Compare waiting, external refill, and completion under equal deadline and memory budgets. **Old-task retention.** Probe ordinary old tasks throughout the overlap and after a missed deadline; do not excuse intermediate degradation by later recovery.

### 13. Bind only a prefix of the recipe after a partial spend — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Divide the witness's fixed update into declared stages. A spend prepares an entry state for the next use's early stage, which fills the rest of the carrier through its ordinary training, before any installed mutation by that use. **Novelty / control.** [Trajectory matching][mtt] regulates learning paths and [return corridors (PR #168)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/168) checks future witness entry. The possible gain is retiring earlier physical state while retaining a callable checked prefix, not claiming partial service as full. **Cheapest first test.** Use two ordered elementary batches and a small nonlinear model where swapping stages never reaches the entry region. Compare an equivalent full-carrier binding, fresh prefix replay, and the partial checked chain at identical utility and peak state. **Old-task retention.** Evaluate A/C after each installed stage, including a case where the late stage fixes damage the early stage would otherwise hide.

### 14. Let the witness raise an alarm before re-binding the wrong feature — improvement
**Mechanism.** Reuse a cheap predicate on the spend's produced activations to flag that a plausible new seed binds the recipe but to the wrong correction class. Block reuse until checked; the predicate cannot use future labels. **Novelty / control.** [Counterexample-guided repair][cegis] already refines failing regions and [#169](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/169) handles correction-class splits. Improvement requires cheaper early detection without rejecting more useful jobs, not renaming refusal. **Cheapest first test.** Hold out a co-occurrence shift that creates an activation lookalike. Compare the alarm to support size, exact class verification, and direct repair at the same total proof budget. **Old-task retention.** Check both rejection and acceptance paths; re-run a rare earlier edit from the original class after the lookalike occurs.

### 15. Make two useful repairs hand off the same checked entry proof — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Each branch learns a different seed through ordinary repair, but both satisfy a common sufficient entry predicate for the next frozen carrier. A borrower checks only the predicate and the finite local binding, not either branch's training trace. **Novelty / control.** [Pacti][pacti] composes contract alternatives and [certificate entry ports (PR #171)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/171) already make proofs inheritable. Candidate savings require *learning both branch entries through the useful repairs* so later independent jobs benefit versus checked transport with external refill. **Cheapest first test.** Pick two distinct ReLU activation regions with a common downstream affine restriction; test proof reuse against incremental exact checking and a union of separately certified proofs. **Old-task retention.** Include an old input in only one branch region and one across the boundary; require A/C for both successors, not only their intersection.

### 16. Separate the examples that fix the job from those that earn the port — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Within one fixed truthful batch, some examples support useful current repair while variation among the rest makes the post-repair feature expose the later decision. Require both parts to matter under equal-size subset replacement, with no separate probing loss. **Novelty / control.** [Gradient-matching coresets][gcr] select examples for update directions and [dual control][dual] jointly optimizes utility and information. The learning claim is a read that survives both equally useful repair choices because of the batch, not a cached label for a later job. **Cheapest first test.** In a factored regression host, hold out independent pairs of factors; cross balanced and matched shuffled correlations in the witness and swap repair branches. Compare information-only selection plus refill and joint trajectory matching. **Old-task retention.** Include old examples for all factor pairings, especially those absent from the witness, to catch a spurious correlation bought at their expense.

### 17. Keep the port cheap under rare but admissible erasure — improvement
**Mechanism.** Choose between redundancy and a stronger invariant read based on the worst feasible continuation cost after either repair, including rare destructive repairs. Pay for redundancy in slots rather than treating diagnostics as free. **Novelty / control.** [Partial-information games][games] and [safe exploration][safe] already model worst-case outcomes; this is a budget trade-off for a learned port, not a new robustness criterion. **Cheapest first test.** Compare no redundancy, a duplicate and an invariant read on the same finite repair pairs with varied erasure rates and the same shadow budget. Include a common-reply null where the cheapest choice is no port. **Old-task retention.** Score rare old clients after each destructive branch as well as the mean; verify duplication does not displace their history.

### 18. Use a two-read protocol without doubling persistent state — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Learn a single seed that supports two lawful read inputs. Which second input is worth paying for depends on the first response; the protocol and all reads must remain executable after either repair. **Novelty / control.** [Dual control][dual] and [partial-information games][games] already optimize contingent queries; the candidate is one readout learned by the ordinary repair that replaces individually allocated probe memories at equal decision quality. **Cheapest first test.** Hide one of four future repair types; enumerate cheap first reads and expensive second reads on identical trained features, compare exact decision trees with one-shot sensing and separate ports, and make one response intentionally ambiguous. **Old-task retention.** Check A/C after each read or fork discard and after each selected response, including the ambiguous path.

### 19. Test the order of repair and transfer as a resource — improvement
**Mechanism.** Compare binding just after the ordinary repair against binding before it with the same allowed moves. A preliminary bind can burn the only slot whose contents the repair would otherwise make usable; a late bind can lose a diagnostic port the repair destroys. **Novelty / control.** [Net2Net][net] provides the transport and [dual control][dual] prices sequencing. Noncommutativity is evidence about the cause, not a primitive by itself. **Cheapest first test.** In a two-donor host, explicitly enumerate both orders, an exact scheduler, and a sham transfer while preserving the same final current utility. Test blind next service and lawful probe actionability. **Old-task retention.** Check intermediate installed outputs on old cases in both orders; an illegal intermediate may not be compared as a cheap alternative.

### 20. Distinguish faithful renewal from a better arbitrary initializer — improvement (audit)
**Mechanism.** Send the learned seed to either the declared frozen witness or an unrelated but compute-matched repair recipe. If both benefit equally, report reusable general initialization rather than witness-specific renewal. **Novelty / control.** [Continual backprop][cbp] and [GradMax][gradmax] already aim at useful future trainability; the comparison asks whether this batch produces a specific binding instead. **Cheapest first test.** Cross two same-utility witnesses with their correctly bound and swapped seed features on unseen jobs; compare fresh initializers at matched margins, rank and width. **Old-task retention.** Test old tasks from both repair families, not just the one the new initializer helps.

### 21. Learn when a common port is impossible without storing another bit — improvement (audit)
**Mechanism.** Derive a collision certificate: after an admissible repair, two latent continuations yield the same permitted transcript but require incompatible verified affordable replies. Reuse the obstruction only while its observation and repair assumptions remain checked. **Novelty / control.** [Partial-information games][games] already supplies the indistinguishability argument. A cheap learned obstruction would help prune batch search, but the lower bound itself is not new. **Cheapest first test.** Exhaust three-bit finite states with one branch erasing the only distinguishing bit and a second instance with one spare read; compare an exact planner, fresh collision search, and cached obstructions. **Old-task retention.** On the collision branch test no-write retention and count the lost useful job; on the feasible branch require the repair and both A/C floors so universal refusal cannot win.

### 22. Hand a seeded carrier to an independent editor — primitive candidate
**Mechanism.** Bind a common feature before the next editor is revealed; a separate editor proposes a previously unobserved job using only the published address and obligation contract. Demand blind repeat service under editor-family shift, with a checked unknown for out-of-scope jobs. **Novelty / control.** [Nonparametric teaching for multiple learners][multiteach] shares examples across learners; [GRACE][grace] reuses fixed repair state. The conditional novelty is a feature generated by actual earlier repair that a different editor can use through the unchanged witness, without a fresh fit or concealed scheduling signal. **Cheapest first test.** Select a witness using one set of linear task generators; hold out a composition of generators and hide their order. Compare data-matched teaching, stacked resupply/cache, and a planner given the same coarse information; separately score the full-information ceiling. **Old-task retention.** Keep old tasks from both generator families and all previous unsuperseded edits, especially ones outside the current recurrence class.

## Ranked deeper questions

1. **Does the spend itself produce reusable state, or is the transfer doing all the work?** Start with 01, 04 and 09. Can the same frozen ordinary batch serve several blind jobs after source retirement, and does seed removal stop that service while seed-only rescue restores it? Match utility and margins, charge binding and donor use, and test the stacked resupply/cache/diagnosis baseline. If per-job trajectory matching or external refill suffices at equal cost, drop the self-seeding claim.
2. **Is preserving the port after either repair worth its price?** Start with 05, 06 and 21. Require an actionable read on both branches of genuinely competitive repairs, including the planted destructive branch and a common-reply null. Test against CRR5 alone, exact contingent sensing with an equally paid log or copy, [#173](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/173) informative replies, and [#174](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/174) decision-relevant queries. If copying or an affordable no-probe reply ties, there is no robust-port gain.
3. **What is the cheapest transferable evidence that certifies both next execution and surviving diagnosis?** Start with 03, 08, 15 and 18. Find the smallest reusable checked representation for blind successors including optimizer reads and lawful timing. Compare against carrying full state and incremental re-verification; a shorter certificate that moves cost into a hidden decoder, calibration set or future labels is not a win.
4. **When do recurrent repairs stop renewing under accumulated commitments and new editors?** Start with 04, 11, 14 and 22. Separate feature drift, changed correction coverage, donor exhaustion and vanished diagnosis. Test long recurring streams separately from net-new obligations and look for a cheap falsifiable stopping rule. A closed orbit on the training order is not evidence of renewal on the real stream.

These are nearest comparisons, not an exhaustive novelty clearance. The first two questions must show useful service or lower decision cost at matched A/C retention before the stronger object claim is worth pursuing.
