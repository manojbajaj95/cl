# Label-free recovery witnesses and sufficient carrier sets

**Parent:** [First point of loss](first-point-of-loss.md) ([PR #134](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/134)), specifically cards #5 (*recovery-path witness*) and #3 (*competition between sufficient carrier sets*), triaged in [`reviews/flood-triage.md` row 218](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/blob/devin/1795531716-reviewer-verdicts/reviews/flood-triage.md). **Related sibling nodes:** [momentum fossils (PR #136)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/136), [label-free forgetting detection (PR #126)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/126), [function-matched twins (PR #123)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/123), [differential fork registers (PR #114)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/114), [merger certificates (PR #110)](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/110). **Status:** ideas only; no probe run; no GPU.

**Node question:** When an old skill fails in live service and even defeats a frozen budgeted reader, can a bounded search over **label-free legal training continuations** (a *recovery witness*) prove the skill was hidden rather than erased—and repair it in live service without old labels? Dually, what are the **inclusion-minimal sufficient carrier sets**—the smallest joint subsets of weights plus optimizer state `(w, m, v, t, ...)` that still allow a distinction to be recovered—and how does protecting a hypergraph of competing carrier sets change continual learning?

## 1. Root cause and identifiability

PR #134 separates three loss regimes at a training cut: *service loss* (fresh A queries fail in normal forward use), *budgeted loss* (a frozen reader bank fails to decode A from the allowed observation), and *exact loss* (two legal A histories `A−` and `A+` have identical complete continuation state `z = (w, m, v, t, ...)` and shared public side information, so no target-blind continuation can ever separate them again; [PR #110](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/110)). Outside rounding, clipping, and hard resets, real-valued SGD rarely hits exact equality—it contracts and masks. That leaves a wide, poorly understood gap between "live accuracy dropped" and "the distinction is gone."

Two dual objects sit in that gap:

1. **Label-free recovery witness (constructive proof + executable repair).** Fix a legal menu `Π` of **target-blind** training continuations (short sequences of public-B steps, unlabeled auxiliary batches, optimizer-knob pulses, slot damping/resets, or normalization updates that receive **no A labels, no pair ID, and no A-specific target**). A continuation `π ∈ Π` is a *recovery witness* at state pair `(z(A−), z(A+))` if applying the **same** `π` to both states restores live forward separation on fresh held-out A queries above a preregistered tolerance.
   - *As a proof:* Finding `π` constructively **refutes exact loss** at the cut without fitting a new A-labelled decoder; it is the label-free counterpart of top-10 #5's relearning read head.
   - *As a repair:* Because `π` is target-blind and executable, running `π` repairs a degraded model *without old labels*—**provided** the learner can select `π` without peeking at A labels at deployment, and provided `π` does not damage new task B or an unrelated old task C.
   - *What failure does not prove:* Exhausting a bounded menu `Π` without finding a witness certifies only *`Π`-unrecoverability at that search budget*, never universal exact loss. Every card must report `(witness found | unresolved within Π)`.
2. **Minimal sufficient carrier sets (joint weight + optimizer-state hypergraph).** Partition the live continuation state `z` into addressable groups `G = {w_ℓ, m_ℓ, v_ℓ, norm_ℓ, t, ...}`. Given a declared witness `π` (or frozen reader) and a neutral/partner reference state `z_ref` (same B history, neutral or opposite A obligation), a subset `S ⊆ G` is a **sufficient carrier set** if transplanting `S` from `z` into `z_ref` (replacing the complement `S̄` with `z_ref`) still recovers the A contrast under `π`. `S` is *minimal* if no proper subset of `S` suffices.
   - *Why a hypergraph, not a salience vector:* Sufficiency is non-additive. Neither a weight block `w_ℓ` alone nor an optimizer slot `m_ℓ` or `v_ℓ` alone may recover the contrast, while their pair `{w_ℓ, m_ℓ}` does (complementarity). At the same time, multiple *competing* minimal sets `S₁, S₂, ...` can coexist (redundancy). An update, cast, or slot reset destroys recoverability **only when it intersects every surviving minimal set**—i.e., forms a *hitting set* of the carrier hypergraph `H(A, z)`.
3. **The two-mode discipline (audit proof vs. deployment repair).** A severe confound in "label-free recovery" is **selection leakage**: searching over 100 label-free continuations `π ∈ Π` and picking the one with the highest A accuracy on a held-out A set is *not* label-free repair—it is black-box labelled relearning via selection. Every card below must declare which mode it operates in:
   - *Audit/proof mode:* Paired `A−`/`A+` states exist in development or evaluation; the **same** `π` is applied to both and scored on fresh A queries to prove non-erasure and map carrier sets.
   - *Deployment repair mode:* `π` is chosen using **only** deployment-legal, target-blind signals (public-B dynamics, unlabeled probe geometry, pre-compiled witness libraries from earlier tasks, or carrier-hypergraph liveness)—never A labels, A rewards, or A-graded validation.

## 3. Shared CPU first-test contract

Every first test below is CPU-scale (no GPU) and enforces the same scoring discipline:

- **Two calibrated hosts.**
  1. *Planted finite-state / tiny synthetic host* with four ground-truth regimes: (a) an exact-merger pair (negative control where no witness can exist), (b) a single-direction spurious-shift pair ([2610.08718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718) control), (c) a weights-only buried pair (`L* ≥ 2`), and (d) a joint weight+slot synergistic pair where neither `w` nor `(m, v)` alone suffices.
  2. *Nonlinear feature-learning host:* width-64 two-layer ReLU MLP trained with Adam and SGD+momentum on paired opposite obligations `A−`/`A+`, shared materialized B batches (same RNG and schedule; B reveals nothing about A), and an unrelated old task **C**, with no task ID at inference. Include a frozen-feature/linear host as a negative control ([PR #114](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/114)).
- **Mandatory shortcut & rival controls.**
  - *For witness claims:* (1) raw same-input logits, (2) complete first gradients, (3) exact one-update endpoints, (4) linearized ISO response ([2608.19762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19762)), (5) random continuations from `Π` at equal search/update budget, (6) plain continued B and single-direction common-shift removal ([2610.08718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718)), and (7) labelled relearning at equal gradient steps (ceiling/rival for repair).
  - *For carrier-set claims:* (1) per-value marginal salience (EWC/diagonal Fisher, magnitude, single-group ablation), (2) equal-byte delta/checkpoint replication or DER, and (3) full optimizer reset ([2306.17833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17833)).
- **Old-task retention & accounting.** Score fresh **live** `A−`/`A+` and unrelated **C** accuracy/loss in normal forward service (no oracle, no A-labelled decoder), at matched held-out **B** progress. Continue shared B for `K` post-repair steps (or re-mix) to reject transient spikes that immediately re-collapse. Charge all search rollouts, fork copies, probe calls, and retained bytes. Replicate across ≥ 10 independent worlds before claiming a win. **P** marks a primitive candidate; **I** marks an improvement.

## 4. Idea cards

### A. Searching and synthesizing label-free recovery witnesses

#### 1. Compositional witness grammar and continuation compiler (P)

- **Mechanism:** Define a small typed grammar of legal, target-blind training primitives—e.g. public-B micro-batches, layer-scoped LR/weight-decay pulses, momentum damping/sign-flip, second-moment clamping, and norm-stat refresh—and search short compositional programs `π = op_k ∘ … ∘ op_1` (`k ≤ 4`) that restore a buried `A−`/`A+` distinction into live forward outputs.
- **Novelty rationale:** Passive benign relearning ([2406.13356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13356), [2501.13453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13453)) and common-shift removal ([2610.08718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718)) apply a single homogeneous update class. Treating label-free recovery as a *compositional program* over heterogeneous optimizer operations can wake distinctions that no single step or linear response exposes (calibrating on [PR #123](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/123)'s two-step wake-up witness).
- **Cheapest first test:** On the planted host and width-64 MLP (Adam + SGD+m), enumerate `k = 1, 2, 3` programs from a 6-op grammar on paired `A−`/`A+` trajectories after service loss. Compare against single-op search, random `k`-op continuations, plain continued B, and all four static/one-step shortcut reads at matched compute. Kill if `k ≥ 2` programs never beat `k = 1` and linearized ISO response on held-out A separation.
- **Old-task retention check:** Execute the winning program on both `A−` and `A+` (no A labels), then run 20 more shared B steps; require live A separation to stay above threshold while C and held-out B stay within 1 pp of unperturbed B training.

#### 2. Self-bifurcation witness selector for blind deployment (P)

- **Mechanism:** To pick a recovery witness `π ∈ Π` at deployment *without A labels*, apply each candidate `π` to two micro-perturbed copies of the current state (or compare `π` across unlabeled public probes) and select the `π` that produces the largest **low-rank, structured directional divergence** on unlabeled old-domain probes while leaving public-B loss invariant—betting that waking a suppressed basin moves old-probe logits coherently rather than isotropically.
- **Novelty rationale:** Solves the core selection-leakage problem left open by passive benign relearning ([2406.13356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13356)). Unlike entropy minimization / test-time adaptation (ARC [2405.14318](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14318), RoSE [2503.16793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16793)), which optimizes confidence on test inputs and can amplify wrong priors (ledger A3), this selects a *training continuation* by structured sensitivity on unlabeled probes.
- **Cheapest first test:** In the width-64 MLP with 20 candidate `π ∈ Π` (some waking A, some shifting priors, some pure noise), score blind selection by top-1 recovery rate vs. random `π`, entropy-minimizing `π`, and a temperature-matched control, with oracle A-selected `π` as ceiling. Include a planted exact-merger negative control where the selector must abstain. Kill if blind selection ties random `π` or entropy minimization.
- **Old-task retention check:** Deploy the blindly chosen `π` into the live model, score fresh A, unrelated C, and held-out B immediately and after 20 further B steps; reject if the selector picks prior-shifting continuations that hurt C.

#### 3. Structural-key public-B witness mining (P)

- **Mechanism:** Exploit the finding that syntactic/structural alignment drives benign relearning without topical overlap ([2602.03379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03379)): given only *unlabeled* structural templates of old task A (input-schema/feature-graph motifs, zero A labels or topics), mine or reweight public-B batches whose structural geometry aligns with A's masked circuit and run them as a label-free wake-up continuation.
- **Novelty rationale:** [2602.03379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03379) diagnoses syntactic benign relearning as an unlearning vulnerability and suppresses it; [2501.13453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13453) uses arbitrary irrelevant tasks. Turning structural-key alignment into a *targeted label-free CL witness generator* is an explicit, testable inversion—distinct from MIR ([1908.04742](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.04742)), which retrieves *labelled old examples*.
- **Cheapest first test:** Build a modular/compositional MLP task where A and B share tunable input-structure motifs (e.g., routing-tree shape) with orthogonal target labels. Compare structurally matched public-B continuations against random public-B, norm-matched irrelevant-task steps ([2501.13453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13453)), and labelled A replay at equal gradient steps. Kill if structurally matched public-B does not beat random public-B beyond seed CI.
- **Old-task retention check:** Measure live A recovery on unseen inputs with both matched and mismatched surface features, plus C accuracy and held-out B progress after returning to uniform B batches.

#### 4. Universal wake-up words (task-agnostic synchronizing witnesses) (P)

- **Mechanism:** Search for a *single* short label-free continuation `π*` (a "wake-up word") that simultaneously restores live separation across an entire family of *unseen* paired obligations `{(A₁−, A₁+), …, (A_m−, A_m+)}` without knowing which obligation was trained or which direction it took.
- **Novelty rationale:** Exact dual of synchronizing reset words ([PR #108](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/108), Černý automata): whereas a reset word *collapses* distinct histories into one canonical state, a wake-up word *un-masks* distinct buried histories into distinct live outputs simultaneously. Distinct from single-direction common-shift removal ([2610.08718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718)) when the wake-up word requires nonlinear multi-step un-masking.
- **Cheapest first test:** Train 20 independent `A_i−`/`A_i+` obligations on the width-64 MLP, fit `π*` on 10 development obligations, and test zero-shot wake-up on the 10 held-out obligations after shared B training. Compare against single-direction shift removal ([2610.08718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718)), momentum reset ([2610.03940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03940)), and random `π`. Kill if `π*` fails to generalize to held-out obligations or ties momentum reset / common-shift removal.
- **Old-task retention check:** Apply `π*` to held-out obligations and verify that live A recovers, unrelated C does not drop > 1 pp, and B loss stays matched after 15 post-wake B steps.

#### 5. Dual non-recovery certificates (obstruction witnesses for `Π`) (P)

- **Mechanism:** Whenever bounded search over continuation menu `Π` fails to separate `z(A−)` and `z(A+)`, compute a *dual obstruction*—e.g., an interval/Lipschitz contraction bound over `Π` or a shared invariant subspace of the step operators in `Π` showing `‖f(π(z−)) − f(π(z+))‖ ≤ ε` for every `π ∈ Π`—distinguishing "our search missed a witness" from "no witness exists in `Π`."
- **Novelty rationale:** PR #134's exact loss requires bitwise state equality, which almost never occurs in floating-point SGD; conversely, search timeout proves nothing. A `Π`-obstruction certificate is a new formal object bridging exact mergers ([PR #110](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/110)) and budgeted reader failure, distinct from input-space verification or parameter-norm bounds ([2609.32818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32818)).
- **Cheapest first test:** In a small piecewise-linear / tiny ReLU host with bounded `Π`, compute interval-bound or linearized-plus-remainder obstruction certificates alongside exhaustive `Π` search across 50 `A−`/`A+` pairs spanning buried and truly contracted regimes. Measure true-positive certification rate vs. naive state-distance threshold (`‖z− − z+‖ < δ`). Kill if the obstruction certificate never certifies unrecoverability where `‖z− − z+‖` is still large.
- **Old-task retention check:** Use the obstruction certificate as a trigger to switch from label-free search to labelled replay; measure live A, C, and total label budget vs. threshold-triggered replay on full B streams.

#### 6. Portable witness recipes under sequential drift (P)

- **Mechanism:** Record a discovered label-free recovery witness `π₁` (discovered after task B₁ masks task A) as a compact symbolic recipe, and test whether replaying `π₁` still wakes A after further unrelated tasks `B₂, B₃, …`—or measure its *recipe decay rate* as a function of cumulative drift.
- **Novelty rationale:** Connects benign relearning ([2406.13356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13356)) to the rate–drift law ([PR #105](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/105), top-10 #4): a witness recipe is a *dynamical decoder* that uses the current model as side information, so its validity should decay at a measurable rate under drift rather than staying valid forever.
- **Cheapest first test:** On a 4-task MLP stream `A → B₁ → B₂ → B₃`, find `π₁` at the end of `B₁`, freeze it, and evaluate its live A recovery after `B₂` and `B₃` against freshly searched `π₂, π₃`, random `π`, and stored-logit DER at equal bytes. Kill if `π₁` either never works past `B₁` (zero portability) or works identically to a fixed momentum reset.
- **Old-task retention check:** Track live A, B₁, B₂, and unrelated C after executing the frozen recipe `π₁` at each stage; reject if stale recipes wake A by wrecking `B₁`/`B₂` or C.

### B. Separating hidden vs. erased: proof and audit primitives

#### 7. Operational burial-depth hierarchy (`L*`) (P)

- **Mechanism:** Define the **burial depth** `L*(z; Π)` of an old distinction as the minimum number of legal label-free continuation steps `k` required to bring `A−`/`A+` live separation above threshold: `L* = 0` (live), `L* = 1` (one-step / linearly accessible), `L* ≥ 2` (nonlinearly buried, hidden from static and first-order reads), `L* = ∞` (erased under `Π`). Track the discrete ladder `L*(t)` across training.
- **Novelty rationale:** Replaces smooth forgetting curves and static probe accuracy with an *operational step-depth invariant* that explicitly subtracts the `L* = 0` and `L* = 1` shortcuts flagged by [PR #114](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/114), [PR #123](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/123), and [2608.19762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19762). Unlike top-10 #5's labelled relearning step count, `L*` uses zero A labels.
- **Cheapest first test:** On the width-64 MLP (and linear negative control), log `L*(t)` over 200 B steps alongside raw-logit, first-gradient, one-update, and linearized ISO readouts. Verify whether a genuine `L* ≥ 2` plateau exists where all `L* ≤ 1` reads sit at chance yet a `k = 2, 3` witness recovers live A. Kill if every recoverable state in the MLP is already `L* ≤ 1` (reducible to linear response).
- **Old-task retention check:** Trigger label-free repair only when `L*` crosses `1 → 2` (before `∞`), and compare final live A, C, and B accuracy against periodic fixed-interval repair at equal total updates.

#### 8. Paired-obligation false-revival discriminator (P)

- **Mechanism:** Guard against *hallucinated recovery*—continuations `π` that raise aggregate A accuracy merely by shifting class priors, undoing recency bias, or sharpening logits (the ledger's A3 temperature/prior confound)—by requiring a witness to pass a **paired-swap symmetry check**: `π` must drive `z(A−)` toward `A−` and `z(A+)` toward `A+` on the *same* public queries, beating an entropy-matched temperature scaling and a class-prior recalibration head.
- **Novelty rationale:** Prior spurious-forgetting studies ([2501.13453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13453), [2512.20634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20634), [2610.08718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718)) measure accuracy recovery on one fixed old task, which mixes true content recovery with output-bias restoration. This primitive isolates *state-specific distinction recovery* from bias/temperature artifacts.
- **Cheapest first test:** In the width-64 MLP, compare candidate witnesses on both single-task A accuracy and paired `A−`/`A+` contrast, alongside post-hoc logit-bias calibration and entropy-matched temperature scaling. Quantify what fraction of "spurious-forgetting recoveries" vanish under the paired-swap check. Kill if single-task recovery and paired-swap recovery never disagree.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score live `A−`, `A+`, and C after applying only witnesses that pass the paired-swap check vs. witnesses that pass single-task accuracy alone; check whether passing the discriminator prevents C bias corruption.

#### 9. Re-burial half-life ratio (surface unmasking vs. structural re-anchoring) (P)

- **Mechanism:** After a label-free witness `π` restores live A to accuracy `α`, resume ordinary shared B training and measure the **re-burial half-life** `τ_witness` (steps until A falls back halfway to pre-repair failure), normalized by `τ_label`, the re-burial half-life of labelled A relearning matched to the same post-repair accuracy `α` and update norm.
- **Novelty rationale:** [2512.20634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20634) defines shallow vs. deep alignment statically by token position; [PR #121](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/121) separates transient excursions from persistent state after re-mixing. The ratio `τ_witness / τ_label` operationally distinguishes *shallow unmasking* (`τ_witness ≪ τ_label`) from *true structural repair* (`τ_witness ≈ τ_label`).
- **Cheapest first test:** On the width-64 MLP, compare `τ_witness / τ_label` across three witness classes (momentum-only pulse, structural public-B continuation, and multi-op compositional witness) matched for immediate post-repair A accuracy and weight-update norm. Kill if all label-free witnesses have `τ_witness / τ_label < 0.2` (purely transient bounce-back) or if update norm alone predicts `τ`.
- **Old-task retention check:** Report area under the live A curve over 100 post-repair B steps, worst-window A, and final C/B accuracy—ensuring a longer `τ_witness` is not bought by freezing B learning.

#### 10. Naive-model zero-leakage differential proof (P)

- **Mechanism:** To prove that a continuation `π` recovered a *hidden old trace* rather than accidentally teaching A from latent correlations in public B data, run the identical `π` on a **matched A-naive control** `z_naive` (same initialization and B history, never trained on A− or A+). Define the certified recovery gain as the excess paired separation `Δ_cert(π) = Sep(π(z−), π(z+)) − max(Sep(π(z_naive), π(z−)), baseline)`.
- **Novelty rationale:** Benign-relearning papers ([2406.13356](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13356), [2602.03379](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.03379)) compare against unlearned vs. retrained models, but in CL with overlapping feature geometry, public B data can partially *re-teach* an old rule from scratch. The naive-model differential is a clean proof-of-memory operator separating *revival* from *incidental re-teaching*.
- **Cheapest first test:** Sweep feature overlap between A and public B from 0% to 80% in the width-64 MLP; run candidate witnesses on `(z−, z+, z_naive)` and measure where naive-model separation becomes nonzero while `Δ_cert` still separates true hidden traces from B-overlap teaching. Kill if `z_naive` stays identically at chance across all realistic overlaps (making the differential redundant).
- **Old-task retention check:** Use `Δ_cert` to audit repair continuations and verify that repairs with high `Δ_cert` preserve both A and unrelated C better than continuations that merely overfit B-correlated shortcuts.

#### 11. Witness-phase switching policy (spurious / recoverable / erased) (I)

- **Mechanism:** Partition a continual stream into three operational phases using bounded witness search: Phase 0 (`L* = 0`, live), Phase 1 (`1 ≤ L* ≤ K`, witness-recoverable without labels), and Phase 2 (`L* > K`, witness-erased). Spend zero replay in Phase 0, run cheap label-free witness maintenance in Phase 1, and escalate to stored-label replay only upon entering Phase 2.
- **Novelty rationale:** Explicitly an **improvement (I)** over [2512.20634](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20634)'s alignment-depth trigger and [PR #113](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/113)'s rejected replay-deadline scheduler (which used scalar damage in a linear host with no cliff). Here the trigger is a discrete phase crossing in *executable label-free recoverability* on a nonlinear host.
- **Cheapest first test:** On a 5-task nonlinear MLP stream with mixed spurious-shift and true-overwrite shocks, compare witness-phase switching against uniform replay, accuracy-threshold replay, and pre-shock escrow ([PR #113](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/113)) at matched total labelled examples and compute. Kill if phase switching does not save ≥ 30% labelled replay at matched live A/C retention.
- **Old-task retention check:** Measure live A, C, and worst-task accuracy across the full stream plus 50 post-stream B steps, charging all witness-search steps against the compute budget.

### C. Minimal sufficient carrier sets (weights + optimizer state)

#### 12. Joint `(w, m, v)` minimal sufficient carrier hypergraph (P)

- **Mechanism:** Given a state `z = (w, m, v, t, …)` partitioned into coarse blocks (layer weights, first-moment blocks, second-moment blocks, norm stats) and a fixed witness `π`, run group-transplantation search (swapping subsets `S` from `z` into partner/reference `z_ref` and applying `π`) to extract the **carrier hypergraph** `H(A, z; π)` of all inclusion-minimal subsets `S` that still recover the A contrast.
- **Novelty rationale:** EWC ([1612.00796](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00796)), Fisher merging ([2111.09832](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.09832)), and subnetwork pruning score *individual* weights marginally; [PR #136](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/136) swaps `m` vs. `v` globally. The carrier hypergraph is a new combinatorial state object capturing both *multi-set redundancy* (multiple minimal sets) and *weight×slot complementarity* (sets containing both `w` and `m`/`v` blocks).
- **Cheapest first test:** On a 4-block width-64 MLP (12 addressable groups: 4 `w`, 4 `m`, 4 `v`), use delta-debugging / minimal-hitting-set search to extract `H(A, z; π)` after 10, 30, and 100 B steps. Check whether `H` actually contains (a) ≥ 2 disjoint minimal sets and (b) at least one mixed `{w, m/v}` minimal set. Kill if `H` always collapses to a single weight-only set predicted by diagonal Fisher magnitude.
- **Old-task retention check:** Verify that preserving any single hyperedge `S ∈ H` while resetting or overwriting `S̄` with B updates still recovers live A under `π` and keeps unrelated C intact when C's hyperedges are also respected.

#### 13. Hypergraph hitting-set protection (protect the last complete set, not every parameter) (P)

- **Mechanism:** Replace per-parameter quadratic penalties with a **combinatorial non-hitting constraint**: given the active carrier hypergraph `H` for protected old distinctions, allow an incoming B update, pruning mask, or slot reset on group set `U` **if and only if** `U` is *not* a hitting set of `H` (i.e., at least one complete minimal carrier set `S ∈ H` remains undisturbed). When `U` would hit the last intact `S`, block or project only on `S`.
- **Novelty rationale:** Direct realization of [PR #134](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/134) card #3. EWC ([1612.00796](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00796)), OGD ([1910.07104](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.07104)), and Adam-NSCL ([2103.07113](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.07113)) protect *all* important coordinates simultaneously, wasting plasticity on redundant copies. Hitting-set protection lets B freely overwrite redundant carriers until the redundancy count drops to 1.
- **Cheapest first test:** On the calibrated synthetic host (with planted redundant and complementary carriers) and the 12-group MLP under correlated slot resets and B updates, compare hitting-set protection against EWC, per-group salience protection, and equal-byte checkpoint replication at matched B learning speed. Kill if per-group marginal salience ties hitting-set protection on the retention–B-progress frontier.
- **Old-task retention check:** After B finishes (and after witness wake-up if needed), score both the joint-carrier A contrast and unrelated single-carrier A/C tasks; reject if freeing redundant A carriers damages C.

#### 14. Weight–slot synergistic carrier discovery (irreducible `w × m/v` interaction) (P)

- **Mechanism:** Explicitly search for **synergistic carrier pairs** `{w_i, slot_j}` (`slot ∈ {m, v}`) where marginal transplantation of `w_i` alone gives near-zero recovery under `π` and marginal transplantation of `slot_j` alone gives near-zero recovery under `π`, yet transplanting `{w_i, slot_j}` together recovers the A contrast—e.g., `w_i` holds a sub-threshold feature whose sign is unlocked only when `v_j`'s coordinate-wise preconditioning or `m_j`'s pending drift steers the witness step.
- **Novelty rationale:** [2608.20442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20442) transplants `m` alone on identical `w`; [PR #136](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/136) tests global `w`/`m`/`v` swaps. Synergistic `{w_i, slot_j}` carriers are strictly invisible to any marginal weight importance (EWC/Fisher) or marginal slot audit, testing whether real Adam/SGD training naturally creates cross-type carriers.
- **Cheapest first test:** First calibrate on a planted 2-layer XOR-style weight+slot gate, then scan all `(w_i, m_j)` and `(w_i, v_j)` pairs in the width-64 MLP after 20–50 Adam B steps, measuring interaction synergy `Rec(w_i, slot_j) − max(Rec(w_i), Rec(slot_j))` against the linearized ISO baseline ([2608.19762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19762)). Kill if natural Adam training produces < 5 pp synergy beyond linearized response.
- **Old-task retention check:** Protect discovered synergistic pairs vs. top-Fisher weight-only pairs of equal byte size during further B training; evaluate live A (post-witness) and C.

#### 15. Carrier-set relay migration across unavoidable resets (P)

- **Mechanism:** When a scheduled reset, quantization pass, or high-norm B phase is about to destroy the last intact sufficient carrier set `S₁ ∈ H`, run a short target-blind or public-B *migration pulse* that writes a *second* sufficient carrier set `S₂` disjoint from the threatened region, verify `S₂` via the witness `π` on a shadow copy, and only then permit the destructive operation on `S₁`.
- **Novelty rationale:** Extends [PR #134](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/134) card #12 (*verified carrier handoff*) from single values to *multi-component weight+slot carrier sets* under a fixed peak-memory budget—distinct from DualOptim+ ([2605.21539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21539)) or frozen adapter banks, because no extra permanent storage is allocated; the skill plays a relay race across transient carrier sets.
- **Cheapest first test:** In the 12-group MLP subjected to two alternating layer/slot-reset waves (first hitting lower blocks, then upper blocks), compare verified carrier-set migration against unverified copy, full-state checkpointing at matched peak bytes, and no migration. Kill if migration fails to keep live A above equal-byte checkpointing or loses the contrast during the handoff window.
- **Old-task retention check:** Track worst-window live A across both reset waves and final live A, C, and B accuracy after both original and migrated slots have been retired.

#### 16. Minimal-carrier selective slot reset and laundering (P)

- **Mechanism:** Use the carrier hypergraph `H` to make optimizer resets *surgical*: (a) for continual learning plasticity resets ([2306.17833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17833), [2610.03940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03940)), zero every optimizer slot *except* the smallest slot subset needed to keep one minimal carrier set `S ∈ H` intact for each old skill; (b) symmetrically, for unlearning ([PR #136](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/136) #12), launder a *minimal hitting set* of the forget-task's carrier hypergraph while leaving retain-task carrier sets untouched.
- **Novelty rationale:** Global momentum reset ([2306.17833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17833), [2610.03940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03940)) and DualOptim+ ([2605.21539](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21539)) treat optimizer state monolithically or per-task. Using minimal carrier sets and minimal hitting sets unifies plasticity-preserving CL resets and revival-proof unlearning under one hypergraph operation.
- **Cheapest first test:** On the width-64 MLP (Adam) with a shift from A to B (plus untouched C), compare carrier-preserving selective reset against full reset, norm-matched momentum reset ([2610.03940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03940)), and magnitude-ranked slot retention at matched fraction of zeroed slots. In a second arm, test minimal-hitting-set laundering against relearning revival. Kill if magnitude-ranked slot retention or full reset ties selective reset.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score live A (after witness if buried), live C, and B convergence speed; in the unlearning arm, verify forget-task revival stays at chance under `Π` while C stays within 1 pp.

#### 17. Cross-witness carrier intersection (intrinsic carriers vs. witness scaffolding) (P)

- **Mechanism:** Extract minimal carrier hypergraphs `H(π₁)` and `H(π₂)` using two *mechanistically distinct* label-free witnesses `π₁, π₂` (e.g., a momentum-pulse witness vs. a structural public-B witness). Partition blocks into **universal carriers** (appearing in minimal sets across all witnesses) and **witness-specific scaffolding** (needed only by one witness). Prioritize protection on universal carriers.
- **Novelty rationale:** Circuit discovery and relearning probes suffer from *probe-specific artifacts*—a block looks essential only because one particular probe or continuation routes through it. Cross-witness intersection isolates *intrinsic skill carriers* without needing ground-truth labels or activation-patching censuses.
- **Cheapest first test:** In the 12-group MLP, construct two distinct witnesses `π₁, π₂` for A, compute `H(π₁)` and `H(π₂)`, and compare protecting the cross-witness intersection vs. protecting `H(π₁)` alone, `H(π₂)` alone, and EWC Fisher top-k at equal protected byte count—evaluated under a *held-out third witness* `π₃` and direct labelled relearning. Kill if `H(π₁) ∩ H(π₂)` does not generalize better to `π₃` than single-witness carriers.
- **Old-task retention check:** After B training with intersection-protected blocks, score live A under `π₃`, live C, and held-out B progress.

### D. Label-free continual learning mechanisms built on witnesses and carrier sets

#### 18. Post-shock witness-crystallized self-distillation (P)

- **Mechanism:** When a label-free witness `π` wakes a buried skill A into live outputs (even transiently with short re-burial half-life), immediately **crystallize** the awakened behavior: evaluate the awakened shadow copy on unlabeled public probes, self-distill those awakened logits back into the main model with a stability-constrained update (or anchor its newly exposed directions), and discard `π`'s temporary slot side-effects.
- **Novelty rationale:** Unlike DER ([2004.07211](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07211)) and pre-shock escrow ([PR #113](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/113)), which must record teacher logits *before* damage occurs, witness-crystallized self-distillation *regenerates* the teacher signal **after** an unannounced shock using zero old labels and zero pre-shock logit buffer.
- **Cheapest first test:** On the width-64 MLP after an unannounced B shock masks A, compare (a) running `π` alone, (b) running `π` + self-distilling its awakened outputs on 128 unlabeled public probes into the main model, (c) pre-shock DER/escrow (upper-bound rival that pays pre-shock bytes), and (d) labelled relearning at equal gradient steps. Kill if crystallization does not extend re-burial half-life `τ` by ≥ 2× over `π` alone or collapses onto wrong priors.
- **Old-task retention check:** Continue shared B for 50 more steps after crystallization; score fresh live A, unrelated C, and held-out B, checking that distilling on public probes does not overwrite B or C.

#### 19. Bounded-burial-depth continual learning (`L* ≤ K` instead of `L* = 0`) (P)

- **Mechanism:** Relax standard continual learning's `L* = 0` requirement (keeping every old skill immediately live during B training). Instead, allow B training to temporarily mask old skill A **as long as** at least one sufficient carrier set `S ∈ H(A)` stays intact and a cheap pre-registered label-free witness `π` keeps `L*(A) ≤ K` (e.g., `K = 2`). Wake A with `π` (and optionally crystallize) only when A service is requested or at task boundaries.
- **Novelty rationale:** Standard CL (EWC, OGD, DER, replay) pays a steep stability–plasticity tax to keep all old tasks live at every intermediate B step. Trading immediate `L* = 0` liveness for *bounded label-free recoverability* (`L* ≤ K`) is a fundamentally different CL objective—distinct from adapter/checkpoint switching (L1/PackNet) because all skills share the same weights and wake via a label-free continuation rather than stored per-task parameters.
- **Cheapest first test:** On a 3-task incompatible/partially-conflicting MLP stream (`A → B`, plus untouched C), compare `L* ≤ 2` carrier-guarded B training + witness wake-up against EWC, OGD, and equal-byte DER constrained to keep `L* = 0` throughout B. Measure the `(post-wake A accuracy, final B accuracy)` Pareto frontier. Kill if `L* ≤ 2` does not strictly expand the Pareto frontier over `L* = 0` OGD/EWC at matched compute and bytes.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score live A after wake-up, live B *after* waking A (verifying wake-up does not erase B's plasticity gain), and live C throughout.

#### 20. Orthogonal wake-up multiplexing for `N` dormant skills (P)

- **Mechanism:** When `N` old skills `A₁, …, A_N` are allowed to go dormant (`L* ≥ 1`) inside shared weights and slots during B training, assign or search for `N` distinct short label-free witnesses `π₁, …, π_N` such that running `π_i` wakes `A_i` into live service while **not** forming a hitting set on the carrier hypergraphs `H(A_j)` of the other dormant skills `j ≠ i`.
- **Novelty rationale:** Prompt-pool CL (L2P/DualPrompt) and LoRA banks add dedicated parameters per skill; L1 prompt-addressable checkpoints failed in the ledger because packing versions into one LoRA under tags lost to stored deltas. Here the "address" is a *label-free training continuation* operating on shared state, and the non-interference condition is explicitly combinatorial (non-hitting on sibling carrier hypergraphs).
- **Cheapest first test:** Train `N = 4` binary obligations on the width-64 MLP, bury them under shared B, and test whether 4 distinct 2-step witnesses can wake `A₁ → A₂ → A₃ → A₄` sequentially (in random order) without erasing later skills' recoverability. Compare against equal-byte stored per-skill deltas (L1's killer control) and naive sequential witness execution without carrier guards. Kill if waking `A₁` destroys `A₂..A₄` or if stored deltas strictly dominate at equal bytes.
- **Old-task retention check:** After waking all `N` skills in sequence and returning to B, verify every `A_i`, B, and unrelated C remain above floor.

#### 21. Witness-driven carrier compaction for dormant skills (P)

- **Mechanism:** Once an old skill A is dormant (`L* ≥ 1`) and has multiple redundant minimal carrier sets `S₁, S₂, S₃ ∈ H(A)`, deliberately **compact** it by releasing/overwriting all redundant carriers down to the single *minimum-byte* sufficient carrier set `S*` (which may mix a tiny weight slice with a long-lived `v` slot) that still passes a bounded witness `π`, freeing the reclaimed parameter and slot capacity for B.
- **Novelty rationale:** PackNet/HAT freeze sparse weight masks for *live* `L* = 0` service; [PR #134](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/134) card #22 reclaims obligations that are logically redundant with other live rules. This compacts a *dormant* skill to its minimum witness-recoverable weight+slot footprint, testing whether dormant storage is strictly cheaper in bytes than live storage.
- **Cheapest first test:** On the 12-group MLP, measure the minimum byte size `|S*|` of a witness-recoverable carrier set vs. the minimum byte size of a live (`L* = 0`) subnetwork mask at matched post-wake A accuracy, then train B on the freed capacity. Compare against PackNet-style live masking and equal-byte DER. Kill if `|S*|` is never smaller than the live subnetwork or if B gains no accuracy from the freed blocks.
- **Old-task retention check:** After B trains on the freed blocks, execute `π` and score fresh live A, unrelated C, and held-out B; reject if compaction silently drops rare A edge cases.

#### 22. Online witness-search amortizer (learned continuation proposer) (I)

- **Mechanism:** Maintain a lightweight bandit/proposer over the witness grammar `Π` that maps cheap, target-blind state summaries (e.g., layer-wise `m`/`v` norm ratios, public-B gradient cosine, and recent operation class from [PR #134](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/134)) to a ranked top-`k` shortlist of candidate witnesses—amortizing search cost across repeated shifts in a long stream.
- **Novelty rationale:** Explicitly an **improvement (I)** that turns expensive witness search into an O(1)-proposal deployment operation; must beat a fixed best-on-dev witness, random `Π` sampling, and [2501.13453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.13453)'s fixed irrelevant-task recipe on *unseen* B shift types.
- **Cheapest first test:** Generate 30 sequential shift episodes (mixing spurious-shift, slot-drain, and feature-rotation B phases) in the width-64 MLP; train the amortizer on 20 episodes and test search-budget-to-recovery on 10 held-out shift episodes against fixed witness, random search, and blind self-bifurcation search (card #2). Kill if the amortizer does not cut search rollouts by ≥ 3× on held-out shifts at matched recovery.
- **Old-task retention check:** Score live A, C, and held-out B across the 10 held-out shift episodes, charging the amortizer's memory and update cost in the shared budget.

## 5. Ranked deeper questions

1. **Can a deployment learner select a recovery witness *completely blind to A labels* and still beat plain continued B and common-shift removal?**
   In paired `A−`/`A+` audit mode, proving non-erasure with a witness is clean. At deployment, choosing `π ∈ Π` without A labels is the hardest bottleneck: if every blind selector (self-bifurcation, structural-key mining, universal wake-up words) either leaks into prior/temperature shifts (ledger A3) or ties single-direction common-shift removal ([2610.08718](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08718)) and momentum reset ([2610.03940](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03940)), then witnesses are a *diagnostic proof tool*, not a label-free repair primitive.
2. **Does a real `L* ≥ 2` nonlinearly buried regime exist in natural neural training, or is almost all label-free recoverability `L* = 1`?**
   [PR #114](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/114) showed differential fork reads have no hidden channel in a linear host, and [2608.19762](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.19762) captures one-step and linearized `(w, m, v)` dynamics. The make-or-break empirical question is whether ordinary Adam/SGD training on a nonlinear network actually spends a substantial window in `L* ≥ 2`—where raw logits, first gradients, one-update endpoints, and linearized ISO response all fail, yet a 2–4 step label-free witness restores live A.
3. **Are minimal sufficient carrier sets genuinely joint (`w` × optimizer slots) and multi-set (hypergraph), or do they collapse to weight-only marginal salience?**
   If real training rarely creates synergistic `{w_i, m_j}` or `{w_i, v_j}` carriers—or if competing minimal sets overlap so heavily that EWC/diagonal-Fisher protection already hits the same coordinates—then hypergraph hitting-set protection collapses to an incremental variant of Fisher regularization.
4. **Can a label-free witness repair be made *dynamically durable* under continued B training (`τ_witness ≈ τ_label`), or is it always shallow re-alignment?**
   Even if a witness wakes live A cleanly at the cut, a repair that collapses again after two B steps is useless in a continual stream. Testing whether post-shock witness-crystallized self-distillation (card #18) closes the re-burial half-life gap against labelled relearning determines whether label-free witnesses can replace any real fraction of replay.
5. **Does bounded-burial-depth CL (`L* ≤ K` with one protected carrier set) strictly beat `L* = 0` CL on the retention–plasticity frontier without collateral damage to C?**
   This is the highest-upside payoff of combining both halves of the node: if tolerating temporary masking during B while enforcing a non-hitting constraint on `H(A)` lets B learn substantially faster than EWC/OGD/DER—and waking A on demand leaves both B and unrelated C intact—then continual learning gains a genuinely new operating regime between live preservation and checkpoint storage.
