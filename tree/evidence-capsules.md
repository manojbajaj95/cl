# Evidence capsules: transfer the grounds, not the skill

Parent: [Harness self-testing skills, next-node #1](https://github.com/manojbajaj95/cl/pull/65). Question: **what is the least evidence that lets a new reader reconstruct what to do and when to do it, without seeing the writer's answer?** Ideation only; no experiments were run.

## Root cause: a result is not an identification witness

A successful execution is compatible with the right procedure, a lucky shortcut, or an irrelevant step. A WITH/WITHOUT result on the same states narrows the old reader's utility, but need not separate those explanations or cover states where their prescriptions differ. A procedure with a guard states the winner of this inference without preserving the observations that made its rivals lose. A fresh reader then has to trust the writer's choice of variables, its procedure, and its extrapolation all at once. More replay of the same aliased states does not help.

There are **three separate questions**: which actions achieve the goal here (world validity); can this reader reconstruct and execute those actions (reader competence); and are they worth loading relative to this reader's unaided alternative (deployment utility)? No source-only gain scalar identifies all three. A reader swap can change competence and unaided performance without changing world validity. A tool swap can break validity without changing the reader. Carrying a proof of one is not a proof of the others.

## Candidate primitive: a portable identification witness

`derive(capsule, reader, local_anchor) -> guarded_program | probe_request | unresolved(rivals)`

The artifact is the input to derivation, not an annotated copy of the answer. A derived skill may be cached, but it can be thrown away and regenerated. A first, inspectable interface:

1. **Grounding:** goal/outcome semantics, action and observation roles, and read-only or sandboxed ways to bind those roles locally. List what must stay invariant and where that binding was observed, assumed or never checked. A schema-name match is not a semantics check.
2. **Discrimination:** identified starting states, available interventions or traces with their controls, measured outcomes and uncertainty, and the assignment/selection rule. Carry evidence for the *steps* (e.g. an order intervention) and *scope* (e.g. a crossed state that defeats a shortcut). Preserve “not observed” separately from “observed failure.” Evidence can refer to a shared checker, but the prerequisite and cost count; a reference to the author or its old private log is not portable.
3. **Decode contract:** a public candidate language or rival family, a notion of behavioral equivalence, the declared query region, and a rule to request a new local observation or abstain if compatible rivals disagree there. No per-skill solution, guard, hidden labels, or writer-specific reasoning trace in this contract. Local probes can establish binding/validity; any claim about the new reader's *utility* also needs its own baseline or an explicit transfer assumption.

**Minimum is relative, not universal.** Fix a candidate family, a class of readers with a shared observable interface, a query region, and an allowed target-probe budget. In the deterministic finite case, a capsule is sufficient if all hypotheses consistent with the witnesses and target answers prescribe equivalent behaviors on the query region, including when not to act. For exact identification of a known oracle behavior using a fixed nonadaptive witness pool with additive costs, the minimum is a weighted set cover: cover each behavior-disagreeing rival with at least one witness that rules it out. This is a specification for a minimum, not an efficient algorithm or a guarantee that an actual reader decodes it. With noise, alternatives need separation at a declared error level; with unrestricted readers or absent shared grounding, universal portability is impossible. Report a bytes–target-interactions–error frontier, charging shared decoders and target probing rather than moving the answer into uncounted infrastructure.

**Indistinguishability example, not a run.** A toy buffer shows `friendly_banner=f` and a `batch_capable=b` flag. Both are true in every successful batch demonstration and both false in every fallback trace. Two compatible policies batch iff `f` or iff `b`. They disagree when `f=true, b=false`; no same-support compression or reader can infer which policy is correct from those rows alone (two equally likely worlds give average forced-choice error at least 1/2 on that query). A matched dry-run showing that batching fails and single writes succeed in the crossed state would break this tie. It still would not identify whether batching requires `append -> commit` rather than `commit -> append`; that needs a distinct contrast on a supported state unless the action semantics already imply it. If the target hides its capability semantics and provides no safe discriminating probe, abstention there is the honest output, at a coverage cost.

### Common scorecard for any future test

- Delete the old procedure, its guard, solved scripts, and writer state in the evidence-only arm. Test held-out combinations and at least one held-out reader family or tool binding. Score **joint** recovery: a behaviorally equivalent execution when applicable, and correct non-use when not, on the *same* hidden task family. Also report false authorization, abstention coverage, and net benefit vs the new reader's unaided baseline separately. Expect retained invariants and recurrence, not retention of a deliberately corrected claim.
- Strong arms: scoped procedure + guard (+ executable contract if available), randomly selected and optimized demonstrations at matched bytes, raw log as a reference, and a query-budget-matched cold-start synthesizer. Include a no-anchor or shuffled-contrast negative control. A reader may synthesize the same answer from its priors; randomize task semantics and require performance above the no-evidence arm. Separate capsule-provided facts from what is given to all readers by the benchmark.
- Freeze hidden task/reader splits and probes before authoring. Hold persistent bytes, inference tokens, and target queries fixed where possible; report separate curves otherwise. After sequentially adding/revising capsules, score fixed unrelated families and recurrence of earlier, unchanged regimes at common checkpoints. These tests are proposals, not results.

## Raw ideas — no cuts or ranking

All are **ambitious but doable** as small falsifiers. `P` = candidate primitive (new reusable object/operation if it works); `I` = likely an improvement using an existing operation. Novelty lines are hypotheses, not claims of priority.

### Identification and decoding

**EC01 · Joint decision teaching number (P).** *Mechanism:* Encode the shortest weighted witness set that eliminates every rival guarded policy making different decisions in scope, with a coverage table and witness cost counted once across procedure and guard. *Why new / risk:* Teaching dimension and the parent's minimum experiment already supply the ingredients; the proposed quantity prices joint behavioral identification across readers, not fit to a writer's routing labels. It may reduce entirely to multi-learner teaching. *Cheapest first test:* Enumerate a two-action/two-cue DSL and compare exact cover with separately minimized procedure and guard sets at equal bytes; require exact recovery on a withheld crossed state. *Retention:* Recheck the first DSL family's action and scope after adding a second family to the same store.

**EC02 · A nonportability certificate (P).** *Mechanism:* Ship two concrete worlds or semantic bindings that reproduce the available evidence but require opposite actions, plus their disagreement region and what observation could separate them. Let a verifier check the pair before it suppresses any action. *Why new / risk:* Makes failure of migration a checkable artifact rather than a confidence score; analogous to countermodels/transport impossibility, but the consumer gets a narrowed capability instead of a global rejection. *Cheapest first test:* Give a tiny verifier aliased buffer logs with either an actual counter-world or an invented one; compare false authorization and coverage with an uncertainty threshold. *Retention:* The certified restriction must leave the first buffer's unambiguous state decisions intact.

**EC03 · Factorial licensing rectangles (P).** *Mechanism:* Package four outcomes crossing a procedure edit (e.g. swap action order) with one world-condition edit at matched starting states. Synthesize a branch only when the action contrast changes as predicted with the condition. *Why new / risk:* Ordinary with/without estimates change skill presence; this object separates *which step* mattered from *where* it mattered. Factorial design is old; the claim is that a packed rectangle transfers both pieces better than matched one-factor traces. *Cheapest first test:* Use a toy order-sensitive buffer with a cosmetic cue and compare the rectangle with four natural success/failure traces of the same size. *Retention:* Preserve execution on an earlier order-insensitive buffer after ingesting the rectangle.

**EC04 · Two-key authorization (P).** *Mechanism:* First prove the target world matches the evidence on a semantic anchor; then have the new reader materialize the relevant action fragment in a hidden dry-run scored by an independent checker. Both keys are needed to authorize the derived fragment. *Why new / risk:* Separates world binding and reader reconstruction, rather than treating success of one end-to-end canary as evidence for both. Risks becoming a conventional conformance suite. *Cheapest first test:* Cross an API rename/semantic break with a competent/step-dropping reader and compare failure attribution and coverage to a generic paired verifier. *Retention:* The old reader and unchanged API keep their original authorizations and task score.

**EC05 · Bidirectional witness lenses (P).** *Mechanism:* A typed lens lifts a source witness to a renamed or refactored target interface and maps the target observation back into its original outcome vocabulary. It must preserve the contrasts the witness was selected to distinguish, not merely round-trip strings. *Why new / risk:* Unlike procedure translation, the transport unit is an evidentiary distinction preserved under a checked mapping; model-based refinement may already cover the formal case. *Cheapest first test:* Map a two-call file mock to a schema with renamed fields and one semantically reversed status; compare outcome decoding with name alignment and guard rewriting. *Retention:* Round-trip the original witnesses and require unchanged scope and action scores on source recurrences.

**EC06 · Confluence sheets (P).** *Mechanism:* Carry only witnessed dependency, commutation and order-sensitive pairs; reconstruct any legal topological ordering, plus a witness for modes in which an edge appears. *Why new / risk:* Proof-carrying workflow patterns are established; the capsule stores observations from which order constraints and conditional exceptions can be rediscovered, not the ordering itself. *Cheapest first test:* In a four-step document workflow make two steps commute in one mode and not another; compare a sheet to one complete trace per mode. *Retention:* An unrelated earlier workflow must still permit both previously valid orderings.

**EC07 · Evidence as a noisy reader channel (P).** *Mechanism:* Select witnesses whose signatures for competing guarded programs have a minimum Hamming distance under known forms of reader erasure. Decode the policy only within that radius, else return an erasure. *Why new / risk:* Functional syndromes encode model behavior; here redundancy is specifically in evidence that rules out rival procedures or guards, with explicit misreading tolerance. Generic error-correcting teaching may tie. *Cheapest first test:* Drop a witness column from controlled reader inputs and compare distance-coded vs repeated or random witnesses at equal bytes. *Retention:* When nothing was dropped, old-reader recovery and load coverage must not decline.

**EC08 · An executable experiment remainder (P).** *Mechanism:* Represent unfinished identification as a decision tree over *differences between compatible policies*. Compile a capsule prefix offline; once bound, a new reader can buy only the unanswered branch whose observation is needed to decode its live local policy. *Why new / risk:* Active diagnosis and the parent's carried experiment are close; the object here is a resumable derivation, with unresolved leaves and already paid-for observations, not a predictor of load probability. *Cheapest first test:* Use four rival policies and three yes/no tool queries with asymmetric prices; compare live queries and joint recovery against scratch active synthesis and an equal-budget fixed suite. *Retention:* Old regimes answerable from the prefix must retain their score without new queries.

### Evidence algebra and transport

**EC09 · Objective polytope (P).** *Mechanism:* Carry unscalarized intervention outcomes (success, time, calls, repair cost) and uncertainties; derive the policy together with the region of local objective weights where it wins. *Why new / risk:* Multi-objective inverse planning is old; the portable object is a reusable license indexed by utility rather than the writer's fitted scalar benefit. A full metrics log may tie. *Cheapest first test:* Use two valid workflows with reversed latency and call costs, change target weights without relabeling, and compare to a scalar-delta capsule. *Retention:* Returning to the old objective must recover its old choices and success.

**EC10 · Censoring envelope (P).** *Mechanism:* Record where intervention outcomes were unobservable or structurally never sampled, assignment probabilities when known, and bounds on what missing results could change. Mark observationally unidentified regions instead of using negative labels. *Why new / risk:* Imports partial identification into the stored skill license; unlike gain calibration, it tells a reader which parts of a guard cannot be inferred even with unlimited replay of this log. *Cheapest first test:* Use a biased policy whose expensive fallback is unobserved in one state, then compare bounded abstention to complete-case and propensity-weighted baselines. *Retention:* Keep the old supported region's joint score and coverage.

**EC11 · Transport kernels (P).** *Mechanism:* Factor the witness likelihood into mechanisms, mark which are allowed to change, and compile the smallest set of new measurements needed to reweight or replace just those factors. Emit a nonidentification certificate if no available measurement closes the gap. *Why new / risk:* Causal transportability is the direct ancestor and may supply the entire algorithm. The additional claim is an independently shippable kernel for deriving both effective procedure and guard, rather than transporting one specified effect. *Cheapest first test:* In a three-mechanism toy pipeline swap one transition and compare target queries against full retesting and standard effect transport plus synthesis. *Retention:* Held-out decisions for the two invariant mechanisms should survive the update.

**EC12 · Witness join with an interaction tax (P).** *Mechanism:* Join two capsules by projecting each on shared assumptions, then obtain only the cross-witnesses needed to exclude composition-specific rivals. Return the unlicensed joint scope if the tax cannot be paid. *Why new / risk:* Composing two tools or contracts is not new. The join must acquire counterfactual information missing from both parents, not train on labels provided for free by their outputs. *Cheapest first test:* Two individually valid operations share a scratch state only in one mode; compare targeted join witnesses to a contract-only join and a full joint resynthesis. *Retention:* The two parent skills retain their independent action and scope scores after the join.

**EC13 · Evidence subtraction (P).** *Mechanism:* Index each eliminated rival by the witnesses that exclude it. When a witness is withdrawn, resurrect exactly the unsupported alternatives and recompute the affected action region without asking a reader to forget a conclusion wholesale. *Why new / risk:* Truth maintenance and the harness node's dependency tracking are close; subtraction acts on derivation support and can selectively unlearn a guard while retaining its procedure. *Cheapest first test:* Retract a mislabeled record shared by three toy skills with different redundant support, compare behavior to whole-skill rollback and clean recompute. *Retention:* Two still-supported old decisions must survive and remain correct on fresh instances.

**EC14 · Refutation grafts (P).** *Mechanism:* Preserve an old witness set and append one locally evidenced distinction that splits a guarded program's equivalence class when regimes recur. The output is two context-indexed derivations, not the new text overwriting the old text. *Why new / risk:* Counterexample-guided rewrite already exists and is killed in the ledger as stated; the proposed object is a new distinguishable partition, rebuilt from old and new evidence with neither answer stored. *Cheapest first test:* Stream A→B→A with a reused superficial cue; compare an equal-byte graft to a text rewrite with retrieved failures. *Retention:* Recover regime A's held-out scope and procedure on recurrence without another update.

**EC15 · Evidence rebase across checker versions (P).** *Mechanism:* When the outcome API changes, carry a bridge with overlapping old/new checker readings, direction-specific comparability, and the witness fields whose interpretation is no longer known. Re-derive under the new checker instead of silently reusing pass bits. *Why new / risk:* Schema migration is known; the unit being rebased is the *exclusion of a rival*, so a checker version can invalidate some permissions without invalidating the procedure everywhere. *Cheapest first test:* Upgrade an exact-output scorer to one requiring a side-effect invariant; use only overlapping witness readings to reconstruct the surviving scope. *Retention:* The unchanged output-only tasks keep their old results under the old-scored recurrence evaluation.

**EC16 · Quotient-preserving surrogate evidence (P).** *Mechanism:* Replace a lengthy or sensitive original trace with synthetic witnesses whose checker outputs exclude the same behavioral rivals over a declared scope. Certify equivalence of the two exclusion sets before discarding the original; abstain from out-of-class claims. *Why new / risk:* Dataset distillation and compression preserve a fit or target answers; preserve the identified set, including unresolved rivals, so the synthetic witness cannot quietly narrow the guard. Could tie against generic specification minimization. *Cheapest first test:* Exhaustively synthesize short witnesses for a toy DSL and compare to matched-token summaries under identifier renaming and two reader heuristics. *Retention:* Both readers recover the old valid branch under later synthetic evidence additions.

### Verification, extension and continual use

**EC17 · Proof of re-derivation (P).** *Mechanism:* Require a reader to emit a lightweight derivation certificate: each rejected guard/action is linked to an observed inconsistency and the final candidate agrees with the checked evidence. Deletions or hidden swaps of a decisive witness must change the certificate or the supported output. *Why new / risk:* Proof-carrying code checks supplied code; this checks whether the evidence warrants the behavior class, not how the reader privately arrived at it. Synthetic evidence identifiers must not leak the expected rule. *Cheapest first test:* Give the same pretraining-friendly cue with swapped witness outcomes, require a checker-accepted change, and compare to an answer-plus-citation reader. *Retention:* After swapping only one capsule's witness, certificates and answers for the old independent families still pass.

**EC18 · Out-of-language escape hatch (P).** *Mechanism:* On evidence inconsistent with every member of the current grammar, return the smallest conflicting witness set; grow a single new production capable of explaining it, and store the extension only with witnesses distinguishing it from old productions. *Why new / risk:* [DreamCoder](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.08381) and synthesis grammar induction are close, but here language expansion is licensed by a concrete contradiction and carried as part of migration, not just optimized for fit or reuse. *Cheapest first test:* Add a missing reset instruction to a tiny workflow family and compare blame/extension to simply increasing DSL depth. *Retention:* Every prior trace and guarded decision must retain a valid old derivation after expansion.

**EC19 · Anchor triangulation without a common dictionary (P).** *Mechanism:* Bind two vocabularies by overlapping observations from several independently typed capsules. Each capsule admits multiple symbol matchings; solve their intersection, and expose residual symmetries as no-license regions. *Why new / risk:* Graph alignment itself is old; changing one capsule can make several *individually nonportable* witness sets jointly portable. Avoid claiming sheaf-style inconsistency detection alone as new. *Cheapest first test:* Hide operation names in three toy sensors with overlapping invariants and remove one overlap; compare to per-skill name matching and a joint graph aligner. *Retention:* Adding an ambiguous sensor must not rotate the already identified old bindings or alter their policy.

**EC20 · Cross-substrate derivation (P).** *Mechanism:* Derive the same guarded behavior from one capsule into a prompt, finite-state policy, or small adapter, with identical external verification and no precompiled artifact shipped. Apply the capsule again in a different substrate when a stored representation becomes unreadable. *Why new / risk:* Diff and IR transport already exist; here no old skill, trace activations, or adapter travels, and intervention-conditioned equivalence is the shared contract. It may just be standard multi-target compilation if the checker does all the work. *Cheapest first test:* Compile a held-out guarded string rewrite from the same synthetic evidence to two prompt readers and a tiny enumerative machine, compare against migrating the generated program. *Retention:* Check the first rewrite after switching substrate and installing a second rewrite in the same store.

**EC21 · Sufficient outcome channels for policy re-pricing (I).** *Mechanism:* Carry observable primitives that bound classes of future scores (e.g. checksum state, idempotence, retry count), and mark which scores are irretrievably unknown when the raw trace is dropped. Optimize witness retention over both discriminating power and future rescorability. *Why new / risk:* EC09 changes weights over known outcomes; this changes what can be *measured* from stored observations. Audit-log design may already give the same benefit. *Cheapest first test:* Change the goal from final value only to final value plus idempotence; compare a minimal observable basis to terminal pass/fail and equally sized random event logs. *Retention:* Original scoring must be reconstructed exactly and preserve old action/abstain decisions where the original goal is still requested.

**EC22 · Horizon-bearing witnesses (P).** *Mechanism:* Carry an action contrast through the earliest convergence or delayed divergence of its two paths, with a stated right-censor point. Authorize the procedure for no longer a horizon than the observed or proven effect. *Why new / risk:* Temporal abstraction already handles delayed effects; the distinctive object is a *duration of evidentiary support* separable from the duration of the action or option. *Cheapest first test:* In a mock cache, two invalidation tactics tie immediately but diverge after a reorder. Compare shortest revealing prefixes against terminal-only and matched-size full traces. *Retention:* On the previous non-reordering regime both previously warranted short-horizon choices must remain legal.

**EC23 · Independence-bearing evidence (I).** *Mechanism:* Attach experimental lineage, such as shared seed, source model and starting checkpoint, before compression. Score distinct evidence lineages, not a count of near-duplicate rows; change the conclusion only when an independent witness supports it. *Why new / risk:* Hierarchical uncertainty is standard; portability without spurious confidence is the incremental gain, particularly when multiple stores silently derive from one old run. *Cheapest first test:* Clone one run into ten paraphrased witnesses and add one independently matched adverse result; compare derived scope to iid voting and exact deduplication. *Retention:* The old family backed by independent replicates must maintain its acceptance and action score.

**EC24 · Compile the common safe prefix (P).** *Mechanism:* Rather than reject a partly identified program, intersect compatible policies on observable prefixes, execute only reversible agreed steps, and pause at the first branch whose outcomes differ. Return the exact question or reversible probe needed for continuation. *Why new / risk:* Active planning with safe prefixes is known; the operation compiles a useful partial skill directly out of residual evidence ambiguity, without picking an arbitrary full policy. EC02 is the certificate, this is the planner that consumes it. *Cheapest first test:* Use three guards sharing setup but diverging at an irreversible commit in a sandbox; compare completion, false commits and repeat effort to full abstention and MAP policy. *Retention:* Once the missing evidence is restored, previously certified full workflows must still complete without redundant pauses.

## Ranked deeper sub-questions

1. **What makes joint procedure-and-guard identification possible at all?** Derive tight evidence/query lower bounds for a guarded workflow family with reader/tool symmetries. EC01–EC03 ask whether there is a useful separation from ordinary teaching dimension or only a change of vocabulary.
2. **What is the least shared anchor that makes evidence portable?** Characterize when local grounding identifies the writer's action, state and outcome semantics, versus when target evidence is indispensable. EC04, EC05 and EC19 give competing routes from a fixed interface to jointly inferred bindings.
3. **Can capsules be joined or retracted without reopening unrelated knowledge?** Find exact conditions for a join or subtraction to alter only the implicated behavior class, and a lower bound on new cross-witnesses when assumptions interact (EC12–EC15).
4. **Can we preserve an identified set after deleting the original traces?** Find when a synthetic or noisy-channel code can preserve both warranted knowledge and warranted uncertainty for a reader held out at write time (EC07, EC16–EC17).
5. **How much useful action survives partial identification?** Derive the best reversible prefix and minimal paid probe needed to complete it, without mistaking abstention for retention or useful adaptation for overreach (EC08, EC22, EC24).
